Bottom line總結 Dubai is five months into the third downturn of its modern history, and the trigger this time is neither credit nor supply. It is a war. Israeli and American strikes on Iran began on 28 February 2026, the Strait of Hormuz closed on 4 March, and the ValuStrat price index has fallen 10% since. Transaction data shows where the damage landed: second-hand sales collapsed 59% year-on-year in the second quarter to 8,512 deals, while off-plan sales, cushioned by developer payment plans, fell only 12% and now account for 76% of all residential transactions.

That split is the whole diagnosis. Eighty-six per cent of Dubai purchases settle in cash, and mortgage transactions were still growing 16% year-on-year as prices fell. Credit is not the binding constraint here — which makes Dubai the structural opposite of Bangkok, where a mortgage rejection rate near 70% is the entire story. What binds in Dubai is the delivery schedule: 77,500 units scheduled for 2026, 146,400 for 2027 and 120,100 for 2028, against a standing stock of roughly 743,000. Whether that pipeline arrives on time is the single question that decides the next three years, and the first-quarter delivery ratio of 42.6% is the reason it has not decided them yet.

The long series should temper any conclusion drawn from the last five years. In nominal terms the BIS index reached 175.4 in the first quarter of 2024, having last stood at 174.8 in the fourth quarter of 2014 — a decade of no nominal gain. Adjusted for inflation the same index sits 13.2% below its 2014 peak and 8.1% below its 2008 peak. Two crashes of 36% and 30% are already in this record. The structural drivers behind all of this are set out in the companion UAE Macro × Property analysis. 杜拜正處於其現代史上第三次下行的第五個月，本次觸發因子既非信用亦非供給，而是戰爭。以色列與美國於 2026 年 2 月 28 日對伊朗發動攻擊，荷莫茲海峽於 3 月 4 日關閉，ValuStrat 價格指數自此累計下跌 10%。成交資料指出損害的落點：第二季二手成交年減 59%，降至 8,512 件；同期受開發商付款計畫緩衝的預售成交僅年減 12%，並占全部住宅成交的 76%。

此一分歧即為本市場的診斷結論。杜拜 86% 的購置以現金交割，且房價下跌期間房貸成交仍年增 16%。信用並非本市場的約束條件，此點使杜拜在結構上與曼谷完全相反：後者的全部解釋落在接近 70% 的房貸退件率。杜拜的約束在交屋排程：2026 年排程 77,500 戶、2027 年 146,400 戶、2028 年 120,100 戶，對照約 743,000 戶的既有存量。該批供給能否如期到位，為決定未來三年的單一問題；第一季 42.6% 的交屋達成率，則是該問題尚未有答案的原因。

長序列足以節制由近五年資料得出的任何結論。以名目計，BIS 指數於 2024 年第一季達 175.4，而上一次相同水準為 2014 年第四季的 174.8，十年之間名目零增值。經物價調整後，同一指數低於 2014 年高點 13.2%，低於 2008 年高點 8.1%。此一紀錄中已包含兩次分別為 36% 與 30% 的下跌。相關結構成因詳見《阿聯總經 × 房產》。

I · Where the Market Stands第一部 · 市場現況

Sixteen years of nominal gain, and no real gain at all十六年的名目上漲，實質報酬為零

Nominal vs real, two completed crashes名目 vs 實質，兩次完成的崩跌
  • In nominal terms the index runs from 58.4 in early 2003 to 175.4 in the first quarter of 2024, a tripling in twenty-one years. Taken alone that reads as one of the strongest property markets on record.以名目計，指數自 2003 年初的 58.4 升至 2024 年第一季的 175.4，二十一年間增為三倍。單就此一數列判讀，本市場屬紀錄上表現最強者之一。
  • The same series contains two completed crashes. The first ran from 151.4 in the second quarter of 2008 to 96.1 in the fourth quarter of 2010, a fall of 36.5%. The second ran from 174.8 in the fourth quarter of 2014 to 122.0 in the third quarter of 2020, a fall of 30.2% spread over six years with no single triggering event.同一序列包含兩次完成的崩跌。第一次自 2008 年第二季的 151.4 跌至 2010 年第四季的 96.1，跌幅 36.5%。第二次自 2014 年第四季的 174.8 跌至 2020 年第三季的 122.0，跌幅 30.2%，歷時六年且無單一觸發事件。
  • Adjusted for inflation the picture inverts. The real index peaked at 156.2 in 2008, made a higher peak of 165.4 in 2014, and stood at 143.5 in the first quarter of 2024. Sixteen years after the 2008 high, real prices are 8.1% below it. Ten years after the 2014 high, they are 13.2% below that.扣除物價後，圖像完全相反。實質指數 2008 年見頂 156.2，2014 年創更高點 165.4，至 2024 年第一季為 143.5。距 2008 年高點十六年，實質房價低於該高點 8.1%；距 2014 年高點十年，低於該高點 13.2%。

What the nominal line is actually measuring名目線實際衡量的內容

UAE consumer prices rose 12.3% in 2008 and fell 4.7% in 2009; they were negative in 2019, 2020 and 2021, and rose 4.8% in 2022. Housing is a heavy weight in that basket, so the deflation years and the years of falling rent are largely the same years. The consequence for an owner is that the nominal index and the real index diverge most precisely when the market is weakest, which is when the nominal number is quoted most often.阿聯消費者物價 2008 年上漲 12.3%，2009 年下跌 4.7%；2019、2020 與 2021 年為負值，2022 年上漲 4.8%。住宅於該指數中權重甚高，故通縮年份與租金下跌年份大致重疊。對持有人的意義為：名目指數與實質指數的背離，恰好在市場最弱時達到最大，而該期間亦為名目數字被引用最頻繁的時候。

Practical rule for this market: a Dubai price quoted without a deflator is not a return figure. Two of the three cycles in this series delivered a nominal gain and a real loss to anyone who bought at the wrong point in them.本市場的操作原則：未經平減的杜拜房價不構成報酬數字。本序列三個循環中有兩個，對進場時點不當者而言，同時產生名目增值與實質減損。

Same downturn, two official answers同一次下跌，兩個官方答案

The Dubai Land Department publishes its own residential sales price index, rebased to 2012 = 1 and running monthly from March 2011. On that measure the market peaked at 1.306 in May 2015 and bottomed at 1.070 in June 2021, a decline of 18.1%. The BIS series for the same downturn peaks in the fourth quarter of 2014 and falls 30.2% to the third quarter of 2020. The two series disagree on the timing of the peak by roughly nine months and on the depth of the fall by twelve percentage points.杜拜土地局發布其自有住宅銷售價格指數，基期改設為 2012＝1，月頻資料自 2011 年 3 月起。依該指數，市場於 2015 年 5 月見頂 1.306，2021 年 6 月觸底 1.070，跌幅 18.1%。BIS 序列就同一次下跌之高點落於 2014 年第四季，至 2020 年第三季跌幅 30.2%。兩者對高點時點的判定相差約九個月，對跌幅的判定相差十二個百分點。

Neither is wrong. They weight the transaction mix differently, and in a market where three-quarters of activity is off-plan, the mix is not a detail. A period in which cheap outlying off-plan units dominate registrations will pull a transaction-weighted index down without any individual asset repricing. The reconciling point is that the direction and the turning points agree; the magnitude does not. Neither series has been updated past 2024, which is why the current-cycle numbers on this page come from ValuStrat and REIDIN instead.兩者均無錯誤，差異來自成交結構的加權方式；而在四分之三活動屬預售的市場，成交結構並非細節。若某期間登記案件以外圍低價預售為主，交易加權指數即會下移，而個別資產未必重新定價。可資調和之處為：兩序列在方向與轉折時點上一致，在幅度上不一致。兩者均未更新至 2024 年以後，此即本頁當期數字改採 ValuStrat 與 REIDIN 的原因。

The absence of property tax is not the absence of holding cost沒有房產稅，不等於沒有持有成本

The UAE levies no annual property tax, no capital gains tax and no income tax on rent. That is a genuine structural advantage over every market covered on this site. The offsetting item is that the service charge performs the same function as a property tax without any of its constraints: it is set by the owners' association and the developer rather than by statute, it is not capped, and it has no published rate schedule. On a AED 1.5m apartment at AED 15 per square foot over 900 square feet, the annual charge is AED 13,500, roughly 0.9% of value — a higher effective rate than Malaysian assessment and quit rent combined.阿聯不課年度房產稅、資本利得稅，租金收入亦不課所得稅。相對本站涵蓋的所有市場，此為實質的結構性優勢。相抵的項目在於，社區服務費履行與房產稅相同的功能，卻不受相同的約束：其費率由業主委員會與開發商決定而非法定，無上限，亦無公布之費率表。以 AED 150 萬、面積 900 平方英尺之公寓、每平方英尺 AED 15 元計，年費為 AED 13,500，約當價值的 0.9%，其有效稅率高於馬來西亞門牌稅與地價稅之合計。

Where Dubai sits杜拜的位置

On a gross basis Dubai is the highest-yielding market in this comparison set, ahead of New York and Kuala Lumpur and roughly three and a half times Taipei. Two qualifications belong next to that ranking. The first is the service charge, which is not netted off in any of the published figures and is materially larger in Dubai than in the other markets shown. The second is that Dubai's rent line is the most volatile of the set: REIDIN annual rental growth for the emirate ran at 6.2% in December 2025 and 1.5% by April 2026, a deceleration of nearly five points in four months. Bangkok's rent line moves in single-digit fractions over a year. The yield is higher because the cash flow underneath it is less predictable, not in spite of that.以毛值計，杜拜為本比較組中收益率最高之市場，高於紐約與吉隆坡，約為台北的三點五倍。此一排序須附兩項限定。其一為社區服務費，各項公開數字均未予扣除，且杜拜的該項成本顯著高於圖中其他市場。其二為杜拜的租金線為本組中波動最大者：REIDIN 全酋長國住宅租金年增率 2025 年 12 月為 6.2%，2026 年 4 月降至 1.5%，四個月內減速近五個百分點；曼谷的租金線全年變動幅度僅為個位數的零頭。收益率之所以較高，成因即為其下方現金流的可預測性較低，而非與之無關。

II · The Delivery Schedule第二部 · 交屋排程

Three hundred and forty-four thousand units are scheduled across three years into a stock of seven hundred and forty-three thousand. Whether that is a threat depends entirely on a delivery ratio that has never once been met.三年排程供給 34.4 萬戶，對照 74.3 萬戶的既有存量。該批供給是否構成威脅，完全取決於一項從未達成過的交屋達成率。

Delivered vs scheduled實際交屋 vs 排程交屋
  • Scheduled deliveries are 77,500 units for 2026, 146,400 for 2027 and 120,100 for 2028. The three-year total of 344,000 is equivalent to 46% of the standing residential stock of roughly 743,000 units.排程交屋量為 2026 年 77,500 戶、2027 年 146,400 戶、2028 年 120,100 戶。三年合計 344,000 戶，相當於約 743,000 戶既有住宅存量的 46%。
  • Against that, the delivery record is modest and remarkably stable. Roughly 11,900 units completed in 2015, 14,900 in 2016, 21,700 in 2018, 28,500 in 2019, 30,900 in 2022 and 30,200 in 2024. How the Smart Rental Index works, and why it matters to a valuation智慧租金指數的運作方式，及其對估值的意義

The Dubai Land Department replaced the older RERA rental index with an AI-assisted Smart Rental Index in January 2025. Permitted increases run on a sliding scale of 5% to 20% depending on how far the current rent sits below the indexed market rent; where the sitting rent is within 10% of the index, no increase is permitted at all. A landlord may raise rent once per renewal and must give 90 days' written notice, failing which the increase is void. The 2026 revision tracks sub-communities and clusters separately.杜拜土地局於 2025 年 1 月以人工智慧輔助之智慧租金指數取代原 RERA 租金指數。可調漲幅度依現行租金低於指數市場租金的程度採 5% 至 20% 之級距；若現行租金落於指數 10% 以內，則不得調漲。房東每次續約僅得調漲一次，並須提前 90 天書面通知，未通知者調漲無效。2026 年改版改為分別追蹤次社區與群集。

The valuation consequence is under-appreciated. A yield calculated on in-place rent overstates what a new owner can actually achieve if that rent is above the current new-let market, because the index caps upward adjustment but does nothing to defend the rent when a tenant leaves. In communities absorbing new supply, the gap between contract rent and achievable rent is the single largest source of error in a Dubai yield quote.其對估值的意涵未受充分重視：若既有租約租金高於當期新租行情，依既有租金計算之收益率即高估新任持有人的可實現水準，因為該指數限制向上調整，卻不提供承租人退租時的租金保護。於正在吸納新供給的社區，契約租金與可實現租金之間的落差，為杜拜收益率報價中最大的單一誤差來源。

Why 332,000 new residents does not mean 110,000 new buyers為何 332,000 名新居民不等於 110,000 名新買方

Dividing annual population growth by an average household size of three gives roughly 111,000 households a year, against 2026 scheduled deliveries of 77,500 and a likely actual figure near 33,000. On that arithmetic the market is severely undersupplied. The arithmetic is misleading and should not be used. The majority of Dubai's population growth is low-wage labour housed in employer accommodation and shared units outside the freehold market entirely. Act可做

Completed apartments in established freehold communities with a published service-charge history and low 2026–28 pipeline exposure. Villas, and anything in the five districts absorbing a third of the 2026 pipeline. The row that decides it for most buyers is ownership form. Dubai grants freehold title including land with no building-level quota, which no other market on this site offers a foreigner.

Bottom line總結 Almost every variable that moves Dubai property is set somewhere else. The dirham has been fixed at 3.6725 to the dollar since November 1997, so the Central Bank of the UAE follows the Federal Reserve almost mechanically and has no independent instrument. The household base is roughly 88% foreign, so it is set by visa policy and by the hiring decisions of employers rather than by births — the fertility rate is 1.21 and the population still grew 88-fold in sixty-five years. And the downside, when it has been tested, has been absorbed by Abu Dhabi's balance sheet rather than by any market mechanism.

The one long series that describes the underlying economy is real GDP per capita, and it runs the wrong way. It peaked at US$117,073 in 1980, stood at US$41,605 in 2024, and is now below the 1970 level. Output grew throughout; population grew faster. That is the arithmetic behind a market where prices have never been driven by residents becoming richer, and where the growth story has always been a headcount story.

Two things have genuinely improved. The fiscal breakeven oil price has fallen from US$91.60 in 2014 to US$50.00 in 2025, which makes an oil shock materially less binding on government spending than it was in the cycle that produced the last six-year property decline. And the non-oil economy has compounded faster than the oil economy for most of two decades. Against that, the 2026 war has moved the central bank's own growth forecast from 5.6% to 1.7%. The transaction-level consequences are set out in the companion Dubai Property Market Report. 驅動杜拜房產的變數，幾乎全部在別處決定。迪拉姆自 1997 年 11 月起固定於 3.6725 兌 1 美元，阿聯央行因此近乎機械地跟隨聯準會，並無獨立工具。家戶基礎約 88% 為外籍，故由簽證政策與雇主的聘僱決策決定，而非由出生數決定：總生育率為 1.21，人口卻於六十五年間增為 88 倍。至於下檔風險，於歷次驗證中係由阿布達比的資產負債表吸收，而非由任何市場機制吸收。

描述其底層經濟的唯一長序列為實質人均 GDP，而該序列的方向相反：1980 年見頂於 117,073 美元，2024 年為 41,605 美元，現已低於 1970 年的水準。總產出於期間內持續成長，惟人口成長更快。此即該市場的基本算式：房價從未由居民所得提升所驅動，其成長敘事始終是人數的敘事。

有兩項確實改善。財政平衡油價自 2014 年的 91.60 美元降至 2025 年的 50.00 美元，使油價衝擊對政府支出的約束力明顯低於促成上一次為期六年房價下跌的那個循環。且非石油經濟於近二十年的多數時間複合成長快於石油經濟。相對地，2026 年的戰事已使央行自身的成長預測自 5.6% 下修至 1.7%。其於成交層面的後果詳見《杜拜住宅市場報告》。

UAE consumer prices rose 12.3% in 2008 and fell 4.7% in 2009. They were negative in 2019 at −1.9%, in 2020 at −2.1% and in 2021 at −0.1%, then rose 4.8% in 2022. Housing carries a heavy weight in that basket, so the years of consumer price deflation and the years of falling rent are substantially the same years. Why this matters more here than in Bangkok or Kuala Lumpur此點在此地的重要性高於曼谷或吉隆坡

Thailand's central bank cut to 1.00% into its own deflation. Malaysia holds the overnight policy rate at 2.75% with room to move. Both have a domestic instrument. The UAE has none, and the offsetting compensation is fiscal: government spending, project awards and visa policy are the levers that respond to local conditions. An investor reading UAE property should watch federal spending and immigration rules where they would watch the policy rate elsewhere.泰國央行於其自身通縮環境下降息至 1.00%；馬來西亞維持隔夜政策利率於 2.75% 且仍有調整空間。兩者均具備本國工具，阿聯則無。其替代性補償為財政面：政府支出、工程發包與簽證政策為回應本地情勢的槓桿。判讀阿聯房產者，應以聯邦支出與移民規則取代其他市場中所觀察的政策利率。

Breakeven price vs production平衡油價 vs 產量

The fiscal breakeven oil price is the level at which the budget balances. It rose from US$13.00 in 2000 to a peak of US$91.60 in 2014 as spending expanded against a high oil price, and has since fallen to US$50.00 in 2025. Production over the same period rose from 2.19 to 3.21 million barrels a day. The combination means the government can sustain its spending at a materially lower oil price than it could in the cycle that preceded the last property decline, and that is a structural improvement rather than a cyclical one.財政平衡油價為預算達成平衡所需的油價水準。該指標自 2000 年的 13.00 美元升至 2014 年的高點 91.60 美元，成因為高油價環境下支出擴張；其後降至 2025 年的 50.00 美元。同期產量自每日 219 萬桶增至 321 萬桶。兩者合計代表政府得於明顯較低的油價下維持其支出水準，該水準低於促成上一次房價下跌之循環的對應值，且此屬結構性改善而非循環性改善。

The Strait of Hormuz closure since March 2026 has cut Gulf crude and LNG shipping volumes by the great majority. What a migration-driven household base changes about property risk移民驅動的家戶基礎如何改變房產風險

In Bangkok or Taipei, demographic risk is slow, one-directional and visible thirty years ahead in the birth series. In Dubai it is fast, two-directional and invisible in advance. The 2020 fall took twelve months. The variables that drive it are employer hiring, visa rules and the relative attractiveness of the emirate against alternatives, none of which is forecastable from a demographic table.於曼谷或台北，人口風險變動緩慢、方向單一，且可自出生數序列提前三十年觀察。於杜拜，該風險變動快速、方向雙向，且無法事前觀察。2020 年的下降在十二個月內完成，其驅動變數為雇主聘僱、簽證規則，以及該酋長國相對於替代地點的吸引力，三者均無法由人口統計表推估。

The compensating property is that it is also fast on the way up. Population grew from 9.40 million in 2020 to 11.51 million in 2025, an increase of 22% in five years, and that inflow is the direct cause of the rental and price cycle documented in the report. This market's demand base is the most responsive and the least predictable of any covered on this site.相抵的性質為其向上時同樣快速：人口自 2020 年的 940 萬增至 2025 年的 1,151 萬，五年增加 22%，該流入即為報告中所載租金與價格循環的直接成因。本市場的需求基礎為本站涵蓋市場中反應最快、亦最難預測者。

Part 3 · The Politics — Who Sets the Rules政治篇 · 規則的制定者

Dubai writes its own property law and does not hold its own backstop杜拜自訂房產法規，但不持有自己的最後支撐

The UAE is a federation of seven emirates governed by a Federal Supreme Council of their rulers. Abu Dhabi holds the presidency and the overwhelming majority of the federation's hydrocarbon reserves; Dubai holds the vice-presidency and the premiership. Each emirate retains control of its own land, natural resources and, critically for this analysis, its own property law. Dubai's freehold regime is emirate-level legislation — Law No. 7 of 2006 with the designated areas set out in Regulation No. 3 of 2006 — and it exists because the Ruler of Dubai decided in 2002 that it should.阿聯為七個酋長國組成的聯邦，由各酋長國統治者組成的聯邦最高委員會治理。阿布達比持有總統職位與聯邦絕大部分的油氣蘊藏；杜拜持有副總統與總理職位。各酋長國保留對其土地、自然資源之控制權，且就本分析而言最關鍵者，保留其自有的房產法規。杜拜的自由持有制度屬酋長國層級立法，即 2006 年第 7 號法律與載明指定區域的 2006 年第 3 號規則，其成立源於杜拜統治者於 2002 年的決定。

The asymmetry matters at the point of stress. Dubai sets the rules that create the market and runs its own budget, but the reserves that absorbed the last crisis are Abu Dhabi's. In November 2009 Dubai World announced a standstill on US$26bn of obligations; on 14 December Abu Dhabi provided approximately US$10bn, of which US$4.1bn met a Nakheel sukuk maturing that day.   • Net migration × scheduled delivery. These are the numerator and denominator of the whole model, and they are not independent. Where區位

Established freehold communities with a delivery pipeline below the citywide average. That excludes Business Bay, Jumeirah Village Circle, Dubai South, Dubai Science Park and Dubai Hills Estate, which between them take more than a third of 2026 scheduled deliveries. The pipeline is the model's binding term, and it is geographically concentrated, so the term can be avoided by location in a way the others cannot.交屋供給低於全市平均之成熟自由持有社區。此排除 Business Bay、Jumeirah Village Circle、Dubai South、Dubai Science Park 與 Dubai Hills Estate，該五區合計占 2026 年排程交屋量的三分之一以上。供給為模型的約束項，且其分布集中，故該變數可透過區位規避，其餘各項則否。

What產品

Completed apartments, not villas and not off-plan. Apartments yield 7.08% gross against the villa's 4.54%, and the villa's 194% run since January 2021 has already paid for its scarcity. Completed stock avoids both the 20% off-plan premium and the 2027 handover cohort. The return on this asset is the rent line; buy the product with the rent line.已完工公寓，非別墅，亦非預售。公寓毛收益率 7.08%，別墅為 4.54%；別墅自 2021 年 1 月起 194% 的漲幅已充分反映其稀缺性。已完工存量同時規避 20% 的預售溢價與 2027 年的交屋批次。本資產的報酬來源為租金線，故應購置具備租金線的產品。

When時點

The information that resolves the decision is the quarterly delivery ratio, and the 2027 readings arrive across the next four quarters. Below 45% the pipeline is absorbed; above 60% the third-crash scenario becomes the base case. Prices have already fallen 10% and the June transaction rebound of 46.8% month-on-month is the first datum inconsistent with a continuing slide. Neither fact settles the question.決定此一判斷的資訊為季度交屋達成率，2027 年的讀數將於未來四個季度陸續揭曉。低於 45% 時供給可被吸納；高於 60% 時第三次崩跌情境成為基準情境。房價已下跌 10%，且 6 月成交月增 46.8% 為首個與持續下滑不相容的觀測值。兩項事實均不足以定論。

What this page concludes本頁的結論

Dubai is the most open property market covered on this site and the one with the least domestic control over its own cycle. Freehold title including land, no ownership quota, no income tax on rent, no capital gains tax and a ten-year residency at AED 2m are terms no other market here offers a foreigner. The price of that openness is that the monetary policy, the currency and the demand base are all set elsewhere.杜拜為本站涵蓋中最開放的房產市場，同時是對其自身循環擁有最少本地控制權者。含土地之永久產權、無持有配額、租金不課所得稅、無資本利得稅，以及 AED 200 萬對應之十年居留，均為本站其他市場未對外國人提供的條件。此一開放性的代價，在於貨幣政策、幣值與需求基礎均在別處決定。

The record does not support underwriting capital growth. Real prices are below both the 2008 and the 2014 peaks after sixteen years, and the series contains completed drawdowns of 36.5% and 30.2%. The return is the rent, and the rent is the most volatile of any market compared here. A 6.57% gross yield falls to roughly 5.3% once the service charge is deducted, which is still the widest in this coverage and still worth owning, provided it is underwritten as income and not as appreciation.歷史紀錄不支持將資本利得納入承作假設。歷經十六年，實質房價低於 2008 年與 2014 年兩個高點，且序列中包含幅度 36.5% 與 30.2% 的兩次完成的下跌。報酬來源為租金，而該租金為本頁所比較市場中波動最大者。6.57% 的毛收益率於扣除社區服務費後降至約 5.3%，該水準仍為本站涵蓋範圍中最高，仍具持有價值，惟前提為以收益型資產而非增值型資產承作。

The single question that decides the next three years is whether 344,000 scheduled units become 150,000 delivered ones or 250,000. That is a construction-management question, not a monetary or demographic one, and it is answered quarter by quarter in a published statistic. Transaction-level detail is in the Dubai Property Market Report.決定未來三年的單一問題，為 344,000 戶的排程供給最終成為 15 萬戶或 25 萬戶的實際交屋量。該問題屬工程管理範疇，非貨幣或人口範疇，且逐季由一項公開統計給出答案。成交層面的細節見《杜拜住宅市場報告》。

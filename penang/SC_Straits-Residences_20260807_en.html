◆ Deep-Dive Narrative

Project Deep-Dive Summary

Straits Residences (formally registered as Residensi Straits) sits on Jalan Seri Tanjung Pinang, on the confirmed last developable plot of Seri Tanjung Pinang Phase 1 (STP1) — roughly 1 acre, directly beside Straits Quay marina mall — developed by Kerjaya Prospek Property Sdn Bhd as a single 28-storey tower with 246 freehold service-suite units, ranging from 861 sqft (1BR) to 1,356 sqft (2BR). Launched around 2016, originally targeted for December 2020 completion, with multiple sources indicating actual completion around 2021 (roughly a one-year delay; another source records 2019, and the discrepancy is disclosed here).

On scarcity, this project reaches nearly the same three-layer audit conclusion as the sibling Quayside card — land layer ✓: STP1's 240 acres of reclaimed land took roughly 20 years to develop, and this project is the concrete embodiment of the last remaining stock; the market cannot recreate a second version of this project within STP1's own boundaries. Product layer ✗: a standard 28-storey strata tower, co-existing with similar large-format waterfront towers along the same corridor (Gurney Paragon, Setia V, Moulmein Rise, Quayside) — replicable. Regulatory layer ✗: STP1 sits outside the George Town UNESCO core/buffer zone. Net 7.5−0.5=7.0 (the −0.5 reflects Andaman/STP2 substitute-supply dilution, consistent with the Quayside card). This card's key task — verifying the 26-acre reclaimed plot: coordinate estimation places it on the Andaman/Gurney side, about 1.5 km from this project, with the address and micro-location outside STP1's own footprint, not constituting a direct substitute threat, and therefore not triggering a d1 land-layer ✗ — it is disclosed only as a valuation-ceiling signal.

On pricing, this project's own Brickz 48-month transaction median is RM 1,226 psf, a ~4.8% gap from the site's locked anchor table entry ("Straits 1,288 psf," the state's highest transacted anchor) — this card uses the verified figure per protocol and honestly discloses the conflict, recommending recalibration in the anchor table's next version; a separate cut ("most popular layout," 2BR/1,335sf, recent median) shows RM 1,300 psf, closer to the anchor table figure, indicating a swing of a few percentage points depending on the cut method. The representative unit (2+1BR 1,292 sqft, estimated asking ~RM 1,680,000) implies a PSF of about RM 1,300, a ~6.0% premium over its own Brickz median, falling within the reasonable new-launch-premium band.

This card's most important finding concerns title structure: although named "Residences," multiple sources consistently confirm this project sits on commercial title land as a Service Residence — not a residential-title condo like Quayside or Setia V. This distinction creates structural, and opposing, effects across the d3 and d6 dimensions — on cashflow, the serviced product's higher rent psf pushes gross yield to ~4.14%, notably better than Quayside (2.6%) and Setia V (3.09%); but on exit, the commercial-title baseline is cut straight to 6.0 (rather than the residential 7.0), compounded by a −0.3 commercial-title next-buyer financing friction, so the Malaysian exit score (5.1) ends up lower than both peers (Quayside 6.5, Setia V 6.4).

Bottom line總結 Bangkok is not short of buyers who want a home. It is short of buyers a bank will lend to. Between 40% and 50% of mortgage applications were declined in the first half of 2026, and in the sub-3-million-baht band — the price point at which most Thai households actually shop — rejection has run near 70%. That single filter explains more about this market than any supply statistic.

The result is a market that has quietly rebuilt itself around cash. At the top, foreign buyers took only 13.6% of transferred units in the first quarter of 2026 but 23.9% of the value. In the middle, second-hand homes now account for two-thirds of all ownership transfers, with 240,000 resale units listed nationwide, up 34.2% in a year as owners, banks and asset managers all try to exit at once. At the bottom, almost nothing clears. Developers have responded by abandoning the mass market — no new project launched in the central business district in the first quarter, 68% of new launches priced below 80,000 baht per square metre, and a booking rate of 24.3% on those launches against 43.8% a quarter earlier.

What makes this market interesting rather than merely weak is the carry. A gross yield near 5% against a 1.00% policy rate is roughly four points of positive carry, the widest in Asia. But the carry is wide because the policy rate is at a two-decade low, and it is at a two-decade low because the economy has been in deflation for most of a year. You are being paid to wait in a currency whose central bank has already spent its ammunition. And since October 2025 there is a second reason to look: a purchase of 3 million baht now carries a renewable long-stay visa for the buyer and their family, the single biggest change to foreign access in years — though one full quarter of data has yet to show it moving demand. The structural reasons behind all of this are set out in the companion Thailand Macro × Property analysis. 曼谷缺的不是想買房的人，而是銀行願意放款的人。2026 年上半年，房貸申請的退件率落在四成到五成之間；在 300 萬泰銖以下這個泰國家庭真正在買的價位帶，退件率一度接近七成。這一道篩子，比任何一項供給統計都更能解釋這個市場。

結果是市場悄悄地繞著現金重建了自己。頂端，外國買方在 2026 年第一季只佔過戶戶數的 13.6%，卻佔了金額的 23.9%。中段，二手屋已佔全部過戶的三分之二，全國二手掛牌量達 24 萬戶、年增 34.2%——屋主、銀行、資產管理公司同時在找出口。底端幾乎不動：第一季中央商業區沒有任何新案推出，新推案中 68% 定價低於每平方公尺 8 萬泰銖，而這些案子的訂購率只有 24.3%，前一季還有 43.8%。

讓這個市場「有意思」而不只是「疲弱」的，是它的持有收益。近 5% 的毛收益率對上 1.00% 的政策利率，正利差約四個百分點，是亞洲最寬的一個。但利差之所以寬，是因為政策利率處在二十年低點；而它之所以在二十年低點，是因為這個經濟體已經通縮了大半年。你是在一個央行子彈已經打完的貨幣裡被付錢等待。而自 2025 年 10 月起還有第二個值得看的理由：購置 300 萬泰銖的物件，即可為買方與家屬換得一張可續期的長期居留簽證，這是多年來外資准入最大的一次改變——儘管已有一整季的數據，還看不到它推動需求。這一切的結構成因，見另文《泰國總經 × 房產》。

Three inventory numbers, three definitions三個存量數字，三種口徑

Same market, different counting rules同一市場，不同計算規則

Every serious discussion of Bangkok oversupply eventually collides with the fact that the headline number depends entirely on where the counter draws the boundary. AREA counts 210,112 unsold units in projects actively being marketed inside the Bangkok metropolitan area. SCB EIC projects roughly 213,000 unsold units across Greater Bangkok by the end of 2026. Knight Frank puts the Greater Bangkok condominium overhang alone at around 350,000 units, five to six years of absorption at the recent run-rate of about 60,000 transfers a year, because it counts stock still under construction and stock in completed projects that have quietly stopped being marketed.任何認真討論曼谷過剩的人，最後都會撞上同一件事：標題數字完全取決於計數者把界線畫在哪裡。AREA 統計曼谷都會區內「仍在銷售中的案場」未售 210,112 戶；SCB EIC 推估大曼谷到 2026 年底未售約 213,000 戶；Knight Frank 則把大曼谷「公寓一項」的存量估在 35 萬戶左右，以近年約每年 6 萬戶過戶的速度計，需要五到六年——因為它把在建中、以及完工後已悄悄停止行銷的存量也算了進去。

The Malaysian mirror馬來西亞的鏡像

In Malaysia the statistical blind spot runs one way: serviced apartments were reclassified as commercial in 2016 and dropped out of the residential overhang series, so the official number understates by roughly 19,000 units. In Thailand the distortion runs the other way and is larger. There is no single official unsold register at all; what exists is a set of private surveys with incompatible boundaries. The honest reading is that Bangkok carries four-plus years of visible stock and an unmeasured tail behind it.在馬來西亞，統計盲點只往一個方向偏：服務式公寓 2016 年被改列商業，退出住宅餘屋序列，官方數字因此低估約 1.9 萬戶。在泰國，偏誤方向相反、幅度更大——泰國根本沒有單一的官方未售登記，有的只是幾份邊界互不相容的民間調查。誠實的讀法是：曼谷背著四年以上的可見存量，後面還拖著一條無法量測的尾巴。

Practical rule: never compare an inventory number across two sources. Compare each source against its own history, and treat the level as a range, not a point.實務規則：不要跨來源比較存量數字。每個來源只跟自己的歷史比；把水準視為區間，不是一個點。

Yield vs the rate you actually pay收益率 vs 你實際付的利率
  • Against the 1.00% policy rate, a 5% gross yield is four points of carry — wider than Malaysia's 2.44 points and the widest in the region. For an unlevered cash buyer, that is the whole investment case.對上 1.00% 的政策利率，5% 的毛收益率有四個百分點的利差——比馬來西亞的 2.44 個百分點更寬，是區域最寬。對不用槓桿的現金買方，這就是全部的投資論述。
  • Against the promotional mortgage rate of roughly 2.65% to 3.85% for the first three years, the carry narrows to around 1.7 points. Thin, but still positive.對上前三年約 2.65% 到 3.85% 的優惠房貸利率，利差收斂到約 1.7 個百分點。很薄，但仍為正。
  • Against the MRR the loan reverts to in year four — averaging 7.38% across domestic commercial banks in June 2026 — the carry is minus 2.4 points. The Thai teaser structure means every levered buyer is underwriting a negative-carry position that begins on a known date.對上第四年跳回的 MRR——2026 年 6 月本地商銀平均 7.38%——利差變成負 2.4 個百分點。泰式前低後高的房貸結構，等於每個用槓桿的買方都在承作一個「已知日期開始的負持有」部位。

This is the single most under-discussed number in the Thai market. Malaysia's borrower pays a floating rate from day one and knows what they signed. Thailand's borrower pays a subsidised rate for 36 months and then faces a step-up of three to four points. A buyer whose debt-service ratio only cleared at the teaser rate is a default waiting for a calendar. It is one reason special-mention loans — the 30-to-90-day bucket that leads non-performing loans — had built to about 1.28 trillion baht by the end of 2025.這是泰國市場最被低估的一個數字。馬來西亞的借款人從第一天就付浮動利率，簽的是什麼很清楚；泰國的借款人先付 36 個月補貼利率，然後面對三到四個百分點的跳升。一個只有在優惠利率下才勉強過關的償債比，本質上是一個「等日曆的違約」。這也是為什麼「特別關注類」貸款——逾期 30 至 90 天、領先於不良債權的那一桶——到 2025 年底已累積到約 1.28 兆泰銖。

Thai consumer prices fell year-on-year for twelve consecutive months through March 2026, so a +1.26% nominal index reading was briefly a genuine real gain. That window has closed. The Bank of Thailand's June 2026 forecast puts headline inflation at 2.8% for 2026 as imported energy costs pass through. Where Bangkok sits曼谷的位置

Bangkok sits in the same cluster as Kuala Lumpur, New York and London on yield, and in a different universe on capital growth. Taipei and Sydney are the mirror image: sub-3% yields carried by two decades of price appreciation. Which of those two engines you are buying determines everything downstream — holding period, product choice, leverage, and how much a stalled price index should worry you. In Bangkok you are buying the rent line. If the rent line breaks, there is no second engine underneath it.在收益率上，曼谷與吉隆坡、紐約、倫敦同群；在資本利得上，則在另一個宇宙。台北與雪梨是鏡像：不到 3% 的收益率，靠二十年的價格上漲撐著。你買的是哪一具引擎，決定了後面所有事——持有期、產品選擇、槓桿，以及一個停滯的價格指數該讓你多擔心。在曼谷，你買的是租金那條線。租金線一斷，底下沒有第二具引擎。

Launches vs absorption推案 vs 吸納
  • Through 2016 to 2023, developers launched an average of about 100,000 units a year into demand of roughly 92,000. The annual gap looks modest. Run it for eight years and it is the overhang.從 2016 到 2023，發展商年均推出約 10 萬戶，對上約 9.2 萬戶的需求。單年缺口看起來不大；跑八年，就是今天的餘屋。
  • Since 2023 launches have halved to roughly 51,000 a year. This is the single most important positive in the Thai market and it is genuinely voluntary — no regulator forced it. Developers cut because presales stopped funding construction.2023 年後推案腰斬到約每年 5.1 萬戶。這是泰國市場最重要的一個正面因素，而且是自願的——沒有監管機關逼他們。發展商減量，是因為預售已經無法為工程提供資金。

Why this breaks the standard overhang model為何這打破了標準的餘屋模型

Every developer-overhang analysis assumes the seller has a balance sheet and can wait. Malaysia's Housing Development Act reinforces that: developers carry rather than cut, so the surplus shows up in time-to-clear rather than in price. A foreclosed unit has no such patience. Second-hand homes already account for about two-thirds of all ownership transfers in Thailand. That threshold has been crossed. The developer market is no longer where the price is set.在泰國，二手屋已佔全部過戶的約三分之二。這個門檻已經跨過了。價格不再由新案市場決定。

The 99-year lease and 75% quota: not law99 年租賃與 75% 配額：未立法

Both reforms have been floated repeatedly and neither has been enacted. The 99-year leasehold bill was debated in 2024 and shelved under domestic political pressure; no draft to raise the condominium quota to 75% had been submitted to parliament as of mid-2026. Underwrite the current law, not the proposal.兩項改革反覆被提出，皆未立法。99 年租賃法案 2024 年曾進入討論，因國內政治壓力擱置；至 2026 年中，未有將公寓配額提高至 75% 的草案送進國會。請以現行法律定價，不要以提案定價。

Since 1 October 2025 a freehold condominium of 3 million baht or more carries a renewable long-stay visa for the buyer and dependants, under Immigration Orders 237/2568 and 238/2568. It changes who may live here, not who may own — the 49% quota is untouched. Immigration Bureau Orders No. 237/2568 and 238/2568, effective 1 October 2025 · qualifying thresholds移民局第 237/2568 與 238/2568 號命令，2025 年 10 月 1 日生效 · 各項門檻

Where the new threshold sits新門檻落在哪裡

On 1 October 2025 Thailand did the one thing on foreign demand that no bill in parliament has managed: it cut the property-linked investment-visa threshold from 10 million baht to 3 million. Two Immigration Bureau orders — 237/2568 covering the initial 90-day alignment and change of category, 238/2568 covering the renewable twelve-month extension — created a long-stay route under the Non-Immigrant "B" (Investment) class. There are three qualifying paths: a freehold condominium of at least 3 million baht, a registered long-term lease with total contract value above 3.06 million, or a high-value rental at 85,000 baht a month with three months prepaid at application and twelve at renewal. Sap-Ing-Sith, the registered right-over-property instrument, is accepted as a standalone route. A leasehold suspension reported in June 2026 has since been lifted.2025 年 10 月 1 日，泰國在外資需求這件事上做成了國會任何法案都沒做成的事：把與房產連動的投資簽證門檻，從 1,000 萬泰銖降到 300 萬。移民局兩道命令——237/2568 處理最初 90 天的簽證對接與類別變更，238/2568 處理可續期的十二個月延長——在非移民「B」（投資）類別下開出一條長期居留路徑。合格路徑有三條：不低於 300 萬泰銖的永久產權公寓、合約總值超過 306 萬的登記長期租賃，或每月 85,000 泰銖的高額租賃（申請時預付三個月、續期時預付十二個月）。已登記的「地上權益」（Sap-Ing-Sith）亦被接受為獨立路徑。2026 年 6 月一度傳出的租賃路徑暫停，其後已解除。

How it works in practice實務上怎麼運作

Ninety days on entry, then a 12-month extension renewable annually for as long as the asset is held, with no border runs. Dependants — spouse, unmarried children under 20, parents aged 50 or over — may be added with no further investment, but only after the principal has the full twelve-month extension. Fees run about 27,000 baht a year plus a one-off membership of 4,000 to 6,000 baht.入境 90 天，之後取得可逐年續期的 12 個月延長，只要資產持有中即可續，不需出境。眷屬——配偶、20 歲以下未婚子女、50 歲以上父母——可不需額外投資納入，但須待主申請人取得完整 12 個月延長之後。費用約每年 27,000 泰銖，另加一次性會籍費 4,000 至 6,000 泰銖。

The conditions that actually bind真正會卡住人的條件

The unit must be completed and registered at the Department of Lands — off-plan does not qualify. The counterparty must be a Thai individual or a Thai-majority company. Funds must be remitted from abroad in foreign currency, evidenced by a Foreign Exchange Transaction form. And the reduced 3-million threshold is only available with a Certification Letter from the Ministry of Tourism and Sports, issued through a designated long-stay operator; without it the applicant falls back to the old 10-million rule.標的必須是已完工並在土地廳完成登記的物件——預售不符資格。交易對手須為泰國籍個人或泰資過半的公司。資金必須自境外以外幣匯入，並取得外匯交易表（FET）為憑。而 300 萬的降低門檻，只有在取得觀光與體育部核發的認證函（透過指定的長期居留營運商辦理）時才適用；沒有它，申請人就退回原本的 1,000 萬規則。

What it is not它不是什麼

It carries no work rights, still requires 90-day reporting and a TM.30 residence notification, and is not a path to permanent residence or citizenship. It is tied to the asset: sell the unit and the basis for the extension goes with it. It also does not change ownership law — the 49% freehold quota per building is untouched.它不附工作權，仍須 90 天報到與 TM.30 住所通報，且不是通往永久居留或公民身分的路徑。它綁在資產上：賣掉物件，延長的依據就一併消失。它也不改變產權法律——每棟 49% 的永久產權外資配額原封不動。

Already under political pressure已經在承受政治壓力

The Phuket Tourism Association has publicly opposed the scheme, arguing the threshold is too low for the residency granted, that it invites undocumented work and unlicensed business, and that it inflates housing costs for residents. Industry submissions ask for a higher minimum or additional deposits per dependant. The direction of any revision is up, not down — which makes the current window the cheap one.普吉觀光協會已公開反對，主張門檻相對於所給予的居留權過低，將引來無證工作與無照經營，並推高當地居民的住房成本。業界的建議是提高最低金額，或對每位眷屬另設存款要求。任何修訂的方向都是往上、不是往下——這使得現在這個窗口是便宜的那一個。

Why the threshold was set where it was — and why it has not worked yet門檻為何訂在這裡 —— 以及它為何還沒發揮作用

Three million baht is roughly US$89,000 at 33.65 to the dollar. The average foreign condominium purchase in 2025 was 4.1 million baht. The threshold was therefore set below the price the average foreign buyer was already paying — so for most of the existing foreign cohort the visa is a free upgrade on a purchase they were making anyway, not an inducement to buy something new.以 33.65 的匯率計，300 萬泰銖約 8.9 萬美元。而 2025 年外國人購買公寓的平均金額是 410 萬泰銖。也就是說，門檻被訂在低於一般外國買方本來就在支付的價格——對既有的外資族群而言，這張簽證是他們原本就要做的那筆交易上的免費加值，不是去買新東西的誘因。

The evidence backs that reading. The scheme has been live since October 2025, and in the first quarter of 2026 foreign condominium transfers still fell 17.3% in units and 17.9% in value, with Bangkok down 35%. A visa did not offset a Chinese balance-sheet recession. Anyone underwriting the 3-million rule as a demand catalyst should note that one full quarter of live data has already failed to show it.數據支持這個讀法。制度自 2025 年 10 月上路，2026 年第一季外國人公寓過戶仍戶數年減 17.3%、金額年減 17.9%，曼谷更減 35%。一張簽證抵不過中國的資產負債表衰退。任何把 300 萬新規當成需求催化劑來承作的人，都該注意：已經有一整季的實際數據沒有支持它。

Where it does bite is product selection. Because off-plan is excluded and the unit must already be registered, the rule channels foreign money into completed standing inventory — precisely the 210,112 units the market needs to clear — and away from developer presales. And the 3-million floor sits at the bottom of the 3-to-5-million band, the one segment where absorption has improved since 2021. Whether by design or accident, it is the best-targeted piece of housing policy Thailand currently has.它真正發揮作用的地方在產品選擇。由於排除預售、且標的必須已完成登記，這條規則把外資導向已完工的在售存量——正是市場需要去化的那 210,112 戶——而非發展商的預售。而 300 萬的下限恰好落在 300 萬至 500 萬這個區間的底部，也就是 2021 年以來吸納唯一改善的那一段。無論是設計還是巧合，這是泰國目前最精準對準目標的一項住宅政策。

Set against Malaysia the comparison is stark. A foreigner buying in Kuala Lumpur faces a RM1 million purchase floor and 8% flat stamp duty, with MM2H Silver requiring RM600,000. A foreigner buying in Bangkok needs 3 million baht, about US$89,000, pays 2% transfer duty, and gets a renewable family visa attached. On entry cost and residency, Thailand is now materially the cheaper door. What Malaysia offers in exchange is a growing population, a functioning mortgage market, and a currency with a central bank that still has room to cut. That is the actual trade.與馬來西亞對照，落差非常明顯。外國人在吉隆坡購屋，面對的是 RM100 萬的購買門檻與 8% 統一印花稅，MM2H 銀級則需 RM60 萬。外國人在曼谷購屋，需要 300 萬泰銖、約 8.9 萬美元，付 2% 過戶稅，並附帶一張可續期的全家簽證。就進場成本與居留權而言，泰國現在明顯是比較便宜的那道門。馬來西亞拿來交換的，是仍在成長的人口、一個運作中的房貸市場，以及一個央行還有降息空間的貨幣。這才是真正的取捨。

What this adds up to加總起來是什麼

Bangkok over the next decade is a carry-and-currency market with a flat real capital line. The rent is real, the yield is genuinely the widest in Asia against the local policy rate, and the stock is cheap in dollar terms after a decade of baht weakness punctuated by sharp rallies. What is not there is an appreciation engine: no population growth, no income breakout, no supply shortage, and a four-year queue of unsold homes ahead of you.未來十年的曼谷，是一個資本線實質持平的持有收益＋匯率市場。租金是真的，收益率相對本國政策利率確實是亞洲最寬，經歷十年泰銖走弱（其間夾雜數次急升）之後，以美元計的存量也不貴。缺的是增值引擎：沒有人口成長、沒有所得突破、沒有供給短缺，而你前面還排著四年的未售屋。

That is not a reason to avoid the market. It is a reason to underwrite it as an income asset with a 15% non-resident withholding, a legally capped ownership share, and a hard four-year clock on the mortgage rate — and to require the entry price to compensate for all three. The buyer who does that in the 3-to-5-million-baht band on a completed, transit-adjacent, foreign-quota unit is being paid well to wait. The buyer who underwrites capital growth is buying the one thing this market has not produced in a decade.這不是避開這個市場的理由，而是把它當成一個「有 15% 非居民扣繳、有法定持有比例上限、房貸利率有四年硬時鐘」的收益型資產來承作的理由——並要求進場價格把這三件事都補償進去。在 300 萬至 500 萬泰銖區間、買一戶已完工、鄰近軌道、外配名額的物件，這樣的買方是被付了不錯的價錢在等待。把資本利得寫進承作假設的買方，買的則是這個市場十年來唯一沒有生產過的東西。

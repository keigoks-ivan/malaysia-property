Bottom line總結 Bangkok is not short of buyers who want a home. It is short of buyers a bank will lend to. Between 40% and 50% of mortgage applications were declined in the first half of 2026, and in the sub-3-million-baht band — the price point at which most Thai households actually shop — rejection has run near 70%. That single filter explains more about this market than any supply statistic.

The result is a market that has quietly rebuilt itself around cash. At the top, foreign buyers took only 13.6% of transferred units in the first quarter of 2026 but 23.9% of the value. In the middle, second-hand homes now account for two-thirds of all ownership transfers, with 240,000 resale units listed nationwide, up 34.2% in a year as owners, banks and asset managers all try to exit at once. At the bottom, almost nothing clears. Developers have responded by abandoning the mass market — no new project launched in the central business district in the first quarter, 68% of new launches priced below 80,000 baht per square metre, and a booking rate of 24.3% on those launches against 43.8% a quarter earlier.

What makes this market interesting rather than merely weak is the carry. A gross yield near 5% against a 1.00% policy rate is roughly four points of positive carry, the widest in Asia. But the carry is wide because the policy rate is at a two-decade low, and it is at a two-decade low because the economy has been in deflation for most of a year. You are being paid to wait in a currency whose central bank has already spent its ammunition. And since October 2025 there is a second reason to look: a purchase of 3 million baht now carries a renewable long-stay visa for the buyer and their family, the single biggest change to foreign access in years — though one full quarter of data has yet to show it moving demand. The structural reasons behind all of this are set out in the companion Thailand Macro × Property analysis. 曼谷不缺想買房的人，缺的是銀行肯放款的人。2026 年上半年，房貸退件率在四成到五成之間；300 萬泰銖以下、也就是泰國家庭真正在看的價位，退件率一度接近七成。這道篩子解釋的事情，比任何一份供給統計都多。

於是市場繞開銀行，改用現金重建了自己。最上面那層，外國買方 2026 年第一季只佔過戶戶數 13.6%，卻拿走 23.9% 的金額。中間那層，二手屋吃掉全部過戶的三分之二；全國二手掛牌 24 萬戶，一年多出 34.2%，屋主、銀行、資產管理公司擠在同一個出口。最下面那層幾乎不動：第一季中央商業區一個新案都沒推，新案有 68% 定價在每平方公尺 8 萬泰銖以下，訂購率只剩 24.3%，前一季還有 43.8%。

真正讓這個市場值得看的，不是它有多弱，是它的持有收益。近 5% 的毛收益率對上 1.00% 的政策利率，正利差約四個百分點，亞洲最寬。但利差寬是有代價的：政策利率落在二十年低點，而它會落在那裡，是因為這個經濟體通縮了大半年。說白了，你是在一個央行已經沒有子彈的貨幣裡收租等待。

2025 年 10 月起還多了一個理由。買下 300 萬泰銖的物件，買方連同家屬就能換一張可續期的長期居留簽證。這是多年來外資准入最大的一次鬆綁，只是已經過了一整季，數據還看不出它把需求推動了多少。這些條件的來源，見另文《泰國總經 × 房產》。

Same market, different counting rules同一市場，不同計算規則

Every serious discussion of Bangkok oversupply eventually collides with the fact that the headline number depends entirely on where the counter draws the boundary. AREA counts 210,112 unsold units in projects actively being marketed inside the Bangkok metropolitan area. SCB EIC projects roughly 213,000 unsold units across Greater Bangkok by the end of 2026. Knight Frank puts the Greater Bangkok condominium overhang alone at around 350,000 units, five to six years of absorption at the recent run-rate of about 60,000 transfers a year, because it counts stock still under construction and stock in completed projects that have quietly stopped being marketed.認真查曼谷有多少餘屋的人，最後都會撞上同一件事：數字大小取決於界線畫在哪。AREA 只算曼谷都會區「還在賣的案場」，得到 210,112 戶。SCB EIC 推估大曼谷到 2026 年底約 213,000 戶。Knight Frank 光算大曼谷的公寓就估到 35 萬戶，因為它把在建中、以及蓋完之後悄悄停售的存量也算進去；照近年每年約 6 萬戶的過戶速度，這批貨要消化五到六年。

The Malaysian mirror馬來西亞的鏡像

In Malaysia the statistical blind spot runs one way: serviced apartments were reclassified as commercial in 2016 and dropped out of the residential overhang series, so the official number understates by roughly 19,000 units. In Thailand the distortion runs the other way and is larger. There is no single official unsold register at all; what exists is a set of private surveys with incompatible boundaries. The honest reading is that Bangkok carries four-plus years of visible stock and an unmeasured tail behind it.馬來西亞的統計盲點只往一個方向偏：服務式公寓 2016 年改列商業、退出住宅餘屋序列，官方數字因此少算了約 1.9 萬戶。泰國的問題不一樣，也更大：它根本沒有官方的未售登記，只有幾份邊界彼此不相容的民間調查。誠實的講法是：曼谷手上有四年以上看得見的存量，後面還拖著一條量不出來的尾巴。

Practical rule: never compare an inventory number across two sources. Compare each source against its own history, and treat the level as a range, not a point.所以有一條實務規則：存量數字不要跨來源比。每個來源只跟它自己的歷史比，水準當區間看，不要當一個點。

Yield vs the rate you actually pay收益率 vs 你實際付的利率
  • Against the 1.00% policy rate, a 5% gross yield is four points of carry — wider than Malaysia's 2.44 points and the widest in the region. For an unlevered cash buyer, that is the whole investment case.對上 1.00% 的政策利率，5% 的毛收益率有四個百分點的利差，比馬來西亞的 2.44 個百分點還寬，區域第一。現金買方不用槓桿，投資論述就到此為止，這四個百分點就是全部。
  • Against the promotional mortgage rate of roughly 2.65% to 3.85% for the first three years, the carry narrows to around 1.7 points. Thin, but still positive.對上前三年 2.65% 到 3.85% 的優惠房貸利率，利差剩約 1.7 個百分點。很薄，還是正的。
  • Against the MRR the loan reverts to in year four — averaging 7.38% across domestic commercial banks in June 2026 — the carry is minus 2.4 points. The Thai teaser structure means every levered buyer is underwriting a negative-carry position that begins on a known date.撐到第四年利率跳回 MRR，2026 年 6 月本地商銀平均 7.38%，利差就變成負 2.4 個百分點。泰國房貸前低後高，等於每個用槓桿的買方，都預先簽下了一個知道哪天開始虧的部位。

This is the single most under-discussed number in the Thai market. Malaysia's borrower pays a floating rate from day one and knows what they signed. Thailand's borrower pays a subsidised rate for 36 months and then faces a step-up of three to four points. A buyer whose debt-service ratio only cleared at the teaser rate is a default waiting for a calendar. It is one reason special-mention loans — the 30-to-90-day bucket that leads non-performing loans — had built to about 1.28 trillion baht by the end of 2025.這是泰國市場最被低估的一個數字。馬來西亞的借款人第一天就付浮動利率，簽了什麼很清楚；泰國的借款人先享受 36 個月補貼利率，接著面對三到四個百分點的跳升。如果一個人的償債比只有在優惠利率下才勉強過關，那不是風險，那是一張排好日期的違約通知。到 2025 年底，逾期 30 到 90 天的「特別關注類」貸款已經堆到約 1.28 兆泰銖，這一桶正是不良債權的上游。

Bangkok sits in the same cluster as Kuala Lumpur, New York and London on yield, and in a different universe on capital growth. Why this breaks the standard overhang model為何這打破了標準的餘屋模型

Every developer-overhang analysis assumes the seller has a balance sheet and can wait. Malaysia's Housing Development Act reinforces that: developers carry rather than cut, so the surplus shows up in time-to-clear rather than in price. A foreclosed unit has no such patience. Once distressed resale becomes the marginal seller, the price-discovery mechanism inverts — the lowest-conviction holder sets the comparable, and the developer's list price becomes fiction.所有「發展商餘屋」的分析，前提都是賣方有資產負債表、等得起。馬來西亞的《房屋發展法》把這個前提寫進制度：發展商寧可扛著也不降價，過剩因此表現在時間上，不在價格上。法拍屋沒有這種耐性。當不良二手變成邊際賣方，價格發現就翻過來了：最不想守的那個人決定了實價，發展商的開價變成一個數字而已。

Second-hand homes already account for about two-thirds of all ownership transfers in Thailand. That threshold has been crossed. The developer market is no longer where the price is set.泰國的二手屋已經佔了全部過戶的約三分之二。這條線已經跨過去了：定價權不在新案市場了。

Rental income for a non-resident foreigner is subject to a flat 15% withholding on gross rent — which converts a 5% gross yield into roughly 4.25% before any operating cost.出場那一端，五年內賣掉課 3.3% 特種商業稅，超過五年改課 0.5% 印花稅。非居民外國人的租金收入，按租金總額扣 15%，等於 5% 的毛收益率在還沒扣任何營運成本之前，就先變成 4.25%。
  • 2026 is also the first full-rate year for the Land and Building Tax: pandemic-era discounts have lapsed and vacant land now faces a step-up penalty. Residential property below 50 million baht in appraised value remains largely exempt, so the burden falls on land banks and on the luxury tier.2026 年還是土地與建物稅的第一個全額年度：疫情期的折扣到期了，空置土地另有遞增罰則。評定價值 5,000 萬泰銖以下的住宅大致還是免的，所以稅落在土地銀行和豪宅那一層。

Put the pieces together and the policy design is self-defeating. The state is subsidising the buyer the banks reject and taxing the buyer who arrives with cash. Malaysia does something structurally similar with its RM1 million foreign-purchase floor and 8% flat stamp duty, but at least the Malaysian rule directs foreign money to a price tier rather than penalising it everywhere. Thailand's 49% foreign quota already caps how much of a building can be sold freehold to non-Thais; adding a fee differential on top means the marginal cash buyer faces a worse round-trip in the one market segment that still clears.把這幾塊拼起來，財政這一側的設計是自打嘴巴：國家在補貼銀行會退件的人，同時對帶現金上門的人課稅。馬來西亞用 RM100 萬的門檻加 8% 統一印花稅做了結構類似的事，但它至少是把外資導到某個價格層，不是到處罰。泰國本來就有 49% 配額限制一棟樓能賣給非泰籍多少永久產權，再疊一層費用差別，等於讓唯一還去化得動的那一段，承受最差的來回成本。

The offset arrived through a different door. Fiscal policy still excludes the foreign buyer, but immigration policy now pays them: since October 2025 a purchase of 3 million baht or more carries a renewable long-stay visa for the buyer and their family. That measure is examined in full below, and it is the reason the foreign-access picture in 2026 is genuinely more open than the tax schedule alone suggests.補償從另一道門進來了。財政政策照樣把外國買方擋在外面，但移民政策開始付錢給他們：2025 年 10 月起，買到 300 萬泰銖以上，買方連同家屬就能拿到可續期的長期居留簽證。這項措施在下面完整檢視。看完就會知道，2026 年的外資准入其實比稅費表上看到的開放得多。

What foreigners can and cannot buy — the 2026 status外國人能買什麼、不能買什麼 —— 2026 現狀

Freehold condominium, 49% quota永久產權公寓，49% 配額

Foreigners may own condominium units in freehold up to 49% of a building's saleable area. Funds must be remitted from abroad in foreign currency with a bank certificate. The quota is per building, so in prime projects it is the binding constraint on foreign demand, and quota units routinely trade at a premium to the Thai-quota equivalent.外國人可持有公寓永久產權，上限為該棟可售面積的 49%。資金須自境外以外幣匯入並取得銀行證明。配額以「棟」為單位，因此在精華案場，這是外資需求的實質約束；外配戶通常較泰配戶溢價成交。

Land: no; lease: 30 years土地：不行；租賃：30 年

Foreigners cannot own land. Registered leases are enforceable for an initial 30-year term; renewal clauses are contractual, not statutory, and courts have not reliably enforced them. Villas marketed as "30+30+30" carry legal risk that is routinely understated at the point of sale.外國人不得持有土地。登記租賃的可執行期為首期 30 年；續約條款屬合約性質而非法定，法院並未穩定支持。以「30＋30＋30」行銷的別墅，其法律風險在銷售端經常被低估。

The 99-year lease and 75% quota: not law99 年租賃與 75% 配額：未立法

Both reforms have been floated repeatedly and neither has been enacted. The 99-year leasehold bill was debated in 2024 and shelved under domestic political pressure; no draft to raise the condominium quota to 75% had been submitted to parliament as of mid-2026. Underwrite the current law, not the proposal.兩項改革反覆被提出，皆未立法。99 年租賃法案 2024 年曾進入討論，因國內政治壓力擱置；至 2026 年中，未有將公寓配額提高至 75% 的草案送進國會。請以現行法律定價，不要以提案定價。

Since 1 October 2025 a freehold condominium of 3 million baht or more carries a renewable long-stay visa for the buyer and dependants, under Immigration Orders 237/2568 and 238/2568. It changes who may live here, not who may own — the 49% quota is untouched. Nominee structures: actively policed人頭公司：正在被查

From January and April 2026, Department of Business Development orders require proof of genuine source of funds and signed investment confirmation letters at company incorporation and amendment. The Thai-majority company route to holding land is under enforcement pressure, not merely legally grey.自 2026 年 1 月與 4 月起，商業發展廳的命令要求公司設立與變更時提出真實資金來源證明與簽署投資確認書。以「泰籍過半公司」持有土地的路徑，如今是正在被執法追查，而不只是法律灰色。

The 3-million-baht visa — the demand policy that actually moved300 萬泰銖簽證 —— 真正動起來的那項需求政策

Immigration Bureau Orders No. 237/2568 and 238/2568, effective 1 October 2025 · qualifying thresholds移民局第 237/2568 與 238/2568 號命令，2025 年 10 月 1 日生效 · 各項門檻

Where the new threshold sits新門檻落在哪裡

On 1 October 2025 Thailand did the one thing on foreign demand that no bill in parliament has managed: it cut the property-linked investment-visa threshold from 10 million baht to 3 million. Two Immigration Bureau orders — 237/2568 covering the initial 90-day alignment and change of category, 238/2568 covering the renewable twelve-month extension — created a long-stay route under the Non-Immigrant "B" (Investment) class. There are three qualifying paths: a freehold condominium of at least 3 million baht, a registered long-term lease with total contract value above 3.06 million, or a high-value rental at 85,000 baht a month with three months prepaid at application and twelve at renewal. Sap-Ing-Sith, the registered right-over-property instrument, is accepted as a standalone route. A leasehold suspension reported in June 2026 has since been lifted.2025 年 10 月 1 日，泰國在外資需求這件事上，做成了國會任何法案都沒做成的事：把房產連動的投資簽證門檻從 1,000 萬泰銖砍到 300 萬。靠的是移民局兩道命令：237/2568 管最初 90 天的簽證對接與類別變更，238/2568 管可續期的十二個月延長，在非移民「B」（投資）類別下開了一條長期居留路徑。合格的走法有三種：300 萬泰銖以上的永久產權公寓、合約總值超過 306 萬的登記長期租賃，或每月 85,000 泰銖的高額租賃（申請時預付三個月，續期時預付十二個月）。已登記的「地上權益」（Sap-Ing-Sith）也算一條獨立路徑。2026 年 6 月一度傳出租賃路徑暫停，後來解除了。

How it works in practice實務上怎麼運作

Ninety days on entry, then a 12-month extension renewable annually for as long as the asset is held, with no border runs. Dependants — spouse, unmarried children under 20, parents aged 50 or over — may be added with no further investment, but only after the principal has the full twelve-month extension. Fees run about 27,000 baht a year plus a one-off membership of 4,000 to 6,000 baht.入境給 90 天，之後轉成逐年可續的 12 個月，房子還在手上就能一直續，不用出境跑簽。眷屬不必再投資就能掛進來：配偶、20 歲以下未婚子女、50 歲以上父母；但要等主申請人先拿到完整的 12 個月。費用大約一年 27,000 泰銖，另加一次性會籍費 4,000 到 6,000。

The conditions that actually bind真正會卡住人的條件

The unit must be completed and registered at the Department of Lands — off-plan does not qualify. The counterparty must be a Thai individual or a Thai-majority company. Funds must be remitted from abroad in foreign currency, evidenced by a Foreign Exchange Transaction form. And the reduced 3-million threshold is only available with a Certification Letter from the Ministry of Tourism and Sports, issued through a designated long-stay operator; without it the applicant falls back to the old 10-million rule.標的必須已完工、且在土地廳登記完成，預售不算。賣方要是泰國籍個人，或泰資過半的公司。錢要從境外以外幣匯進來，拿得到外匯交易表（FET）。最容易被忽略的一條：300 萬這個降低門檻，要有觀光與體育部的認證函才適用，而且得透過指定的長期居留營運商辦；沒有這張函，就退回原本的 1,000 萬。

What it is not它不是什麼

It carries no work rights, still requires 90-day reporting and a TM.30 residence notification, and is not a path to permanent residence or citizenship. It is tied to the asset: sell the unit and the basis for the extension goes with it. It also does not change ownership law — the 49% freehold quota per building is untouched.它不給工作權，90 天報到和 TM.30 住所通報照跑，也不通往永久居留或公民身分。它綁在資產上：房子賣掉，續簽的依據就沒了。它更不動產權法律，每棟 49% 的永久產權配額原封不動。

Already under political pressure已經在承受政治壓力

The Phuket Tourism Association has publicly opposed the scheme, arguing the threshold is too low for the residency granted, that it invites undocumented work and unlicensed business, and that it inflates housing costs for residents. Industry submissions ask for a higher minimum or additional deposits per dependant. The direction of any revision is up, not down — which makes the current window the cheap one.普吉觀光協會已經公開反對，理由是門檻相對於給出去的居留權太低，會引來無證工作與無照經營，也推高當地人的住房成本。業界的建議是拉高最低金額，或每多一位眷屬就多一筆存款。要改也是往上改，不會往下。所以現在這個窗口，是便宜的那一個。

Why the threshold was set where it was — and why it has not worked yet門檻為何訂在這裡 —— 以及它為何還沒發揮作用

Three million baht is roughly US$89,000 at 33.65 to the dollar. The average foreign condominium purchase in 2025 was 4.1 million baht. The threshold was therefore set below the price the average foreign buyer was already paying — so for most of the existing foreign cohort the visa is a free upgrade on a purchase they were making anyway, not an inducement to buy something new.用 33.65 的匯率換算，300 萬泰銖大約 8.9 萬美元。而 2025 年外國人買公寓的平均金額是 410 萬泰銖。門檻訂在低於外國買方本來就在付的價格。換句話說，對已經在買的那群人，這張簽證是本來就要做的交易上多送的贈品，不是讓人多買一戶的誘因。

The evidence backs that reading. The scheme has been live since October 2025, and in the first quarter of 2026 foreign condominium transfers still fell 17.3% in units and 17.9% in value, with Bangkok down 35%. A visa did not offset a Chinese balance-sheet recession. Anyone underwriting the 3-million rule as a demand catalyst should note that one full quarter of live data has already failed to show it.數據站在這個讀法這邊。制度 2025 年 10 月上路，2026 年第一季外國人公寓過戶還是戶數年減 17.3%、金額年減 17.9%，曼谷跌 35%。一張簽證抵不過中國那邊的資產負債表衰退。要把 300 萬新規當需求催化劑來承作的人，先看清楚：已經有一整季的實際數據沒有站在你這邊。

Where it does bite is product selection. Because off-plan is excluded and the unit must already be registered, the rule channels foreign money into completed standing inventory — precisely the 210,112 units the market needs to clear — and away from developer presales. And the 3-million floor sits at the bottom of the 3-to-5-million band, the one segment where absorption has improved since 2021. Whether by design or accident, it is the best-targeted piece of housing policy Thailand currently has.它真正有效的地方在產品選擇。排除預售、只認已登記的物件，等於把外資推向已完工的在售存量，也就是市場最需要清掉的那 210,112 戶，而不是發展商的預售。而 300 萬這個下限，剛好卡在 300 萬到 500 萬區間的底部，也就是 2021 年以來吸納唯一持續改善的那一段。不管是刻意還是碰巧，這是泰國目前瞄得最準的一項住宅政策。

Set against Malaysia the comparison is stark. A foreigner buying in Kuala Lumpur faces a RM1 million purchase floor and 8% flat stamp duty, with MM2H Silver requiring RM600,000. A foreigner buying in Bangkok needs 3 million baht, about US$89,000, pays 2% transfer duty, and gets a renewable family visa attached. On entry cost and residency, Thailand is now materially the cheaper door. What Malaysia offers in exchange is a growing population, a functioning mortgage market, and a currency with a central bank that still has room to cut. That is the actual trade.跟馬來西亞放在一起看，落差很清楚。外國人在吉隆坡買房要跨 RM100 萬的門檻、付 8% 統一印花稅，走 MM2H 銀級則是 RM60 萬起跳。在曼谷只要 300 萬泰銖、約 8.9 萬美元，過戶稅 2%，還附一張可續期的全家簽證。論進場成本與居留權，泰國明顯是比較便宜的那道門。馬來西亞拿來換的，是還在成長的人口、一個能運作的房貸市場，以及一個央行還有降息空間的貨幣。這才是真正要選的東西。（完整逐項對照見本頁最下方的比較表。）

That is one variable, not four markets, and it means diversifying across Thai cities does not diversify the risk.泰國凡是還在運作的區域市場，靠的都是外資或非本地資金；凡是仰賴本國房貸的，全都卡住。這其實是同一個變數，不是四個市場。所以在泰國跨城市分散，並不會分散到風險。

Bangkok over the next decade is a carry-and-currency market with a flat real capital line. The rent is real, the yield is genuinely the widest in Asia against the local policy rate, and the stock is cheap in dollar terms after a decade of baht weakness punctuated by sharp rallies. How to read the table這張表該怎麼讀

The headline gap is capital efficiency. Thailand asks for US$89,000, all of it working inside the asset. Malaysia asks for about US$298,000, half of it sitting idle in a fixed deposit earning a deposit rate while the visa lasts. On entry cost alone Thailand is roughly a third of the price.最明顯的差距是資金效率。泰國要 8.9 萬美元，而且每一塊都在資產裡工作；馬來西亞要約 29.8 萬美元，其中一半躺在定存裡，簽證有效期間就領著存款利率。單看進場成本，泰國大約是三分之一的價錢。

What Malaysia sells back is time and certainty. Five years on one approval — twenty at Platinum — against Thailand's annual renewal cycle and a 90-day reporting obligation. For anyone who wants to stop thinking about immigration paperwork, that is worth real money.馬來西亞賣回來的東西是時間與確定性。一次核准管五年，白金級管二十年，對上泰國一年一續、外加 90 天報到。對不想每年為簽證文件費神的人，這確實值錢。

The row that decides it for most people is Can you sell? Both tie you to the asset, in opposite ways. Thailand lets you exit any time and takes the visa back. Malaysia lets you keep the visa and locks the asset for ten years — into a market whose own report on this site shows a national overhang at a three-year high. Buying illiquidity in an oversupplied market is a materially different risk from buying liquidity in one where the price line is flat.對多數人來說，真正決定選哪一邊的是「能不能賣」那一列。兩邊都把你綁在資產上，方向剛好相反：泰國讓你隨時走，但收回簽證；馬來西亞讓你留著簽證，卻把資產鎖十年，而本站的馬來西亞報告顯示，那個市場的全國餘屋正處在三年高點。在一個供給過剩的市場買下流動性不足，跟在一個價格線持平的市場買下可以隨時脫手，是兩種很不一樣的風險。

One caution applies to both. Neither is written in statute — Thailand's sits in two immigration orders, Malaysia's in ministry guidelines revised as recently as July 2024. Underwrite the property on its own numbers and treat the visa as a bonus that can be withdrawn, not as part of the return.有一點兩邊都適用：兩套制度都不是法律。泰國那套寫在兩道移民命令裡，馬來西亞那套是部會指引，而且 2024 年 7 月才剛改過。房子要用它自己的數字算得過，簽證當成隨時可能被收回的附加品，不要把它算進報酬裡。

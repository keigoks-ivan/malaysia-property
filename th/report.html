Bottom line總結 Bangkok is not short of buyers who want a home. It is short of buyers a bank will lend to. Between 40% and 50% of mortgage applications were declined in the first half of 2026, and in the sub-3-million-baht band — the price point at which most Thai households actually shop — rejection has run near 70%. That single filter explains more about this market than any supply statistic.

The result is a market that has quietly rebuilt itself around cash. At the top, foreign buyers took only 13.6% of transferred units in the first quarter of 2026 but 23.9% of the value. In the middle, second-hand homes now account for two-thirds of all ownership transfers, with 240,000 resale units listed nationwide, up 34.2% in a year as owners, banks and asset managers all try to exit at once. At the bottom, almost nothing clears. Developers have responded by abandoning the mass market — no new project launched in the central business district in the first quarter, 68% of new launches priced below 80,000 baht per square metre, and a booking rate of 24.3% on those launches against 43.8% a quarter earlier.

What makes this market interesting rather than merely weak is the carry. A gross yield near 5% against a 1.00% policy rate is roughly four points of positive carry, the widest in Asia. But the carry is wide because the policy rate is at a two-decade low, and it is at a two-decade low because the economy has been in deflation for most of a year. You are being paid to wait in a currency whose central bank has already spent its ammunition. And since October 2025 there is a second reason to look: a purchase of 3 million baht now carries a renewable long-stay visa for the buyer and their family, the single biggest change to foreign access in years — though one full quarter of data has yet to show it moving demand. The structural reasons behind all of this are set out in the companion Thailand Macro × Property analysis. 曼谷住宅市場的瓶頸不在購屋意願，而在授信取得。2026 年上半年房貸退件率介於 40% 至 50%；於 300 萬泰銖以下、即泰國家庭主要成交價位帶，退件率一度接近 70%。我們認為此一授信篩選機制對本市場的解釋力，高於任何單一供給指標。

市場已繞過銀行體系，重新以現金交易為核心。高價段方面，外國買方於 2026 年第一季僅佔過戶戶數 13.6%，卻貢獻 23.9% 的成交金額。中價段方面，二手屋已佔全部過戶的三分之二，全國二手掛牌量 24 萬戶、年增 34.2%，反映屋主、金融機構與資產管理公司同步處分。低價段則近乎停滯：第一季中央商業區無新案推出，新推案中 68% 定價低於每平方公尺 8 萬泰銖，訂購率降至 24.3%，前一季為 43.8%。

本市場的投資價值不在基本面強度，而在持有收益。毛收益率近 5%，對照 1.00% 的政策利率，正利差約 4 個百分點，為亞洲最高。惟利差之所以擴大，係因政策利率處於二十年低點，而其成因為該經濟體已歷經近一年的通縮。換言之，此一收益來自貨幣政策空間已近耗盡的環境。

另一項變數自 2025 年 10 月生效：購置 300 萬泰銖以上物件者，本人及眷屬可取得可續期之長期居留簽證。此為近年外資准入幅度最大的一次放寬，惟制度上路已滿一季，數據尚未顯示其對需求的實質推升。相關結構成因詳見《泰國總經 × 房產》。

  • 210,112 units are sitting on the market21 萬 112 戶正在市場上, and at the pace units are currently being absorbed that is 49.5 months of stock — a shade over four years. A healthy market carries 12 to 18 months.。以現行吸納速度計，去化期為 49.5 個月，逾四年。健全市場的合理水準為 12 至 18 個月。
  • The overhang is not a condo story alone. Townhouses are the single largest block at 68,831 units (32.8%), ahead of condominiums at 61,543 (29.3%) and single-detached houses at 51,739 (24.6%). Bangkok's problem spans the whole product ladder.餘屋並非集中於公寓單一產品。連棟屋 68,831 戶為最大宗，佔 32.8%；公寓 61,543 戶、佔 29.3%；獨棟 51,739 戶、佔 24.6%。去化壓力橫跨整條產品線。

Same market, different counting rules同一市場，不同計算規則

Every serious discussion of Bangkok oversupply eventually collides with the fact that the headline number depends entirely on where the counter draws the boundary. AREA counts 210,112 unsold units in projects actively being marketed inside the Bangkok metropolitan area. SCB EIC projects roughly 213,000 unsold units across Greater Bangkok by the end of 2026. Knight Frank puts the Greater Bangkok condominium overhang alone at around 350,000 units, five to six years of absorption at the recent run-rate of about 60,000 transfers a year, because it counts stock still under construction and stock in completed projects that have quietly stopped being marketed.曼谷餘屋規模的各家估計差異，主要來自統計邊界而非市場本身。AREA 僅計入曼谷都會區「仍在銷售中之案場」，得 210,112 戶。SCB EIC 推估大曼谷至 2026 年底約 213,000 戶。Knight Frank 單就大曼谷公寓即估至 35 萬戶，因其納入在建中及完工後已停止行銷之存量；以近年每年約 6 萬戶的過戶量計，需 5 至 6 年方能消化。

The Malaysian mirror與馬來西亞的對照

In Malaysia the statistical blind spot runs one way: serviced apartments were reclassified as commercial in 2016 and dropped out of the residential overhang series, so the official number understates by roughly 19,000 units. In Thailand the distortion runs the other way and is larger. There is no single official unsold register at all; what exists is a set of private surveys with incompatible boundaries. The honest reading is that Bangkok carries four-plus years of visible stock and an unmeasured tail behind it.馬來西亞的統計偏誤方向單一：服務式公寓於 2016 年改列商業類，退出住宅餘屋序列，官方數字因此低估約 1.9 萬戶。泰國的情況不同且幅度更大——該國並無官方未售登記制度，僅有數份統計邊界互不相容的民間調查。合理的表述為：曼谷持有逾四年的可見存量，另有一部分規模無法量化。

Practical rule: never compare an inventory number across two sources. Compare each source against its own history, and treat the level as a range, not a point.由此可得一項操作原則：存量數據不宜跨來源比較。各來源僅與自身歷史序列對照，水準值應視為區間而非單點。

Yield vs the rate you actually pay收益率對照實際適用利率
  • Against the 1.00% policy rate, a 5% gross yield is four points of carry — wider than Malaysia's 2.44 points and the widest in the region. For an unlevered cash buyer, that is the whole investment case.對照 1.00% 的政策利率，5% 的毛收益率隱含 4 個百分點的正利差，高於馬來西亞的 2.44 個百分點，為區域最高。對無槓桿之現金買方而言，此 4 個百分點即為投資論述的全部內容。
  • Against the promotional mortgage rate of roughly 2.65% to 3.85% for the first three years, the carry narrows to around 1.7 points. Thin, but still positive.對照前三年 2.65% 至 3.85% 的優惠房貸利率，利差收斂至約 1.7 個百分點，幅度雖薄仍為正值。
  • Against the MRR the loan reverts to in year four — averaging 7.38% across domestic commercial banks in June 2026 — the carry is minus 2.4 points. The Thai teaser structure means every levered buyer is underwriting a negative-carry position that begins on a known date.至第四年利率回歸 MRR（2026 年 6 月本地商銀平均 7.38%），利差轉為負 2.4 個百分點。泰國房貸採前低後高結構，意味使用槓桿之買方於承作時，即已鎖定一個負利差起算日確定的部位。

This is the single most under-discussed number in the Thai market. Malaysia's borrower pays a floating rate from day one and knows what they signed. Thailand's borrower pays a subsidised rate for 36 months and then faces a step-up of three to four points. A buyer whose debt-service ratio only cleared at the teaser rate is a default waiting for a calendar. It is one reason special-mention loans — the 30-to-90-day bucket that leads non-performing loans — had built to about 1.28 trillion baht by the end of 2025.我們認為此為泰國市場最被低估的變數。馬來西亞借款人自首期即適用浮動利率，還款條件透明；泰國借款人先享 36 個月補貼利率，其後面臨 3 至 4 個百分點的跳升。若借款人之償債比僅在優惠利率下勉強達標，該部位的劣化時點在承作當日即可推算。至 2025 年底，逾期 30 至 90 天之「特別關注類」貸款已累積至約 1.28 兆泰銖，該科目為不良債權的前置指標。

Nominal vs real名目 vs 實質

The national index rose 1.26% year-on-year in the first quarter of 2026, up from 0.63%. On that measure nothing looks wrong.以名目值觀察，曼谷房價指數自 1991 年的 62.3 升至 2025 年的 182.6，三十四年間近乎三倍。全國指數 2026 年第一季年增 1.26%，前一季 0.63%，表面上並無惡化。

The nominal-real gap is this market's normal state名目與實質的差距，是本市場的常態

Thai consumer prices fell year-on-year for twelve consecutive months through March 2026, so a +1.26% nominal reading was briefly a genuine real gain. That window has closed. The Bank of Thailand's June 2026 forecast puts headline inflation at 2.8% for 2026 as imported energy costs pass through. Hold the index at its current growth rate and Bangkok housing loses roughly 1.5% to 3% of real value a year — before transaction costs, vacancy or the mortgage step-up. The thirty-five-year series says this is not an aberration in this market: it has already delivered one thirteen-year real plateau and one twenty-two-year round trip to a previous high.泰國物價至 2026 年 3 月已連續 12 個月年減，故 1.26% 的名目增幅一度構成實質增值。此一條件已不復存在。泰國央行 6 月預測，在進口能源成本傳導下，2026 年全年通膨為 2.8%。若價格指數維持現行增速，曼谷住宅每年將產生 1.5% 至 3% 的實質價值減損，且尚未計入交易成本、空置損失與第四年的房貸利率跳升。三十五年的序列顯示，此種情形在本市場並非例外：實質房價曾有十三年橫盤、二十二年才收復前高的紀錄。

Where Bangkok sits曼谷的位置

Bangkok sits in the same cluster as Kuala Lumpur, New York and London on yield, and in a different universe on capital growth. Taipei and Sydney are the mirror image: sub-3% yields carried by two decades of price appreciation. Which of those two engines you are buying determines everything downstream — holding period, product choice, leverage, and how much a stalled price index should worry you. In Bangkok you are buying the rent line. If the rent line breaks, there is no second engine underneath it.以收益率衡量，曼谷與吉隆坡、紐約、倫敦同屬一組；以資本利得衡量則分屬不同類別。台北與雪梨為對照組：收益率低於 3%，報酬來源為二十年的價格上漲。報酬來源的差異決定後續所有配置決策——持有期間、產品選擇、槓桿使用，以及價格指數停滯時的應對。曼谷的報酬來源為租金收益；租金端一旦轉弱，並無第二項報酬來源可資替代。

Launches vs absorption推案 vs 吸納
  • Through 2016 to 2023, developers launched an average of about 100,000 units a year into demand of roughly 92,000. The annual gap looks modest. Run it for eight years and it is the overhang.2016 至 2023 年間，發展商年均推案約 10 萬戶，市場年吸納量約 9.2 萬戶。單年缺口有限，惟連續八年累積後，即形成當前的餘屋規模。
  • Since 2023 launches have halved to roughly 51,000 a year. This is the single most important positive in the Thai market and it is genuinely voluntary — no regulator forced it. Developers cut because presales stopped funding construction.2023 年後推案量腰斬至年均約 5.1 萬戶。我們認為此為泰國市場當前最重要的正面因素，且屬發展商自主決策，並非監管要求。減量的直接成因為預售資金已不足以支應工程進度。

Completions and completed condo value完工戶數與完工公寓貨值

Registered completions in the Bangkok Metropolitan Region fell to 72,584 units in 2025 from 97,284 a year earlier, a drop of 25.4%, with condominium completions down 22.7%. Why the threshold was set where it was — and why it has not worked yet門檻設定的邏輯與其效果未顯現的原因

Three million baht is roughly US$89,000 at 33.65 to the dollar. The average foreign condominium purchase in 2025 was 4.1 million baht. The threshold was therefore set below the price the average foreign buyer was already paying — so for most of the existing foreign cohort the visa is a free upgrade on a purchase they were making anyway, not an inducement to buy something new.以 33.65 匯率換算，300 萬泰銖約 8.9 萬美元；而 2025 年外國人購置公寓之平均金額為 410 萬泰銖。門檻設定低於外國買方既有的平均成交價。就既有客群而言，該簽證屬原有交易之附帶利益，而非增量購買的誘因。

The evidence backs that reading. The scheme has been live since October 2025, and in the first quarter of 2026 foreign condominium transfers still fell 17.3% in units and 17.9% in value, with Bangkok down 35%. A visa did not offset a Chinese balance-sheet recession. Anyone underwriting the 3-million rule as a demand catalyst should note that one full quarter of live data has already failed to show it.數據支持此一判讀。制度於 2025 年 10 月生效，2026 年第一季外國人公寓過戶仍戶數年減 17.3%、金額年減 17.9%，曼谷減幅達 35%。簽證誘因不足以抵銷中國家計部門資產負債表的收縮。將 300 萬新規視為需求催化劑者應注意：已有完整一季的實績數據未能支持該假設。

Where it does bite is product selection. Because off-plan is excluded and the unit must already be registered, the rule channels foreign money into completed standing inventory — precisely the 210,112 units the market needs to clear — and away from developer presales. And the 3-million floor sits at the bottom of the 3-to-5-million band, the one segment where absorption has improved since 2021. Whether by design or accident, it is the best-targeted piece of housing policy Thailand currently has.該措施的實質效果體現於產品導向。排除預售、僅認可已登記物件，使外資流向已完工之在售存量，即市場最需去化的 210,112 戶，而非發展商預售案。300 萬泰銖之下限恰位於 300 萬至 500 萬區段底部，亦即 2021 年以來吸納唯一持續改善之區段。無論是否為刻意設計，我們認為此為泰國現行住宅政策中標的最為精準者。

Set against Malaysia the comparison is stark. A foreigner buying in Kuala Lumpur faces a RM1 million purchase floor and 8% flat stamp duty, with MM2H Silver requiring RM600,000. A foreigner buying in Bangkok needs 3 million baht, about US$89,000, pays 2% transfer duty, and gets a renewable family visa attached. What this adds up to綜合結論

Bangkok over the next decade is a carry-and-currency market with a flat real capital line. The rent is real, the yield is genuinely the widest in Asia against the local policy rate, and the stock is cheap in dollar terms after a decade of baht weakness punctuated by sharp rallies. What is not there is an appreciation engine: no population growth, no income breakout, no supply shortage, and a four-year queue of unsold homes ahead of you.未來十年的曼谷屬收益與匯率驅動的市場，資本利得實質持平。租金收益具實質性；相對本國政策利率，收益率為亞洲最高；經歷十年泰銖走弱（其間夾雜數次急升）後，以美元計價之房價亦不昂貴。所缺者為增值動能：無人口成長、無所得突破、無供給短缺，且前方尚有四年的未售存量。

That is not a reason to avoid the market. It is a reason to underwrite it as an income asset with a 15% non-resident withholding, a legally capped ownership share, and a hard four-year clock on the mortgage rate — and to require the entry price to compensate for all three. The buyer who does that in the 3-to-5-million-baht band on a completed, transit-adjacent, foreign-quota unit is being paid well to wait. The buyer who underwrites capital growth is buying the one thing this market has not produced in a decade.此非迴避該市場的理由，而是調整承作方式的理由：應以收益型資產評價，並計入三項附帶條件——非居民租金扣繳 15%、持有比例受法定上限限制、房貸利率存在四年期的重設時點。進場價格須就此三項提供補償。於 300 萬至 500 萬泰銖區間，購置已完工、鄰近營運軌道、屬外配名額之物件，其風險調整後報酬具合理性。若將資本利得納入承作假設，則所買進者為本市場十年來未曾產生的報酬來源。

How to read the table本表的判讀重點

The headline gap is capital efficiency. Thailand asks for US$89,000, all of it working inside the asset. Malaysia asks for about US$298,000, half of it sitting idle in a fixed deposit earning a deposit rate while the visa lasts. On entry cost alone Thailand is roughly a third of the price.最顯著的差異在資金效率。泰國要求 8.9 萬美元，且全數配置於資產本身；馬來西亞要求約 29.8 萬美元，其中約半數為閒置定存，於簽證有效期間僅取得存款利率。單就進場成本比較，泰國約為馬來西亞的三分之一。

What Malaysia sells back is time and certainty. Five years on one approval — twenty at Platinum — against Thailand's annual renewal cycle and a 90-day reporting obligation. For anyone who wants to stop thinking about immigration paperwork, that is worth real money.馬來西亞所提供之對價為期間與確定性：單次核准效期五年，白金級二十年，對照泰國之逐年續期並附 90 天報到義務。對重視行政程序穩定性的申請人而言，此一差異具實質價值。

The row that decides it for most people is Can you sell? Both tie you to the asset, in opposite ways. Thailand lets you exit any time and takes the visa back. Malaysia lets you keep the visa and locks the asset for ten years — into a market whose own report on this site shows a national overhang at a three-year high. Buying illiquidity in an oversupplied market is a materially different risk from buying liquidity in one where the price line is flat.對多數申請人而言，決定性項目為資產處分限制。兩者均將申請人綁定於資產，方向相反：泰國容許隨時處分，惟同時終止簽證；馬來西亞保留簽證效力，卻鎖定資產十年，而依本站馬來西亞報告，該市場全國餘屋正處於三年高點。於供給過剩之市場承擔流動性不足，與於價格持平之市場保有處分彈性，二者為性質不同的風險。

One caution applies to both. Neither is written in statute — Thailand's sits in two immigration orders, Malaysia's in ministry guidelines revised as recently as July 2024. Underwrite the property on its own numbers and treat the visa as a bonus that can be withdrawn, not as part of the return.有一項限制適用於兩者：均非法律位階。泰國之依據為兩道移民命令，馬來西亞之依據為部會指引，且於 2024 年 7 月方才修訂。標的應以其自身財務數字評價，簽證則視為可能被撤回之附帶條件，不宜計入預期報酬。

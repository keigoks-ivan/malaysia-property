Bottom line總結 Thailand is the first economy in Southeast Asia to hit the demographic wall before it finished getting rich. Deaths have exceeded births every year since 2021, the population has fallen for six consecutive years, and gross national income per head sits at US$7,690 against a high-income threshold of US$14,375. Vietnam and the Philippines were promoted into Thailand's own income tier in July 2026. Being overtaken from below is not a ranking problem; it is the labour-cost arbitrage that built Thai manufacturing now running in reverse.

The macro numbers look benign and are not. Policy rates are at 1.00%, the lowest in over two decades, yet credit growth is 0.2% and half of mortgage applications are declined. Consumer prices fell year-on-year for twelve straight months through March 2026, which means the "loose" nominal rate was a positive real rate for most of a year. Household debt at 86.7% of GDP is coming down, but through refusal to lend rather than through repayment. Public debt at 66.8% is inside a 70% legal ceiling that leaves roughly three points of room. Every conventional lever is either pulled or jammed.

For property, the operative conclusion is that Thailand's housing market cannot be read off the interest rate. It is set by three things the central bank does not control: who is eligible for credit, how many households are being formed, and who is allowed to buy. Those three variables are analysed against the market data in the companion Bangkok Property Market Report. 泰國是東南亞第一個在「還沒富起來」之前就撞上人口牆的經濟體。2021 年起死亡數年年多於出生數，人口已連續六年下降，人均國民所得停在 7,690 美元，而高收入門檻是 14,375 美元。2026 年 7 月，越南與菲律賓被升進泰國所在的收入層級。從下方被追上不是排名問題，而是當年造就泰國製造業的那個勞動成本套利，如今反向運轉。

總經數字看起來溫和，實際上不是。政策利率 1.00%，二十餘年最低，但信用成長只有 0.2%，房貸申請有一半被退。消費者物價至 2026 年 3 月連續 12 個月年減——這代表「寬鬆」的名目利率，在大半年裡其實是正的實質利率。家庭債務佔 GDP 86.7%，正在下降，但靠的是「不放款」而不是「還款」。公共債務 66.8%，法定上限 70%，只剩約三個百分點的空間。每一根常規槓桿不是已經拉到底，就是卡住了。

對房地產而言，可操作的結論是：泰國房市不能用利率來讀。決定它的是央行管不到的三件事——誰有資格取得信用、有多少家戶正在形成、以及誰被允許購買。這三個變數與市場數據的對照，見另文《曼谷住宅市場報告》。

Part 1 · The Cycle週期篇 · 我們在哪裡

Monetary cycle × house prices貨幣週期 × 房價

Policy rate path against the residential price index · 2023–2026 · Bank of Thailand政策利率路徑對上住宅價格指數 · 2023–2026 · 泰國央行

Six cuts, no housing response六次降息，房市沒有反應
  • The Bank of Thailand took the policy rate from a cycle peak of 2.50% down to 1.00% between October 2024 and February 2026, and has held it unanimously since — 7–0 in June 2026.泰國央行在 2024 年 10 月至 2026 年 2 月間，把政策利率從循環高點 2.50% 降到 1.00%，之後一致決維持——2026 年 6 月為 7 比 0。
  • Over the same period the national residential price index moved from roughly flat to +1.26% year-on-year, and the Bangkok index went negative. A 150-basis-point easing cycle produced no measurable price response in the capital.同一期間，全國住宅價格指數由大致持平走到年增 1.26%，而曼谷指數轉為負值。150 個基點的寬鬆循環，在首都沒有產生任何可衡量的價格反應。
  • The transmission failure is not mysterious. Thai mortgages are written at a discounted fixed rate for three years and then revert to MRR, which averaged 7.38% across domestic commercial banks in June 2026. Policy cuts move the reversion rate slowly and the teaser rate barely at all, so the payment a borrower is underwritten against hardly changes.傳導失效的原因並不神祕。泰國房貸是前三年優惠固定利率、之後跳回 MRR，而 2026 年 6 月本地商銀 MRR 平均為 7.38%。降息只能緩慢帶動跳升後的利率，對優惠利率幾乎沒有影響，所以借款人被核貸時所依據的月付金幾乎沒變。

The loose rate that was actually tight那個其實很緊的寬鬆利率

Headline CPI year-on-year and the implied real policy rate · 2025–2026 · Ministry of Commerce / BOTCPI 年增率與隱含實質政策利率 · 2025–2026 · 商業部／泰國央行

Deflation makes cheap money expensive通縮讓便宜的錢變貴

Thai consumer prices fell year-on-year for twelve consecutive months to March 2026: −0.66% in January, −0.88% in February, −0.08% in March. With the policy rate at 1.00% to 1.25% through that stretch, the real policy rate was positive by 1.1 to 2.1 points — genuinely restrictive — at precisely the moment the headlines described monetary policy as maximally accommodative. That is the cleanest single explanation for why five rate cuts did not restart the housing market.泰國消費者物價至 2026 年 3 月連續 12 個月年減：1 月 −0.66%、2 月 −0.88%、3 月 −0.08%。這段期間政策利率在 1.00% 至 1.25%，實質政策利率因此為正 1.1 至 2.1 個百分點——實質上是緊縮的——而當時的標題正把貨幣政策描述為極度寬鬆。這是「五次降息為何沒能重啟房市」最乾淨的單一解釋。

And now the inflation is the wrong kind而現在來的又是錯的那種通膨

The Bank of Thailand's June 2026 forecast puts headline inflation at 2.8% for 2026, falling to 1.4% in 2027 — driven by energy and production-cost pass-through, not by demand. Cost-push inflation with 0.2% credit growth is the worst combination available: it erodes the real value of rent and wages without giving the central bank any reason to think demand has recovered, and it makes further cuts harder to justify.泰國央行 2026 年 6 月的預測，把全年通膨放在 2.8%、2027 年降至 1.4%——推力來自能源與生產成本傳導，不是需求。在信用成長 0.2% 的情況下出現成本推動型通膨，是最糟的一種組合：它侵蝕租金與工資的實質價值，卻不給央行任何「需求已復甦」的理由，也讓進一步降息更難被合理化。

Real economy × house prices實體經濟 × 房價

Real GDP growth · 2015–2027F · NESDC / BOT / consensus forecasts實質 GDP 成長 · 2015–2027 預估 · NESDC／泰國央行／機構預測

A decade below the regional line十年低於區域線
  • Thailand grew 2.4% in 2025. The Bank of Thailand forecasts 2.3% for 2026 and 1.8% for 2027; the World Bank has 1.6% for 2026, the OECD 1.5%, and the Ministry of Finance 2.5%. The spread of forecasts is wide, and every one of them is below the ASEAN average of 4.2% to 4.5%.泰國 2025 年成長 2.4%。泰國央行預估 2026 年 2.3%、2027 年 1.8%；世界銀行 2026 年 1.6%，OECD 1.5%，財政部 2.5%。預測分歧很大，但每一個都低於東協 4.2% 至 4.5% 的平均。
  • In the fourth quarter of 2025 Vietnam grew 8.46%, Malaysia 5.7%, Indonesia 5.39% and Thailand 2.5%. This is not a cyclical gap. Thailand has grown at roughly half the regional pace for a decade, and the compounding shows up directly in the income line that determines who can service a mortgage.2025 年第四季，越南成長 8.46%、馬來西亞 5.7%、印尼 5.39%，泰國 2.5%。這不是循環性落差。泰國以區域約一半的速度成長已經十年，而複利效果直接顯現在決定「誰付得起房貸」的那條所得線上。
  • Property is a levered claim on nominal income growth. At 2% real growth and roughly 1% inflation, Thai nominal income compounds at about 3% a year, against a mortgage that reverts to 7.38%. The arithmetic of leverage does not work at these inputs, which is why the Thai market has quietly become a cash market.房地產是對名目所得成長的槓桿求償權。在 2% 實質成長與約 1% 通膨下，泰國名目所得每年約複合 3%，而房貸會跳回 7.38%。在這組輸入下，槓桿的算術不成立——這正是泰國市場悄悄變成現金市場的原因。

Part 2 · The Structural Economy經濟結構篇 · 為什麼這影響房價

Malaysia deindustrialised too soon. Thailand industrialised into the wrong product, and then it got old.馬來西亞是太早去工業化。泰國是工業化到了錯的產品上，然後老了。

Stuck below the high-income line — and being overtaken卡在高收入門檻前 —— 而且正被追上

Gross national income per capita, US$ · 2026 · World Bank classification人均國民所得（美元）· 2026 · 世界銀行分類

Where Thailand sits泰國的位置
  • Thailand reached middle-income status in the early 1990s, among the first in populous Southeast Asia. Thirty years later GNI per capita is US$7,690 against a high-income cut-off of US$14,375. Real GDP per capita grew 2.2% in 2025; at that rate the gap takes roughly 29 years to close.泰國在 1990 年代初期進入中等收入，是東南亞人口大國中最早的一批。三十年後，人均 GNI 為 7,690 美元，高收入門檻為 14,375 美元。2025 年實質人均 GDP 成長 2.2%；以此速度，缺口約需 29 年才能補上。
  • In July 2026 the World Bank moved Vietnam and the Philippines into the upper-middle-income tier Thailand has occupied for years — Vietnam at US$4,970 and the Philippines at US$4,850, both clearing the US$4,636 entry line. Thailand's comparative advantage was cheap, disciplined factory labour; that advantage now belongs to countries with younger populations and lower wages.2026 年 7 月，世界銀行把越南與菲律賓升入泰國停留多年的中高收入層級——越南 4,970 美元、菲律賓 4,850 美元，皆跨過 4,636 美元的入門線。泰國過去的比較優勢是便宜、有紀律的工廠勞動力；那個優勢如今屬於人口更年輕、工資更低的國家。
  • The property translation is direct. A housing market's long-run price line is set by income, and Thailand's income line has been flattening for a decade while its construction cost line has not. That is the whole reason the average newly launched Bangkok unit now costs 19 years of an average Bangkok household's income.轉譯到房地產很直接。房市的長期價格線由所得決定，而泰國的所得線已經平坦了十年，營建成本線卻沒有。這就是為什麼曼谷新推案的平均單價，如今相當於一個曼谷平均家庭 19 年的所得。

The root: it aged before it got rich病根：它在富起來之前先老了

Births, deaths and age structure · Department of Provincial Administration / national projections出生、死亡與年齡結構 · 內政部地方行政廳／全國推估

The crossover already happened交叉點已經發生
  • Thailand recorded 462,240 births against 571,646 deaths in 2024 — the first time births fell below 500,000 since 1949 — and 416,574 against 559,684 in 2025. The population fell to 65.8 million at end-2025, down 142,199 in a year and declining for a sixth straight year.泰國 2024 年出生 462,240 人、死亡 571,646 人——1949 年以來出生首度低於 50 萬——2025 年為 416,574 對 559,684。人口在 2025 年底降至 6,580 萬，一年減少 142,199 人，連續第六年下降。
  • The total fertility rate is around 1.1, among the lowest anywhere. The under-15 population fell from 11.7 million in 2015 to 9.6 million; the 60-plus population rose from 8.4 million to 12.6 million. The working-age share fell from 68% to 64% and is projected at 58% by 2040.總生育率約 1.1，屬全球最低之列。15 歲以下人口從 2015 年的 1,170 萬降至 960 萬；60 歲以上從 840 萬升至 1,260 萬。工作年齡人口佔比從 68% 降到 64%，預估 2040 年降至 58%。
  • Thailand crosses the 28% elderly threshold around 2033, with some projections at 2036. Japan crossed the equivalent line at roughly four times Thailand's current income per head; Korea at roughly five times. Thailand is the first major economy to reach a super-aged structure at under US$8,000 per capita.泰國約在 2033 年跨過 28% 的高齡門檻，部分推估為 2036 年。日本跨過同等界線時，人均所得約為泰國現值的四倍；南韓約五倍。泰國是第一個在人均不到 8,000 美元就達到超高齡結構的主要經濟體。

Why this is the property variable, not a social one為何這是房地產變數，不只是社會議題

Net new housing demand comes from household formation. Everything else — investment purchase, upgrading, foreign buying — reallocates existing stock without adding a household. Thailand's household-formation engine went into reverse in 2021 and the country has kept building. Malaysia's demographic engine, by contrast, is the last in Asia to cool: population growth near 1.4% with a median age in the low thirties. The two markets carry similar overhangs, and only one of them has a growing base of future buyers underneath it.淨新增住宅需求來自家戶形成。其他一切——投資置產、換屋、外資購買——都只是既有存量的再配置，不增加任何一個家戶。泰國的家戶形成引擎 2021 年開始倒退，而這個國家仍在蓋房子。相對地，馬來西亞的人口引擎是亞洲最晚降溫的：人口成長約 1.4%，中位年齡在三十出頭。兩個市場背著相似的餘屋，但只有其中一個底下還有正在成長的未來買方基數。

The auto shock — the second pillar cracking汽車業衝擊 —— 第二根支柱裂開

Vehicle production, units · 2023–2026 · Federation of Thai Industries; 2023 and 2025 derived from reported changes汽車產量（輛）· 2023–2026 · 泰國工業聯合會；2023 與 2025 由公布變動率推導

Production產量

Thailand built its industrial identity as the "Detroit of Asia", assembling internal-combustion pickups and passenger cars for Japanese manufacturers. Production fell to 1,468,997 units in 2024, down 19.9%, and kept falling through 2025; output hit a five-year low in April 2026. The mechanism has two parts and both are structural. Domestically, tighter auto lending against 86.7%-of-GDP household debt removed the buyer, and repossessions rose. Externally, Chinese electric-vehicle makers built Thai capacity faster than the Japanese incumbents converted their existing plants, so the transition is happening at the expense of the incumbent supply chain rather than through it.泰國的工業身分建立在「亞洲底特律」上——為日系車廠組裝內燃機皮卡與轎車。產量 2024 年降至 1,468,997 輛、年減 19.9%，2025 年續跌；2026 年 4 月創五年新低。機制有兩個部分，兩個都是結構性的。國內方面，在家庭債務佔 GDP 86.7% 的背景下，車貸收緊移除了買方，收車件數上升。國外方面，中國電動車廠在泰國建產能的速度，快過日系既有廠房的轉型速度，於是這場轉型是以既有供應鏈為代價發生，而不是透過它發生。

For property this matters in two specific places. The Eastern Economic Corridor — Chonburi, Rayong, Chachoengsao, and by extension the Pattaya residential market — is where auto employment concentrates. And the auto supply chain is the largest single source of the formal-sector, documented wages that Thai banks will actually lend against. A contraction there does not just cut GDP; it shrinks the mortgage-eligible population.對房地產，這件事落在兩個具體位置。東部經濟走廊——春武里、羅勇、北柳，以及延伸到芭達雅的住宅市場——是汽車業就業的集中地。而汽車供應鏈是泰國銀行真正願意放款的那種正式部門、可舉證薪資的最大單一來源。那裡的收縮不只砍 GDP，它會縮小「有資格辦房貸的人口」。

Tourism — the pillar with a China-shaped hole觀光 —— 一根有中國形狀破洞的支柱

Foreign arrivals, total and Chinese · 2024–2026F · Ministry of Tourism and Sports / ATTA外國旅客人次，總數與中國 · 2024–2026 預估 · 觀光與體育部／ATTA

Arrivals旅客人次
  • Total arrivals fell 7.23% to 32.97 million in 2025, the first annual decline outside the pandemic years. Chinese arrivals collapsed from about 6.73 million in 2024 to 4.47 million in 2025, down roughly 34%.2025 年總旅客人次年減 7.23% 至 3,297 萬，是疫情年份之外的首次年度下滑。中國旅客從 2024 年約 673 萬降到 2025 年 447 萬，約減少 34%。
  • The proximate cause was safety perception — a widely publicised kidnapping in January 2025 and continuing coverage of regional scam networks. Operators cut the 2026 Chinese forecast from 9 million to 7 million. Through 4 July 2026 Thailand had received 16.21 million visitors, with China at 2.65 million.直接原因是安全觀感——2025 年 1 月一起廣受報導的綁架案，以及區域詐騙園區的持續曝光。業者把 2026 年中國旅客預測從 900 萬下修到 700 萬。至 2026 年 7 月 4 日，泰國累計接待 1,621 萬人次，其中中國 265 萬。
  • Tourism is where the Thai economy converts foreign demand into domestic wages without going through the factory. It is also the demand base under Phuket, Pattaya and every short-let yield model in the country. A structurally smaller Chinese cohort re-rates the resort markets permanently, not cyclically, and the replacement cohorts — Indian, Russian, Middle Eastern — spend differently and stay differently.觀光是泰國把國外需求轉換成國內薪資、又不必經過工廠的環節；它同時也是普吉、芭達雅以及全國每一個短租收益模型底下的需求基礎。結構性變小的中國客群，會永久性、而非循環性地重評度假市場，而替補的客群——印度、俄羅斯、中東——花錢方式與停留方式都不同。

Growth that doesn't reach a bank statement到不了銀行對帳單的成長

Labour market structure · latest available · OECD / National Statistical Office / ILO勞動市場結構 · 最新可得 · OECD／國家統計局／ILO

Informality and sector mix非正式就業與部門結構
  • Roughly 53% to 55% of Thai workers are informal — about 21 million people. Agriculture accounts for around 30% of employment and about 54% of all informal workers, some 11.4 million.泰國約有 53% 至 55% 的勞動人口屬非正式就業——約 2,100 萬人。農業約佔就業的 30%，並佔全部非正式工作者的約 54%、約 1,140 萬人。
  • The minimum wage runs 337 to 400 baht a day by province. Average national monthly pay is around 15,000 baht and the median around 12,000; Bangkok's formal-sector average is 22,274 baht and average household income 39,100.最低工資按府別為每日 337 至 400 泰銖。全國月均薪資約 15,000 泰銖、中位數約 12,000；曼谷正式部門平均為 22,274 泰銖，家庭平均所得 39,100 泰銖。
  • Informality is the hinge between macro and housing. An informal worker may earn well and still be unbankable, because the loan officer needs twelve months of documented payroll. Half of Thailand is structurally outside the mortgage system regardless of income — which is why a 70% rejection rate in the cheap price band is a structural reading, not a cyclical one.非正式就業是總經與住宅之間的樞紐。一個非正式工作者可能賺得不錯，卻仍然不可貸——因為授信人員需要 12 個月的薪資證明。無論收入多少，泰國有一半人口在結構上就在房貸體系之外——這也是為什麼低價帶 70% 的退件率是結構性讀數，不是循環性讀數。

Household debt — the decade that is still being paid for家庭債務 —— 仍在償還的那個十年

Household debt as a share of GDP · 2008–4Q2025 · Bank of Thailand家庭債務佔 GDP · 2008–2025Q4 · 泰國央行

Household debt / GDP家庭債務／GDP

The ratio ran from 66% in 2008 to a 95.5% peak in the first quarter of 2021 and has eased to 86.7% at end-2025, or 16.44 trillion baht. Malaysia sits at 84.8% and is generally treated as ASEAN's leverage outlier; Thailand is higher, on half the growth rate, with a shrinking population. Two features make the Thai stock worse than the headline. First, an unusually large share is non-productive — auto, credit card and personal lending rather than mortgage or business debt. Second, the deleveraging is being achieved through refusal to lend: credit growth was 0.2% in the first quarter of 2026 with SME and consumer lending still contracting outright. Special-mention loans, the 30-to-90-day bucket, stood near 1.28 trillion baht against 535.8 billion of recognised non-performing loans.這個比率從 2008 年的 66% 升到 2021 年第一季 95.5% 的峰值，回落至 2025 年底的 86.7%、16.44 兆泰銖。馬來西亞為 84.8%，通常被視為東協的槓桿異數；泰國比它更高，成長率只有一半，而且人口在萎縮。有兩點讓泰國的債務存量比標題更糟。第一，其中非生產性債務比重異常高——車貸、信用卡與個人信貸，而非房貸或營運債務。第二，去槓桿是靠不放款達成的：2026 年第一季信用成長 0.2%，中小企業與消費貸款仍在絕對值收縮。逾期 30 至 90 天的「特別關注類」貸款接近 1.28 兆泰銖，相對已認列的不良貸款 5,358 億泰銖。

The baht — a currency that strengthens for the wrong reasons泰銖 —— 因為錯的理由而升值的貨幣

USD/THB, selected points · 2025–2026美元兌泰銖，選定時點 · 2025–2026

USD/THB美元／泰銖

The baht strengthened nearly 8% over the year to February 2026, touching 30.84 on 25 January, its firmest since March 2021, before easing back to 33.65 by 30 July 2026. The appreciation was not a vote of confidence in Thai growth. It reflected a large current-account surplus produced by weak imports, gold flows, and the Federal Reserve's easing path — a currency rising because the domestic economy is too soft to buy anything. A strong baht then does three damaging things at once: it prices Thai exports out, it makes Thailand expensive for the tourists it is trying to win back, and it imports deflation. For a foreign property buyer it means the entry price in dollar terms moves ten per cent on the currency before the asset moves at all, and that swing has recently been larger than anything the housing market itself delivered.至 2026 年 2 月的一年間，泰銖升值近 8%，1 月 25 日觸及 30.84，為 2021 年 3 月以來最強，之後回落至 2026 年 7 月 30 日的 33.65。這波升值不是對泰國成長的信任票。它反映的是進口疲弱、黃金流與聯準會寬鬆路徑所產生的龐大經常帳順差——一個因為國內經濟弱到買不動東西而上漲的貨幣。強勢泰銖接著同時做三件有害的事：把出口的價格擠出去、讓泰國對它想爭取回來的旅客變貴、以及輸入通縮。對外國房產買方而言，這代表以美元計的進場價，在資產本身還沒動之前，就先因匯率移動了一成——而近期這個擺盪比房市本身給出的還大。

The fiscal trap — three points of room財政陷阱 —— 只剩三個百分點

Public debt against the statutory ceiling · FY2026 · Public Debt Management Office / IMF公共債務對上法定上限 · 2026 會計年度 · 公共債務管理辦公室／IMF

Debt, ceiling, deficit債務、上限、赤字
  • Public debt was 66.8% of GDP in May 2026 and is projected near 67.8% by fiscal year-end, against a legislated ceiling of 70%. The FY2026 deficit is 860 billion baht, about 4.4% to 4.5% of GDP.公共債務在 2026 年 5 月為 GDP 的 66.8%，預估至會計年度末接近 67.8%，法定上限為 70%。2026 會計年度赤字 8,600 億泰銖，約當 GDP 的 4.4% 至 4.5%。
  • Roughly three points of headroom, against a deficit running at four and a half points a year, is less than one budget cycle of space before the ceiling has to be raised or the deficit cut. The medium-term framework openly assumes debt stays high for five more years.約三個百分點的餘裕，對上每年四個半百分點的赤字，等於不到一個預算循環的空間，之後就必須調高上限或削減赤字。中期財政框架已公開假設債務將在未來五年維持高檔。
  • This is why Thai property stimulus takes the form it does. The 0.01% transfer-fee cut and the co-payment consumer schemes are cheap; a serious housing subsidy, a public-housing programme, or a mortgage-guarantee facility is not. The state can waive a fee. It cannot underwrite a borrower.這解釋了泰國房市刺激為何長成現在這樣。0.01% 的過戶費優惠與共付型消費方案都很便宜；真正的住宅補貼、公共住宅計畫或房貸保證機制則不便宜。國家可以豁免一筆規費，卻沒有能力去擔保一個借款人。

Human capital — the input that is shrinking twice人力資本 —— 一個縮兩次的投入項

Education spending share and PISA outcomes · OECD Economic Survey of Thailand教育支出佔比與 PISA 表現 · OECD 泰國經濟調查

Spending share and top performers支出佔比與頂尖表現者

Education's share of government spending fell from 16.9% in 2014 to 11.2% in 2022. In the most recent PISA round, 1% of Thai students were top performers in mathematics against an OECD average of 9%. The OECD's 2025 survey concluded that productivity growth has slowed and that product-market regulation is more restrictive than the OECD average. Put beside a shrinking cohort of children, this is the same variable failing twice: fewer young people, each receiving less. Any escape from the middle-income trap requires productivity, productivity requires human capital, and the human-capital line has been falling for a decade while the demographic line falls underneath it.教育支出佔政府支出的比重，從 2014 年的 16.9% 降到 2022 年的 11.2%。在最近一輪 PISA 中，泰國只有 1% 的學生是數學頂尖表現者，OECD 平均為 9%。OECD 2025 年的調查結論是：生產力成長趨緩，且產品市場管制較 OECD 平均更為限制。把這放在正在縮小的兒童世代旁邊，就是同一個變數失敗了兩次：年輕人更少，每個人分到的更少。任何跳出中等收入陷阱的路徑都需要生產力，生產力需要人力資本，而人力資本這條線已經下滑十年，人口線還在它底下繼續掉。

Part 3 · The Politics — Who Sets the Rules政治篇 · 誰在寫規則

In Malaysia the political question is who gets the growth. In Thailand it is who is permitted to govern at all.在馬來西亞，政治問題是誰分得到成長。在泰國，問題是誰被允許執政。

The veto architecture否決架構

The 2017 constitution2017 年憲法

Drafted after the 2014 coup and approved in a referendum held under military rule, it was designed to constrain elected majorities rather than express them. It is the document under which every subsequent dispute has been adjudicated.2014 年政變後起草、在軍政府統治下經公投通過，設計目的是約束民選多數而非表達它。此後每一場爭議，都是依這部文件裁決的。

The Senate參議院

Senators appointed under the junta held the power to vote on prime ministerial candidates, and confirmed the judges now sitting on the Constitutional Court. The successor selection process still excludes the general public.軍政府時期任命的參議員握有總理人選的投票權，並確認了如今在憲法法院席上的法官。後續的遴選程序仍將一般公眾排除在外。

The Constitutional Court憲法法院

Over twenty years the courts have removed three prime ministers and dissolved several opposition parties, including the election-winning Move Forward Party in August 2024. Dissolution is an available remedy for offences as broad as proposing a constitutional amendment.二十年間，法院拉下三位總理、解散多個反對黨，包括 2024 年 8 月贏得選舉的前進黨。解散是一種可用的救濟手段，其構成要件寬到包含「提出修憲主張」。

The Election Commission and NACC選委會與國家反貪委員會

Independent agencies with the power to refer, disqualify and prosecute. In practice they are the first stage of a pipeline that ends at the Constitutional Court, and they operate outside electoral accountability.具有移送、褫奪資格與起訴權的獨立機關。實務上，它們是一條終點在憲法法院的管道的第一站，並且不受選舉問責。

A record of removals被拉下台的紀錄

  • 2006Military coup removes Thaksin Shinawatra. The cycle of elected government, judicial or military removal, and re-contest begins in its modern form.軍事政變推翻他信。「民選政府—司法或軍事罷黜—再競逐」的現代循環自此成形。
  • 2014Second coup. The 2017 constitution and the appointed Senate follow, embedding the veto architecture in law.第二次政變。隨後產生 2017 年憲法與被任命的參議院，將否決架構寫入法律。
  • 2024The Constitutional Court dissolves the Move Forward Party, which had won the most seats in the 2023 election, over its proposal to amend the lèse-majesté law. Prime Minister Srettha Thavisin is removed in a separate ruling the same month.憲法法院以主張修改冒犯君主罪法律為由，解散在 2023 年選舉中贏得最多席次的前進黨。同月，總理賽塔在另一項裁決中被解職。
  • 2025Paetongtarn Shinawatra is removed. Parliament elects Anutin Charnvirakul prime minister on 5 September with 311 votes, heading a minority government.貝東丹被解職。國會於 9 月 5 日以 311 票選出阿努廷為總理，領導少數政府。
  • Dec 2025Anutin dissolves the House after three months in office, citing an unworkable parliament.阿努廷上任三個月後解散國會下議院，理由是國會無法運作。
  • Feb 2026Bhumjaithai wins a clear election victory — reported as the first win this century for a party aligned with the royalist establishment. Anutin returns with a mandate rather than a bargain.泰自豪黨在選舉中明確勝出——被報導為本世紀第一次由親王室建制立場的政黨獲勝。阿努廷帶著民意授權、而非交易而歸。

Why this matters to a property investor這對房地產投資人為何重要

The pattern is not instability in the sense of violence or expropriation. Thai property rights have held through every one of these episodes. The cost is policy horizon. A government that expects to be removed does not legislate a ten-year housing programme; it renews an annual fee waiver. That is exactly the policy mix Thailand has: the 0.01% transfer fee has been extended repeatedly one year at a time, while the 99-year lease and the 75% foreign quota — both of which would move genuine capital — have sat undrafted for years.這裡的問題不是暴力或徵收意義上的不穩定。泰國的財產權在上述每一次事件中都撐住了。代價是政策視野。一個預期自己會被拉下台的政府，不會立一部十年的住宅法案，它會續行一年期的規費減免。這正是泰國目前的政策組合：0.01% 過戶費一年一年地反覆延長，而真正能牽動資本的 99 年租賃與 75% 外資配額，多年來連草案都沒有。

The 2026 government and what it will and will not do2026 年的政府，會做與不會做的事

  • Will do: investment liberalisation. The Bhumjaithai platform targets 3%-plus growth through foreign direct investment in six priority sectors — future food, data centres and cloud, smart electronics and printed circuit boards, EVs and batteries, digital and AI, and international business centres — mainly by removing approval friction. This is cheap, fits the fiscal constraint, and is already visible in BOI numbers.會做：投資自由化。泰自豪黨的政綱以外人直接投資六大優先產業——未來食品、資料中心與雲端、智慧電子與印刷電路板、電動車與電池、數位與 AI、國際商業中心——推動 3% 以上的成長，主要手段是拆除審批摩擦。這很便宜、符合財政約束，而且已經在 BOI 的數字上看得見。
  • Will not do: casinos. Anutin has stated Thailand will "wait for another prime minister" for casino legalisation, distancing his party from the entertainment-complex bill. Any integrated-resort thesis for Thai property — the Pattaya and Bangkok riverside land plays in particular — is on hold for this parliament.不會做：賭場。阿努廷已表態，賭場合法化要「等下一任總理」，並與娛樂綜合區法案劃清界線。任何以綜合度假村為前提的泰國房地產論述——特別是芭達雅與曼谷河岸的土地題材——在本屆國會期間都要暫停。
  • Unlikely: foreign ownership reform. Raising the condominium quota to 75% or enacting a 99-year lease requires spending domestic political capital on a policy whose beneficiaries cannot vote. No draft was before parliament as of mid-2026. Meanwhile the Department of Business Development began requiring proof of source of funds and signed investment confirmation letters at company incorporation from January and April 2026 — on ownership, the direction of travel is tighter, not looser.不太可能：外資持有改革。把公寓配額提高到 75%，或制定 99 年租賃法，都需要為一項「受益者沒有投票權」的政策消耗國內政治資本。至 2026 年中，國會並無草案。同時，商業發展廳自 2026 年 1 月與 4 月起，要求公司設立時提出資金來源證明與簽署投資確認書——在產權這一面，方向是收緊而非放鬆。
  • Already done, by order rather than by law: the 3-million-baht long-stay visa. On 1 October 2025 Immigration Bureau Orders 237/2568 and 238/2568 cut the property-linked investment-visa threshold from 10 million baht to 3 million, giving a foreign buyer and their dependants a renewable twelve-month extension. This is the cleanest available demonstration of the rule below: the change that needed only two administrative orders happened in months; the change that needs a statute has not happened in years. It also means foreign access in Thailand is now asymmetric — the right to reside was liberalised while the right to own was not.已經做完，靠命令而非靠法律：300 萬泰銖長期居留簽證。2025 年 10 月 1 日，移民局第 237/2568 與 238/2568 號命令把與房產連動的投資簽證門檻從 1,000 萬泰銖降到 300 萬，讓外國買方與眷屬取得可續期的十二個月延長。這是下方那條規律最乾淨的實證：只需要兩道行政命令的改變，幾個月內完成；需要法律的改變，多年沒有發生。這也意味著泰國的外資准入如今是不對稱的——居留的權利被放寬了，持有的權利沒有。
Three tradeable political rules三條可交易的政治規律

One — price the current law, never the proposal. The 99-year lease and the 75% quota have been "close" for years. Every valuation that assumes them has been wrong so far.一 —— 用現行法律定價，永遠不要用提案定價。99 年租賃與 75% 配額「快要通過」已經好幾年了。至今每一個把它們寫進假設的估值都是錯的。

Two — annual measures get renewed; multi-year measures do not get written. Assume the 0.01% fee waiver keeps rolling and assume nothing structural arrives. Build the cliff into the model anyway, because the renewal is a decision, not a certainty.二 —— 年度措施會被延，跨年度措施不會被寫出來。假設 0.01% 規費優惠會一路續行，同時假設沒有任何結構性措施會到來。但仍要把懸崖放進模型，因為續行是一個決定，不是必然。

Three — administration changes faster than legislation, in both directions. Nominee-company crackdowns, source-of-funds rules and tax administration can be tightened by order in weeks; the 3-million-baht visa was loosened the same way, by two immigration orders rather than an act of parliament. That is the channel through which foreign property exposure in Thailand actually gets repriced — so watch the orders, not the bills.三 —— 行政比立法變得更快，而且雙向都是。人頭公司查察、資金來源規則、稅務行政，都可以在幾週內以命令收緊；300 萬泰銖簽證則是用同樣的方式放寬的——靠兩道移民命令，不是靠國會的法律。這才是泰國外資房產曝險真正被重新定價的管道——所以要盯命令，不要盯法案。

Part 4 · The Model總合模型 · 一條式子讀懂泰國房市

One equation一條式子

Δ Price ≈ ƒ( Credit eligibility × Household formation ) − Standing supply + External cash access
Interest rates enter only through the first term, and weakly. Everything that has actually moved Thai house prices in the past five years sits in one of these four buckets.利率只透過第一項進入，而且影響很弱。過去五年真正推動泰國房價的每一件事，都落在這四個桶子裡的某一個。
Credit eligibility

Rejection 40–50% overall, near 70% below ฿3m. Set by credit-bureau history, existing debt burden and documented income — and 53–55% of workers are informal. Negative and structural.整體退件率 40–50%，300 萬泰銖以下接近 70%。由聯徵紀錄、既有債務負擔與可舉證收入決定——而 53% 至 55% 的勞動力屬非正式就業。負向，且是結構性的。

Household formation

Deaths above births since 2021; population down six straight years; TFR ≈ 1.1; working-age share 64% heading to 58%. Negative and irreversible on any investable horizon.2021 年起死亡多於出生；人口連六年下降；總生育率約 1.1；工作年齡佔比 64% 走向 58%。負向，且在任何可投資期間內不可逆。

Standing supply

210,112 units on market in Bangkok, 49.5 months of stock; launches already halved to ~51,000 a year nationally. Negative now, improving slowly — the one term developers control.曼谷在售 210,112 戶、相當於 49.5 個月存量；全國推案已腰斬至年約 5.1 萬戶。目前為負，但緩慢改善——這是發展商唯一能控制的一項。

External cash access

Foreigners: 13.6% of units, 23.9% of value, capped at a 49% freehold quota, taxed at 2% transfer and 15% rental withholding, and down 17% year-on-year. The only positive term, and it is shrinking and legally capped.外國人佔戶數 13.6%、金額 23.9%，受 49% 永久產權配額限制，課 2% 過戶費與 15% 租金扣繳，且年減 17%。唯一的正項，但正在縮小，而且有法定上限。

The conclusions live in the interactions真結論藏在交互作用裡

  • Credit eligibility × standing supply. Supply discipline only helps if the buyer exists. Halving launches into a 70% rejection rate shortens the queue without shortening the wait, because the absorption denominator is falling as fast as the numerator.信用資格 × 在售存量。供給紀律只有在買方存在時才有用。把推案腰斬、卻面對 70% 的退件率，只會縮短隊伍而不縮短等待，因為吸納這個分母跌得跟分子一樣快。
  • External cash × the 49% quota. The only functioning demand channel has a legal ceiling. As foreign demand concentrates in the same prime buildings, the quota binds first exactly where the money is, which is why foreign-quota units trade at a premium and why raising the cap would be the single highest-leverage policy available. It is also the one no government will spend capital on.外部現金 × 49% 配額。唯一還在運作的需求管道有法定天花板。當外資需求集中在同一批精華大樓，配額會先在錢最多的地方卡住——這也是外配戶溢價成交的原因，以及「提高上限」為何是現有政策中槓桿最大的一項。它同時也是沒有任何政府願意為之消耗資本的那一項。
  • Household formation × the mortgage step-up. A shrinking buyer base and a three-year teaser structure interact badly. Each cohort of borrowers reaches year four into a smaller resale market than the one it underwrote against.家戶形成 × 房貸利率跳升。萎縮的買方基數與三年優惠結構的交互作用很糟。每一批借款人走到第四年時，面對的都是一個比他們當初承作時更小的二手市場。
  • Deflation × the widest carry in Asia. The one genuinely favourable interaction. A 5% gross yield against a 1.00% policy rate exists because the real economy is weak. An unlevered foreign buyer is paid to hold, and is not exposed to the mortgage step-up that ruins the levered case.通縮 × 亞洲最寬利差。唯一真正有利的交互作用。5% 的毛收益率對上 1.00% 的政策利率之所以存在，正是因為實體經濟很弱。無槓桿的外國買方被付錢持有，且不暴露於那個毀掉槓桿論述的利率跳升。

How this model dies模型的可證偽條件

Falsified to the upside if若出現以下，向上證偽

  • Mortgage rejection falls below 35% and stays there for three consecutive quarters. This would break the central claim that the constraint is eligibility rather than price.房貸退件率跌破 35% 並連續三季維持。這會推翻「約束在資格而非價格」這個核心主張。
  • A foreign-ownership bill — 75% quota or a statutory long lease — is actually tabled in parliament with government backing.外資持有法案——75% 配額或法定長期租賃——在政府支持下真的送進國會。
  • Registered births stabilise above 450,000, or net migration turns materially positive with pathways to formal employment.登記出生數穩定在 45 萬以上，或淨移入大幅轉正並具備轉入正式就業的路徑。
  • Bangkok's price index turns positive year-on-year for four consecutive quarters while transfer value grows faster than volume.曼谷價格指數連續四季年增為正，且過戶金額成長快於戶數。

Falsified to the downside if若出現以下，向下證偽

  • Special-mention loans migrate and the NPL ratio moves above 4%. The resale-cascade scenario becomes the base case and standing stock stops falling.關注類貸款劣化、不良率升破 4%。二手瀑布情境成為基準，在售存量停止下降。
  • Foreign condominium transfers fall more than the forecast 20% and the non-China cohort also contracts. The cash layer goes and prime price discovery stops.外國人公寓過戶跌幅超過預估的 20%，且非中國客群同步收縮。現金層消失，精華段的價格發現停擺。
  • Public debt breaches 70% of GDP and the ceiling is raised without a credible consolidation path, putting the baht and the fee-waiver programme both at risk.公共債務突破 GDP 的 70%，且在沒有可信整併路徑下調高上限，同時危及泰銖與規費減免計畫。
  • A second major seismic event or a renewed border conflict. Both are low-probability and both would close the high-rise end-user market for years rather than quarters.再發生一次重大地震，或邊境衝突再起。兩者機率都低，但都會讓高層住宅的自住市場關閉「數年」而非「數季」。

The Answer — Where, What, When答案篇 · 哪裡、什麼、何時

Locations: follow the operating rail, not the announced rail地點：跟著營運中的軌道，不要跟著宣布中的軌道

Land prices are rising fastest where trains are planned — Phra Khanong–Bang Na–Suan Luang–Prawet at +36.3%, the Green Line extension corridor at +17.6% — which is where developer land cost is being set, not where tenants are. For an income buyer the correct target is the opposite: mature, already-operating stations in inner and mid-Sukhumvit, Ratchada and Rama 9, where the rent line is proven and the secondary market is deep enough to exit. The 20-baht flat fare quietly improves the economics of outer stations by extending effective commuting range, and is the most underrated policy on the board — but it is a rent-support measure, not a price-support measure.地價漲最快的地方，是鐵路計畫中的地方——帕卡儂—邦那—萱鑾—巴威 +36.3%、綠線延伸走廊 +17.6%——那裡決定的是發展商的土地成本，不是租客所在。對收益型買方，正確的目標恰好相反：素坤逸內段與中段、拉差達、Rama 9 等成熟且已營運的車站，租金線經過驗證、二手市場也深到能出場。20 泰銖統一票價透過延長有效通勤半徑，悄悄改善了外圍車站的經濟性，是目前檯面上最被低估的政策——但它是租金支撐措施，不是價格支撐措施。

Products: rank by who finances them產品：依「誰出錢」排序

Rank Thai residential products not by quality but by the financing they depend on. Cash-financed luxury clears at about 93% and is exposed only to foreign flow. The ฿3–5m band is financed by documented formal-sector income and has improved since 2021. The sub-฿3m band is financed by a mortgage that is declined seven times out of ten. Landed townhouses are the largest single block of Bangkok inventory at 68,831 units, which is a reminder that the problem is not the high-rise form factor. And leasehold villas depend on a legal structure Thai courts have not reliably enforced past the first thirty years, which is a financing risk wearing a lifestyle costume.泰國住宅產品的排序，不要依品質，要依它們仰賴的融資來源。以現金支付的豪宅去化率約 93%，只暴露於外資流動。300 萬至 500 萬泰銖區間由可舉證的正式部門收入支撐，2021 年以來持續改善。300 萬泰銖以下的區間，仰賴一種每十件被退七件的房貸。連棟屋是曼谷單一最大的存量區塊、68,831 戶，提醒我們問題不出在「高層」這個形式上。而租賃制別墅仰賴的法律結構，泰國法院在第一個三十年之後並未穩定執行——那是一個穿著生活方式外衣的融資風險。

Timing: three clocks時機：三個時鐘

Fast clock — policy, months快時鐘 —— 政策，以月計

The LTV relaxation lapsed on 30 June 2026; the 0.01% fee waiver runs to 30 June 2027. Both are binary, dated and checkable. Expect a pull-forward of transfers into the first half of 2027 and an air pocket after it.LTV 鬆綁已於 2026 年 6 月 30 日到期；0.01% 規費優惠實施至 2027 年 6 月 30 日。兩者都是二元、有日期、可查核的。可預期過戶被拉前到 2027 上半年，之後出現真空。

Medium clock — credit, two to four years中時鐘 —— 信用，二到四年

The ฿1.28 trillion of special-mention loans has to resolve one way or the other. Either it cures — and lending reopens — or it migrates, and asset managers set the price for two to three years. This is the clock that actually matters.1.28 兆泰銖的關注類貸款終究要有個結果。要嘛痊癒、放款重開；要嘛劣化，由資產管理公司定價兩到三年。這才是真正重要的那個時鐘。

Slow clock — demography, a generation慢時鐘 —— 人口，一個世代

The 2040 first-time buyer has already been born and counted. Working-age share falls from 64% to 58%. No policy on any current agenda changes this number, and it sets the terminal value in every Thai property model.2040 年的首購族已經出生、已經被算過。工作年齡佔比從 64% 降到 58%。目前任何議程上的政策都改不了這個數字，而它決定了每一個泰國房地產模型的終值。

The currency clock runs across all three匯率時鐘橫跨這三者

USD/THB moved from 30.84 in January 2026 to 33.65 in July. That 9% swing is larger than anything the price index delivered in the same period. For a foreign buyer, currency is not a footnote — it is the first-order variable.美元兌泰銖從 2026 年 1 月的 30.84 走到 7 月的 33.65。這 9% 的擺盪，比同期價格指數給出的任何變化都大。對外國買方，匯率不是註腳，而是第一階變數。

Tailwinds順風

  • Policy rate at 1.00% with a 4-point gross carry, the widest in Asia for an unlevered holder.政策利率 1.00%，毛利差 4 個百分點，對無槓桿持有者是亞洲最寬。
  • Launches already halved to about 51,000 units a year nationally; Bangkok condo launches at a decade low in 2025.全國推案已腰斬至年約 5.1 萬戶；曼谷公寓 2025 年推案為十年低點。
  • BOI approvals of ฿1.876tn in 2025 and ฿1.306tn in the first half of 2026, concentrated in electronics, PCBs and data centres.BOI 核准金額 2025 年 1.876 兆泰銖、2026 上半年 1.306 兆，集中在電子、印刷電路板與資料中心。
  • Rail expansion plus the 20-baht flat fare extends effective commuting range and supports outer-station rents.軌道擴張加上 20 泰銖統一票價，延長有效通勤半徑，支撐外圍車站租金。
  • A stable, non-expropriating property-rights regime that has survived two coups and multiple judicial removals.一個穩定、不徵收的財產權體制，經歷兩次政變與多次司法罷黜仍然存續。

Risks風險

  • Mortgage rejection at 40–50%, near 70% below ฿3m, driven by credit history rather than by price.房貸退件率 40% 至 50%，300 萬泰銖以下接近 70%，成因是信用紀錄而非價格。
  • ฿1.28tn of special-mention loans against ฿535.8bn of NPLs — more than 2x — with foreclosures already up 210%.1.28 兆泰銖關注類貸款對上 5,358 億不良貸款，超過 2 倍；法拍已年增 210%。
  • Population falling for a sixth year; working-age share heading from 64% to 58% by 2040.人口連續第六年下降；工作年齡佔比至 2040 年由 64% 走向 58%。
  • Foreign transfers forecast −20% in 2026, the first fall in five years, with Chinese purchases down 38.8%.外資過戶預估 2026 年 −20%，五年來首次下滑，中國買方年減 38.8%。
  • Public debt at 66.8% against a 70% ceiling; deficit at 4.4–4.5% of GDP. No fiscal room for a real housing programme.公共債務 66.8%，上限 70%；赤字佔 GDP 4.4% 至 4.5%。沒有財政空間做真正的住宅計畫。

What to watch觀察重點

  • Mortgage rejection rate.房貸退件率。 Below 35% sustained rewrites the model; above 55% brings the distress scenario forward. 持續低於 35% 會改寫模型；高於 55% 會把壓力情境提前。
  • Special-mention loans versus NPLs.關注類貸款對不良貸款。 The ratio is above 2x. Migration of a quarter of that stock doubles the NPL ratio. 目前倍數在 2 以上。其中四分之一劣化，就會讓不良率翻倍。
  • Registered births (annual, DOPA).登記出生數（年頻，內政部地方行政廳）。 Below 400,000 confirms the terminal-value assumption in every Thai housing model is too high. 跌破 40 萬，等於確認每一個泰國住宅模型的終值假設都偏高。
  • Headline CPI versus the policy rate.CPI 對政策利率。 The real policy rate, not the nominal one, is what the housing market responds to. Cost-push inflation with 0.2% credit growth is the worst combination. 房市反應的是實質政策利率，不是名目的。成本推動型通膨配上 0.2% 的信用成長，是最糟的組合。
  • Public debt against the 70% ceiling.公共債務對上 70% 上限。 A breach forces either consolidation or a ceiling change, and the fee-waiver programme is the first casualty of the former. 一旦突破，就必須整併或修改上限；而規費減免計畫會是前者的第一個犧牲品。
  • USD/THB.美元兌泰銖。 A 9% move in seven months exceeded anything the price index produced. For a cross-border buyer this is the first-order variable, not a footnote. 七個月 9% 的變動，超過價格指數同期的任何表現。對跨境買方，這是第一階變數，不是註腳。
  • Any foreign-ownership bill actually tabled.是否真的有外資持有法案送進國會。 Not floated, not "close" — tabled with government backing. Until then, price the current law. 不是被提及，也不是「快了」——是在政府支持下送進國會。在那之前，用現行法律定價。

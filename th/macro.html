Bottom line總結 Thailand is the first economy in Southeast Asia to hit the demographic wall before it finished getting rich. Deaths have exceeded births every year since 2021, the population has fallen for six consecutive years, and gross national income per head sits at US$7,690 against a high-income threshold of US$14,375. Vietnam and the Philippines were promoted into Thailand's own income tier in July 2026. Being overtaken from below is not a ranking problem; it is the labour-cost arbitrage that built Thai manufacturing now running in reverse.

The macro numbers look benign and are not. Policy rates are at 1.00%, the lowest in over two decades, yet credit growth is 0.2% and half of mortgage applications are declined. Consumer prices fell year-on-year for twelve straight months through March 2026, which means the "loose" nominal rate was a positive real rate for most of a year. Household debt at 86.7% of GDP is coming down, but through refusal to lend rather than through repayment. Public debt at 66.8% is inside a 70% legal ceiling that leaves roughly three points of room. Every conventional lever is either pulled or jammed.

For property, the operative conclusion is that Thailand's housing market cannot be read off the interest rate. It is set by three things the central bank does not control: who is eligible for credit, how many households are being formed, and who is allowed to buy. Those three variables are analysed against the market data in the companion Bangkok Property Market Report. 泰國為東南亞首個在達到高所得水準前即遭遇人口結構反轉的經濟體。自 2021 年起死亡數逐年高於出生數，人口已連續六年下降，人均國民所得停留於 7,690 美元，高收入門檻則為 14,375 美元。2026 年 7 月，越南與菲律賓調升至泰國所處之收入層級。此一追趕的實質意義不在排名，而在當年支撐泰國製造業的勞動成本套利已轉為不利因素。

總體數據表面溫和，實質並非如此。政策利率 1.00%，為二十餘年低點，惟信用成長僅 0.2%，房貸申請約半數遭退件。消費者物價至 2026 年 3 月連續 12 個月年減，意即名目利率雖處於寬鬆水準，該期間內實質利率為正。家庭債務佔 GDP 86.7% 且呈下降，惟成因為貸放緊縮而非債務償還。公共債務 66.8%，法定上限 70%，餘裕僅約三個百分點。各項常規政策工具已接近用盡或受限。

就房地產而言，可操作的結論為：泰國住宅市場無法以利率解讀。其決定變數有三，且均不在央行的政策範圍內——授信取得資格、家戶形成數量、以及購買主體的法律准入。三項變數與市場數據的對照，詳見《曼谷住宅市場報告》。

Part 1 · The Cycle週期篇 · 我們在哪裡

Monetary cycle × house prices貨幣週期 × 房價

A full rate cycle, no housing response一輪完整的利率循環，住宅市場無反應
  • Over a longer window: the policy rate sat at its record low of 0.50% through 2020–22, rose to a cycle peak of 2.50% in 2023, then fell to 1.00% between October 2024 and February 2026, held unanimously since (7–0 in June 2026). The all-time high since inflation targeting began in 2000 was 5.00% in December 2006.以更長的區間觀察：政策利率於 2020 至 2022 年間停留在 0.50% 的歷史低點，2023 年升至本輪高點 2.50%，隨後於 2024 年 10 月至 2026 年 2 月間降至 1.00%，其後以一致決維持，2026 年 6 月為 7 比 0。自 2000 年採行通膨目標制以來，歷史高點為 2006 年 12 月的 5.00%。
  • Across that window the national residential price index moved from roughly flat to +1.26% year-on-year (0.63% in 4Q25), while the Bangkok index turned negative at −0.18%. The policy rate has travelled a full cycle — 0.50% to 2.50% and back to 1.00% — with no corresponding response in capital-city prices.在此期間，全國住宅價格指數自大致持平升至年增 1.26%（2025 年第四季 0.63%），曼谷指數則轉為負值（−0.18%）。政策利率完整走過 0.50% → 2.50% → 1.00% 的一輪循環，首都房價並未出現可衡量的對應反應。
  • The transmission failure is not mysterious. Thai mortgages are written at a discounted fixed rate for three years and then revert to MRR, which averaged 7.38% across domestic commercial banks in June 2026. Policy cuts move the reversion rate slowly and the teaser rate barely at all, so the payment a borrower is underwritten against hardly changes.傳導失效的機制明確。泰國房貸採前三年優惠固定利率、其後回歸 MRR 的結構，而 2026 年 6 月本地商銀 MRR 平均為 7.38%。降息對重設後利率的影響緩慢，對優惠利率則幾無作用，故授信審查所依據的月付金額變動有限。

Deflation makes cheap money expensive通縮環境下的實質利率為正

Thai consumer prices fell year-on-year for twelve consecutive months to March 2026: −0.66% in January, −0.88% in February, −0.08% in March. With the policy rate at 1.00% to 1.25% through that stretch, the real policy rate was positive by 1.1 to 2.1 points — genuinely restrictive — at precisely the moment the headlines described monetary policy as maximally accommodative. That is the cleanest single explanation for why five rate cuts did not restart the housing market.泰國消費者物價至 2026 年 3 月連續 12 個月年減：1 月 −0.66%、2 月 −0.88%、3 月 −0.08%。該期間政策利率介於 1.00% 至 1.25%，實質政策利率因而為正 1.1 至 2.1 個百分點，實質上屬緊縮立場，而同期市場論述普遍將貨幣政策描述為高度寬鬆。我們認為此為多次降息未能重啟住宅市場最直接的解釋。

And now the inflation is the wrong kind當前通膨的性質不利

The Bank of Thailand's June 2026 forecast puts headline inflation at 2.8% for 2026, falling to 1.4% in 2027 — driven by energy and production-cost pass-through, not by demand. Cost-push inflation with 0.2% credit growth is the worst combination available: it erodes the real value of rent and wages without giving the central bank any reason to think demand has recovered, and it makes further cuts harder to justify.泰國央行 2026 年 6 月預測全年通膨 2.8%、2027 年降至 1.4%，推力來自能源與生產成本傳導而非需求。在信用成長僅 0.2% 的環境下出現成本推動型通膨，屬最不利的組合：其侵蝕租金與工資的實質價值，卻未提供需求復甦的訊號，並使進一步降息的正當性下降。

Real economy × house prices實體經濟 × 房價

Real GDP growth · 2015–2027F · NESDC / BOT / consensus forecasts實質 GDP 成長 · 2015–2027 預估 · NESDC／泰國央行／機構預測

A decade below the regional line連續十年低於區域平均
  • Thailand grew 2.4% in 2025. The Bank of Thailand forecasts 2.3% for 2026 and 1.8% for 2027; the World Bank has 1.6% for 2026, the OECD 1.5%, and the Ministry of Finance 2.5%. The spread of forecasts is wide, and every one of them is below the ASEAN average of 4.2% to 4.5%.泰國 2025 年成長 2.4%。泰國央行預估 2026 年 2.3%、2027 年 1.8%；世界銀行 2026 年 1.6%，OECD 1.5%，財政部 2.5%。各家預測分歧顯著，惟均低於東協 4.2% 至 4.5% 的平均水準。
  • In the fourth quarter of 2025 Vietnam grew 8.46%, Malaysia 5.7%, Indonesia 5.39% and Thailand 2.5%. This is not a cyclical gap. Thailand has grown at roughly half the regional pace for a decade, and the compounding shows up directly in the income line that determines who can service a mortgage.2025 年第四季，越南成長 8.46%、馬來西亞 5.7%、印尼 5.39%，泰國 2.5%。此非循環性落差。泰國以區域約半數的速度成長已達十年，其複利效果直接反映於決定房貸負擔能力的所得曲線。
  • Property is a levered claim on nominal income growth. At 2% real growth and roughly 1% inflation, Thai nominal income compounds at about 3% a year, against a mortgage that reverts to 7.38%. The arithmetic of leverage does not work at these inputs, which is why the Thai market has quietly become a cash market.房地產本質上為對名目所得成長的槓桿求償權。在 2% 實質成長與約 1% 通膨的組合下，泰國名目所得年複合約 3%，而房貸利率將重設至 7.38%。在此一參數組合下，槓桿的報酬算式不成立，此即泰國市場轉為以現金交易為主的成因。

Part 2 · The Structural Economy經濟結構篇 · 為什麼這影響房價

Malaysia deindustrialised too soon. Thailand industrialised into the wrong product, and then it got old.馬來西亞的問題為過早去工業化；泰國的問題為工業化押注於錯誤產品，並在轉型完成前進入高齡化。

Stuck below the high-income line — and being overtaken停滯於高收入門檻之下，並遭後進者追趕

Where Thailand sits泰國的相對位置
  • Thailand reached middle-income status in the early 1990s, among the first in populous Southeast Asia. Thirty years later GNI per capita is US$7,690 against a high-income cut-off of US$14,375. Real GDP per capita grew 2.2% in 2025; at that rate the gap takes roughly 29 years to close.泰國於 1990 年代初期進入中等收入，為東南亞人口大國中最早者。三十年後人均 GNI 為 7,690 美元，高收入門檻為 14,375 美元。2025 年實質人均 GDP 成長 2.2%，依此速度缺口需約 29 年方能弭平。
  • In July 2026 the World Bank moved Vietnam and the Philippines into the upper-middle-income tier Thailand has occupied for years — Vietnam at US$4,970 and the Philippines at US$4,850, both clearing the US$4,636 entry line. Thailand's comparative advantage was cheap, disciplined factory labour; that advantage now belongs to countries with younger populations and lower wages.2026 年 7 月，世界銀行將越南與菲律賓調升至泰國長期所處之中高收入層級：越南 4,970 美元、菲律賓 4,850 美元，均跨過 4,636 美元的入門線。泰國過往的比較優勢為低成本且具紀律的工廠勞動力，該優勢現已轉移至人口結構更年輕、工資更低的國家。
  • The property translation is direct. A housing market's long-run price line is set by income, and Thailand's income line has been flattening for a decade while its construction cost line has not. That is the whole reason the average newly launched Bangkok unit now costs 19 years of an average Bangkok household's income.對房地產的意涵直接。住宅市場的長期價格水準由所得決定，而泰國的所得曲線已平坦十年，營建成本曲線則未同步。此即曼谷新推案均價現已相當於當地平均家庭 19 年所得的成因。

The root: it aged before it got rich結構成因：所得成長完成前即進入高齡化

The crossover already happened出生死亡交叉已發生
  • Thailand recorded 462,240 births against 571,646 deaths in 2024 — the first time births fell below 500,000 since 1949 — and 416,574 against 559,684 in 2025. The population fell to 65.8 million at end-2025, down 142,199 in a year and declining for a sixth straight year.泰國 2024 年出生 462,240 人、死亡 571,646 人，出生數為 1949 年以來首度低於 50 萬；2025 年為 416,574 人對 559,684 人。人口於 2025 年底降至 6,580 萬，年減 142,199 人，為連續第六年下降。
  • The total fertility rate is around 1.1, among the lowest anywhere. The under-15 population fell from 11.7 million in 2015 to 9.6 million; the 60-plus population rose from 8.4 million to 12.6 million. The working-age share fell from 68% to 64% and is projected at 58% by 2040.總生育率約 1.1，屬全球最低之列。15 歲以下人口自 2015 年的 1,170 萬降至 960 萬，60 歲以上自 840 萬升至 1,260 萬。工作年齡人口佔比自 68% 降至 64%，預估 2040 年降至 58%。
  • Thailand crosses the 28% elderly threshold around 2033, with some projections at 2036. Japan crossed the equivalent line at roughly four times Thailand's current income per head; Korea at roughly five times. Thailand is the first major economy to reach a super-aged structure at under US$8,000 per capita.泰國約於 2033 年跨越 28% 的高齡門檻，部分推估為 2036 年。日本跨越同一界線時，人均所得約為泰國現值的四倍，南韓約五倍。泰國為首個在人均所得不足 8,000 美元即達超高齡結構的主要經濟體。

Why this is the property variable, not a social one此為房地產變數，而非僅屬社會議題

Net new housing demand comes from household formation. Everything else — investment purchase, upgrading, foreign buying — reallocates existing stock without adding a household. Thailand's household-formation engine went into reverse in 2021 and the country has kept building. Malaysia's demographic engine, by contrast, is the last in Asia to cool: population growth near 1.4% with a median age in the low thirties. The two markets carry similar overhangs, and only one of them has a growing base of future buyers underneath it.淨新增住宅需求的來源為家戶形成。其餘管道（投資置產、換屋、外資購買）均僅為既有存量的再配置，不增加家戶數。泰國的家戶形成自 2021 年起轉為負值，供給端則持續興建。相對而言，馬來西亞的人口紅利為亞洲最晚結束者：人口成長約 1.4%，中位年齡在三十歲出頭。兩個市場的餘屋規模相近，惟僅其中之一具備持續擴張的未來買方基數。

The auto shock — the second pillar cracking汽車業衝擊 —— 第二支柱弱化

Vehicle production, units · 2017–2025 · Thailand Automotive Institute / FTI; 2023 chained from the reported year-on-year change, 2025 estimated汽車產量（輛）· 2017–2025 · 泰國汽車協會／FTI；2023 由公布年減率回推，2025 為估計值

Production產量

Thailand built its industrial identity as the "Detroit of Asia", assembling internal-combustion pickups and passenger cars for Japanese manufacturers. Output peaked at 2,459,504 units in 2013, recovered to 2.17 million in 2018 without retesting that high, ran 1.88 million in 2022, then fell to 1,468,997 in 2024, down 19.9%, and kept falling through 2025; April 2026 was a five-year low. Production is down roughly 40% over twelve years, and the decline began well before the pandemic. The mechanism has two parts and both are structural. Domestically, tighter auto lending against 86.7%-of-GDP household debt removed the buyer, and repossessions rose. Externally, Chinese electric-vehicle makers built Thai capacity faster than the Japanese incumbents converted their existing plants, so the transition is happening at the expense of the incumbent supply chain rather than through it.泰國的工業定位建立於「亞洲底特律」，主要業務為代日系車廠組裝內燃機皮卡與轎車。產量於 2013 年見頂 2,459,504 輛，2018 年回升至 216.8 萬輛後未再挑戰高點，2022 年 188.4 萬輛，2024 年降至 1,468,997 輛、年減 19.9%，2025 年續降，2026 年 4 月創五年新低。十二年間產量減少約四成，且此一衰退早於疫情。其成因有二，且均屬結構性。內需方面，在家庭債務佔 GDP 86.7% 的背景下，車貸緊縮排除了買方，收回件數同步上升。外部方面，中國電動車廠在泰國建置產能的速度高於日系既有廠房的轉型速度，使此一轉型以既有供應鏈的萎縮為代價，而非透過其升級完成。

For property this matters in two specific places. The Eastern Economic Corridor — Chonburi, Rayong, Chachoengsao, and by extension the Pattaya residential market — is where auto employment concentrates. And the auto supply chain is the largest single source of the formal-sector, documented wages that Thai banks will actually lend against. A contraction there does not just cut GDP; it shrinks the mortgage-eligible population.對房地產的影響集中於兩處。其一為東部經濟走廊（春武里、羅勇、北柳，並延伸至芭達雅住宅市場），該區為汽車業就業的集中地。其二為汽車供應鏈本身，其為泰國金融機構願意承作之正式部門、可舉證薪資的最大單一來源。該產業的收縮不僅減損 GDP，更直接縮減具房貸申請資格的人口規模。

Arrivals旅客人次
  • Arrivals peaked at 39.92 million in 2019, fell to 6.70 million and 0.43 million through the pandemic, and recovered to 35.55 million in 2024 — still short of 2019. In 2025 they fell 7.23% to 32.97 million, the first annual decline outside the pandemic years. The gap is Chinese: 10.99 million in 2019, 6.73 million in 2024, 4.47 million in 2025, still under half the pre-pandemic level.旅客人次於 2019 年見頂 3,992 萬，2020 至 2021 年因疫情降至 670 萬與 43 萬，2024 年回升至 3,555 萬，仍低於 2019 年高點。2025 年年減 7.23% 至 3,297 萬，為疫情年份以外首次年度下滑。中國旅客為主要缺口：2019 年 1,099 萬，2024 年 673 萬，2025 年 447 萬，仍不及疫情前的一半。

It is also the demand base under Phuket, Pattaya and every short-let yield model in the country. A structurally smaller Chinese cohort re-rates the resort markets permanently, not cyclically, and the replacement cohorts — Indian, Russian, Middle Eastern — spend differently and stay differently.觀光為泰國將境外需求轉換為國內薪資、且不經由製造部門的主要管道，同時構成普吉、芭達雅及全國各短租收益模型的需求基礎。中國客群的結構性縮減將使度假市場的評價永久性下修，而非循環性波動；替補客群（印度、俄羅斯、中東）的消費結構與停留型態均不相同。

Growth that doesn't reach a bank statement無法轉化為可舉證所得的成長

Informality and sector mix非正式就業與部門結構
  • Roughly 53% to 55% of Thai workers are informal — about 21 million people. Agriculture accounts for around 30% of employment and about 54% of all informal workers, some 11.4 million.泰國約有 53% 至 55% 的勞動人口屬非正式就業，約 2,100 萬人。農業約佔總就業的 30%，並佔全體非正式工作者約 54%、約 1,140 萬人。
  • The minimum wage runs 337 to 400 baht a day by province. On the BIS series, which runs thirty-four years, the ratio climbed from 30.7% in 1991 to 64.6% at the 1997 crisis, fell to 47.2% by 2001, rose again from the mid-2000s to peak at 96.6% in the first quarter of 2021, and stood at 87.5% at end-2025 — 86.7% on the Bank of Thailand's domestic measure, or 16.44 trillion baht. Malaysia sits at 84.8% and is generally treated as ASEAN's leverage outlier; Thailand is higher, on half the growth rate, with a shrinking population. Two features make the Thai stock worse than the headline. First, an unusually large share is non-productive — auto, credit card and personal lending rather than mortgage or business debt. Second, the deleveraging is being achieved through refusal to lend: credit growth was 0.2% in the first quarter of 2026 with SME and consumer lending still contracting outright. The contrast with 1997 is instructive — that episode cut 17.4 points in four years (64.6% to 47.2%) alongside a currency collapse and a recession; this one has managed 9.1 points in five (96.6% to 87.5%) with neither. Over a longer window the baht sat near 25.6 under its pre-1997 peg, broke to 44.3 in the crisis year, and has traded between 30 and 44 in the twenty-odd years since without regaining its pre-crisis level. More recently it strengthened nearly 8% over the year to February 2026, touching 30.84 on 25 January, its firmest since March 2021, before easing back to 33.65 by 30 July 2026. The appreciation was not a vote of confidence in Thai growth. It reflected a large current-account surplus produced by weak imports, gold flows, and the Federal Reserve's easing path — a currency rising because the domestic economy is too soft to buy anything. A strong baht then does three damaging things at once: it prices Thai exports out, it makes Thailand expensive for the tourists it is trying to win back, and it imports deflation. For a foreign property buyer it means the entry price in dollar terms moves ten per cent on the currency before the asset moves at all, and that swing has recently been larger than anything the housing market itself delivered.拉長看，泰銖在 1997 年之前固定於 25.6 附近，危機當年貶至 44.3，其後二十餘年在 30 至 44 之間往復，並未回到危機前水準。近期方面，至 2026 年 2 月的一年間升值近 8%，於 1 月 25 日觸及 30.84，為 2021 年 3 月以來最強，其後回落至 2026 年 7 月 30 日的 33.65。此波升值並非反映對泰國成長前景的信心，而是進口疲弱、黃金流與聯準會寬鬆路徑共同形成的龐大經常帳順差所致，即貨幣因內需疲弱而走強。強勢泰銖同時產生三項不利影響：削弱出口價格競爭力、提高泰國對目標客源的相對旅遊成本、並輸入通縮壓力。對外國房產買方而言，以美元計價的進場成本在資產價格變動之前，即已因匯率變動約一成，且近期該匯率波動幅度大於住宅市場本身的價格變動。

Part 3 · The Politics — Who Sets the Rules政治篇 · 規則的制定者

In Malaysia the political question is who gets the growth. In Thailand it is who is permitted to govern at all.馬來西亞的政治問題在於成長的分配對象；泰國的政治問題在於執政資格本身。

The veto architecture否決架構

The 2017 constitution2017 年憲法

Drafted after the 2014 coup and approved in a referendum held under military rule, it was designed to constrain elected majorities rather than express them. It is the document under which every subsequent dispute has been adjudicated.於 2014 年政變後起草，並在軍政府統治下經公投通過，其設計目的為約束民選多數而非反映之。此後各項政治爭議均依該文件裁決。

The Senate參議院

Senators appointed under the junta held the power to vote on prime ministerial candidates, and confirmed the judges now sitting on the Constitutional Court. The successor selection process still excludes the general public.軍政府時期任命之參議員握有總理人選投票權，並完成現任憲法法院法官之同意程序。後續遴選機制仍排除一般公眾參與。

The Constitutional Court憲法法院

Over twenty years the courts have removed three prime ministers and dissolved several opposition parties, including the election-winning Move Forward Party in August 2024. Dissolution is an available remedy for offences as broad as proposing a constitutional amendment.二十年間，法院解職三位總理並解散多個反對黨，包括 2024 年 8 月贏得選舉的前進黨。政黨解散為可資運用之法律手段，其構成要件寬泛至涵蓋提出修憲主張。

The Election Commission and NACC選委會與國家反貪委員會

Independent agencies with the power to refer, disqualify and prosecute. In practice they are the first stage of a pipeline that ends at the Constitutional Court, and they operate outside electoral accountability.具移送、褫奪資格與起訴權之獨立機關。實務上為終點在憲法法院之程序的第一階段，且不受選舉問責。

Why this matters to a property investor對房地產投資人的意涵

The pattern is not instability in the sense of violence or expropriation. Thai property rights have held through every one of these episodes. The cost is policy horizon. A government that expects to be removed does not legislate a ten-year housing programme; it renews an annual fee waiver. That is exactly the policy mix Thailand has: the 0.01% transfer fee has been extended repeatedly one year at a time, while the 99-year lease and the 75% foreign quota — both of which would move genuine capital — have sat undrafted for years.此處的風險並非暴力或徵收意義上的不穩定。泰國的財產權於上述每一事件中均維持完整。實際代價在於政策視野。預期任期不確定的政府不會制定十年期住宅法案，而會續行一年期的規費減免。此即泰國現行的政策組合：0.01% 過戶費逐年延長，而真正能牽動資本的 99 年租賃與 75% 外資配額，多年來並無草案。

  • Will do: investment liberalisation. It also means foreign access in Thailand is now asymmetric — the right to reside was liberalised while the right to own was not.已完成，且係經由命令而非立法：300 萬泰銖長期居留簽證。2025 年 10 月 1 日，移民局第 237/2568 與 238/2568 號命令將房產連動之投資簽證門檻自 1,000 萬泰銖調降至 300 萬泰銖，外國買方與眷屬得取得可續期之十二個月延長。此為下述規律最明確的實證：僅需兩道行政命令的改革於數月內完成，須經立法的改革則多年未有進展。其意涵為泰國的外資准入現呈不對稱狀態——居留權已放寬，所有權則未變動。
Three tradeable political rules三條可交易的政治規律

One — price the current law, never the proposal. The 99-year lease and the 75% quota have been "close" for years. Every valuation that assumes them has been wrong so far.其一 —— 以現行法律定價，不以提案定價。99 年租賃與 75% 配額已被描述為「接近通過」多年。迄今將其納入假設的估值均已被證明為錯誤。

Two — annual measures get renewed; multi-year measures do not get written. Assume the 0.01% fee waiver keeps rolling and assume nothing structural arrives. Build the cliff into the model anyway, because the renewal is a decision, not a certainty.其二 —— 年度性措施會續行，跨年度措施不會出現。應假設 0.01% 規費優惠持續延長，同時假設不會出現任何結構性措施。惟模型仍須納入到期風險，因續行屬政策決定而非必然結果。

Three — administration changes faster than legislation, in both directions. Nominee-company crackdowns, source-of-funds rules and tax administration can be tightened by order in weeks; the 3-million-baht visa was loosened the same way, by two immigration orders rather than an act of parliament. That is the channel through which foreign property exposure in Thailand actually gets repriced — so watch the orders, not the bills.其三 —— 行政程序的變動速度高於立法，且為雙向。人頭公司查核、資金來源規則與稅務行政均可於數週內經命令收緊；300 萬泰銖簽證則以相同方式放寬，依據為兩道移民命令而非國會立法。此為泰國外資房產曝險實際被重新定價的管道，故追蹤標的應為行政命令而非法案。

Part 4 · The Model總合模型 · 單一方程式架構

One equation方程式

Δ Price ≈ ƒ( Credit eligibility × Household formation ) − Standing supply + External cash access
Interest rates enter only through the first term, and weakly. Everything that has actually moved Thai house prices in the past five years sits in one of these four buckets.利率僅透過第一項進入模型，且影響有限。過去五年實際驅動泰國房價的各項因素，均可歸入上述四個變數之一。
Credit eligibility

Rejection 40–50% overall, near 70% below ฿3m. Set by credit-bureau history, existing debt burden and documented income — and 53–55% of workers are informal. Negative and structural.整體退件率 40–50%，300 萬泰銖以下接近 70%。決定因素為聯徵紀錄、既有債務負擔與可舉證收入，而 53% 至 55% 的勞動力屬非正式就業。負向，且屬結構性。

Household formation

Deaths above births since 2021; population down six straight years; TFR ≈ 1.1; working-age share 64% heading to 58%. Negative and irreversible on any investable horizon.2021 年起死亡數高於出生數；人口連續六年下降；總生育率約 1.1；工作年齡佔比自 64% 降向 58%。負向，且於任何可投資期間內不可逆。

Standing supply

210,112 units on market in Bangkok, 49.5 months of stock; launches already halved to ~51,000 a year nationally. Negative now, improving slowly — the one term developers control.曼谷在售 210,112 戶，相當於 49.5 個月存量；全國推案已腰斬至年約 5.1 萬戶。現為負向惟緩慢改善，且為發展商唯一可控之變數。

External cash access

Foreigners: 13.6% of units, 23.9% of value, capped at a 49% freehold quota, taxed at 2% transfer and 15% rental withholding, and down 17% year-on-year. The only positive term, and it is shrinking and legally capped.外國人佔戶數 13.6%、金額 23.9%，受 49% 永久產權配額限制，適用 2% 過戶費與 15% 租金扣繳，且年減 17%。唯一正向變數，惟規模收縮且受法定上限約束。

The conclusions live in the interactions關鍵結論位於交互作用

  • Credit eligibility × standing supply. Supply discipline only helps if the buyer exists. Halving launches into a 70% rejection rate shortens the queue without shortening the wait, because the absorption denominator is falling as fast as the numerator.授信資格 × 在售存量。供給紀律須以買方存在為前提。推案腰斬而退件率仍達 70%，僅縮減存量而未縮短去化期間，因吸納量（分母）與存量（分子）同步下降。
  • External cash × the 49% quota. The only functioning demand channel has a legal ceiling. As foreign demand concentrates in the same prime buildings, the quota binds first exactly where the money is, which is why foreign-quota units trade at a premium and why raising the cap would be the single highest-leverage policy available. It is also the one no government will spend capital on.外部現金 × 49% 配額。唯一仍運作的需求管道設有法定上限。當外資需求集中於同一批精華案場，配額將先於資金最集中處觸頂，此即外配戶溢價成交的成因，亦說明調高上限為現行政策中槓桿效果最大者。惟該項同時為各屆政府均不願消耗政治資本推動者。
  • Household formation × the mortgage step-up. A shrinking buyer base and a three-year teaser structure interact badly. Each cohort of borrowers reaches year four into a smaller resale market than the one it underwrote against.家戶形成 × 房貸利率重設。買方基數萎縮與三年優惠結構的交互作用具負面效果：各批借款人於第四年面對的二手市場規模，均小於其承作當時。
  • Deflation × the widest carry in Asia. The one genuinely favourable interaction. A 5% gross yield against a 1.00% policy rate exists because the real economy is weak. An unlevered foreign buyer is paid to hold, and is not exposed to the mortgage step-up that ruins the levered case.通縮 × 亞洲最寬利差。唯一具正面效果的交互作用。5% 毛收益率相對 1.00% 政策利率的組合之所以成立，正因實體經濟疲弱。無槓桿之外國買方取得持有收益，且不承擔使槓桿論述失效的利率重設風險。

How this model dies模型的可證偽條件

Falsified to the upside if若出現以下，向上證偽

  • Mortgage rejection falls below 35% and stays there for three consecutive quarters. This would break the central claim that the constraint is eligibility rather than price.房貸退件率跌破 35% 並連續三季維持。此將推翻「約束在授信資格而非價格」的核心主張。
  • A foreign-ownership bill — 75% quota or a statutory long lease — is actually tabled in parliament with government backing.外資持有法案（75% 配額或法定長期租賃）於政府支持下正式送交國會。
  • Registered births stabilise above 450,000, or net migration turns materially positive with pathways to formal employment.登記出生數穩定於 45 萬以上，或淨移入顯著轉正並具備進入正式就業的路徑。
  • Bangkok's price index turns positive year-on-year for four consecutive quarters while transfer value grows faster than volume.曼谷價格指數連續四季年增為正，且過戶金額成長率高於件數成長率。

Falsified to the downside if若出現以下，向下證偽

  • Special-mention loans migrate and the NPL ratio moves above 4%. The resale-cascade scenario becomes the base case and standing stock stops falling.關注類貸款劣化，不良率升破 4%。二手處分情境成為基準情境，在售存量停止下降。
  • Foreign condominium transfers fall more than the forecast 20% and the non-China cohort also contracts. The cash layer goes and prime price discovery stops.外國人公寓過戶減幅超過預估之 20%，且非中國客群同步收縮，現金結算層消失，精華區段的價格發現失效。
  • Public debt breaches 70% of GDP and the ceiling is raised without a credible consolidation path, putting the baht and the fee-waiver programme both at risk.公共債務突破 GDP 的 70%，且於缺乏可信財政整併路徑下調高上限，同時危及泰銖與規費減免計畫。
  • A second major seismic event or a renewed border conflict. Both are low-probability and both would close the high-rise end-user market for years rather than quarters.再度發生重大地震，或邊境衝突再起。兩者機率均低，惟均將使高層住宅之自住市場關閉數年，而非數季。

The Answer — Where, What, When結論篇 · 區位、產品、時點

Locations: follow the operating rail, not the announced rail區位：以營運中軌道為準，非以規劃中軌道為準

Land prices are rising fastest where trains are planned — Phra Khanong–Bang Na–Suan Luang–Prawet at +36.3%, the Green Line extension corridor at +17.6% — which is where developer land cost is being set, not where tenants are. For an income buyer the correct target is the opposite: mature, already-operating stations in inner and mid-Sukhumvit, Ratchada and Rama 9, where the rent line is proven and the secondary market is deep enough to exit. The 20-baht flat fare quietly improves the economics of outer stations by extending effective commuting range, and is the most underrated policy on the board — but it is a rent-support measure, not a price-support measure.地價漲幅最高者為軌道規劃中之區位：帕卡儂—邦那—萱鑾—巴威 +36.3%、綠線延伸走廊 +17.6%。該處決定的是發展商的土地成本，而非租客的實際分布。就收益型買方而言，目標應為相反方向：素坤逸內段與中段、拉差達、Rama 9 等成熟且已營運之車站，其租金水準已經驗證，二手市場深度亦足以支撐出場。20 泰銖統一票價透過擴大有效通勤半徑改善外圍車站的經濟條件，為現行政策中最被低估者；惟其性質為租金支撐措施，非價格支撐措施。

Products: rank by who finances them產品：依融資來源排序

Rank Thai residential products not by quality but by the financing they depend on. Cash-financed luxury clears at about 93% and is exposed only to foreign flow. The ฿3–5m band is financed by documented formal-sector income and has improved since 2021. The sub-฿3m band is financed by a mortgage that is declined seven times out of ten. Landed townhouses are the largest single block of Bangkok inventory at 68,831 units, which is a reminder that the problem is not the high-rise form factor. And leasehold villas depend on a legal structure Thai courts have not reliably enforced past the first thirty years, which is a financing risk wearing a lifestyle costume.泰國住宅產品的排序基準應為其依賴的融資來源，而非產品品質。以現金支付的豪宅去化率約 93%，其風險僅來自外資流動。300 萬至 500 萬泰銖區間由可舉證之正式部門收入支撐，自 2021 年以來持續改善。300 萬泰銖以下區間所依賴的房貸，退件率達七成。連棟屋為曼谷單一最大存量區塊、68,831 戶，顯示問題不在高層此一產品型態。至於租賃制別墅，其法律結構於首期三十年之後未獲泰國法院穩定支持，構成以生活方式產品形式呈現的融資風險。

Timing: three clocks時機：三個時鐘

Fast clock — policy, months快時鐘 —— 政策，以月計

The LTV relaxation lapsed on 30 June 2026; the 0.01% fee waiver runs to 30 June 2027. Both are binary, dated and checkable. Expect a pull-forward of transfers into the first half of 2027 and an air pocket after it.LTV 鬆綁已於 2026 年 6 月 30 日屆期；0.01% 規費優惠實施至 2027 年 6 月 30 日。兩者均為二元、具明確日期且可查核之政策。可預期需求提前實現於 2027 上半年，其後成交量走弱。

Medium clock — credit, two to four years中時鐘 —— 信用，二到四年

The ฿1.28 trillion of special-mention loans has to resolve one way or the other. Either it cures — and lending reopens — or it migrates, and asset managers set the price for two to three years. This is the clock that actually matters.1.28 兆泰銖的關注類貸款終將出現結果：或回復正常、貸放重啟，或劣化後由資產管理公司主導定價二至三年。此為真正具決定性的時間變數。

Slow clock — demography, a generation慢時鐘 —— 人口，一個世代

The 2040 first-time buyer has already been born and counted. Working-age share falls from 64% to 58%. No policy on any current agenda changes this number, and it sets the terminal value in every Thai property model.2040 年的首購客群已出生且已完成統計。工作年齡佔比自 64% 降至 58%。現行議程上的任何政策均無法改變此一數值，而該數值決定所有泰國房地產模型的終值假設。

The currency clock runs across all three匯率變數橫跨上述三者

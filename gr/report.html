Bottom line總結 The Greek migration ministry's own golden-visa page still publishes a €250,000 threshold and still cites Law 4251/2014 as the governing statute, in both language versions, as of 1 August 2026. Both statements were superseded on 5 April 2024 by article 64 of Law 5100/2024, which set tiers of €400,000 and €800,000. Enterprise Greece removed its golden-visa page entirely rather than update it. The administering ministry's front door contradicts the statute it administers, and that is a useful warning about how much of the published material on this market is out of date.

Athens is the completed version of the cycle that Bangkok and Dubai are still inside. The Bank of Greece apartment price index for Athens peaked at 101.4 in the second quarter of 2008, fell to 56.1 in the first quarter of 2017, a drawdown of 44.7% across thirty-five quarters, and reads 116.4 in the first quarter of 2026. That is 107.5% above the trough and 14.8% above the 2008 peak in nominal terms. On the BIS real index the same market fell 48.0% peak to trough and is still 18.9% below its 2007 peak. The nominal recovery completed in the first quarter of 2024; the real recovery has not completed after eighteen and a half years. Every number on this page has to be read against that gap.

Two dated restrictions on this market have now been tested, and both produced front-running rather than compliance. The golden-visa threshold rose on 5 April 2024 with a transitional deposit deadline of 31 August 2024: permit filings in August 2024 were 550, below July's 715, while Bank of Greece non-resident real-estate inflows reached €783.7m and €824.5m in the third and fourth quarters of 2024, the two highest quarters on record, before collapsing to €356.8m in the first quarter of 2025. The money moved before the deadline and the paperwork followed months later. The short-term-let registration freeze in central Athens took effect on 1 January 2025 and central-Athens listings rose about 11% in its first year. The macro conditions behind all of this are set out in the companion Greece Macro × Property analysis. 截至 2026 年 8 月 1 日，希臘移民部自身的黃金簽證頁面仍公布 25 萬歐元門檻，並仍引用第 4251/2014 號法律為適用法源，兩種語言版本皆然。兩項陳述均已於 2024 年 4 月 5 日經第 5100/2024 號法律第 64 條取代，該條設定 40 萬歐元與 80 萬歐元兩級門檻。希臘企業署則直接移除其黃金簽證頁面，而非更新。主管機關的對外頁面與其所執行的法律相矛盾，此點足以說明本市場公開材料的過時程度。

Article 100(7A) prohibits short-term letting and subletting of property acquired for an investor permit. The sanction is verbatim revocation of the residence permit plus a standalone administrative fine of €50,000. The circular defines a short lease by reference to article 111 of Law 4446/2016, namely under sixty days with no services beyond accommodation and bed linen, so long leases are expressly permitted and are not caught. What it requires is full ownership and possession plus full payment before filing.第 100 條第 7A 項禁止為取得投資人許可而購置之物件進行短期出租與轉租。制裁依條文為撤銷居留許可，並另處 5 萬歐元行政罰鍰。通函依第 4446/2016 號法律第 111 條定義短期租賃，即六十日以下且未提供住宿與床單以外之服務，故長期租賃為明文允許且不受拘束。該條完全未就抵押、貸款、負擔或預售物件作出規定。其所要求者為完全所有權與占有，以及提出申請前全額付清價金。

The second correction concerns financing. A mortgage does not disqualify a purchase as a matter of statute, and any guidance stating that it does is describing a rule that article 100 does not contain. What this report concludes本報告的結論

Athens is the only market documented on this site that has completed a full property cycle within the available record, and the completed cycle says something the markets still inside one cannot. The Athens index fell 44.7% over thirty-five quarters, recovered 107.5% off the trough and stands 14.8% above its 2008 nominal peak, while the real index fell 48.0% nationally and is still 18.9% below its 2007 peak. The nominal recovery took fifteen and three-quarter years. The real recovery has not happened. Any market currently arguing that a nominal recovery is a recovery has an eighteen-year worked example available in Athens.雅典是本站所記載的市場中，唯一於可得紀錄內走完一整個房產循環者，而完整的循環能說出仍身處循環之中的市場所無法說出的事。雅典指數於三十五季內下跌 44.7%，自谷底回升 107.5%，現高於 2008 年名目高點 14.8%；同期全國實質指數下跌 48.0%，且仍低於 2007 年高點 18.9%。名目層面的收復耗時十五年九個月，實質層面的收復並未發生。任何目前主張「名目復甦即為復甦」的市場，於雅典有一個十八年的實例可資參照。

The access rule moved capital and not applications, and the separation is measurable to the quarter. Non-resident real-estate inflows ran €783.7m and €824.5m in the two quarters straddling the 31 August 2024 deposit deadline, the two highest in a series that begins in 2002, then fell to €356.8m; permit filings in the deadline month were 550, below the month before, and peaked four months later at 1,262. Türkiye ran the same experiment and it appeared in the transaction count instead, with foreign sales setting an all-time monthly record immediately before the tightening and falling 55% the next month. The behaviour is identical and only the observable channel differs.准入規則移動的是資金而非申請件數，其分離可精確至季。非居民房地產流入於跨越 2024 年 8 月 31 日訂金期限的兩季分別為 7.837 億與 8.245 億歐元，為一條始於 2002 年之序列中最高的兩季，其後降至 3.568 億歐元；期限當月的許可申請為 550 件，低於前一個月，並於四個月後見頂於 1,262 件。土耳其進行了同一實驗，其結果顯現於成交件數：外資成交於緊縮前夕創下歷史單月紀錄，次月下跌 55%。兩者行為完全相同，僅可觀察的管道不同。

An announced restriction on this market produces front-running rather than compliance, and there are now two independent instances. The short-term-let registration freeze covering central Athens took effect on 1 January 2025 and central-Athens listings rose about 11% in its first year, with the historic-core micro-markets up 20%, because owners secured registration numbers in the four weeks between publication and effect. The supply argument that holds up is not the vacancy headline but the utilisation finding: between the 2011 and 2021 censuses total stock rose 3.5% while dwellings for rent fell 10.4% and dwellings for sale fell 33.1%. Greece is not short of dwellings. It is short of dwellings offered to the market, and that is a mechanism a policy can address and a construction cycle cannot. The macro conditions behind all of it are in the companion Greece Macro × Property analysis.本市場的預告型限制措施產生搶跑而非遵循，現已有兩個彼此獨立的實例。涵蓋中心雅典的短租登錄凍結於 2025 年 1 月 1 日生效，其第一年的中心雅典掛牌數增加約 11%，歷史核心次區增幅達 20%，成因為所有權人於公布至生效之間的四週內取得登錄號碼。站得住腳的供給論述不是空屋的表面數字，而是利用率的發現：2011 與 2021 兩次普查之間，總存量上升 3.5%，出租用住宅減少 10.4%，出售用住宅減少 33.1%。希臘缺的不是住宅，是被提供到市場上的住宅；此為政策可以處理、而營建循環無法處理的機制。促成上述全部情形的總體條件詳見對照的《希臘總經 × 房產》。

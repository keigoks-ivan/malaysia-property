Three instruments, one page三套儀器，一頁讀 A house market asks three questions that one number can't answer at once — where prices are headed, whether the market is healthy, and whether it is flashing the specific warning signs that have, historically, preceded a real loss. This page keeps them on three separate instruments. The Compass orders direction from momentum and credit — a description of the past; a later attempt to validate that ordering as a forward call failed its own pre-registered gates. The Report Card diagnoses condition — valuation, population, supply — describing health without pretending to time it. The Risk Gauge is the one instrument here with a tested forward claim: a frozen, pre-registered test found that Starved-plus-supply-glut quarters carry real, elevated odds of a loss or a bottom-quartile outcome, in the markets that have ever had one to learn from. Three jobs, three tools, never blended into a single score.一個房市同時問三個問題，而一個數字無法一次回答——價格接下來往哪走、這個市場健不健康、以及它是否亮起那種過去曾預示真實虧損的特定警示訊號。這頁把三者放在三套分開的儀器上。羅盤只用動能與信貸為方向排序——這是對過去的描述；後續一次想把這個排序驗證為前向預測的嘗試，未能通過自己預先凍結的門檻。健檢卡診斷體質——估值、人口、供給——描述健康狀況，而不假裝拿它來抓時機。風險警報是這裡唯一一套通過前向驗證的儀器：一次凍結、預先設定門檻的測試發現，斷炊疊加供給過剩的季度，在曾經出現過虧損可供學習的市場中，確實帶有真實、明顯偏高的虧損或落入最差四分之一表現的機率。三種工作、三套工具，絕不合併成單一分數。

The Risk Gauge — Evaluated Here, and It Failed風險警報 · 在此市場已評估，未通過

A third instrument, kept separate from the compass and the report card: it answers one narrow, pre-registered question — is the market flashing the specific combination (no price momentum, credit still contracting, and a live supply glut) that has, historically, preceded either a 3-year nominal loss or a bottom-quartile outcome? The classification rule and the pass/fail bars were frozen before this test ran (scripts/compass_v3_protocol.md), and on the four frozen markets it passed. Pooled across the markets that qualify for each claim, the odds ratio comes out to 13.5× for an absolute loss and 16.1× for a bottom-quartile outcome.第三套儀器，與羅盤和體檢表分開設計：它只回答一個窄、且已預先凍結的問題——市場現在是否亮起那個特定組合（房價動能消失、信貸持續收縮、且供給過剩警示正在亮燈），而這個組合過去曾預示三年期名目虧損，或落入最差四分之一表現？分類規則與通過／失敗門檻，都在這次測試跑之前就已凍結（scripts/compass_v3_protocol.md），且於凍結的四個市場通過驗證。把符合資格的市場合併計算，絕對虧損的勝算比為13.5倍，落入最差四分之一的勝算比為16.1倍。

EVALUATED · FAILED已評估 · 未通過as of 截至 2025Q4 · permits-flow basis · 許可流量基礎

The risk gauge was evaluable in Greece, was evaluated, and failed. Greece is the first hold-out market to clear the applicability gate: 36 matured three-year nominal-loss quarters against a pre-registered minimum of 8, produced by a completed multi-year crash. The classifier under test is unchanged, a starved cell AND a live supply glut, and on the frozen thresholds it scored an odds ratio of 0.26x with precision 0.0% and recall 0.0% against a 34.3% base rate. It fired in 3 matured quarters, 2003Q3, 2004Q2 and 2005Q2, each of which was followed by a three-year gain of 23% to 34%, and it was silent through every one of the 36 loss quarters from 2006Q4 to 2015Q3. The failure is specifically the glut term and not the instrument as a whole: momentum alone, declared in the protocol solely to isolate the glut term's contribution, scored 36.75x with precision 82.3% and recall 77.8% on the same quarters and caught the crash. Adding the glut requirement took a working signal to zero, and the pre-declared expanding-window variant does not rescue it (odds ratio 0.20, still no true positives). The mechanism is the permits-flow basis set out in the supply note below. Greece is also the first hold-out to enter the pooled absolute-loss stratum, which pulls the labelled pooled odds ratio from 13.5x to 9.6x. Distinguish this state from the other two negatives on this site: Australia is NOT RATED, its gauge computes but only two matured loss quarters exist to score it against; Thailand is NOT EVALUABLE, no supply series exists so the classifier cannot be formed at any sample size. Greece is neither unrated nor unevaluable. It is refuted on this market's supply basis, and the consequence is published rather than softened: no supply warning is shown for Greece at 2025Q4, in either direction, and where a market's glut input is a flow rather than a stock the overlay should be treated as invalid rather than as reassuring.風險警報在希臘可以評估，已經評估，並且未通過。希臘是第一個通過適用性門檻的樣本外市場：已完成觀測的三年期名目虧損季度共 36 個，高於預先設定的 8 個門檻，來源是一次已完成的多年期崩盤。受測的分類規則未作任何更動，仍為斷炊格且供給過剩警示亮燈；以凍結門檻計分，勝算比 0.26 倍，精確率 0.0%，召回率 0.0%，基準比例 34.3%。該規則在 3 個已完成觀測的季度觸發，分別為 2003Q3、2004Q2 與 2005Q2，其後三年房價各上漲 23% 至 34%；而 2006Q4 至 2015Q3 這 36 個虧損季度，全程未曾觸發。失敗的是供給過剩這一項，不是整套儀器：協定中純為隔離該項貢獻而宣告的「僅動能」規則，在同一批季度上勝算比為 36.75 倍（精確率 82.3%，召回率 77.8%），並且抓到了該段崩盤。在動能之上追加供給過剩條件，使一個有效訊號歸零；預先宣告的擴張窗變體亦無法挽救（勝算比 0.20，仍無任何真陽性）。成因即下方供給說明所述的許可流量基礎。希臘同時是第一個進入絕對虧損合併分層的樣本外市場，使標示為含希臘的合併勝算比由 13.5 倍降至 9.6 倍。此一狀態須與本站另外兩種否定狀態區分：澳洲為未評級，其警報可以計算，但可用於計分的已完成虧損季度僅有兩個；泰國為無法評估，因不存在供給數列，分類規則在任何樣本數下都無法構成。希臘既非未評級，也非無法評估，而是在本市場的供給基礎上遭到推翻。此一結論如實發布，不予淡化：2025Q4 的希臘不顯示任何供給警示，兩個方向皆然；凡市場的供給過剩輸入為流量而非存量者，此一疊加應視為不成立，而非視為安全。

Important caveat: the absolute-loss validation above (US, Japan) rests on essentially two historical busts in this sample — the US 2006-08 crash and Japan's 1990s-2000s crash. The test was pre-registered and passed honestly, but the history behind it is thin. A third crash, whenever it comes, is the real out-of-sample test this page hasn't faced yet. The Compass — Momentum × Credit羅盤 · 動能 × 信貸

The horizontal axis is momentum — the recent trend in house prices; the vertical axis is credit impulse — whether mortgage and household credit is accelerating or slowing. Crossing them gives four cells: Uptrend·Fuelled (prices rising, credit accelerating), Uptrend·Draining (prices rising but credit fading), Downtrend·Reflating (prices falling but credit turning up), and Downtrend·Starved (prices falling, credit still tightening). The glowing dot marks the latest quarter; a supply-glut warning lights up separately when vacancy or completions run loose relative to the recent norm (trailing z ≤ −1), since a glut can make even a fuelled uptrend risky. For Greece that warning is deliberately absent: its only supply series is a permits flow, the overlay was tested on it and inverted, so the current point carries a basis note where a glut ring would be.橫軸是動能——房價近期的趨勢；縱軸是信貸脈衝——房貸與家庭信貸正在加速還是放緩。兩軸交叉出四格：上升·助燃（房價漲、信貸加速）、上升·抽乾（房價漲但信貸轉弱）、下降·回溫（房價跌但信貸開始回升）、下降·斷糧（房價跌、信貸仍在收緊）。發光的點是最新一季；供給過剩警示會另外亮起——當空置率或完工量相對近期常態轉為寬鬆（滾動 z ≤ −1）時，即使是助燃中的上升格也可能變得危險。希臘的該項警示為刻意不顯示：其唯一的供給數列是許可流量，該疊加在其上受測且方向相反，因此當前點位以基礎說明取代供給過剩環。

The Report Card — Health, Not Prediction體檢表 · 診斷體質，不預測

Valuation, population growth and supply sit here as separate, transparent gauges — each one shows only whether that single force looks strained or comfortable right now. They are deliberately never averaged into one score and never used to predict where prices go next; that job belongs to the compass. Valuation and supply are standardised over the market's whole history, so they read position versus that market's own past — not a cross-country absolute (a market that is always expensive, like Taiwan near 9× income, reads mid-range against itself). Population instead reads an absolute growth direction on a fixed scale, not standardised against history — the same growth rate reads the same regardless of that market's own past.估值、人口成長與供給在這裡各是一個獨立、透明的儀表——每一個只顯示該力量目前偏緊繃還是偏寬鬆，僅此而已。它們刻意不被平均成一個分數，也不用來預測房價接下來會怎麼走；那是羅盤的工作。估值與供給以該市場整段歷史標準化，讀的是相對該市場自身過去的位置——不是跨國絕對值（一個一直很貴的市場，如台灣約 9 倍所得，相對自己會落在中段）。人口則是固定尺度的絕對成長方向讀數，不是相對歷史標準化——不論該市場過去長什麼樣子，同樣的成長率讀數都一樣。

Valuation估值

Valuation reads -1.27 at 2025Q4, on the expensive side of Greece's own record; the 2026Q1 row, where price and income are published although credit is not, deepens it to -1.67. Construction: Greece publishes no price-to-income ratio and is absent from Eurostat's house-price collection entirely, so this gauge is computed as BIS nominal prices over Eurostat household gross disposable income — a genuine household aggregate, concept-comparable with the Australian denominator used on this site, not a per-worker wage. The income leg begins 1999Q1, so the first standardised reading is 2001Q4 and the 24-quarter base is not fully populated until about 2005. The gap driving the reading is documented rather than inferred: nominal prices are +90.0% from their 2017Q3 trough while household disposable income is +48.3% from its own 2014Q1 trough. Two cautions: the last four price quarters should be treated as provisional, since the Bank of Greece flags its parallel Athens index as provisional and revises it while BIS publishes no status flag at all; and the BIS series has no tier-1 corroboration, because Greece files no house-price index to Eurostat.估值於 2025Q4 為 −1.27，落在希臘自身紀錄偏貴的一側；2026Q1 一列（房價與所得已發布，信貸未發布）進一步降至 −1.67。口徑說明：希臘不發布房價所得比，且完全未列入 Eurostat 的房價指數統計，本量表因此以 BIS 名目房價除以 Eurostat 家庭毛可支配所得計算。該分母為真正的家庭加總所得，概念上與本站澳洲所用分母可比，並非每受僱者薪資。所得腿自 1999Q1 起算，第一個標準化讀數落在 2001Q4，24 季基期至約 2005 年才完整。驅動此一讀數的落差有據可查，非推估：名目房價自 2017Q3 谷底上漲 90.0%，家庭可支配所得自其 2014Q1 谷底上漲 48.3%。兩項保留：其一，最近四季房價應視為初步值，希臘央行對其並行的雅典指數標示為初步值並持續修正，BIS 則完全不發布狀態註記；其二，BIS 數列缺乏第一層來源的交叉驗證，因為希臘未向 Eurostat 報送房價指數。

Population人口

Population reads +0.32 on the absolute scale at 2025Q4, which is quarterly national-accounts population up 0.22% year-on-year. The positive sign is recent and small, and it sits inside a large structural decline: the level peaked at 11.12 million in 2010Q4 and stands at 10.55 million in 2026Q1, 5.16% lower, with the loss concentrated in the crisis and emigration years; it has ticked up only since 2024Q3. Read the year-on-year change alone. The source is Eurostat's quarterly national-accounts population, compiled by interpolating annual demographic statistics onto quarters, so quarter-on-quarter movement is the compiler's smoothing rather than an observation. That concept also runs 70,000 to 126,000 above the World Bank series from 2021, because the two absorbed the 2021 census differently; they agree on direction throughout.人口於 2025Q4 讀數為 +0.32（絕對值尺度），對應季度國民所得帳人口年增 0.22%。此一正號屬近期現象且幅度很小，並且位於一段大幅的結構性下滑之內：人口於 2010Q4 見頂於 1,112 萬人，2026Q1 為 1,055 萬人，累計減少 5.16%，減幅集中於債務危機與外移年份，2024Q3 之後才轉為回升。判讀時只應採用年增率。資料源為 Eurostat 季度國民所得帳人口，係將年度人口統計內插至季頻編製，季增變動屬編製者的平滑，不是獨立觀測值。此一口徑自 2021 年起亦較世界銀行數列高出 7 萬至 12.6 萬人，原因是兩者對 2021 年普查的併入方式不同，惟兩者在方向上全期一致。

Supply供給

Supply here is a permits FLOW, and that is why the glut overlay above is switched off. The series is Eurostat quarterly dwellings authorised (index, 2021=100, not seasonally adjusted, complete from 1997Q1); Greece has no usable vacancy or inventory series at all, its only tier-1 vacancy observations being the 2011 and 2021 censuses. The gauge itself stays, and reads -0.05 at 2025Q4 against +0.08 at 2026Q1, close to the middle of Greece's own history: permits authorised are a real measurement of how much new supply is being cleared for construction, and that is worth showing. What they are not is a measure of stock hanging over the market, and the pre-registered test in the section above showed that substituting one for the other does not merely weaken the glut signal, it inverts it. Greek dwellings authorised fell from an index near 500 in 2005 to 20.3 in 2016Q1: permits collapsed with prices rather than ahead of them, so the trailing standardisation read tight throughout the 2007-2013 bust and loose during the 2001-2005 and 2017-2024 upswings. Two regulatory artefacts must be carried with any recent reading. 2005Q4 is a pull-forward ahead of the 19% VAT applied to buildings permitted from 1 January 2006, and 2025 is depressed by the suspension of the NOK floor-area and height bonuses between December 2024 and May 2025, so the +70.2% year-on-year print in 2026Q1 sits on an artificial base. One definitional conflict is left unaveraged: for 2025 ELSTAT counts 39,219 dwellings authorised against Eurostat's 31,900, because ELSTAT covers building activity that Eurostat's residential cut excludes. Both are tier-1 sources and neither is adjusted to the other.希臘此處的供給是許可「流量」，這也是上方供給過剩疊加關閉的原因。數列為 Eurostat 季度住宅核發戶數（指數，2021=100，未季調，自 1997Q1 起完整）；希臘完全沒有可用的空置或庫存數列，其第一層空置資料僅有 2011 年與 2021 年兩次普查。本量表本身予以保留，2025Q4 讀數為 −0.05，2026Q1 為 +0.08，接近希臘自身歷史的中段：核發戶數是對「有多少新供給獲准興建」的真實衡量，值得呈現。但它不是對市場上方存量壓力的衡量，而上一節的預先登記檢定顯示，以流量替代存量並非只是削弱過剩訊號，而是使其反向。希臘住宅核發戶數自 2005 年指數接近 500 降至 2016Q1 的 20.3：核發量與房價同步崩落，並未領先，因此滾動標準化在 2007 年至 2013 年整段衰退期間讀為「緊」，反而在 2001 年至 2005 年與 2017 年至 2024 年兩段上行期讀為「鬆」。近期讀數另須攜帶兩項法規造成的失真。2005Q4 為 2006 年 1 月 1 日起對新建物課徵 19% 加值稅前的提前申請；2025 年則因新建築法規（ΝΟΚ）的樓地板面積與高度獎勵於 2024 年 12 月至 2025 年 5 月暫停而受壓抑，2026Q1 年增 70.2% 係建立在人為偏低的基期之上。另有一項定義衝突不做平均處理：2025 年 ELSTAT 統計核發住宅 39,219 戶，Eurostat 為 31,900 戶，差異在於 ELSTAT 涵蓋 Eurostat 住宅口徑所排除的建築活動。兩者皆為第一層來源，不相互調整。

Why It's Built This Way為什麼這樣設計

The split isn't a stylistic choice — it's what the data forces. Test each force on its own against three-year forward house-price returns across the US, Taiwan, Malaysia and Japan, and momentum is the only one that works everywhere: quarters following price uptrends beat quarters following downtrends by 7 to 14 points, in every market, no exceptions. Valuation fails the same test — it mean-reverts the way textbooks promise in cyclical markets, but in Japan it reversed by 18.9 points, because post-1990 cheapness there was structural decay, not a bargain waiting to close. That is exactly why valuation can't sit on the forecast axis: it describes condition, and condition points in different directions in different markets. Blend it into one “cycle” score and you answer two questions at once — where prices go next, and whether the market is expensive — and get both muddy. Kept apart, each instrument can be judged on its own terms. The Compass's four-cell ordering — Fuelled beating Starved — holds in the US, Malaysia and Japan; it breaks in Taiwan, but that's a sample problem, not a method one: 101 quarters of a nearly one-directional bull run means almost every “downtrend” quarter was simply followed by the next leg up. Momentum is also what finally reads Japan right — the one market that had a real crash to get right.這樣拆開不是風格選擇，是資料逼出來的結果。把每一種力量單獨拿去對美國、台灣、馬來西亞、日本的三年前向房價報酬做檢驗，動能是唯一在所有市場都有效的一項：房價處於上升趨勢之後的季度，報酬比處於下降趨勢之後的季度高出七至十四個百分點，四個市場、無一例外。估值撐不過同一套檢驗——在有循環性的市場裡它照教科書均值回歸，但在日本反轉了十八點九個百分點，因為 1990 年後的便宜是結構性衰敗，不是等待兌現的划算。這正是估值不能放上預測軸的原因：它描述的是體質，而體質在不同市場指向不同方向。把它攪進一個「週期」分數，等於同時回答兩個問題——價格接下來往哪走、以及市場貴不貴——結果兩個都答得含糊。拆開來，每套儀器才能各自被檢驗。羅盤的四格排序——燃料格贏過飢餓格——在美國、馬來西亞、日本都成立；在台灣不成立，但這是樣本問題，不是方法問題：一百零一季幾乎單向的多頭，意味著「下降趨勢」的季度後面幾乎都接著下一段漲勢。也是動能，終於讓日本這個唯一真正崩盤過的市場被讀對。

Three reference frames sit on this page, and each answers a different question. The compass is trailing and cyclical — momentum and credit impulse — and answers where prices are likely headed next. The self-history gauges are expanding, measuring valuation, population and supply against a market's own multi-decade record, and answer whether the market looks strained relative to its own past. The cross-country ranks are neither: they measure a market against the other 11 right now, and answer whether it's actually cheap or expensive in absolute terms. The US and Taiwan show why one frame alone misleads — the US reads expensive against its own history (z=-3.0) but only middling against the world (composite +0.30), while Taiwan reads neutral against its own history (z≈0) yet is the most expensive of the four against the world (composite -0.93, worst yield of all 12 markets). Use all three together, not one in isolation.這個頁面有三個參照座標，各自回答不同問題。羅盤是落後、順週期的——動能與信貸脈衝——回答「價格接下來可能往哪走」。健檢卡的自身歷史量表是擴張型的，把估值、人口與供給拿去對比該市場自己數十年的紀錄，回答「相對自己的過去，這個市場現在算不算緊繃」。跨國排名則兩者皆非：它把一個市場放進當下其他11個市場中比較，回答「就絕對水準而言，這個市場到底貴不貴」。美國與台灣正好說明只用單一座標會誤導：美國相對自身歷史顯得昂貴（z=-3.0），但相對世界卻只是中段（綜合分數+0.30）；台灣相對自身歷史持平（z≈0），但相對世界卻是這四個市場中最貴的一個（綜合分數-0.93，租金報酬率為全部12個市場中最差）。三者要合起來看，不能只取其一。

What This Does and Does Not Claim承諾什麼、不承諾什麼

  • The risk gauge is real, but thinly tested. Starved-plus-supply-glut quarters carried elevated odds of a 3-year nominal loss (US OR 16.2×, Japan OR 13.2×, pooled OR 13.5×) and of landing in a market's own worst quartile (US, Malaysia, Japan; pooled OR 16.1×) — validated on frozen, pre-registered thresholds, not fit to the data afterward. But the absolute-loss claim rests on essentially two historical busts in this sample: the US 2006-08 crash and Japan's 1990s-2000s crash. A third crash, whenever it comes, is the real out-of-sample test this page hasn't faced yet.風險警報是真實的，但驗證樣本很薄。斷炊疊加供給過剩的季度，三年期名目虧損的勝算明顯偏高（美國勝算比16.2倍、日本勝算比13.2倍、合併勝算比13.5倍），落入市場自身最差四分之一表現的勝算也偏高（美國、馬來西亞、日本；合併勝算比16.1倍）——這是在凍結、預先設定門檻的條件下驗證出來的，不是事後套進資料調出來的。但絕對虧損這項宣稱，基本上只建立在樣本中僅有的兩次歷史崩盤上：美國2006年至2008年的崩盤，以及日本1990年代至2000年代的崩盤。下一次崩盤，不論何時到來，才是這頁尚未面對過的真正樣本外測試。
  • The four-cell return ordering is still descriptive, not a forecast. A separate attempt to validate Fuelled-beats-Starved as a forward-return ranking failed its own pre-registered gates ; the compass panel and the forward-return table above describe the past, they are not a tested prediction of what comes next.四格報酬排序仍是描述性的，不是預測。另一次想把「助燃格贏過斷炊格」驗證為前向報酬排序的嘗試，未能通過自己預先凍結的門檻；上方的羅盤面板與前向報酬表描述的是過去，不是經過測試、對未來的預測。
  • Greece: the gauge finally met a real crash, and failed on it. The bust this page said it was waiting for has arrived in the hold-out set. Greece is the first market outside the frozen four on which the starved-plus-glut classifier could be scored at all: it clears the applicability gate with 36 matured three-year nominal-loss quarters against a pre-registered minimum of 8. Scored on the frozen thresholds it returned an odds ratio of 0.26× with precision 0.0% and recall 0.0%, missing the entire 36-quarter loss episode of 2006Q4 to 2015Q3, while momentum alone returned 36.75× (precision 82.3%, recall 77.8%) and caught it. Requiring a supply glut on top of momentum did not add a filter, it removed a working signal. The diagnosis is specific rather than general: Greece is the only market here whose glut term is a supply FLOW, quarterly dwellings authorised, instead of a stock or absorption measure, and Greek permits collapsed with prices rather than ahead of them. So the overlay is refuted on a flow basis and no warning is published for Greece; on the stock basis used by the other five it stands, now carrying one out-of-sample stratum against it, which pulls the labelled pooled odds ratio from 13.5× to 9.6×.

◆ DEEP NARRATIVE

Project Deep Summary

Orion Residence sits in the heart of Bukit Bintang, between Jalan Gading and Jalan Palmer — a single 46-storey luxury serviced residence tower of just 298 units (a rare low-density design for BB). Bukit Bintang is KL CBD's tourist retail core, adjacent to Pavilion KL, Sungei Wang Plaza, Imbi, Bukit Bintang City Centre (BBCC) and other KL flagship retail clusters; Bukit Bintang MRT station (two-line interchange: Kajang Line + Putrajaya Line) is only 300m / 3-min walk from Orion — existing MRT is BB's core structural advantage versus Mont Kiara / DPC. Orion's micro-position is slightly off Bintang Walk's main spine (Jalan Bukit Bintang artery), but BB's high integration density allows this distance penalty to be offset by Pavilion + TRX + Wyndham Garden Hotel and other walkable assets.

Orion Residence is developed by Orion Tower Sdn Bhd (a unit of Johor-based Welton Development, founded 2013) — this is Welton's first KL high-rise premium SR project. Welton's flagship to date is the Green Haven residential high-rise in Johor Bahru (winning Asia Pacific Property Award "Highly Commended" in 2016), led by founder Ling Kee Tong; KL high-end SR is entirely new territory for Welton with no comparable adjacent track record. Orion commissioned RIBA-chartered architect Jason Yee, whose signature features include the Orion constellation-themed moiré façade, SEA's first outdoor high-speed glass lift, and an automated concierge parking system. The build profile: 46-storey single tower of 298 units (G-42F residential floors), unit types: Type A Studio 491-588 sqft (singles / investment entry), Type B 2BR/2BA 680-746 sqft (entry 2BR), Type C 2BR/2BA 838-1,186 sqft (mid-to-large 2BR), Type D 3BR/3BA Dual Key 1,180-1,329 sqft (family + manager suite dual-key); VP reached March 2025 on time, currently ~1 year completed within 2-yr warranty. The final 38 units (12.8% of inventory) operate under Wyndham Registry Collection international hotel brand on a 70/30 Profit Sharing Pool model — Wyndham handles global marketing, bookings, housekeeping, maintenance; owners receive 70% of pool profit; the remaining 86% units are self-managed or owner-delegated.

On pricing: Orion's current asking PSF averages ~RM 2,500, range RM 2,165-3,851 — Brickz < 10 tx (no deep transaction validation post 2025-03 VP). Versus BB broad non-branded SR median RM 2,000-2,300 psf, Orion premium is 25-30%, reflecting Wyndham international brand + freehold (vs most BB SA leasehold) + low density (298 units vs PavSq 1,000+ units) triple premium. But Welton is a first-time KL entrant sponsor, no prior KL same-class brickz comparison exists; whether the premium sustains requires 3-5 years of secondary transaction validation. Starting price RM 1.58M (likely Type B 2BR 680 sqft) corresponds to PSF ~RM 2,324; the largest Type E 5BR 4,047 sqft × RM 3,851 psf = RM 15.6M+, with penthouse-tier single units reaching RM 25M as PSF top. 298-unit low density implies secondary turnover of ~1-2 tx/month (estimated), narrow buyer pool but matched to hotel/investment-tier clientele.

Rental market: using 933 sqft 2BR Type C mid-floor as representative, monthly rent estimated at RM 7,500 (@ RM 8 psf BB branded SR median), gross yield ~4.00%. BB broad SR rent RM 6.5-10 psf variation range, Orion's Wyndham brand + freehold + MRT proximity supports mid-to-upper RM 7.5-9 psf range; Local 15% progressive tax: Net Yield ~3.3%, spread −1.3%; Foreign 30% non-resident tax: Net Yield ~2.6%, spread −2.5%. The Wyndham 70/30 hotel pool provides an elevated yield path for the final 38 units (but ROI depends on Wyndham's global occupancy performance + brand premium), only 12.8% of inventory; the remaining 86% buyers rely on self-managed rental, with BB tourist seasonality (Ramadan / public holidays) creating variability, the 1 mo/yr vacancy assumption potentially optimistic. Tenant pool comprises BB tourists (short-let), corporate business travelers (mid-to-long-let), local middle-class white-collar (long-let).

Holding structure: Freehold + service residence — SR sits between commercial SA and residential condo, with some banks applying 80-85% LTV to SR (vs residential 90% LTV); buyers should confirm mortgage terms pre-entry. MM2H asset eligibility: Studio 491 sqft × RM 2,500 psf ≈ RM 1.23M, 2BR Type B 680 sqft × RM 2,500 psf ≈ RM 1.70M, 2BR Type C 933 sqft × RM 2,500 psf ≈ RM 2.33M — all unit types meet RM 1M Federal threshold ✓. Completed ~1 year means buyers can inspect directly, verify Wyndham co-management quality, and confirm JMB operation — zero execution risk; within 2-yr warranty allows defect claims against Welton. 10-year exit expectation: at base 3%/yr appreciation, Yr 10 price ~RM 3.12M, Local net P&L approximately +RM 406K (IRR 5-6%), Foreign +RM 198K (IRR 3-4%).

Key caveats: ①Welton is a Johor sponsor's first KL case, no comparable KL asset preservation track — "no-discount preservation" needs Wyndham brand sustain + Welton's subsequent KL project execution to support; ②Wyndham 70/30 hotel pool covers only 38 units (12.8% inventory) actively managed; the remaining 86% buyers rely on self-managed rental with high achievable yield variability; ③KL SA overhang 3,643 units (NAPIC Q3 2025) does not trigger foreign exit cap of 6 at Orion's 4.5 foreign exit, but BB area peer SR (PavSq, Pavilion KL Suites, The Mews) are direct competitors — secondary exit must compete with KL internal SA pool; ④BB is sensitive to KL tourism cycle, external shocks (e.g. 2020 pandemic) impact BB SR rent more than DPC / MK residential areas, achievable rent in downside can drop 20-30%; ⑤SR classification means some banks may not apply 90% LTV (some apply 80-85%); buyers should confirm with bank pre-entry; ⑥MYR systemic depreciation is a common implicit risk for all KL property.

  • Bull case: MM2H 2,637 pending buyers concentrate landing + Wyndham brand drives hotel investor pool + prices +5%/yr. Local IRR ~7-8% (RM 1,063K+), Foreign IRR ~5-6% (RM 788K+).
  • Bear case: BB tourist cycle downturn + KL SA overhang intensifies + prices 0%/yr. Local IRR near −2% (10-year loss RM 367K), Foreign IRR near −3% due to 8% stamp + 30% rental tax + perpetual RPGT (loss RM 505K).
10-year exit liquidity: 298-unit low density limits supply + Wyndham brand anchor attracts hotel investor pool, but Brickz data thin needs 3-5 yrs to validate; expected listing-to-sale 2-4 months (branded BB 2BR), discount 3-7%; foreign buyers sensitive to Wyndham brand sustain + BB tourist cycle, downturn exits may face larger discounts.
◆ CONCLUSION

Highlights & Caveats

✦ Highlights
  • Bukit Bintang freehold service residence is a scarce asset in KL CBD tourist retail core; Bukit Bintang MRT 300m / 3-min walk (existing two-line interchange) + Pavilion KL and TRX walkable
  • Wyndham Registry Collection international hotel brand (final 38 units in 70/30 hotel pool) — rare for KL private developments, can serve as exit anchor for foreign hotel investors
  • 298-unit low density (vs PavSq 1,000+ unit large SA) is rare "boutique supply" design for BB; narrow secondary turnover and buyer pool but matched to hotel/investment-tier clientele
  • Already completed 2025-03, execution risk = 0; within 2-yr warranty allows defect claims against Welton; buyers can directly verify Wyndham co-management quality and JMB
  • Freehold (vs most leasehold BB SA) + MM2H qualifying (all unit types ≥ RM 1M) + foreign hotel investor pool sensitive to brand, Wyndham can serve as attraction anchor
  • RIBA-chartered architect Jason Yee design + Orion constellation-themed moiré façade + SEA's first high-speed outdoor glass lift, distinctive architectural signature for long-term brand memory
⚠ Caveats
  • Welton Development is a Johor sponsor's first KL project — KL premium SR is entirely new territory with no prior KL same-class brickz comparison; "asset preservation" needs Wyndham brand sustain + Welton's subsequent KL execution to support
  • Current asking PSF RM 2,500-3,500 is BB SR upper end (25-30% premium to non-branded BB SR median RM 2,000-2,300 psf) — whether Wyndham + freehold dual premium sustains requires 3-5 years of secondary transaction validation
  • Gross 4.0% below 5% baseline; Local spread −1.3%, Foreign −2.5% both negative — this is not a high yield play; Wyndham 70/30 hotel pool only 38 units (12.8%), 86% buyers self-manage with high yield variability
  • BB is sensitive to KL tourism cycle, external shocks (pandemic, policy changes) impact BB SR rent more than DPC / MK; downturn achievable rent may drop 20-30%
  • Service residence classification (some banks apply 80-85% LTV to SR vs residential 90% LTV); buyers should confirm mortgage rate and LTV with bank pre-entry
  • MYR systemic depreciation is a common implicit risk for all KL property; foreign buyers should factor currency conversion volatility into position sizing

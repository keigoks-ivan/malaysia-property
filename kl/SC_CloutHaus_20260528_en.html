◆ Deep Narrative

Project Deep Summary

CloutHaus Residences sits at 28 Jalan P. Ramlee KLCC (~400m to KLCC Twin Towers, 5-min walk), TA Global Berhad's (Bursa:5158) KLCC luxury flagship. TA Global is the real estate subsidiary of TA Group of Companies (Bursa T1, market cap RM 1.3B); TA Group parent conglomerate has 30+ yr financial + property integration experience, operating international hotel portfolios across Australia, Canada, Singapore, China, Thailand (5 countries Ramada / Radisson / Paradox brands). CloutHaus develops 2.259-acre freehold commercial parcel into two towers: Tower 1 58 storeys (242 private residences + Paradox Hotel KLCC 548 keys, Malaysia's first Paradox); Tower 2 66 storeys (615 serviced apartments). Tower 2 VP estimated Q2 2029, currently Off-plan with 3+ yr wait.

On location: KLCC (Bukit Ceylon / Conlay) district anchors at 8.0 baseline in v2.0.1 anchor table. CloutHaus adjustments: (1) Jalan P. Ramlee is main connectivity road to KLCC; ~400m to Twin Towers is on par with Armani Hallson (300m) for closeness to KLCC core (+0.2); (2) Paradox Hotel KLCC same-master integration (Malaysia's first Paradox Hotel; backed by US-based Concord Hospitality listed group operating 100+ Hyatt/Marriott/Hilton-branded hotels globally) +0.1. Location slightly weaker than Armani Hallson 8.5 (which has direct covered link bridge to Twin Towers) but on par with KLCC Conlay 8.3; overall d1 reaches 8.3. MRT Bukit Bintang, Monorail Raja Chulan, KLCC LRT multi-line accessible; Suria KLCC, Avenue K, KLCC Park, KLCC Convention Centre walkable.

Project scale: 857 units (242 + 615) is mid-scale among KLCC luxury, significantly lighter internal supply than Armani Hallson 2,215-unit large-scale community (gap -1,358 units). Tower 2 unit menu spans Studio 549 sqft to 3BR Dual-key 1,216 sqft — diverse range for multiple client segments (investment-light + Airbnb + small families). Facilities: 50m infinity pool, rooftop bar with panoramic views, fitness center on high floors; same-master Paradox Hotel service spillover expected (24h concierge, housekeeping). Sinking fund RM 0.80 psf (including sinking fund) is highest among scorecards (vs SWNK Houze 0.60, Armani Hallson 0.65, Conlay 0.65), reflecting Paradox-influenced higher operating cost service standard.

On the developer side: TA Global Berhad is Bursa T1 developer (Bursa:5158, market cap RM 1.3B), TA Group parent conglomerate has 30+ yr financial + property integration experience (TA Securities is well-known subsidiary). TA Global's international hotel portfolio spans 5 countries (Australia Radisson, Canada, Singapore Pacific Regency, China, Thailand), with real international hospitality management experience. Paradox Hotel KLCC is TA Global × Paradox Hotels (US Concord Hospitality) partnership project, providing same-master service spillover + Paradox brand marketing support. Annual revenue RM 85.2M (2025) relatively small but focused on niche luxury positioning, market cap RM 1.3B. CloutHaus is TA Global's KL luxury flagship (GDV undisclosed but estimated RM 2-3B range), execution and Paradox integration long-term continuity are watch items. Overall d5 = 7.5 is Bursa T1 baseline + international brand integration minus off-plan risk.

On pricing: CloutHaus Tower 2 asking PSF RM 2,732-2,823 psf, average selling price RM 2,900 psf; starting RM 1,548,800 (549 sqft studio). Compared against KLCC luxury cluster: vs Four Seasons Place RM 3,010 psf ~4-5% discount; vs Pavilion Suites RM 3,150 psf ~8% discount; vs Banyan Tree Signatures RM 1,716 psf ~+70% premium; vs The Conlay RM 2,030-2,500 psf ~+20% premium; vs Armani Hallson RM 1,449-3,662 psf in mid-top range. CloutHaus positions clearly in KLCC luxury cluster upper-mid, reflecting Paradox Hotel integration + TA Global Bursa T1 + smaller-scale community combined premium. Entry price d2 = 6.0 (baseline + strong sponsor bonus), slightly above Armani Hallson 5.5 reflects TA Global vs Armani Group developer brand differential.

On the rental market: KLCC overall gross yield 4-5%. CloutHaus Tower 2 2BR 860 sqft fully furnished long-let est. RM 9,000/mo (RM 10.47 psf), gross yield ~4.49% — at v2.0.1 anchor 4.5-5.0% boundary. Local net yield ~2.7%, spread −1.9%; foreign 30% non-resident tax-after net yield ~1.7%, spread −3.4%. Studio 549 sqft is KLCC Airbnb golden size, can combine with Paradox Hotel same-master service to form hybrid short-stay model (hotel residence-style operation). Tenant pool: KLCC tourists (Airbnb short-let) + multinational finance senior expats + Twin Towers business. Paradox Hotel real international brand (vs Armani Hallson "Armani" brand ambiguity) provides brand credibility supporting both long-let and short-let pricing. Watch: (1) Mgmt fee RM 0.80 psf KLCC luxury highest materially pressures net yield; (2) Off-plan VP 2029 post-completion 1-2 yr rental ramp; (3) Same-master 857-unit post-completion KLCC SA cluster competition.

On holding structure: Freehold + commercial SA core: (1) Freehold permanent title is long-term anchor (vs Leasehold 99 discount); (2) Commercial SA structure means 80-85% LTV (vs residential 90%), commercial utilities may apply (1.3-1.6x), HDA Schedule H scope variance; (3) EPF Account 2 withdrawal and MM2H "residential property" definition applicability for commercial SA requires individual confirmation. Key things to watch: (1) Off-plan VP 2029 3+ yr wait — TA Global construction progress + Paradox integration execution + JMB handover quality are core watch items; (2) Commercial SA structure: LTV 80-85% + commercial utilities 1.3-1.6x + HDA scope + EPF + MM2H applicability requires individual confirmation; (3) Mgmt fee RM 0.80 psf KLCC luxury highest materially affects net yield (annual RM 8,256 × 10 = RM 83k); (4) Tower 1 + Tower 2 simultaneous ramping + Paradox Hotel same-master impact: Tower 1 private residences vs Tower 2 service apartments distinction, but shared facilities and services mechanism needs SPA confirmation; (5) TA Global's ability to maintain Paradox international brand long-term management is continuity watch item; (6) KL SA overhang 3,643 overall pressure still impacts RM 2M+ segment; (7) Tower 1 Paradox Hotel commercial operations may impose same-master internal competition controls on Tower 2 service apartment Airbnb / short-let policies.

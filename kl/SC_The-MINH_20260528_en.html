◆ Deep dive

Project deep dive

The MINH sits on Jalan Kiara 7 in Mont Kiara — a 6.2-acre freehold parcel adjacent to the Bukit Kiara Hill nature reserve. Mont Kiara is KL's top expat enclave, progressively developed since 1996 by UEM Sunrise (formerly Sunrise Bhd). Thirty years have produced a complete lifestyle ecosystem: four major international schools (MKIS, Garden, French, IGB), 1 Mont Kiara / Plaza Mont Kiara / Solaris Dutamas retail clusters, and multiple international hospitals. The MINH is UEM Sunrise's latest MK flagship (launched May 2023). The name "MINH" stands for "Mont'Kiara Indochine-inspired Nature Homes," with "minh" meaning "bright" in Vietnamese. The largest structural gap remains MK-wide: no existing MRT and MRT3 Mont Kiara station only opens 2032 (construction 2027); the next 6 years are car / Grab dependent. The MINH's adjacency to Bukit Kiara Hill gives it a "greenery and seclusion" micro-premium that is rare within the district.

The development is 6.2 acres with 2 towers (Tower A 41 storeys + Tower B 42 storeys) totalling 496 units at 6 units/floor (low density), GDV RM 979M. The unit mix: Type A 1,607 sqft (bungalow concept, 3BR), Type B 2,076 sqft (bungalow concept), Type C 2,733 sqft (semi-detached + private lift + lanai + junior suite), Type D 3,010 sqft (large semi-detached). All layouts are 3BR+ — no studio / 1BR / 2BR options — clearly positioned as MK premium family residential. Each unit gets 4 parking lots. The design fuses French Colonial and Vietnamese vernacular into an Indochine style, certified GreenRE for sustainability. Amenities include a hydrotherapy spa hut, resort-style cascading pool, multi-generational playground, EV charger-ready infrastructure, automated waste collection system, and private lift lobbies — meaningfully differentiated for an MK flagship.

Sponsor UEM Sunrise Berhad (Bursa Code 5148) is Mont Kiara's original developer, with 30 years of MK delivery history: 28 Mont Kiara (2020 completion, Brickz RM 774 psf), Arcoris Soho, Mont Kiara Damai, Mont Kiara Astana, Mont Kiara Aman, Mont Kiara Pelangi — all delivered on time. Parent company is UEM Group (controlled by Khazanah Nasional, the national investment fund), giving Bursa T1 financial transparency. Tower B's May 2023 soft launch (Tresorians preview) achieved 70% take-up — a clear demand-side validation signal. 2026-2027 is the Tower B VP completion window; Tower A remains in sales. Project SPV is Laser Tower Sdn Bhd, a UEM Sunrise subsidiary.

Pricing: UEM Sunrise's official launch range is RM 872-891 psf, weighted average approximately RM 880 psf. Market validation references: Mont Kiara overall Brickz median RM 808 psf (Feb 2025-Mar 2026, 627 transactions), The MINH +9% premium over MK aggregate reflects a reasonable new-build + Indochine design + GreenRE premium; against same-sponsor adjacent 28 Mont Kiara (Brickz RM 774 psf), +14% premium remains within acceptable range (7-year age difference + Indochine thematic premium). The MINH itself has 0 Brickz transactions (no samples pre-VP 2027); secondary-market pricing calibration should wait until 1-2 years post-VP for market samples to accumulate. Early subsale listings appearing at ~RM 841 psf suggest some early investors willing to exit near launch PSF — typical for MK new builds.

Rental market: for the 1,607 sqft Type A 3BR representative unit, monthly rent is estimated at RM 7,500 (28 Mont Kiara and Kiaramas Danai similar-size range RM 5,500-9,500 as anchor, The MINH carrying a 1.2-1.3× new-build Indochine premium). Gross yield is approximately 6.4%, clearing the v2.0.1 5.5% high-yield calibration threshold; Malaysian net yield ~4.2% is close to break-even with a small −0.4% spread; foreign net yield after 30% non-resident tax is ~3.3% with spread −1.8%. Three drivers: (1) freehold residential title = residential electricity tariffs (no commercial 1.3-1.6× premium); (2) MK expat-corporate 2-year+ leases keep vacancy low; (3) large-format 3BR matches expat-family and local HNW owner-occupier demand; (4) UEM Sunrise brand + Indochine design justify a rental premium. However, the negative spreads mean returns still depend on capital appreciation, not pure cash flow.

Holding structure: freehold residential title (non-commercial-SA) + HDA Schedule H protection + residential 90% LTV + residential electricity tariffs together create a structurally low holding-friction regime. MM2H asset eligibility: Type A entry at 1,607 sqft × RM 880 psf = RM 1.41M clears the RM 1M Federal Territory threshold comfortably ✓. The 1-year off-plan wait (VP 2027) is time friction, but UEM Sunrise's 30-year MK delivery record keeps execution risk low; Tower B's 70% take-up confirms the demand-side validation. On exit liquidity, The MINH's 0 Brickz transactions is normal pre-VP; post-VP 1-2 years will establish the secondary-market depth. MK aggregate secondary market is active (627 trans/year), and The MINH should normalise to typical turnover after VP. 10-year exit expectations: at base 3% pa appreciation, Year 10 price ≈ RM 1.90M, Malaysian net P&L approximately +RM 518K (IRR 5-7%), Foreign +RM 317K (IRR 4-6%).

Things to note: (1) no MRT and MRT3 Mont Kiara station only opens 2032 — long-dated catalyst; (2) large-format from 1,607 sqft means each ticket is RM 1.41M+, narrower buyer pool vs small-format SA investor products; (3) same-period MK competition (Kiaramas deDaun 2028 + Papyrus Dutamas 2027 + cumulative MK stock) creates rental market competition; (4) Tower B 70% take-up is at soft-launch stage; remaining 30% + Tower A sales progress needs monitoring; (5) early subsale listings ~RM 841 psf (slightly below launch RM 880) reflect some investor turnover pressure — buyers can use this for negotiation but should verify Tower / floor; (6) UEM Sunrise is Khazanah-controlled Bursa-listed (transparent financials) but still subject to KL property cycle.

10-year scenario baseline: Assuming entry May 2026, exit May 2036:
  • Base case: MRT3 opens on time 2032, MK holds YoY +3-4%, rent +3% pa, vacancy 1 month/yr. Malaysian 10-year net ~RM 518K, IRR ~5-7%; Foreign ~RM 317K, IRR ~4-6%.
  • Bull case: MRT3 opens early + MK international school enrolment rebounds + price +5% pa + MM2H qualifications fully convert. Malaysian IRR ~8-9% (RM 911K+), Foreign IRR ~6-7% (RM 672K+).
  • Bear case: MRT3 slips to 2034+, same-period MK supply stretches vacancy to 2-3 months/yr, price 0% pa. Malaysian IRR approaches 0% (10-year small profit RM 44K), Foreign IRR closer to −2% (10-year loss RM 112K) given 8% stamp duty + 30% rental tax + permanent 10% RPGT.
10-year exit liquidity: Post-VP 2027, 1-2 years for Brickz sample accumulation; expected listing-to-sale 3-6 months, list-to-sale discount 5-8%. MK aggregate active secondary market (627 transactions/year) provides liquidity support.

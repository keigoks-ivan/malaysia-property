Foreign purchase threshold note: The minimum foreign purchase price in KL (Federal Territory) is RM 1,000,000. Most Sunway Velocity 3 units sit in the RM 560,000–900,000 range (Type A 2BR 721 sqft entry ~RM 580,000), so any unit below RM 1M cannot be bought by foreigners; foreign buyers are limited to the high-floor, larger units in Tower A (up to ~RM 1,279,000) / Tower B (up to ~RM 1,230,000). This threshold not only restricts which units foreigners may enter, it also means a foreign owner can only exit to "another foreigner (≥RM1M) or a local buyer" — structurally narrowing the foreign secondary pool. The foreign columns in this scorecard (including the 10-year cost table) use a representative 2BR unit for cross-development standardization, but readers should note: that representative unit is below the foreign threshold, so a foreign buyer's actual entry price and PSF will be higher than the representative shown.

◆ DEEP DIVE

Project Deep Summary

Sunway Velocity 3 sits in Cheras Maluri (Jalan Peel) and is the third phase of Sunway Property's (Sunway Berhad, Bursa:5211) Sunway Velocity integrated township, with total GDV of about RM 1.8 billion. Plot A (3.43 acres) carries Tower A (61 storeys, 1,036 units) + Tower B (61 storeys, 568 units) for 1,604 serviced-residence units, with Plot B adding Tower C; the project previewed in July 2024 reaching about 60% take-up at launch, with VP expected in 2028. The unit mix spans 2BR / 3BR / 3+1 at 721-1,076 sqft, built around a "co-living-ready" concept — each bedroom has a smart door lock and independent sub-metering, targeting KL's young professionals, working adults and small families for shared / room-by-room renting; all bedrooms face outward and the design includes accessibility ramps.

On location, Cheras / Maluri is "other KL" baseline 6.0 — a mature mixed urban district with moderate land scarcity, high density and many competing launches, ranking below KLCC (8.5) / Damansara Heights (8.0) / Mont Kiara (7.5) / Bangsar (7.2). Sunway Velocity 3's locational edge is not "irreplaceable land" but "the self-contained township ecosystem": within the same Velocity precinct Sunway owns and operates Sunway Velocity Mall (1M+ sqft regional mall), Sunway Medical Centre Velocity (on-site private hospital) and Sunway Velocity Hotel, plus the three residential phases V Residence (SV1) / Sunway Velocity TWO / SV3 — a rare Cheras "mall + hospital + hotel + residences" one-stop ecosystem. This integration is the only irreplaceable plus (+0.3), but the three-layer supply-side audit fails on all counts — land is redevelopable (the developer keeps launching Velocity 1/2/3 itself) −0.5, the product is endlessly replicable, and the leasehold commercial SA has no cap −0.3 — netting a location score of 5.5, the lowest among scorecards, honestly reflecting both the replaceability of Cheras land itself and the absence of a supply-side moat.

The project's real strengths are connectivity and developer. Connectivity 8.0: Maluri is a dual-line interchange of the MRT1 Kajang Line (underground) and the LRT Ampang Line (elevated), with the township linked by a covered pedestrian bridge (~400m; Entrance C 2-minute in-station transfer between the two lines) — one of KL's few "MRT + LRT dual-line + direct large mall + on-site private hospital" locations; Cochrane MRT is also within an 861m walk, and KLCC is ~5km away (10-15 min via Jalan Tun Razak / SMART Tunnel). It is held at 8.0 (not higher) because Cheras's international-school cluster is weaker and Velocity Mall is a mid-market regional mall (not Pavilion / IGB luxury tier). Developer & handover 8.0: Sunway Berhad is a Bursa Tier 1 vertically-integrated conglomerate (property + construction Suncon + healthcare Sunway Medical + education Sunway University + malls + REIT) that owns and operates the mall, hospital, hotel and property-management arm inside the same township — landowner/operator interests are highly aligned with long-term precinct value; it has delivered V Residence and Sunway Velocity TWO with no abandonment record, and parent financials are solid (1QFY26 property PBT +52%). This places the developer score in the top tier among scorecards (on par with The Conlay / Park Place, behind only ParkCity × CapitaLand 9.0).

Pricing and valuation are the positive surprise. The representative 2BR 721 sqft mid-floor is ~RM 680,000 (≈ RM 943 psf; entry Type A 2BR 721 sqft ~RM 580,000, ≈ RM 804 psf). Against the same-precinct, same-type "real transaction" anchor — Sunway Velocity TWO (also leasehold commercial SA within the township, completed) 2BR 678 sqft resale median RM 1,050 psf — SV3's new product is roughly a −10% discount; even against the blended Velocity Brickz median RM 836 psf (which includes the older freehold V Residence) it is only +13% (a reasonable new-build premium). Pricing a new launch below the developer's own completed same-type resale is relatively restrained (peer-aggressiveness +0.3), hence Entry Valuation Discipline scores 6.5 (confidence: medium — SV Two is a good same-type anchor, but the representative mid-floor price is estimated). Buyers should still note actual transactions may be 5-10% lower via early-bird discounts, furniture packages or legal-fee waivers; confirm the final net price with Sunway sales in writing.

On rent and cashflow, this is a "yield + connectivity" asset rather than a "capital appreciation" play. The representative 2BR is estimated at RM 3,000/mo whole-unit (≈ RM 4.16 psf), gross ~5.3%, within the 5.0-5.5% anchor band and upper-mid among scorecards; the co-living room-by-room design (independent sub-metering per room) offers a yield-amplification option — potentially RM 1,700-1,900/room × 2 = RM 3,400-3,800/mo — but the JMB room-letting policy must be confirmed. The Maluri MRT/LRT dual lines + mall + on-site hospital drive deep tenant demand (students, young professionals, commuters) and strong occupancy resilience. Malaysian holding cashflow scores 6.0, foreign 4.0 (30% non-resident rental tax). Key things to note (by importance): ① the RM 1M foreign threshold is make-or-break for foreign buyers — most sub-RM1M units are off-limits, foreign entry is confined to high-floor larger units at higher PSF and absolute cost, and the resale pool is narrow; ② commercial SA leasehold structure: LTV 80-85%, commercial electricity tariff possibly 1.3-1.6×, EPF Account 2 withdrawal / MM2H "residential property" eligibility to be confirmed with banks / the MM2H Centre individually; ③ SV3's three towers (A/B/C) + existing township units create internal supply that may keep pressure on long-term PSF and rents; ④ appreciation is muted (weak realized upside in the prime Velocity precinct), so 10-year returns center on yield + the MRT3 catalyst rather than price gains; ⑤ off-plan VP ~2028 (~2-year wait) + the JMB co-living room-letting policy must be confirmed (the room-by-room yield thesis depends on whether the management body permits it); ⑥ gross 5.3% is decent but both Malaysian and foreign carry are negative — leveraged holding needs an independent source of positive carry; ⑦ 60% launch take-up reflects affordable-tier demand but there is still room to negotiate — confirm the final net price in writing.

◆ Deep Narrative

Project Deep Summary

TRX Residences sits at Jalan Tun Razak 55100 Kuala Lumpur, the flagship residential project within the 70-acre master plan of Tun Razak Exchange (Malaysia's first international financial district). The development is a JV between Lendlease (ASX:LLC, Australian global integrated real estate group founded 1958, with notable projects like Sydney Barangaroo, London Elephant Park, Milan MIND) holding 60% and TRX City Sdn Bhd (wholly-owned subsidiary of Malaysia's Ministry of Finance) holding 40%. The full master plan envisions 6 residential towers; Phase 1 comprises Tower A (53 storeys, 443 units) + Tower B (57 storeys, 453 units), totaling 896 units, topped out September 2023 and delivered Q1 2024, with 90% sold. Phase 2-6 (4 remaining towers) timing and planning have not been announced — this uncertainty is directly affected by Lendlease's May 2024 strategy update announcing its full exit from international property development. Whether MOF takes over solo, brings in a new partner, or adjusts strategy is the key watch item for long-term holders.

On location: TRX district anchors at 7.0 baseline in v2.0.1 anchor table ("emerging CBD, not fully mature; MRT in place"), with TRX Residences earning project-level integration adjustments: TRX MRT 2-line interchange 70m walk +0.5 (Kajang Line + Putrajaya Line transfer at the same station, Malaysia's largest underground interchange, operational 2017), The Exchange TRX 500-shop mall direct walk +0.3 (120,773 sqm NLA, opened 2023-11-29), 10-acre TRX City Park integration +0.2, totaling 8.0. This location score is on par with Desa ParkCity 8.0 and Pavilion Damansara Heights 8.0, just below KLCC Conlay 8.3 (30-year established + Pavilion walking dual-anchor). TRX represents KL's strongest current transit-oriented integration (2 MRT lines + direct mall + central park + Kimpton Hotel + Exchange 106), but the financial CBD is still in tenant ramping (Exchange 106 occupancy, multinational financial tenant timing). SMART Tunnel + MEX + SPE expressway network coverage adds commuting layer.

Phase 1 project scale: 896 units. Density is high, but the master plan's mixed-use design integrating mall + park + hotel + offices is TRX's differentiation core. Unit menu spans 1BR 474 sqft entry (RM 960,000) to 3BR 1,636 sqft, covering investment-light users (1BR), corporate expats (2BR), and family residences (3BR). The 2BR 850 sqft is the "investment sweet-spot unit" and matches this scorecard's v2.0 cross-project standard representative unit. GBI Gold + LEED dual green certification is relatively rare among KL serviced residences and offers 20-30% lower operational energy vs standard, reducing long-term management fee and utility pressure. Lendlease global integrated service standards (24h concierge, housekeeping) and ASX group international hospitality experience are brand differentiators — but Lendlease's May 2024 international exit means long-term service standard maintenance becomes MOF and successor partners' responsibility. Buyers should track JMB handover quality + management company continuity.

On the developer side: Lendlease is an ASX-listed Australian global integrated real estate group founded 1958, market cap USD $4B+ (2025), FY25 OPAT $386M AUD, with major projects including Sydney Barangaroo, Melbourne Docklands, London Elephant Park, Milan MIND. In v2.0.1 anchor it qualifies for SGX/HKEx/Tokyo-tier global institutional 8.5 baseline. TRX City Sdn Bhd as MOF wholly-owned subsidiary provides government institutional structural endorsement (+0.3). But Lendlease's May 2024 strategy update announcing full exit from international property development (including Malaysia) −1.0 is the core caveat. Buyers should evaluate: (1) Phase 1 delivered 896 units still under Lendlease warranty; (2) Phase 2-6 remaining 4 towers' development mode (MOF solo, new partner, or pause); (3) long-term JMB / property management Lendlease standard continuity. MOF as master developer and 40% shareholder provides permanent anchor, but Lendlease international brand long-term commitment is gray area. Final developer score 7.5 (baseline 8.5 − Lendlease exit 1.0 − JMB short 0.2 + MOF JV 0.3) — slightly below The Conlay's E&O × Mitsui Fudosan JV 8.0; main differentiation is Lendlease exit's downward adjustment on future continuity.

On pricing: TRX Residences' Brickz sample is very mature: Residensi TRX 1 accumulated 59 transactions during 2024-04 to 2025-03 (12 months post-VP), median RM 2,293 psf / median total RM 1,644,000 (sample mostly 1BR and small 2BR, reflecting entry buyer composition). Vs 2024 launch RM 2,200 psf, 12-month PSF +4% is early appreciation validation — strong performance among newly-completed KL SA, reflecting TRX integrated ecosystem partial market acceptance. Current asking range RM 2,200-2,400 psf is at-market with Brickz median, limited negotiation. Comparators: Core Residence @ TRX (leasehold 99-yr neighbor) RM 1,500-2,000 psf reflects TRX Residences' freehold + Lendlease brand 25% premium; vs KLCC luxury cluster (Four Seasons RM 3,010, Pavilion Suites RM 3,150) TRX 25-30% discount reflects KLCC 30-yr maturity vs TRX emerging positioning. Entry price 7.0 (Brickz 50+ depth baseline) reflects mature secondary market price transparency.

On the rental market: 2BR 850 sqft fully furnished long-let estimated at RM 8,000/mo (RM 9.41 psf) is current PropertyGuru/iProperty midpoint (2BR overall RM 8.24-10.98 psf mid-range), 1BR 700 sqft RM 5,800/mo (RM 8.29 psf) is entry unit reference. Vs 2BR 850 sqft @ RM 1.95M gross yield ~4.92% — within 4.5-5.0% anchor band, comparable to The Conlay (4.90%), Pavilion Hilltop (5.08%). Local net yield ~3.0%, spread −1.6%; foreign 30% non-resident tax-after net yield ~2.0%, spread −3.0% — both negative spread, capital gain dependency. TRX Residences' core tenant pool is TRX financial district corporate expats: Exchange 106 (KL's tallest at 106 storeys) with Prudential, HSBC, Affin Bank, Touch 'n Go and other financial institutions, multinational financial professionals as stable long-let pool; Kimpton Hotel service spillover + Exchange TRX mall tourism as Airbnb/short-let potential upside (subject to JMB policy). Phase 1 completed 14 months ago; rental market still ramping. Stabilization in 2-3 years may see 0.2-0.5% yield uplift (with corporate demand maturity).

On holding structure: Freehold residential title (Serviced Residence but classified residential) + HDA Schedule H protection + residential 90% LTV + residential utilities — low institutional holding friction (vs commercial-titled SA 70-85% LTV + commercial utilities 1.3-1.6x). MM2H mandatory asset: 2BR 850 sqft × RM 1.95M > RM 1M Federal Territory threshold ✓ clears, and RM 1.95M is relatively light entry for MM2H Silver holders. 10-yr exit outlook: under base 3%/yr growth, Year 10 price ~RM 2.63M, local net P&L ~RM 547k (IRR 4-5%), foreign ~RM 308k (IRR 2-3.5%). Exit buyer pool: Brickz 59 tx/yr (896 units = 6.6% annual turnover, above Park Regent 1.43%, R22 5%, Hilltop 3.4%) — most active secondary among KL completed projects, meaning extremely strong exit liquidity, but also more seller competition; TRX cluster foreign buyer 30-40% + Lendlease ASX international brand recognition are key exit supports.

Key things to watch: (1) Lendlease May 2024 international exit is the core caveat — Phase 2-6 development mode + long-term JMB / property management continuity is the main watch item; track MOF subsequent announcements and new partner selection. (2) Phase 2-6 same-project 4 future towers' launch PSF potential comparison effect on Phase 1 secondary prices (if Phase 2 launch < Phase 1 secondary PSF, Phase 1 prices may be pressured). (3) Brickz 59 tx/yr 6.6% annual turnover reflects active seller competition; buyers have 5-10% negotiation space. (4) Mgmt fee RM 0.55-0.70 psf materially affects net yield; confirm in writing with JMB on latest rates + GBI/LEED energy savings benefit. (5) TRX financial district tenant ramping ongoing; Exchange 106 occupancy + multinational financial timing are 2-3-year stabilization variables. (6) KL SA overhang 3,643 units (NAPIC Q3 2025) overall pressure mostly concentrated below RM 1M, but TRX RM 1.95M tier buyer market-cycle sensitivity still warrants tracking.

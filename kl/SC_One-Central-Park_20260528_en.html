◆ Deep dive

Project deep dive

One Central Park sits on Jalan Residen 2 inside Desa ParkCity, immediately adjacent to the 70-acre Central Park. OCP is the only "Parkfront condo" within DPC — a 1.8-acre private park connects directly to the central lake — a micro-location that cannot be replicated within DPC's 473-acre integrated community. Desa ParkCity itself is KL's only single-master-developer planned community: Perdana ParkCity (parent Samling Strategic Group, founded 1977) has progressively developed all 473 acres since 1995, integrating a 70-acre Central Park, the ParkCity Medical Centre (Perdana-owned, a rarity in KL private estates), International School @ ParkCity, Plaza Arkadia and The Waterfront retail clusters — forming KL's only "no-Grab-needed" self-contained lifestyle ecosystem. OCP residents walk to schools, hospitals, malls, restaurants, parks and the ParkCity Club; this level of walking integration is rare in KL. The structural gap: no existing MRT and no MRT3 coverage — car commute is the DPC default, 25 minutes to KLCC, a norm for owner-occupiers and a liquidity dampener for pure investors.

The development is a single 45-storey tower with 359 units on a 4.08-acre parcel, density 88 units/acre (low). The unit mix spans Type A-H (8 layouts): 2BR 1,034-1,303 sqft (entry), 3BR 1,377-1,743 sqft (dominant family cohort), 3+1BR 1,722-3,153 sqft (large-format luxury). Amenities include private park access to Central Park, sky pool, Sky Lounge, gym and 24-hour concierge; the absence of any studio layout reflects the family-residential positioning. 2013 launch price RM 800-850 psf fully sold, on-time VP January 2017, now in its 9th year of operation with JMB / MC transition long complete — buyers can physically inspect management quality and community operations directly. For mature secondary buyers, this is the "seeing is believing" stage.

Sponsor Perdana ParkCity Sdn Bhd is the DPC 473-acre master developer with 30+ years of DPC track record and 27 completed projects to date, zero abandonments. Parent Samling Strategic Group was founded 1977. Perdana is privately held (not listed on Bursa), so quarterly financial transparency is structurally weaker than Park Regent's CapitaLand JV which provides SGX-listed disclosure. Perdana publicly maintains a "no discount sales" policy — an unusual industry stance that signals long-term DPC owner confidence and prevents fire-sale pressure, but also means buyer negotiation room is structurally narrow (do not expect 5-15% rebates). DPC community management consistently receives top-tier rankings within KL private estates, covering the ParkCity Club, Central Park maintenance and community security — translating directly into the daily experience of the "self-contained lifestyle" for OCP owners.

Pricing: Brickz 18 transactions Feb 2025-Jan 2026 cumulative median RM 1,219 psf (median price RM 1.595M) is an 11-month mature-market observation sample. By size band: 2BR 1,303 sqft entry RM 1,180 psf, dominant 3BR 1,377 sqft RM 1,230 psf (iProperty confirms as "most popular layout"), 3BR 1,700 sqft high-floor RM 1,400 psf. Against the DPC overall median RM 1,001 psf, the ~22% premium reflects the "DPC's only-Parkfront" micro-location pricing and is reasonable; against 2013 launch RM 800-850 psf, this is +50% over 12 years already realised (+3.4%/yr average) — lower than Park Regent's 2023-completion +40% in 5 years (+7%/yr post-VP uplift) but typical for a mature asset's normalised growth curve. The Park Regent vs OCP contrast gives buyers a clear choice: post-VP velocity vs mature stability.

Rental market: for the 1,377 sqft 3BR representative unit, monthly rent is estimated at RM 6,500 (v1.3 DD 3BR 1,412-1,743 sqft range RM 6,500-9,000 as anchor, taking the lower bound for 1,377 sqft); DPC general 2BR rents RM 5,000-7,000/mo. Gross yield is approximately 4.61%, close to but below the v2.0 5% calibration baseline, slightly above Park Regent's 4.26% because OCP's entry PSF is 21% lower and the rental premium is smaller. Malaysian net yield is approximately 3.4% with spread −1.2%; foreign net yield after 30% non-resident tax is approximately 2.6% with spread −2.5% — meaningfully negative but milder than Park Regent (−2.8%). The tenant base — DPC expat families, corporate-posted executives, local middle-class owner-occupiers — typically signs 2-year+ corporate leases, keeping vacancy stable. OCP commands the "Parkfront view" premium primarily in PSF rather than monthly rent (owners pay premium for view; renters typically pay premium for location).

Holding structure: freehold residential title + HDA Schedule H protection + residential 90% LTV + residential electricity tariffs together create a structurally low holding-friction regime. MM2H asset eligibility: the 2BR smallest at 1,034 sqft × RM 1,180 psf ≈ RM 1.22M clears the RM 1M Federal Territory threshold ✓. Completion 9 years ago means buyers can physically inspect management quality, verify JMB transparency and check community operations — zero execution risk, zero defect-period negotiations. On exit liquidity, Brickz 1.5 transactions/month on 359 units = 5% annual turnover — a mature, healthy benchmark (below Park Regent's 14% annual turnover from new-build heat, but comfortably above a "frozen market"); iProperty 113 listings / 359 units = 31% listing rate is typical for mature secondary markets (vs 60% listing rates that signal price-discovery in large luxury). 10-year exit expectations: at base 3% pa appreciation, Year 10 price ≈ RM 2.28M, Malaysian net P&L approximately +RM 402K (IRR 4-5%), Foreign +RM 213K (IRR 3-4%).

Things to note: (1) no MRT and no MRT3 coverage is a DPC-wide condition, car commute is the long-term default; (2) gross 4.61% is close to but below the 5% baseline — large-format luxury is inherently yield-compressed; (3) Perdana's "no discount" policy means buyer negotiation room is structurally narrow; (4) Brickz 18 transactions range RM 1.18M-2.45M shows wide variation by unit size — buyers should anchor to the appropriate size-band PSF; (5) OCP is now an 8-year mature secondary market with normalised growth at +3.4%/yr (no longer enjoys post-VP uplift); future growth depends on DPC-wide +6.06% PSF growth continuing; (6) Perdana is privately held, so parent-company financial transparency is structurally weaker than Park Regent's CapitaLand JV.

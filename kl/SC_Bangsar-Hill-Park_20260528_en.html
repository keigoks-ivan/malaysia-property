◆ Deep Narrative

Project Deep Summary

Bangsar Hill Park sits on Lorong Maarof (Bangsar Utama, the core of Bangsar), developed by Bangsar Hill Park Development Sdn Bhd — the project SPV owned by Sunsuria Bhd (Bursa:5135) and MKH Bhd (Bursa:6114). The full master plan envisions residential + retail + commercial, rolled out in multiple blocks: Block D + Block E (totaling 812 units across a 57-storey tower) delivered March 2025 with 90% sold; Talisa Phase 2 (Block B + C) is future development with VP estimated January 2028. Block D/E unit menu: 2BR 917-1,000 sqft / 3BR 1,100-1,300 sqft / 4BR 1,478 sqft, pricing RM 835,000-1,599,000, corresponding to PSF RM 997-1,168. This scorecard uses 2BR 970 sqft @ RM 1,050 psf = RM 1,019,000 as cross-project standard representative unit.

On location: Bangsar district anchors at 7.0 baseline in v2.0.1 anchor table (freehold residential area, MRT/KTM coverage, but density high as common trait). BHP earns project-level adjustments: Bank Rakyat-Bangsar LRT station 600m walk +0.2 (KJ16 station, Kelana Jaya Line, operational 1999, mature) + Bangsar mature lifestyle hub (Bangsar Shopping Centre, Bangsar Village, Telawi/Lucky Garden F&B circle, KL Sentral 5-min drive), totaling 7.2. Bangsar is KL's mature lifestyle circle for local middle class and foreign relocators, covering local professionals, KL Sentral commuters, and an alternative foreign-preferred area outside Mont Kiara — but high density and frequent traffic congestion are common traits. MRT3 Circle Line completion 2032 is long-term pipeline but does not directly cover BHP micro-location. Notably, the 57-storey tower is a structural shift in Bangsar's historically low-density residential culture (Bangsar was traditionally bungalow + low-rise condo). This density-perception divergence may affect long-term acceptance among local buyers.

Project scale: Block D/E totals 812 units across 57 storeys, one of Bangsar's tallest, reflecting Bangsar's recent shift toward high-rise. Talisa Phase 2 (B/C) VP January 2028 will further increase same-project internal supply pressure, which is the core watch item for Block D/E secondary price comparison effect. Facilities are mid-luxury residential standard (swimming pool, gym, 24h security, basement parking, resort-style facilities), with integrated retail and commercial within master plan. The 5% Bumi discount is a Bumi-buyer-exclusive mechanism (non-Bumi buyers don't get this discount); units associated with 5% Bumi discount are typically locked to Bumi resale on secondary, which affects non-Bumi holders' exit buyer-pool breadth — a structural nuance in Bangsar / KL urban projects.

On the developer side: Sunsuria Bhd × MKH Bhd is a Bursa T2-tier developer dual-equity 6.5 baseline. Sunsuria's reps include Sunsuria City (Selangor Salak Tinggi), Suria Setapak, Suria Putra — mid-tier developer, 2024 revenue RM 200M+; MKH Bhd is a mixed group: plantation (oil palm) + property + commercial mall, 2024 revenue RM 1.6B+. Both have 10+ project delivery records, but neither belongs to Bursa T1 flagships like Pavilion / IGB / SP Setia / KLCC Property. Block D/E completed 2025-03, JMB only ~12 months operational, ≥2-yr defect liability not yet validated. Buyers should track: (1) both parent companies' financial transparency + JMB handover quality; (2) Talisa Phase 2's ability to maintain Block D/E execution standards; (3) management company continuity + facility maintenance level. Overall developer score is relatively weaker among scorecards analyzed (vs Pavilion / Mitsui × E&O / Lendlease × MOF / Perdana / UEM Sunrise higher-scored sponsors).

On pricing: Block D/E pricing PSF RM 997-1,168 vs Bangsar overall Brickz median RM 765 psf (229 tx 2024-04 to 2025-03) is a 30-50% premium, but this comparison is unfair — Bangsar Brickz sample includes many older low-density condos and landed bungalows that drag down median. Comparing same-tier new launches: Sri Penaga (Bangsar high-end luxury anchor) Brickz 11 tx median RM 975 psf is at-parity with BHP; Cascadium RM 866 psf, Suasana Bangsar RM 658 psf are mid-tier. BHP overall positioning is the high-end tier of Bangsar new launches. Block D/E 90% sold reflects market demand resilience for Bangsar new launches, but Brickz sample still <10 tx (no depth) — PSF transparency relies on Bangsar cluster comparisons. Buyers should track Phase 2 launch PSF's effect on Block D/E secondary PSF — if Phase 2 launch > D/E secondary, D/E holders benefit; conversely, they may face pressure.

On the rental market: Bangsar overall gross yield 4-5% (rates.my, bambooroutes 2026 forecast). BHP 2BR 970 sqft fully furnished long-let estimated at RM 4,500/mo (RM 4.64 psf), corresponding gross yield ~5.30% — within v2.0.1 anchor table 5.0-5.5% band. Local net yield ~3.5%, spread −1.1%; foreign 30% non-resident tax-after net yield ~2.5%, spread −2.6% — both negative spread but local's is smaller. Bangsar's core tenant pool: local professionals (KL Sentral commute, Bangsar/Mid Valley business workers), KLIA commuters, foreign families and single expats preferring Bangsar over Mont Kiara. Bangsar's foreign recognition is lower than MK / KLCC (foreign ecosystem not as mature as MK), but it fits well with local middle class + international middle-tier expats seeking city + lifestyle balance. Early ramp rental still stabilizing; 2-3 year stabilization may bring 0.2-0.3% yield uplift.

On holding structure: Leasehold 99-yr (~94 yrs remaining) residential title is BHP's most significant structural caveat — v2.0.1 anchor table's leasehold residential 6.5 baseline already embeds this discount, but long-term holders should evaluate: (1) each 10-yr leasehold residual implies -0.5% to -1% annual discount to price; (2) renewal procedure (typically must apply within 30 years remaining); (3) next-generation inheritance acceptance of leasehold term. HDA Schedule H protection + residential 90% LTV + residential utilities — other institutional friction is low. MM2H mandatory asset: 2BR 970 sqft × RM 1.02M just clears RM 1M Federal Territory threshold ✓, but only just — for MM2H Gold/Platinum higher-threshold holders, less suitable. 10-yr exit outlook: under base 3%/yr growth, Year 10 price ~RM 1.37M, local net P&L ~RM 297k (IRR 3-4%), foreign ~RM 157k (IRR 1-2%). Exit buyer pool: Bangsar overall Brickz 229 tx/yr active, but BHP itself sample <10 + 5% Bumi units locked to Bumi resale — expected list-to-deal 4-8 months with 5-10% discount.

Key things to watch: (1) Leasehold 99-yr (94 yrs remaining) is core structural caveat; long-term hold requires evaluating each 10-yr implied discount. (2) Talisa Phase 2 (Block B/C) 2028 same-project internal supply + Phase 2 launch PSF potential comparison effect on Block D/E secondary. (3) 5% Bumi discount-tagged units locked to Bumi resale narrows non-Bumi holder's structural buyer pool. (4) Sunsuria × MKH JV is Bursa T2 mid-tier developer; long-term management + JMB handover standards vs Bursa T1 flagship sponsors require continued validation. (5) 57-storey tower in Bangsar's historically low-density residential culture is a structural shift; local buyers' long-term acceptance of high density is a watch item. (6) Mgmt fee RM 0.40-0.50 psf materially affects net yield; confirm in writing with JMB. (7) Bangsar's foreign recognition lower than MK / KLCC; foreign exit pool narrower than MK / KLCC same-tier projects.

  • Bull case: Bangsar lifestyle hub continued appreciation + MM2H sustained inflow + price +5%/yr. Local IRR ~6-7% (RM 580k+), Foreign IRR ~4-5% (RM 411k+).
  • Bear case: Talisa Phase 2 launch pressures D/E secondary + Bangsar overall high-density supply pressure + Leasehold discount deepens + price 0%/yr. Local IRR ~−1% (10-yr loss RM 45k), Foreign IRR ~−2.5% (10-yr loss RM 150k) due to 8% stamp + 30% rental tax + permanent RPGT.

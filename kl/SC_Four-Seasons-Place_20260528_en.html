◆ The Narrative

Project Deep-Dive

Four Seasons Place sits at 145 Jalan Ampang in the heart of KLCC — adjacent to the Petronas Towers and Suria KLCC. The 65-storey mixed-use tower houses 242 Four Seasons Private Residences, the 209-key Four Seasons Hotel, 27 serviced apartments and a five-storey 300,000 sqft luxury retail podium. The address is genuinely irreplaceable: a freehold large-parcel development of this scale at KLCC core is unlikely to be repeated over the next 10-20 years. Five-minute walk to KLCC LRT, twelve to Bukit Bintang MRT, five-minute drive to Prince Court Medical Centre, and several international schools (ISKL, Garden) sit within 5 km.

Project scale: 242 residential units, ranging from 1,098 sqft (1BR) to 7,039 sqft (5BR Duplex) — the typical large-unit positioning of a branded residence, targeting UHNW families and globally mobile executives. All units are fully furnished and benefit from Four Seasons hotel-grade service: 24-hour concierge, in-room dining, branded gym and pool access, airport transfers. Interior design by Yabu Pushelberg (New York-based) ensures brand consistency. No balconies — a typical design choice for branded residences.

The developer is Venus Assets Sdn Bhd, a private firm whose principals include the Sultan of Selangor (HRH Sultan Sharafuddin Idris Shah), Tan Sri Syed Yusof bin Tun Syed Nasir, and Singaporean tycoon Ong Beng Seng (a long-standing Four Seasons global partner). Four Seasons Place is their flagship project; it received three Asia Property Awards, including Best Hotel Development. Completed in 2017 (hotel opened 2018), the project has been operating stably for eight years with full JMB / MC handover, and brand service is maintained to Four Seasons global standards — the strongest long-term value-retention argument for this project.

On pricing: Brickz government-registered 2024-04 to 2025-03 records 27 transactions with a median PSF of RM 3,010 and median total of RM 10.5M (P25-P75 range RM 6.78M-11.46M). Current listing PSF spans RM 2,361-4,333. Same-area comparables: The Conlay (E&O × Mitsui Fudosan, no hotel brand) launches at RM 2,450 psf — comparable tenure but no hotel service premium; Stonor 3 (completed mature) sits at ~RM 1,800 psf as the KLCC mid-tier. Four Seasons Place's RM 3,010 median reflects the branded-residence premium in KLCC (~25-67% above non-branded same-area projects).

On the rental market: iProperty lists 92 rental units, with 1,345 sqft 2BR asking RM 13,500-15,000/mo — gross yield ~4.15%. Brickz secondary at 27 txns/yr makes this one of the most actively traded high-end KL projects on the government register. Tenant mix skews to corporate expat executives, embassy staff, and finance/legal/consulting professionals based in KLCC — high stability with 2+ year leases common. But maintenance at RM 1.20 psf (branded service tier) plus 30% non-resident tax compress foreign net yield to ~2.2% — rent alone cannot anchor 10-yr returns; the thesis must rest on sustained brand premium and KLCC location appreciation.

Hold-period notes: freehold residential is KL's best tenure structure — inheritable, no runoff, residential 90% LTV applies. Brickz 27 txns/yr makes secondary market the most liquid in the comparable set; KLCC's active foreign-buyer pool makes foreign resale relatively straightforward. The 10-year capital appreciation thesis rests on three legs: (1) Four Seasons global brand continues to deliver a price premium to KLCC residential (already 8 years of evidence); (2) MRT3 Circle Line completes by 2032, enhancing KLCC connectivity; (3) KL continues to internationalise with KLCC sustained as a multinational headquarters hub. The opportunity-cost breakeven of ~5%/yr (foreign) and ~3.7%/yr (local) is moderately ambitious for a mature KLCC location — clear catalysts are required.

◆ The Verdict

Highlights & Items to Note

✦ Highlights
  • KLCC flagship freehold residential — one of KL's most irreplaceable micro-locations, adjacent to the Petronas Towers with location scarcity and brand exclusivity in one parcel
  • Four Seasons global branded residence with hotel-level 24h concierge — the only project in KL operating at this brand tier (St. Regis has been transferred; Ritz-Carlton is outside KLCC core)
  • Brickz 27 txns/yr at median RM 10.5M — most liquid project in the comparable set with 8 years of transaction evidence
  • Completed 8 years + stable JMB operation + 3 Asia Property Awards: execution risk resolved
  • Stable tenant mix: corporate expat executives + embassy staff with 2+ year leases common
⚠ Items to note
  • Entry PSF RM 3,010 (median) is top of KL — 10-yr upside depends on sustained Four Seasons brand premium + KLCC catalysts (MRT3 2032)
  • Foreign opportunity-cost breakeven at ~5%/yr is steeper than other KL high-end (PavDH 4.5%, TS2 4.0%); recommend ≥10-yr hold horizon
  • Maintenance fee RM 1.20 psf (branded service tier) is among the highest in the set — 1,345 sqft = RM 1,614/mo management fee
  • Foreign stamp duty doubled to 8% from 4% in 2026-01 — RM 4M+ entry means an additional RM 160K cost for foreign buyers; time entry accordingly
  • Large 4-5BR Duplex units (≥ 3,000 sqft) have a narrower buyer pool — 10-yr exit liquidity weaker than 2BR/3BR mainstream

🏖Pied-à-Terre (Part-time, < 30% annual use)

Compromisable: Cash Flow (no rental or short-let by choice)
Project fit: One of the best fits in the comparable set — Four Seasons 24h service minimises the cost of leaving a unit empty, with on-arrival turnkey service (laundry, housekeeping, guest reception integrated with the hotel); absent periods can be partially filled via long/short-let to offset management fees. KLCC location is highly appealing for occasional KL visitors.
🌳Generational (20+ yr)
Lifeline dimensions: Location Moat (25%), Capital Appreciation (long-term anchor), Exit Liquidity (next-gen sale)
Compromisable: Short-term yield, short-term catalysts
Project fit: KL's most irreplaceable address + freehold + global branded residence is highly suited to generational holding. KLCC will remain KL's core 20+ years out; Four Seasons' global stability supports brand persistence; the next generation faces a ready international buyer pool. Best treated as the anchor of a cross-border KL family-asset allocation rather than held alone.

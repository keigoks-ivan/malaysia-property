◆ DEEP NARRATIVE

Project Deep Summary

28 Mont' Kiara (MK28) sits in Mont Kiara's core on Jalan Kiara, a 5.81-acre plot housing twin 40-storey towers with 460 units — this is a typical "2010s dense large-unit" MK design with unit types: 3+1BR/5BA 2,535-2,700 sqft, 4+1BR/5BA 3,000 sqft, 6 penthouses 5,790-6,465 sqft; no 1BR/2BR/smaller units, with positioning clearly for "large-unit owner-occupier + mid-to-large families" or "MK high-yield holders" — not suitable for "2BR investment entry" target buyers. MK broadly is KL's #1 expat residential enclave (30+ yrs mature), integrating 4 international schools (GIS, Mont Kiara International, Garden International, ISKL) + 1MK Mall + Plaza Mont Kiara + Mont Kiara Linkk and other retail/lifestyle cores, making it a mainstream choice for KL expat families. The structural gap remains: no existing MRT or MRT3 coverage — car commuting is the long-term norm.

MK28 is developed by UEM Sunrise Berhad (Bursa T1 listed, UEM Group GLC subsidiary) — a company that has been MK's master developer for 28+ years, with projects spanning 28MK, R22, The MINH and multiple launches all successfully delivered. Launched 2010 at average RM 721 psf, VP reached 2012 on schedule, currently 14 years completed with warranty long expired; JMB ≥ 5 yrs stable operation, UEM Sunrise parent 2024 quarterly reports stable with no major losses. Facilities include sky pool, gym, podium BBQ area, 24-hour security and other standard 2010s MK configuration; 14-yr aged building may enter maintenance cycle, with individual unit quality variance (floor, view, renovation status) larger than new launches. MK28's same-sponsor adjacent R22 (2017 completed, 33-unit boutique design) Brickz median ~RM 1,400 psf shows UEM Sunrise's new launches command significant premium — MK28's age + 460-unit density is the root cause of PSF discount.

On pricing: MK28 Brickz 28 transactions Oct 2024 to Sep 2025 cumulative sale median RM 787 psf (10-50 sample medium depth), median price RM 2.0M, P25 RM 1.70M, P75 RM 2.57M. Current iProperty asking PSF range RM 761-944 (average ~RM 850) vs Brickz median RM 787 close alignment. 2010 launch RM 721 psf → 2025 RM 787 = 14-yr +9.2% cumulative (0.65%/yr CAGR) is the weakest 13-yr appreciation among MK projects analyzed, reflecting the "aged MK high-density condo non-appreciation" common pattern. Versus MK overall median RM 900-1,400 psf (mixed old + new), MK28's RM 787 psf is aged condo discount ~30%. Brickz 12-mo 28-tx turnover = 6.1% annual rate (> KL healthy 3 tx/mo baseline), indicating secondary market sales velocity remains active — aged MK condos continue to attract yield-hunting buyers.

Rental market: with 2,535 sqft 3+1BR mid-floor as representative, monthly rent estimated at RM 10,800 (@ RM 4.26 psf), gross yield ~6.49% — the highest gross yield among MK projects analyzed (vs R22 4.2% / PavHT 5.08% / The MINH 6.4%). Anchor comes from MK 14-yr aged condo corresponding rent range RM 4-4.5 psf (vs new launches RM 5.5-6 psf), MK28 in the mid-band of 4-4.5 psf; MK broad 3BR avg RM 18,000+/mo (incl. new launches), MK28 reflects aged condo discount. Local 15% progressive tax: Net Yield ~5.4%, spread +0.5% (positive carry!) — the only project among MK analyzed where Local has positive carry, reflecting 14-yr aged condo's "asset discount + high yield" structural cashflow advantage; Foreign 30% non-resident tax: Net Yield ~4.4%, spread −0.6%. Tenant pool comprises MK expat families (international school routing), corporate expat assignments, local high-net-worth professionals; 2-yr+ corporate contracts stabilize occupancy, 1 mo/yr vacancy assumption reasonable.

Holding structure: Freehold residential title (not commercial SA) + HDA Schedule H protection + residential 90% LTV + residential electricity tariff — institutional friction is low. MM2H asset eligibility: 3+1BR @ RM 2.0M ≥ RM 1M Federal Territory threshold ✓ qualifying; 4+1BR / penthouse well exceeds. Completed 14 years means buyers can directly inspect, verify unit quality, confirm JMB operation — zero execution risk; but 14-yr aged building individual unit quality variance is large, individual renovation status determines negotiation room. Exit liquidity: Brickz 6.1% annual turnover is healthy level, buyer pool comprises local HNW, corporate expat residents, MM2H holders three groups providing diverse support; but 14-yr aged building less attractive to new-launch buyers (seeking new architecture design, new facilities) — versus R22 / The MINH and other new launches, MK28's target buyers are yield-focused not capital growth-focused. 10-year exit expectation: at base 3%/yr appreciation, Yr 10 price ~RM 2.68M, Local net P&L approximately +RM 728K (IRR 7-8%), Foreign +RM 463K (IRR 5-6%).

Key caveats: ①13-yr only +9% cumulative appreciation (0.65%/yr CAGR) is the weakest appreciation track among MK projects analyzed — this project is not capital appreciation thesis, buyers should accept "yield + hold" thesis; ②Large-unit positioning (minimum 2,535 sqft 3+1BR, only 3+1BR / 4+1BR / penthouse) makes single transaction RM 2M+, buyer pool narrower than 1BR/2BR investment-type condos — target buyers are MK large-unit families / senior expat manager assignments / high-yield holders; ③14-yr aged building may enter maintenance cycle, expected next 10 years renovation costs higher than new launches (already includes RM 50K renovation estimate); ④The MINH (2027 VP, same UEM Sunrise sponsor) + other MK new launches entering market within 5 yrs, new architecture design + Indochine GreenRE standards will attract some expat high-end buyers — indirect competition; ⑤MK28's 460-unit large supply internal competition for secondary exit is an ongoing factor — buyer pool is yield-focused dominant, capital growth-focused buyers will not select MK28; ⑥MYR systemic depreciation is a common implicit risk for all KL property.

10-Year Scenario Baseline: assume 2026-05 entry, 2036-05 exit:
  • Base case: MK broad stable, MK28 aged condo discount sustains, prices +3%/yr, rent +3%/yr, 1 mo vacancy. Local 10-year cumulative net return ~RM 728K, IRR ~7-8% (higher than R22 / Park Place due to positive carry structural advantage); Foreign ~RM 463K, IRR ~5-6%.
  • Bull case: MK overall +5%/yr (MM2H 2,637 pending buyers concentrate landing boosts MK high-yield project demand) + aged condo post-renovation PSF jump. Local IRR ~9-10% (RM 1,292K+), Foreign IRR ~7-8% (RM 970K+).
  • Bear case: MK aged condo discount expands (post-The MINH completion PSF competition intensifies) + prices 0%/yr. Local still positive +RM 64K due to high yield (the rare "0% appreciation Local still profits" case); Foreign loses RM 141K.
10-year exit liquidity: Brickz 6.1% annual turnover is healthy level, expected listing-to-sale 3-5 months (large-unit 3+1BR), discount 5-8%; the thesis: MK aged condo high yield play, target buyers are yield-focused holders, not capital growth-focused investors.

◆ DEEP NARRATIVE

Project Deep Summary

Park Place sits in the TownCentre core of Desa ParkCity, a 4.55-acre plot anchoring a single 54-floor tower of 537 units — its micro-position differentiates from other DPC projects (OCP's lakefront boutique, Park Regent's lakefront family flagship, Casaman's high-end enclave) by being "DPC's most convenient lifestyle hub": 5-min walk to Plaza Arkadia retail/F&B and 11-min walk to The Waterfront mall + banks + groceries + salons + SOHO. It is the lowest-car-dependency point within DPC. DPC itself is KL's only 473-acre single-master-developer planned township, with Perdana ParkCity progressively developing the land since 1995 over 30+ years, controlling masterplan, construction standards, and community governance. The integrated 70-acre Central Park, ParkCity Medical Centre, and International School @ ParkCity create a low-density park-oriented living ecosystem comparable to Singapore's Bukit Timah. The structural gap remains the absence of any existing MRT or MRT3 coverage — but Park Place's TownCentre integration mitigates car dependency more than DPC's other projects.

Park Place is solely developed by Perdana ParkCity Sdn Bhd (Samling Group's property arm, founded 1977) — not a JV with institutional partners like CapitaLand (that's Park Regent's structure). Perdana has completed 27 prior DPC projects (including OCP, South Brooks, Westside, Park Regent), all successfully delivered; South Brooks @ DPC achieved 86% QLASSIC, the national high for high-rise residential, demonstrating Perdana's solo construction quality benchmark. Park Place launched September 2020 at average RM 860 psf, achieving 86% weekend take-up despite the pandemic, and reached VP in 2024 on schedule (4-yr launch-to-handover). The build profile: 1 tower of 54 floors with 537 units across 5 unit types — Type A 2BR 1,109-1,173 sqft (100 entry-level units), Type B 3BR 1,367-1,399, Type C 3BR 1,636-2,250, Type D 4BR 2,271-2,390, Type E 5BR 2,917-4,047 sqft (penthouse-level). Facilities include high-floor pool, Sky Lounge, gym, and shared workspaces; the distinguishing feature is the combination of high-rise views with TownCentre walkable integration.

On pricing: Brickz 86 transactions Feb 2025-Jan 2026 cumulative median RM 1,489 psf provides the deepest 12-month market validation of DPC's 3 scored projects — monthly avg 7.2-tx turnover far exceeds Park Regent's 5.8 and OCP's < 2, making Park Place DPC's most active secondary market. 2020 launch RM 860 psf → 2025-26 transactions RM 1,489 = 5-year +73% realized appreciation, far surpassing Mont Kiara's +10-20% and KL average +5-10% during the same period, validating both DPC and the Perdana brand premium; relative to JLL Q2 2025's KL high-end median of RM 1,100-1,200 psf, Park Place trades at a 24-35% premium reflecting both DPC's integrated township premium and Perdana's "no-discount preservation" reputation. However, current iProperty listings of RM 1,397-1,550 psf sit slightly below the Brickz median, with the 2BR Type A 1,109 sqft listing at RM 1.55M @ RM 1,397 psf as entry-level reference; 283 listings / 537 units = 52.7% listing rate is highest among DPC's 3 projects, indicating high investor-owner concentration and potential competitive exit pressure.

Rental market: with 1,141 sqft 2BR Type A as representative, monthly rent estimated at RM 6,000, gross yield ~4.24%. Anchor comes from PropertyGuru's building-wide Park Place rental average of RM 5.50 psf (× 1,141 sqft = RM 6,275), bracketed by iProperty's best-case listing at RM 7,000 (1,109 sqft, RM 6.31 psf) on the upper end and PROPCAFE's bearish review of RM 3.5-4.5K on the lower end. With 246 rental listings / 537 units = 45.8% rental listing rate, rental supply is abundant and achievable rent likely sits below the upper-end listing. Local Net Yield ~3.1%, spread −1.5%; Foreign after 30% non-resident tax: Net Yield ~2.4%, spread −2.7% clearly negative. Gross 4.2% sits slightly below Park Regent's 4.3% and OCP's 4.6%, but this reflects DPC's common large-unit yield-compressed dynamic — the thesis here is also capital appreciation and lifestyle hold, not pure yield play. Tenant pool comprises DPC foreign families (international school routing), corporate expat assignments, and local high-net-worth professionals; 2-yr+ corporate contracts stabilize occupancy, so the 1-mo/yr vacancy assumption is conservative.

Holding structure: freehold residential title (not commercial SA) + HDA Schedule H protection + residential 90% LTV + residential electricity tariff — institutional friction is low. MM2H asset eligibility: 2BR Type A entry units of 1,109 sqft × RM 1,397-1,489 psf ≈ RM 1.55-1.70M ≥ RM 1M Federal Territory threshold ✓ qualifying; 3BR median at RM 2.19M well exceeds. Completed ~2 years means buyers can inspect, verify management quality, and confirm JMB operation directly — zero execution risk; the 2-year warranty period allows defect claims against Perdana. Exit liquidity: monthly avg 7.2-tx turnover is the best among DPC's 3 projects, with a buyer pool spanning local HNW, corporate expat residents, and MM2H holders providing structural diversity. 10-year exit expectation: at base 3%/yr appreciation, Yr 10 price ~RM 2.28M, Local net P&L approximately +RM 366K (IRR 5-6%), Foreign +RM 195K (IRR 3-4%).

Key caveats: ①No existing MRT or MRT3 coverage is a DPC-wide trait, meaning catalysts lack a hard transit driver; ②Noöra (Perdana same-group, 1,156-unit SA, 6.63 acres, 2026-27 completion) is entering DPC — the first major internal supply event DPC has faced in 5+ years; while SA and residential are different sub-markets, internal DPC supply may dilute Park Place's premium foundation; ③5-year +73% realized appreciation means "easy gains already captured", forward 10-year expectation should reset to 3-4%/yr in line with Park Regent / OCP; ④Brickz 86-tx P25 RM 1.92M / P75 RM 2.95M spread reflects unit-type pricing dispersion — 2BR Type A entry units likely transact slightly below the cross-type median, so buyers should map PSF to target size band; ⑤52.7% high listing rate signals high investor ownership, meaning future 5-10 year downside scenarios could see clustered secondary supply release; ⑥MYR systemic depreciation is a common implicit risk for all KL property.

  • Base case: DPC supply cap holds, Noöra absorbed within 1-2 years, prices +3%/yr, rent +3%/yr, 1 mo vacancy. Local 10-year cumulative net return ~RM 366K, IRR ~5-6%; Foreign ~RM 195K, IRR ~3-4%.
  • Bull case: MM2H 2,637 pending buyers concentrate landing + KL new-starts −14.9% drives boutique supply squeeze + prices +5%/yr. Local IRR ~7-8% (RM 846K+), Foreign IRR ~5-6% (RM 625K+).
  • Bear case: Noöra supply pressure extends to 3 years + MYR depreciation + prices 0%/yr. Local IRR near −1% (10-year loss RM 198K), Foreign IRR near −2% due to 8% stamp + 30% rental tax + perpetual RPGT (loss RM 314K).
10-year exit liquidity: monthly avg 7.2-tx turnover is DPC's best, expected listing-to-sale 1-3 months (2BR entry units), discount 2-5%; 2BR Type A is MM2H-friendly (lowest total price + meets RM 1M threshold), liquidity structurally mature and stable.

Highlights & Caveats

✦ Highlights
  • DPC is KL's only 473-acre single-master-developer planned township; Park Place sits at TownCentre core (Plaza Arkadia 5-min + Waterfront 11-min walk), the lowest car-dependency micro-position within DPC
  • Perdana ParkCity solo developed (Samling Group, 1977-founded) DPC master developer with 27 prior projects all delivered + South Brooks 86% QLASSIC national high + public "no-discount preservation" brand stance
  • Completed 2024, execution risk = 0; within 2-yr warranty allows defect claims against Perdana; buyers can directly verify management quality and JMB

⚠ Caveats
  • Noöra (Perdana same-group, 1,156-unit SA, 6.63 acres, 2026-27 completion) is entering DPC — the first major internal supply event in 5+ years; SA and residential are different sub-markets but internal DPC supply may dilute Park Place's premium

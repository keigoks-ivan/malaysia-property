◆ DEEP NARRATIVE

Project Deep Summary

Noöra is Perdana ParkCity's 4th condominium-class project within Desa ParkCity (preceded by OCP 2017 / Park Regent 2023 / Park Place 2024), occupying a 6.63-acre plot on the southern fringe of the 473-acre DPC township. Two 52-floor residential towers house 1,156 units, with Noora Walk's 50,000 sqft 3-storey retail podium integrated at the tower base — a rare mixed-use-within-building configuration for KL private developments, providing 0-min indoor walk access to groceries, F&B, and daily life amenities. Compared to other DPC projects (OCP adjacent to Central Park, Park Regent lakefront, Park Place TownCentre with 5-11 min walk to retail), Noöra is the "groceries within the tower" integrated residence, a distinct positioning. The trade-off: Noöra's micro-position sits on DPC's southern fringe (~10-min walk to Plaza Arkadia, ~15-min to Waterfront), slightly less central than Park Place TownCentre core.

Noöra is solely developed by Perdana ParkCity Sdn Bhd (Samling Group property arm, founded 1977) — same standalone structure as OCP and Park Place (not a JV). Perdana has completed 27 prior DPC projects all successfully delivered; South Brooks @ DPC achieved 86% QLASSIC, the national high for high-rise residential, demonstrating Perdana's solo construction quality benchmark. Noöra launched November 2022 at average RM 900-1,000 psf, achieving 73% weekend take-up + 4,500+ pre-registrants — DPC's most enthusiastically received launch in 5 years; VP 2026 (4-year period, matching Park Place's on-time delivery model). The build profile: 1,156 units across 5 unit types — 1BR 520 sqft, 1+1BR 604/651 sqft, 2BR & 2+1BR 1,010-1,084 sqft, 3BR 1,282 sqft; significantly smaller than Park Place (1,109-4,047 sqft), product positioning shifts toward mass-mid tier investment + entry-level owner-occupier buyers. Noöra adopts a Scandinavian design theme with Forest Trail, Sky Lounge L37, Sky Garden L48, Work Loft, Makers' Studio, infinity pool, BBQ pavilions; Noora Walk's curated tenants (groceries, F&B) managed by Perdana serve as a daily-life amenity anchor.

On pricing: Noöra is still under construction (VP 2026), Brickz post-completion transactions have not yet formed; current sub-sale market PSF ranges RM 700-1,488 reflecting off-plan sub-sale dynamics — RM 700 low end represents some launch-price holders exiting (Perdana's "no-discount preservation" stance means deep discounts shouldn't occur, but small early-bird launch-price exits 1-2 years post-launch are reasonable), RM 1,488 high end represents post-SPA appreciation or large-unit premium. Current PSF average RM 1,019 sits slightly above launch RM 900-1,000 psf, indicating ~2-13% off-plan appreciation. Versus same-sponsor adjacent Park Place's Brickz median RM 1,489 psf, Noöra's ~32% discount is reasonable given SR vs Condo + smaller-unit positioning differences. EdgeProp's current 27 listings (early sub-sale) and iProperty's 51 listings — sub-sale liquidity is forming but remains thin.

Rental market: with 1,047 sqft 2BR/2+1BR as representative, monthly rent estimated at RM 4,400, gross yield ~5.0% — slightly higher than Park Place 4.2% / Park Regent 4.3% / OCP 4.6%. Anchor comes from DPC mass-mid tier triangulation: Nadia Parkfront RM 2.70-3.75 psf (mass-market) as floor, Park Place RM 5.50-6.22 psf (mid-tier) as ceiling, Noöra's estimated RM 4.2 psf is a reasonable midpoint; Noora Walk's 50,000 sqft retail integration + Forest Trail premium amenities support rental slightly above market average, but 1,156-unit concentrated post-completion release will compress achievable rent in the first 2 years. Local 15% progressive tax: Net Yield ~3.9%, spread −0.6%; Foreign 30% non-resident tax: Net Yield ~3.2%, spread −1.8%; both negative but significantly narrower than Park Place (local −1.5% / foreign −2.7%), meaning Noöra is the "closest to cash-flow play" choice among DPC's 4 projects. Tenant pool comprises DPC foreign families, corporate expat assignments, and local middle-class investor tenants; 2-yr+ corporate contracts stabilize occupancy.

Holding structure: Freehold + service residence classification, per iProperty designated as "residential strata" — a hybrid between freehold commercial SA and freehold pure-residential condo. The critical foreign buyer structural constraint: Federal Territory (incl. KL) minimum foreign purchase threshold is RM 1,000,000, and Noöra's 1BR 520 sqft @ RM 590K, 1+1BR 604/651 sqft, 2BR 1,010 sqft @ RM 958K all sit below the RM 1M threshold; foreigners can only practically buy 1,084+ sqft units (~RM 1.08M+) or 3BR 1,282 sqft (~RM 1.48M+), with ~50% of stock blocked. MM2H asset eligibility: 2BR @ RM 1.05M ≥ RM 1M ✓ but only marginally. 10-year exit expectation: at base 3%/yr appreciation, Yr 10 price ~RM 1.41M, Local net P&L approximately +RM 245K (IRR 6-7%), Foreign +RM 116K (IRR 3-4%).

Key caveats: ①Foreign RM 1M Federal threshold is Noöra's unique structural caveat among DPC's 4 projects — ~50% stock (1BR + 1+1BR + some 2BR) blocked, foreign buyer pool is half of DPC's other projects, exit pool also smaller than Park Place / OCP / Park Regent; ②Off-plan 4-year wait, VP 2026 ~6 months execution risk remaining — though Perdana's track record is strong, non-zero risk; ③1,156-unit concentrated post-completion release + Park Place's 537-unit existing inventory = ~1,700 DPC internal new supply in 5 years, a new variable DPC hasn't faced before; ④Service residence classification means banking policy may not apply 90% LTV (some banks apply 80-85% LTV to SR), buyers should confirm before entry; ⑤Noöra's micro-position is DPC's southern fringe — though Noora Walk's tower-base retail compensates for daily convenience, distance to Plaza Arkadia / Waterfront is longer; ⑥MYR systemic depreciation is a common implicit risk for all KL property.

10-Year Scenario Baseline: assume 2026-Q4 entry (post-VP), 2036-Q4 exit:
  • Base case: DPC supply cap holds, Noöra absorbed within 2 years post-completion, prices +3%/yr, rent +3%/yr, 1 mo vacancy. Local 10-year cumulative net return ~RM 245K, IRR ~6-7% (higher than Park Place / Park Regent due to lower base + higher yield); Foreign ~RM 116K, IRR ~3-4%.
  • Bull case: Noora Walk retail tenants fully occupied + MM2H 2,637 pending buyers concentrate landing + prices +5%/yr. Local IRR ~9-10% (RM 497K+), Foreign IRR ~6-7% (RM 347K+).
  • Bear case: Post-completion 1,156 units + Park Place investor exits + MYR depreciation + prices 0%/yr. Local IRR near −2% (10-year loss RM 147K), Foreign IRR near −3% due to 8% stamp + 30% rental tax + perpetual RPGT (loss RM 234K).
10-year exit liquidity: Brickz sample thin (not yet completed), but Perdana DPC track record + same-sponsor Park Place's monthly avg 7.2-tx Brickz speed serves as proxy; expect 1BR/2BR entry-tier sub-sale to enter active phase 1-2 years post-completion; 2BR entry tier (~RM 1.05M) is marginal for MM2H buyer threshold — buyers should assess currency variation risk.
◆ CONCLUSION

Highlights & Caveats

✦ Highlights
  • Noora Walk 50,000 sqft 3-storey retail podium integrated at tower base (rare for KL private developments) — 0-min indoor walk to groceries / F&B vs 5-15 min walk required at DPC's other projects
  • Same-sponsor (Perdana) DPC's 4th project, 27 prior projects all delivered + South Brooks 86% QLASSIC national high + "no-discount preservation" brand stance + Marina ParkCity Miri JV + ParkCity Hanoi international expansion
  • DPC overall median is RM 1,400-1,600 psf, Noöra's RM 1,019 psf is ~32% discount reflecting SR vs Condo + smaller-unit reasonable positioning; entry tier (1BR @ RM 590K / 2BR @ RM 1.05M) is DPC's most accessible
  • Gross 5.0% above DPC's 3 completed projects (Park Place 4.2% / OCP 4.6% / Park Regent 4.3%); Local spread −0.6% / Foreign −1.8% is DPC's closest to positive carry
  • 2022-11 launch 73% weekend take-up + 4,500+ pre-registrants validates strong demand; 2026 VP expected on schedule (4-yr period)
  • Scandinavian design theme + Forest Trail + Sky Lounge L37 + Sky Garden L48 + Work Loft + Makers' Studio rich premium amenities attractive for hybrid work / investment-tier tenants
⚠ Caveats
  • Foreign RM 1M Federal threshold is a unique structural caveat — 1BR 520 sqft (RM 590K) + 1+1BR 604/651 sqft + 2BR 1,010 sqft (RM 958K) all below threshold, foreigners only access 1,084+ sqft (RM 1.08M+); ~50% stock blocked, foreign buyer pool is half of DPC's other projects
  • Off-plan 4-year wait, VP 2026 ~6 months execution risk remaining; though Perdana's track record is strong, Park Place / OCP / Park Regent are all completed — this is DPC's only project still under construction
  • 1,156-unit concentrated post-completion release (vs Park Place 537 / OCP 258 / Park Regent 505) + Park Place investor exits = ~1,700 DPC internal new supply in 5 years, a new variable DPC hasn't faced before
  • Service residence classification sits between freehold commercial SA and freehold pure-residential condo — banking policy may not apply 90% LTV (some banks apply 80-85% LTV to SR); buyers should confirm with bank pre-entry
  • Noöra's micro-position is DPC's southern fringe, ~10-min walk to Plaza Arkadia / ~15-min to Waterfront; though Noora Walk's tower-base retail compensates for daily convenience, distance to DPC core is longer on foot
  • MYR systemic depreciation is a common implicit risk for all KL property; foreign buyers should factor currency conversion volatility into position sizing

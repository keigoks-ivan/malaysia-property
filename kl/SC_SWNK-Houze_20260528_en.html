◆ Deep Narrative

Project Deep Summary

SWNK Houze sits on Jalan Hang Tuah (Bukit Bintang), the Phase 2 flagship residential project of the Bukit Bintang City Centre (BBCC) 19.4-acre integrated master plan. BBCC is developed by BBCC Development Sdn Bhd — a three-party institutional-grade JV: EcoWorld Development Group (Bursa:8206, T1 large listed developer) + UDA Holdings Berhad (Malaysian government urban regen wholly-owned subsidiary, providing the former Pudu Prison land) + Employees Provident Fund (EPF, Malaysia's largest pension fund, AUM RM 1.2T+ institutional investor). BBCC is KL CBD's last large-scale emerging integrated land — an urban regeneration case on the former Pudu Prison site. BBCC Phase 1 has delivered LaLaport BBCC (Mitsui Shopping Park, opened 2023-11-29) and Lucky Residences; SWNK Houze is the Phase 2 flagship residential — 31 storeys, 441 units, VP estimated 2026.

On location: Bukit Bintang district anchors at 7.5 baseline in v2.0.1 anchor table (KL CBD tourism/retail core, freehold, Pavilion/TS integration). SWNK Houze's core location adjustments: BBCC Transit Hub 3-station interchange (+0.3) — KL's only Hang Tuah Monorail + Hang Tuah LRT + Merdeka MRT (300m walk) 3-line directly-connected residential, a unique anchor in KL's public transport network, exceeding Conlay's single-MRT-line, TRX's dual-MRT-line integration. Plus walkable to LaLaport BBCC (Mitsui Shopping Park 500+ shops), Sungei Wang Plaza, Berjaya Times Square, Furama Hotel and other malls (+0.2), totaling 8.0 d1. Adjacent Merdeka PNB 118 (118-storey KL's second tallest) is an international landmark. BBCC is currently KL's strongest walking density and transit-oriented integration, on par with KLCC Conlay 8.3 and TRX 8.0. Bukit Bintang location strongly supports tourism + nightlife, but commercial SA + small-unit structure means main client is investment-light users / young professionals / Airbnb operators.

Project scale: 31 storeys 441 units as BBCC Phase 2 flagship. Unit structure spans Studio 463 sqft / 1BR / 2BR 861 sqft / Loft / Dual-key / up to 1,238 sqft (8 floor plan choices). This small-unit + dual-key dominant design reflects BBCC's positioning toward "young urbanites, young professionals, Airbnb investors", differentiated from family-scale unit menus of KLCC Conlay (1BR-3BR 743-1,335 sqft) and TRX Residences (1BR-3BR 474-1,636 sqft). Facilities are mid-tier service residence standard (pool, gym, 24h security, resort-style facilities); sinking fund RM 0.60 psf is project official announcement (moderate-high for BB commercial SA). BBCC overall 7,000 parking + LaLaport mall + Hilton + Merdeka 118 is flagship-level master plan integration, but watch BBCC same-master multi-phase (Lucky series + other phases) internal supply pressure for long-term PSF and rental impact.

On the developer side: the three-party JV is relatively unique among scorecards analyzed: EcoWorld (Bursa T1, Liew family, reps EcoMajestic, EcoArdence, EcoSanctuary, 2024 revenue RM 2.4B+) + UDA (government urban regen, land provider) + EPF (largest pension fund, structural capital + institutional continuity). JV advantages: (1) EPF as government retirement fund manager provides long-term holding intent + structural endorsement; (2) UDA government land ensures development legitimacy + infrastructure; (3) EcoWorld provides execution + brand. But SWNK Houze is Off-plan VP 2026, JMB not yet operational; buyers should track post-completion management quality. Lendlease-style international brand discontinuity concern doesn't apply here (EcoWorld is local T1, relatively stable). Final developer score 7.5 = Bursa T1 7.5 baseline + EPF institutional +0.3 + UDA government +0.2 − Off-plan JMB unvalidated -0.5.

On pricing: SWNK Houze launch PSF range RM 1,000-1,742 (floor + unit type variance large), launch PSF RM 1,200 median, pricing RM 740,000-1,399,000. Compared against same-BB district projects: Times Square 2 (freehold commercial SA at Imbi) RM 1,200-1,400 psf at-parity, reflects BB overall pricing; Pavilion Square (leasehold commercial @ BB) RM 1,400-1,800 psf slight premium (Pavilion brand). SWNK Houze is Off-plan VP 2026 + Brickz 0 tx; PSF transparency relies entirely on BB cluster + BBCC same-master peer comparisons. 5% Bumi discount is Bumi-buyer-exclusive; 2025-06 soft launch may still have early-bird discounts, furniture packages, legal fee waivers (typical 5-10%), but BBCC Phase 2 flagship + institutional JV limit negotiation space narrower than other BB launches.

On the rental market: BB overall gross yield 4-5%. SWNK Houze 2BR 861 sqft long-let est. RM 4,500/mo (RM 5.23 psf), gross yield ~5.23% — in v2.0.1 anchor 5.0-5.5% band, comparable to Conlay (4.90%), TRX (4.91%), BHP (5.30%), Hilltop (5.08%). Local net yield ~3.4%, spread −1.2%; foreign 30% non-resident tax-after net yield ~2.3%, spread −2.8%. SWNK Houze's tenant structure mixes BB tourists (strong Airbnb potential, Pavilion + Bukit Bintang Walk + Jalan Alor nightlife walkable) + young urban professionals (KL Sentral commute + multinational expat) + students (La Salle / IACT KL education institutions adjacent). Studio + Dual-key are Airbnb-friendly designs — SWNK Houze's differentiation vs family-positioned projects. Watch: (1) BBCC overall ecosystem ramping with Phase 1 + Phase 2 + other phases creating rental supply pressure post-completion; (2) JMB policy on short-let Airbnb; (3) KL SA overhang 3,643 effect on BBCC small-unit segment.

On holding structure: Leasehold (estimated 99-yr, BBCC government land) + commercial SA is core structural caveat — leasehold's implied long-hold discount + commercial SA's 70-85% LTV (vs residential 90%) + commercial utilities (1.3-1.6x) + HDA Schedule H scope variance + non-residential title affects EPF Account 2 withdrawal eligibility (SWNK Houze service-residence-designed but commercial-land-classified). MM2H mandatory asset: 2BR 861 sqft × RM 1.03M just clears RM 1M Federal Territory threshold, suitable for MM2H Silver only; not for higher MM2H Gold/Platinum thresholds. 10-yr exit outlook: under base 3%/yr growth, Year 10 price ~RM 1.39M, local net P&L ~RM 280k (IRR 3-4%), foreign ~RM 139k (IRR 1-2%). Exit pool: BB cluster active secondary but BBCC itself 0 sample, expected list-to-deal 4-8 months with 5-10% discount.

Key things to watch: (1) Leasehold (estimated 99-yr) + commercial SA dual structural caveats; long-term hold requires evaluating implied discount + commercial LTV constraints. (2) BBCC same-master multi-phase (Lucky series etc.) potential impact on long-term PSF and rental. (3) Off-plan VP 2026 waiting period — execution risk + post-completion 1-2 yr rental ramp. (4) Small-unit + Dual-key + Studio-dominant unit structure means main client base is investment-light users + Airbnb, foreign family buyer pool narrow. (5) JMB short-let Airbnb policy is core variable — confirm with BBCC Development in writing. (6) Sinking fund RM 0.60 psf is moderate-high in BB, materially affects net yield. (7) 5% Bumi discount-tagged units locked to Bumi resale narrows non-Bumi exit pool. (8) BBCC integrated ecosystem (LaLaport tenant occupancy, Hilton performance, Merdeka 118 onboarding) is 5-7 yr catalyst variable.

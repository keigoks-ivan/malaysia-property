Bottom line總結 Kuala Lumpur is Malaysia's commercial property main stage by a wide margin — its private office stock alone is 51.7% of the national total, and its shopping-complex space is the country's best-occupied at 87.95%. But the headline hides a city split against itself, and a pipeline that isn't finished delivering. The office story runs between a City Centre corridor still holding above 73% occupancy and an Outside City Centre periphery six points weaker on a NAPIC classification too coarse to separate genuine fringe winners from ageing towers nobody wants — towers that, this report now shows, are trading hands for tens of millions of ringgit, not the price of a strata floor. The retail story splits harder still: Bukit Bintang-corridor precincts sit essentially full — several above 98% — while Jalan Ipoh, a single-building precinct, runs at just 30.3%, and five precincts carry no shopping-complex stock at all. Shophouses have gone the opposite direction entirely: KL's overhang has shrunk to 10 units, the cleanest small market in the country, the product of a city with almost no land left to build new ones on. SOHO is the one place the "clean" story doesn't hold up on closer inspection: a 2025 completion wave pushed unsold stock past 1,100 units before absorption pulled it back to 793 within two quarters — but incoming supply now sitting at 3,362 units, 4.2 times the current overhang, means a second, larger wave is already queued behind it. Serviced apartments, covered at length elsewhere on this site, remain the one category to actively avoid. 吉隆坡是馬來西亞商業房產無可爭議的主場——單是私人辦公室存量就佔全國 51.7%，商場（SC）入駐率 87.95% 更是全國最高。但這個頭條數字底下，是一座與自己分裂的城市，而且供給線還沒交完。辦公室的故事，走在市中心走廊仍守住 73% 以上入駐率、與外圍低了六個百分點之間，而 NAPIC 的分區口徑太粗，無法把真正的外圍贏家與沒人要的老樓分開來看——本篇新增的資料顯示，這些老樓正以數千萬令吉的量級整棟易手，跟一層分層辦公室的價格完全不在同一個世界。零售的分裂更劇烈：Bukit Bintang 走廊沿線的分區幾乎滿租——多個超過 98%——但只有一棟建物的 Jalan Ipoh 分區入駐率僅 30.3%，另有五個分區完全沒有商場存量。店屋則走了完全相反的方向：KL 的餘屋已縮到僅 10 戶，是全國最乾淨的小市場，原因是這座城市幾乎沒有土地能再蓋新的店屋。SOHO 則是「乾淨」這個說法一細看就站不住腳的一項：2025 年一波完工潮曾一度把未售存量推過 1,100 戶，隨後兩季內去化拉回至 793 戶——但即將供給已達 3,362 戶，是目前餘屋的 4.2 倍，代表下一波更大的浪已經排隊等著。服務式公寓的完整論證已見於本站他處，作為被動投資，結論仍是主動迴避。

KL's commercial world is built around offices and malls, not shophouses, and Johor's is close to the opposite. KL holds 51.7% of the nation's private office space (9.63 of 18.64 million sm, derived) against Johor's 5.2%; KL holds 19.5% of national shopping-complex space (3.39 of 17.36 million sm, derived) against Johor's 13.7%. Flip to shophouses and the ranking reverses: KL's 30,973-unit stock is just 5.5% of the national 560,624 total, while Johor's 99,298 units make up 17.7% — the largest shop base of any state. Office space is where large employers, government tenants and MNC headquarters concentrate; shophouses are what a city builds when it still has raw land at the edge of town. KL, largely built out, has almost none left; Johor, still expanding along the Causeway corridor, has plenty.KL 的商業世界，是圍繞辦公室與商場建立的，不是店屋；柔佛則幾乎是相反的組成。KL 佔全國私人辦公空間 51.7%（9.63／18.64 百萬平方米，derived），柔佛僅 5.2%；KL 佔全國購物中心空間 19.5%（3.39／17.36 百萬平方米，derived），柔佛為 13.7%。但換成店屋，排名整個反轉：KL 的 30,973 戶存量，僅佔全國 560,624 戶的 5.5%，柔佛的 99,298 戶卻佔了 17.7%——是全國店屋基數最大的一州。辦公空間，是大型雇主、政府租戶與跨國企業總部聚集之處；店屋，則是一座城市邊緣還有生地時才會大量興建的產品。已大致開發殆盡的 KL，幾乎沒有生地可用；仍沿新柔長堤走廊擴張的柔佛，則供給充足。

The pipeline tells the same story from a different angle. Weigh each category's incoming supply against its own existing stock and KL splits into a closed market and two that are still building: shop pipeline is just 605 units against 30,973 standing — 2.0% (derived: 605 ÷ 30,973) — while SOHO's 3,362 incoming units run 27.4% of its 12,266-unit stock (derived: 3,362 ÷ 12,266), and serviced apartments' 50,918 incoming run 33.1% of 153,646 (derived: 50,918 ÷ 153,646). Shophouses are a closed, resale-only market; SOHO and serviced apartments are supply machines still running near full output. Nationally the same three ratios read 3.8%, 23.2% and 40.9% — KL's shop pipeline runs at roughly half the national ratio (less land, less left to add), its SOHO pipeline runs slightly ahead of the national pace, and only serviced apartments run meaningfully cooler than the national pipeline.供給線從另一個角度講的是同一個故事。把每個類別的即將供給拿去對比自己的既有存量，KL 分裂成一個封閉市場、與兩個仍在全速生產的市場：店屋供給僅 605 戶，對照存量 30,973 戶——僅 2.0%（derived：605 ÷ 30,973）——SOHO 即將供給 3,362 戶，佔存量 12,266 戶的 27.4%（derived：3,362 ÷ 12,266），服務式公寓即將供給 50,918 戶，佔存量 153,646 戶的 33.1%（derived：50,918 ÷ 153,646）。店屋是一個封閉、只靠轉手撐著的市場；SOHO 與服務式公寓，則是仍以接近全速運轉的供給機器。全國同樣三個比率分別是 3.8%、23.2%、40.9%——KL 的店屋供給比率大約是全國的一半（地少，能加的也少），SOHO 供給比率略高於全國步調，唯獨服務式公寓的供給明顯比全國步調更冷。

NAPIC publishes KL office occupancy at only two levels of granularity: City Centre (Seksyen 1–100, Bandar KL — 306 buildings, 7.47 million sm) and Outside City Centre (113 buildings, 2.16 million sm). The two do not sit where intuition suggests. City Centre runs 73.38% occupied, Outside City Centre 66.63% — a 6.8-point gap in the city centre's favour, against a national private-only occupancy of 72.34%. Combined, KL's 419 buildings average 71.87%, just under the national figure despite carrying more than half the country's office stock.NAPIC 對 KL 辦公室的入駐率，只公布兩個分區層級：市中心（Seksyen 1–100，Bandar KL——306 棟，747.5 萬平方米）與城外（113 棟，215.9 萬平方米）。兩者的高低，並不如直覺所想。市中心入駐率 73.38%，城外 66.63%——市中心領先 6.8 個百分點，對照全國僅私人口徑的 72.34%。合計 KL 419 棟大樓平均 71.87%，雖持有全國過半辦公存量，讀數卻略低於全國。

Read the gap carefully. "Outside City Centre" does not mean "emerging fringe" — NAPIC's own bucket is wide enough to lump genuine winners (the Knight Frank-defined "KL Fringe" of Mid Valley, Bangsar South and KL Sentral) together with a much larger population of ageing, 2000s-vintage towers scattered across the rest of the city that are losing tenants to exactly that fringe. The two-precinct split cannot separate them; a 66.63% average could equally describe a fringe cluster near-full and a periphery running well below 50%, or a periphery that is uniformly middling. Availability data hints at which: within Outside City Centre, 38.9% of buildings report zero available space (fully let) while 18.6% sit more than half empty — a wider internal spread than City Centre's own 25.5% / 21.2% split on the same two measures. The periphery is not evenly weak; it is bifurcated in miniature, the same pattern playing out one level down.這個差距要仔細讀。「城外」不等於「新興外圍」——NAPIC 自己的這個分類，寬到足以把真正的贏家（Knight Frank 定義的「KL Fringe」：Mid Valley、Bangsar South、KL Sentral）跟散布在城市其他角落、正在把租客輸給這些外圍的一大群 2000 年代老樓，混在同一個數字裡。兩分區的口徑無法把兩者拆開；66.63% 這個平均值，既可能是「外圍聚落幾乎滿租、其餘遠低於 50%」的組合，也可能是「整個城外普遍中庸」的組合。可用空間的資料，能給一點提示：城外的大樓中，38.9% 完全無可用空間（滿租），18.6% 卻超過一半是空的——這個內部落差，比市中心自己的 25.5%／21.2% 更寬。城外不是均勻地弱，而是縮小版的兩極分化，同一種型態在下一層再上演一次。

A single quarter cannot say whether a gap is a new development or business as usual, so this report pulled the full quarterly history behind Exhibit 4 to check — back to Q1 2020. Over that full run the gap peaked much higher than anything seen recently, at 9.83 points in both Q4 2023 and Q1 2024 (City Centre 72.52%/72.46% vs Outside City Centre 62.68%/62.63%), before narrowing sharply through 2024 to a post-peak low of 4.84 points in Q3 2025 (72.11% vs 67.27%, following 4.98 points in Q1 2025). The individual investor's participation map個人投資者的參與路徑

So: can an individual actually buy into KL office? Yes — but only in one specific, narrow way, and the honest answer has three parts, not one.所以：個人投資者到底能不能買進KL辦公室？可以——但只有一種特定、條件嚴苛的方式，而誠實的答案有三個部分，不是一個。

Three paths, in order of who can actually take them三條路徑，依實際能走的人排序

Path 1 — buy a strata office unit directly: possible, conditional.路徑一——直接買一戶分層辦公室：可行，但有條件。 The annual report's own named transactions (Pavilion Damansara Heights, Suasana Bukit Ceylon, KL Eco City) prove this market exists and trades at a checkable price. But "exists" is not "safe": which side of the flight-to-quality line a given floor sits on depends on building age and green-certification status far more than on price alone — Section II's own availability-rate data shows fully-let towers and half-empty towers standing in the same precinct, sometimes the same street. Before treating a specific strata unit as buyable, run through: (1) building age and green/ESG certification — the line Section II has been drawing throughout; (2) the precinct's own availability-rate band from Exhibit 5 — is this building's precinct one where full and half-empty towers coexist; (3) verified, current tenant covenant and the unit's ability to absorb a vacancy period, not just a listed asking rent; (4) resale-pool depth on the same floor plate or building — strata offices trade in a shallow secondary market and a slow exit is the default, not the exception; (5) financing terms, which run less favourably than residential mortgages on a commercial title (no specific rate quoted here — verify with a lender directly). Pass all five and a direct strata purchase in Damansara Heights or KL Eco City is a defensible position, not a guess.年報自己點名的成交案（Pavilion Damansara Heights、Suasana Bukit Ceylon、KL Eco City）證明這個市場確實存在，也有價格可查。但『存在』不等於『安全』：一戶樓層落在品質出走哪一側，取決於樓齡與綠建認證的程度，遠多於價格本身——第二部自己的可用率資料顯示，同一個分區、有時甚至同一條街上，滿租樓與半空樓並存。在把某一戶分層單位當成可買之前，先過這五關：（一）樓齡與綠建／ESG認證——這正是第二部一路在畫的那條線；（二）圖5的分區可用率帶——這棟樓所在的分區，是不是滿租樓與半空樓並存的那種；（三）核實過、當下有效的租戶履約能力，以及這戶單位能不能撐過一段空置期，而不只是看掛牌租金；（四）同一樓層／同棟大樓的二手轉手池深度——分層辦公室的二手交易池淺，出場慢是常態，不是例外；（五）融資條件，商業產權的貸款條件通常不如住宅房貸優惠（這裡不列具體利率，需直接向貸方核實）。五關都過，在Damansara Heights或KL Eco City直接買一戶分層辦公室，就是站得住腳的判斷，不是賭一把。

KL shophouses are the one CRE sub-sector where "choose the street, not the scheme" is close to the entire strategy. Limitations of this report本篇的侷限

In the interest of being read the same way it asks its own sources to be read: this report carries no rental index for offices, shops or SOHO — NAPIC does not publish one, and every rental figure cited is a named-building illustrative comparable, not a market series. It carries no verified cap rate or investment yield for any KL commercial category (Section III). Corridor-level office occupancy (Section II's six corridors) is Knight Frank's own submarket classification, not a NAPIC boundary, and should not be read as more precise than NAPIC's own two-bucket City Centre/Outside City Centre split. Its net-absorption analysis (Section II) is a self-derived calculation with a known Q2 2025 data gap, not a NAPIC-published series. And its Capital Markets tables (Section III) transcribe NAPIC's own named-transaction tables, which this report's own reconciliation shows are illustrative samples of the year's largest deals (68% and 98.5% of stated sector totals respectively) rather than exhaustive transaction censuses.為求本篇被閱讀的方式，跟它要求讀者看待自身來源的方式一致：本篇對辦公室、店屋、SOHO都沒有租金指數——NAPIC不發布這個，本篇引用的每一個租金數字，都是具名大樓的示意比較案例，不是市場整體序列。本篇對任何KL商業類別都沒有經核實的資本化率或投資收益率（第三部）。走廊級辦公室入駐率（第二部六條走廊）是Knight Frank自有的分區分類，不是NAPIC的邊界，不應被誤讀為比NAPIC自己的市中心／城外兩分區口徑更精確。淨去化分析（第二部）是本篇自行推導的計算，已知存在2025Q2資料缺口，不是NAPIC發布的序列。第三部的資本市場表格，轉錄自NAPIC自己具名的成交表，本篇自己的核對顯示，這些表格分別只是當年最大成交的示意樣本（各佔官方口徑總額的68%與98.5%），不是完整的成交普查。
The closing frame總結框架

KL is the main stage of Malaysian commercial property, but "main stage" is not the same as "one market." Inside the headline numbers sits a city split against itself on almost every axis: prime office corridor against ageing periphery, footfall-dense retail band against precincts with no shopping-complex stock at all, a shophouse market so scarce it has nothing left to be sick with, and a SOHO segment that cleared one completion wave within two quarters only to find a second, larger one already queued behind it. The institutional layer — offices, malls — is REIT territory, priced daily and managed at a scale no strata owner can match. The one product an individual can actually run diligence on is the shophouse, and there are ten of them left.KL 是馬來西亞商業房產無可爭議的主場，但「主場」不等於「單一市場」。頭條數字底下，是一座幾乎在每一個軸線上都與自己分裂的城市：精華辦公走廊對照老化的外圍，人流密集的零售帶對照完全沒有商場存量的分區，一個稀缺到已無病可生的店屋市場，以及一個兩季內消化掉一波完工潮、卻發現後面已排著更大一波的 SOHO 板塊。機構級的那一層——辦公室、商場——是 REIT 的地盤，天天有報價、以分層業主無法企及的規模管理。個人真正能親自盡調的那一項，是店屋，而它只剩十戶。

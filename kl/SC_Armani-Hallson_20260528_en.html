◆ Deep Narrative

Project Deep Summary

Armani Hallson SOHO sits on Jalan Ampang KLCC (300m to KLCC Twin Towers — closest physical distance among scorecards), Armani Group's flagship RM 3 billion GDV mega-project. Armani Group is an Ipoh-based 2010-founded private developer led by Datuk Seri Bryan Wong Sze Chien (chairman + major shareholder) + Datuk Seri Azlan Azmi; financing backed by AmBank Group. The project launched 12 August 2025 and reached 70% take-up by November 2025 (within 4 months). VP estimated 2029 (off-plan 3+ yr wait). The 2.6-acre freehold site features three towers: Block C is SOHO 68 storeys with 775 units (538-558 sqft single-spec); Blocks A + B are SOVO 78 storeys with 1,440 combined units (406-1,182 sqft including Studio + Loft + Dual-key), totaling 2,215 units as a large-scale community.

"Armani" Brand Ambiguity (Important): The "Armani" in "Armani Hallson" refers to the Malaysian local Armani Group company name (Ipoh-based developer, founded 2010) and is NOT Italian Giorgio Armani / Casa Armani fashion brand. There is no corporate affiliation, brand license, or product collaboration between the two. Buyers should note that this naming may create confusion with the Italian luxury brand — KLCC does not host a true Armani Hotel / Casa Armani branded residence (Italian Armani globally has only two own branded residences: Dubai Burj Khalifa and Milan). This scorecard is based on Armani Group's actual Malaysian developer profile, with NO premium for any Italian international luxury brand association. Buyers should confirm brand positioning in writing before purchase to avoid misunderstanding.

On location: KLCC (Bukit Ceylon / Conlay) district anchors at 8.0 baseline in v2.0.1 anchor table (CBD core, multi-MRT/LRT coverage). Armani Hallson's location advantages: (1) Just 300m straight-line distance to KLCC Twin Towers — closest among scorecards (vs The Conlay 1.5km, TRX ~5km, BBCC ~2km); (2) First-ever KLCC direct covered link bridge to Twin Towers — unique infrastructure not found in any other scorecard, making a significant difference during KL rainy season (half the year) + sun heat walking experience; (3) Suria KLCC, Avenue K mall, KLCC Park, KLCC Convention Centre all walkable; (4) 600m to Bukit Nanas Monorail Station (secondary transit, KLCC area is primarily LRT-served). Overall d1 reaches 8.5 — highest location moat among scorecards analyzed, reflecting KLCC's irreplaceable prime core position. Note: MRT3 Circle Line 2032 is long-term pipeline, does not directly cover this micro-location.

Project scale: 2,215 units across 3 towers (68-78 storeys) is large-scale community. Density 852 units/acre is highest density among scorecards (vs Conlay 341, Pavilion Hilltop 197), reflecting KLCC's premium-on-space + SOHO/SOVO investment-light positioning strategy. Unique unit structure: Block C SOHO is single-spec 538-558 sqft small-unit targeting KLCC Airbnb operators and single young professionals; Blocks A/B SOVO 406-1,182 sqft covers Studio + Loft + Dual-key with product diversity but still small-unit dominant. Facilities include 80+ across multiple levels, highlights: Level 33 open terrace, Level 67 swimming pool + Jacuzzi, Level 68 gymnasium (high-floor pool + gym are KLCC luxury SA standard). Watch: (1) 2,215-unit scale means high facility usage density, elevator queues, parking — actual daily living experience challenges; (2) Block-to-Block facility allocation needs SPA confirmation.

On the developer side: Armani Group is Ipoh-based 2010-founded private mid-tier developer. Notable projects: (1) Armani Hallson KLCC (this case, GDV RM 3B, largest project to date); (2) Armani Prestige Jalan Mayang KL (59 storeys 350 units, 2024-08 launch, also off-plan); (3) Oakwood Cameron Highlands (383-key hotel). AmBank Group is financing partner providing structural banking support.

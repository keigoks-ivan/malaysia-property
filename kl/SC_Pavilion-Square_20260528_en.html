◆ The Narrative

Project Deep-Dive

Pavilion Square sits at the crossroads of Jalan Raja Chulan, Jalan Bukit Bintang, and Jalan Imbi — on the last large 2.02-acre development parcel in KL's Golden Triangle. The development is anchored by "living next to Pavilion KL": a Link Bridge connects directly to Pavilion KL Mall (KL's flagship retail destination with 700+ brands and 30M+ annual visitors), and the project is a 5-minute walk from Bukit Bintang MRT, Raja Chulan Monorail, and Jalan Alor's food street. Lot 10, Starhill Gallery, and Sungei Wang are within walking distance — the highest walkable retail density of any KL residential project, full stop.

Scale: a 67-storey residential tower with 960 units (Studio to 3BR) plus a 25-storey office tower (106 suites) and 6 retail lots, all delivered fully furnished. Unit sizes range from Type A 504-624 sqft (Studio/1BR) to Type C 1,255-1,272 sqft (3BR). Signature amenities include KL's longest 118m rooftop infinity pool, a 15,000 sqft Sky Wellness Zone, a Courtyard Garden, a Sky Terrace, and 24-hour concierge service. Fully furnished delivery lowers the move-in threshold for foreign owners.

The developer is Armani Hartajaya Sdn Bhd — not Pavilion Group directly, but a brand-licensed subsidiary linked to WCT Holdings Berhad and Malton Berhad (both Bursa-listed). The Pavilion brand is anchored by flagship projects such as Pavilion KL and Pavilion Bukit Jalil; the same-brand predecessor Pavilion Residences (completed 2009) provides a successful precedent. Buyers should still confirm with legal counsel the relationship between Armani Hartajaya and Pavilion Group, and the contractual status of the Link Bridge in the SPA.

On pricing: launch RM 2,300-2,420 psf sits 47% above same-brand Pavilion Residences (brickz 16-txn median RM 1,564 psf), with a worse tenure structure than the predecessor (Leasehold commercial vs Leasehold residential). For same-period comparison, TRX Residences (Freehold + Commercial HDA, materially superior tenure) launched at RM 2,293 psf — Pavilion Square asks more with worse tenure, which is a pricing-logic contradiction worth taking seriously. Brickz government-registered transactions: zero (off-plan), so all valuation reference is iRumah estimates and PropertyGuru listings — FIX-03 fails.

On the rental market: Studio 504 sqft asking rent is estimated at RM 4,000-5,000/mo (based on Bukit Bintang short-let / long-let medians), implying gross yield ≈ 4.7% — close to the 5% baseline. Management fees at RM 0.90 psf are 63% higher than Pavilion Residences (RM 0.55 psf); after 30% non-resident tax, foreign net yield is roughly 2.5%; under v1.3's assumption of 2.5-month vacancy reflecting Bukit Bintang SA overhang competition plus commercial electricity drag, this compresses to 1.5-1.9% — the lowest in the comparable KL set. Bukit Bintang SA overhang is the most stressed sub-segment in KL (Sky Suites YoY -6.6%).

Hold-period notes: Leasehold 99-yr (to 2122) + commercial-strata + off-plan 2028-2029 stack as multiple structural discounts. Foreign loan LTV varies most across banks (50-70%) — pre-confirmation with at least three banks is strongly recommended. Studio RM 1.159M just clears the RM 1M MM2H threshold, but Leasehold + commercial-strata is the least certain configuration for MM2H eligibility in the comparable set; obtain written legal confirmation. The only entry frame that holds water: "lifestyle-first owner-occupier, treating Pavilion KL access as the highest priority". For investment-led buyers (capital gain or rental yield), TRX Residences (Freehold + HDA, brickz already validated) is a structurally better choice at the same price point.

◆ The Verdict

Highlights & Items to Note

✦ Highlights
  • Direct link to Pavilion KL Mall (30M+ annual visitors) + Bukit Bintang MRT 5 min — the only "living inside Pavilion" experience in KL
  • Highest walkable retail density in KL: Jalan Alor, Lot 10, Starhill Gallery, Sungei Wang, KLCC all on foot
  • 118m rooftop infinity pool — KL's longest; 15,000 sqft Sky Wellness Zone; Fully Furnished delivery
  • Genuine scarcity: 2.02-acre Golden Triangle land is among the last large development parcels in the corridor
  • Same-brand predecessor Pavilion Residences delivered +74% appreciation over 16 years (launch RM 900 → now RM 1,564 psf), providing a brand-trajectory reference
⚠ Items to note
  • Leasehold 99-yr + commercial-strata is the worst tenure combination in the comparable set; launch RM 2,300 psf exceeds same-area Freehold + HDA TRX Residences — pricing logic warrants scrutiny
  • Brickz government-registered transactions: zero (off-plan, FIX-03 fails) — all valuation references are estimates, not real transactions; confidence is medium-low
  • Foreign LTV varies most across banks (50-70%); pre-confirm with at least 3 banks; MM2H Leasehold + commercial-strata eligibility needs lawyer's written confirmation
  • Foreign net yield 1.5-2.5% is lowest in the comparable set; maintenance RM 0.90 psf is 63% above the predecessor; commercial electricity conversion needs JMB confirmation
  • Bukit Bintang SA segment carries systemic overhang (Sky Suites YoY -6.6%); 960 new units will join post-VP supply competition; confirm whether Link Bridge is a contractual SPA obligation
◆ Audience

Buyer Profile Fit

Three typical buyer types and how this project fits each. Readers can position themselves accordingly.

👤Owner-occupier (expat executive / walking-lifestyle resident)
The strongest fit. The Pavilion KL direct link + Jalan Alor 5 min + Lot 10 walking combination has no equivalent in KL. Studio 504 sqft meets the MM2H threshold, and fully furnished delivery lowers the move-in barrier. Premise: accept Leasehold + commercial-strata structure, commercial electricity tariff, and the 2028-2029 completion wait.
📈Investment buyer (better options exist at the same budget)
The investment framing is weak here: Leasehold + commercial-strata discount + net yield 1.5-1.9% (lowest in the comparable set) + entry psf above same-budget Freehold competitors + zero Brickz transactions. At the same budget (RM 1.159M), TRX Residences (Freehold + Commercial HDA, brickz YoY +5.56%) offers better investment metrics across the board. The investment case here relies on Pavilion brand long-term appreciation potentially mirroring Pavilion Residences' 74% trajectory — but with worse underlying tenure.
🏠Pied-à-terre (lifestyle priority over returns)
Suits buyers explicitly "buying a lifestyle, not an investment asset" — anchored to the Pavilion KL experience with returns as secondary. Fully Furnished allows immediate occupancy; the 118m rooftop infinity pool and Sky Wellness Zone provide differentiated lifestyle amenities. Accept the higher management fee (RM 0.90 psf), commercial electricity, and Leasehold runoff. Short-let policy may be restricted — confirm Airbnb permissibility with the JMB in advance.

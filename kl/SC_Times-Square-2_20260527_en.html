◆ The Narrative

Project Deep-Dive

Times Square 2 sits on Jalan Imbi in the heart of Bukit Bintang, KL's most established tourism-and-retail district. A 41-storey serviced apartment block, its standout location advantage is the 100m walk to Imbi Monorail station (1–2 minutes); MRT Bukit Bintang is 750m away. Direct aerial skybridges connect to the developer group's adjacent Berjaya Times Square mall and LaLaport BBCC, while Pavilion Bukit Bintang, Lot 10, and Jalan Alor food street are 5–10 minutes on foot — making this the densest walking-retail catchment of any KL residential project.

The development holds 629 residential units plus 15 commercial lots on a 0.96-acre site — at 655 units/acre, this is a high-density build. Unit sizes range from 488 sqft (1BR) to 1,356 sqft (3BR + Dual Key), with the dual-key layout designed for Airbnb flexibility. Amenities include sky pools and sky gyms on Levels 7 and 41, pet-friendly common areas, fully fitted kitchens and bathrooms, smart locks, and GreenRE Gold sustainability certification.

The developer, Berjaya Times Square Sdn Bhd, is a subsidiary of Berjaya Asset within the Berjaya Corp group. The adjacent Berjaya Times Square mall (completed 2003) is the parent group's anchor asset, creating clear landlord-incentive alignment for property management and ecosystem integration. However, Berjaya Corp posted its largest-ever annual loss of RM556M in FY2025 (The Edge, August 2025); the group has no abandoned-project history, but quarterly financials and construction progress warrant ongoing monitoring.

Phase 1 launch pricing starts at RM 1,500 psf, with PropertyGuru listings ranging RM 1,138–1,869 psf. Versus the closest completed comparable — Dorsett Residensi Bukit Bintang at a Brickz 46-transaction median of RM 1,637 psf — TS2 carries roughly 8% of valuation cushion at launch. However, the same Bukit Bintang corridor is set to absorb 1,500 units from Agile Bukit Bintang plus pipeline from SWNK Houze BBCC over the next 2–3 years, putting downward pressure on rents and resale values. KL's serviced-apartment segment grew just ~1.5% annually (BambooRoutes Jan 2026), the slowest of any KL property type.

For the rental market, 678 sqft units in the area lease at RM 3,000–5,000/month for a gross yield around 3.85%. Bukit Bintang's Airbnb occupancy runs 80–95% with ADRs of RM 200–600/night (Ericanfly, October 2025) — the strongest short-let location in KL. For foreign holders, after 30% non-resident rental tax and elevated commercial electricity rates, long-let net yield drops to ~1.8% and Airbnb net yield to 2.8–3.2%. Rental income alone is insufficient to justify the hold; the investment thesis must combine yield with capital gain expectations.

Hold-period notes: freehold commercial SA is the best tenure within the SA segment, but commercial electricity rates (5–6× residential), higher mortgage rates (+0.5–1%), and weaker HDA protection are structural costs. The project meets the RM 1M threshold and qualifies as an MM2H mandatory asset (tenure type is not a restriction). The buyer pool excludes local first-home applicants (no EPF, weaker loan terms); secondary-market exit cycles average 3–5 years (NAPIC), so factor exit price flexibility into the hold plan.

◆ The Verdict

Highlights & Items to Note

✦ Highlights
  • Imbi Monorail at 100m — among the strongest transit walks of any KL launch in the RM1M+ tier
  • Bukit Bintang Airbnb occupancy 80–95%, ADR RM 200–600/night — the best short-let location fit in KL
  • Freehold commercial SA — the strongest tenure available in the SA segment; clear advantage vs leasehold competitors in the same corridor (e.g. SWNK Houze BBCC)
  • Launch PSF of RM 1,500 vs Dorsett Residensi BB completed comparable at RM 1,637 — roughly 8% valuation cushion at entry
  • Meets the RM 1M foreign-purchase threshold and qualifies as an MM2H mandatory asset (price threshold + freehold tenure)
⚠ Items to note
  • Commercial-tariff utility rates are 5–6× residential; consider confirming with the management committee whether residential rates can be applied
  • Parent Berjaya Corp posted a record FY2025 net loss of RM 556M; recommend tracking BJCORP / BERJ quarterlies and construction progress
  • Foreign purchase of commercial-title SA typically requires FIC (Foreign Investment Committee) approval or exemption — recommend confirming the exact procedure with legal counsel at the SPA stage
  • KL SA broad overhang of 22,314 units (NAPIC Q3 2025), plus Agile Bukit Bintang's 1,500-unit pipeline — monitor same-tower resale listing volumes

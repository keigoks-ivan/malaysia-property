◆ Deep Narrative

Project Deep Summary

The Conlay sits at the corner of Jalan Conlay and Jalan Kia Peng, the southwest tip of KLCC's golden triangle — a 1.44-acre freehold parcel jointly developed by Eastern & Oriental Berhad (Bursa:3417, T1 tier) and Mitsui Fudosan Group (TSE:8801, one of Japan's largest property developers). The joint venture has been active since 2013, with Conlay completed Q1 2025 as the latest of three KL projects (Conlay/KLCC, The Mews/Jalan Yap Kwan Seng, The Peak/Damansara Heights). Mitsui Fudosan's persistent commitment to KL luxury via E&O represents one of the rare Tokyo-listed institutional capital deployments in the segment. Conlay's architecture is by Kerry Hill Architects — the firm behind the Aman resort series — with a 51-storey picture-framed façade as its signature visual identity. Conlay MRT station (PY22 Putrajaya Line) sits within 200m / 5-min walk, operational since March 2023, making The Conlay one of the few mature transit-oriented luxury assets at the KLCC fringe.

On location: Conlay anchors against the KLCC core 8.0 district baseline; under the v5.0 three-layer supply audit both the land and product layers fail ✗ (KLCC core keeps producing branded luxury towers; the 491-unit high-density branded SR is replicable in-district), only the freehold regulation layer passes ✓, so with front-row +0.2 and high replicability −0.5 the scarcity & location moat nets 7.7 — the location brand is still KL top-tier, but supply is not structurally capped. Lifestyle coverage: Pavilion KL (KL's flagship retail) 8-10 min walk; KLCC Twin Towers 1.5km; KLCC Park; TRX one MRT stop away. KLCC is KL's strongest walking-density lifestyle cluster, covering international retail, five-star hotels, Michelin-listed dining, Twin Towers commercial, and Bukit Bintang tourism nightlife. MRT3 Circle Line completion 2032 (groundbreaking 2027) will further integrate KL's ring rail network — Conlay MRT as an intersection node will benefit from a second-wave re-rating.

Project scale: 1.44 acres, single 51-storey tower, 491 units, sizes 1BR 743 sqft to 3BR 1,335 sqft. Density 341 units/acre is typical of KLCC luxury cluster (vs Mont Kiara 80-200, Desa ParkCity 30-50), reflecting the high-rise design logic of KLCC's land scarcity. E&O provides 5-star hospitality services: 24h concierge, housekeeping, personal chef option — top-tier configuration among KL serviced residences, targeting international corporate expatriates, MM2H high-net-worth holders, and KLCC vacation short-stay segment. Phase 1 was delivered Q1 2025; Phase 2 ("Conlay Signature Suites") soft-launched July 2025 with 40% take-up — reflects sustained KLCC high-end demand but also means ~27 final units remain negotiable (data as of 2025-11).

On the developer side: E&O × Mitsui Fudosan scores 8.0 — the highest among scorecards analyzed alongside Park Regent and OCP (both 8.5). E&O is Bursa T1 tier; Q4 2025 (ended 2025-03) net profit RM 69.85M vs prior year RM 36.48M nearly doubled; FY2025 full-year revenue RM 741.1M (+75% YoY) — but EPS missed estimates by 34%, reflecting operational pressure during the peak of new launches + delivery. Buyers should track subsequent quarterly financials. Mitsui Fudosan as a Tokyo-listed century-old enterprise brings persistence + institutional execution — core to The Conlay's brand value. Caveat: project completed only 8 months ago, JMB operation has not passed ≥2-year defect liability validation. Buyers should inspect facility operations, Mitsui hospitality service delivery quality, and Phase 1 vs Phase 2 management consistency on-site.

On pricing: Conlay cross-unit PSF range RM 2,030 (2BR) to RM 2,500 (3BR), median ~RM 2,200 — 15-30% discount to top KLCC luxury cluster (Four Seasons Place RM 3,010, Pavilion Suites RM 3,150), parity with direct peer 8 Conlay (YNH × Kempinski, RM 1,278-3,293 averaging ~RM 2,200), but above Banyan Tree Signatures (RM 1,716 median). Vs KLCC overall non-luxury 2024 sold avg RM 1.2M / RM 1,200 psf median, Conlay commands 60-100% premium reflecting branded luxury positioning. Buyers should note Phase 2 soft-launch early-bird discounts, furniture packages, legal fee waivers — typical 5-15% rebate space; actual net price may be significantly below asking PSF. Brickz sample <10 tx provides no transacted price transparency; all pricing judgement relies on KLCC luxury cluster peer comparisons.

On the rental market: 2BR 1,206 sqft fully furnished long-let RM 10,000/mo (RM 8.30 psf) as current listed benchmark (PropertyGuru/iProperty 2025-11); 1BR 710 sqft RM 5,800/mo (RM 8.17 psf). Gross yield ~4.9% — within the 4.5-5.0% anchor band. Local net yield ~3.0%, spread −1.6%; foreign 30% non-resident tax-after net yield ~2.0%, spread −3.0% — both negative-spread, capital gain dependency. KLCC luxury serviced has two main tenant streams: (1) long-term international corporate expat (Shell, Petronas, multinational bank senior, 2-3-year contracts with stability); (2) short-stay tourism + business. Conlay's 5-star hospitality + Kerry Hill brand supports rental premium, but as the full 491 units (Phase 1 + Phase 2) flow into the long-let market, the KLCC SA cluster competition (incl. 8 Conlay post-completion) on rental PSF over the 5-10 year horizon is a core variable.

On holding structure: Freehold commercial serviced-apartment title (commercial SA, like Stonor 3) + HDA Schedule H protection retained + commercial ~85% LTV local (vs residential 90%) + commercial utilities 1.3-1.6x — moderate institutional holding friction, with the commercial-title financing premium (~+0.75% on the mortgage) embedded. MM2H mandatory asset: 2BR 1,206 sqft × RM 2.45M ≫ RM 1M Federal Territory threshold, ≫ all MM2H Silver/Gold/Platinum asset thresholds ✓ comfortably clears. 10-yr exit outlook: under base 3%/yr growth, Year 10 price ~RM 3.29M, local net P&L ~RM 495k (IRR 2-4%), foreign ~RM 187k (IRR 1-2%). Exit buyer pool: KLCC luxury high-end foreign buyers 30-40% typical (including MM2H holders, multinational business, regional investors); Mitsui Fudosan international brand recognition is the key exit support. (4) E&O FY2025 EPS missed 34%; track parent financials + Mitsui ongoing investment commitment. (6) KL SA overhang 3,643 units (NAPIC Q3 2025) overall pressure mostly concentrated below RM 1M, but RM 2.45M+ tier buyer sensitivity to market cycle is higher.

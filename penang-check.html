Airbnb yield is an estimated gross short-stay yield (area ADR × ~60% occupancy ÷ entry price, anchored to comparable stock in the same Penang submarket), shown only for serviced apartments that permit and market owner-operated short-stay — an upside scenario for an owner who actively operates (or appoints a manager for) the unit, not the base case. It is not shown for hotel rental-pool products (e.g. a branded 70/30 pool), whose short-stay income accrues on different operator-split economics, nor where building by-laws restrict daily lets. Holding-cost note: a commercial-title SA can switch from the commercial to the domestic electricity tariff (and claim owner-occupier RPGT treatment) only when genuinely used as a residence; running it as short-stay keeps the commercial tariff and forfeits those reliefs — so the cash-flow read differs for a long-let/owner-occupier versus an Airbnb operator. Airbnb 收益是估算的短租毛收益率（拿區域 ADR 乘上約 60% 入住率、再除以入手價，並對齊同一檳城次市場內的可比案例），只標在「容許、而且主打屋主自營短租」的服務式公寓上，算的是屋主自己跑（或請人代管）的潛力情境，不是基準。酒店租賃池那一種，例如品牌方代管的 70/30 分潤池，走的是另一套分潤帳；分層細則禁日租的也一樣，都不列這個數。持有成本補一句：商業地契的 SA，要真的拿來自住，才能把商業電費改回住宅費率、也才適用自住 RPGT；一旦拿去跑短租，電費照商業算、那些好處也一併沒了。所以同一間房的現金流，長租自住跟 Airbnb 營運，得拆開來看。

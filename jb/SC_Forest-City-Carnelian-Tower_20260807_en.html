Forest City — Carnelian Tower

This card deliberately anchors the low end of the JB scale — it is not a "worth buying" recommendation. Of five phases currently on-sale, this card selects Carnelian Tower (a mixed mall + office + serviced-apartment tower) per R-J5 phase-selection priority — reasoning under location. Location floor of 3.0 is the lowest of all eight JB districts, commercial title triggers R-J1's overhang veto, tower-level transaction evidence is thin (only one published transaction found), and asking prices carry a substantial premium over the district reference anchor. Thin evidence is itself part of this card's conclusion: this is what the low end of the JB scale looks like.

◆ Project Deep Dive

Project Deep Dive

Forest City is Country Garden's large-scale cross-border development built on man-made islands off the coast of Iskandar Puteri in western Johor, developed by Country Garden Pacificview Sdn Bhd (CGPV, a joint venture between Country Garden and Esplanade Danga 88, an affiliate of Johor state entity KPRJ). The official website currently lists five phases on sale: Golf Villa and Anise Pearl (both landed), Carnelian Tower, Nigella Park, and a 62-tower "Serviced Apartments" label. This card, per R-J5 phase-selection priority 1, selects Carnelian Tower — a 45-storey mixed mall + office + serviced-apartment tower — because it simultaneously satisfies both the "currently on-sale" and "completed" conditions, and its pricing (RM479,000-1,488,000) shows the highest overlap with the existing six JB scorecards' price band, with no ambiguity in the selection process (see Location for details).

Tenure is this project's most notable dispute: the official website currently lists all phases as "Freehold," but in 2017 the then-Prime Minister publicly clarified this was actually a "99-year land lease," while the Sultan of Johor separately clarified that "the land itself is held freehold by the company, and buyers receive freehold strata title" — two positions coexist without a clear final official resolution, and this card discloses both sides honestly rather than picking one. On title category, given this project's mixed mall + office + serviced-apartment product form, this card classifies it as commercial title (triggering R-J1), moderate confidence — no single authoritative source was found explicitly stating "commercial title," one of this card's honestly disclosed data gaps.

On the Location Moat dimension, Forest City's district floor is only 3.0 — the lowest of all eight JB districts. This is not this card's judgment but an existing locked framework fact: occupancy below 10% (two independent sources, Bloomberg and Tomorrow.City, mutually corroborating), secondary transactions down 40-60% from launch, and parent company Country Garden's 2025-12-30 offshore debt restructuring, which carved Forest City out of the listed corporate structure. On Connectivity, this card's newly mandatory individual-level measurement found that the roughly 5km stretch between Forest City and CIQ/Bangunan Sultan Iskandar has no footpath at all, with public cross-border transit guides explicitly warning it is extremely dangerous and pedestrians should not attempt it — triggering a negative item on the connectivity individual-adjustment table, in sharp contrast to this framework's other Danga Bay/JB CBD cases, which commonly feature a covered 10-minute walking bridge.

Appreciation and Developer carry this project's most serious evidence. Forest City's overall occupancy has remained persistently low, meeting this framework's "sister phase from the same developer, delivered ≥12 months, occupancy <50%" individual-deduction trigger; Country Garden Holdings' global offshore debt default and restructuring explicitly carved Forest City out of the listed corporate structure in the restructuring documents — a document-level fact naming this specific development, deducted at a magnitude calibrated against this framework's most severe existing precedent (MB World/Astaka's governance failure). Valuation is equally severe: this card's only known real Carnelian Tower transaction (RM500,890, reported by EdgeProp's "Done Deal" column in September 2025, reconstructed via search-engine index snippets since the original article returned a 403) sits at a 53-131% premium over Forest City's district reference anchor (596 psf, the five-year, more-conservative reading), depending on whether asking or transacted psf is used — far beyond this framework's harshest valuation-ladder tier's definition, and the direct reason this card's valuation score sits at this framework's floor.

The Exit narrative is more layered. On the local buyer-pool side, the representative-unit quantum (RM500,890) implies a required monthly income of approximately RM5,400 — actually below Johor's median household income of RM7,712, one of this card's few positive findings, meaning this project is not unaffordable to local buyers in pure DSR terms. On the foreign buyer-pool side, this project's quantum happens to sit just above Forest City's MM2H SEZ RM500,000 exception threshold (exceeding it by only RM890, a genuine boundary case), allowing it to qualify for the cap-4.0 treatment rather than the harsher cap-2.5 treatment that would apply outside Forest City — a concrete institutional advantage unique to Forest City as the only development in Johor retaining an intermediate RM500,000 threshold. ◆ Conclusion

Highlights & Caveats

✦ Highlights
  • The representative-unit quantum (RM500,890) implies a required household monthly income of approximately RM5,400 — below Johor's median household income of RM7,712, affordable to a local household in pure debt-service terms
  • Forest City retains Johor's only MM2H SEZ RM500,000 intermediate threshold, giving this project (whose quantum sits just above that threshold) a narrow but legal foreign-buyer channel that a comparable Johor project at the same quantum outside Forest City would not have
  • If eligible for the SFZ 50% stamp-duty remission (not applied in this card's 10-year cost table, eligibility unverified case-by-case), both Malaysian and foreign stamp-duty costs could be roughly halved
  • The claimed freehold tenure covers the strata-title level (see caveats below regarding the land-level dispute), and the developer is a globally listed, financially transparent HKEX group (transparency itself is a double-edged sword, see caveats)
⚠ Caveats
  • 【Lowest location floor in JB】Forest City's overall occupancy is <10% and secondary prices are down 40-60% from launch — locked framework facts, not this card's speculation, and the direct basis for this project's 3.0 location score (the lowest of all eight JB districts)
  • 【Unresolved tenure dispute】the official website lists "Freehold," but a 2017 official clarification produced two different accounts — "99-year land lease" versus "freehold land held by the company, with buyers receiving strata title" — readers should independently verify the tenure documents with the developer
  • 【CIQ walking not feasible】this round's measurement found no footpath along the roughly 5km stretch between Forest City and the main Causeway checkpoint, with official travel guides explicitly warning it is dangerous to walk — a sharp contrast with this framework's other Danga Bay/JB CBD cases' walking advantages
  • 【Parent-company financial and governance risk】Country Garden Holdings' global offshore debt default and restructuring became effective 2025-12-30 and explicitly carved Forest City out of the listed corporate structure, leaving future funding sources and governance transparency less clear
  • 【Substantial valuation premium】a 53-131% premium over Forest City's district reference anchor, combined with extremely thin tower-level transaction evidence (only one known transaction), leaves both buyers and sellers without a reliable self-referencing case for negotiation or exit pricing

How this is written這些內容怎麼寫的

Three rules, applied to every page三條規則，套用在每一頁

Numbers need a reference frame數字要有參照系

A figure on its own says nothing. Every important number is shown against its own history and against comparable markets on the same definition — and where two sources disagree, both are shown rather than one being quietly picked.單一數字說明不了任何事。重要的數字一律同時給出它自己的歷史序列，以及同口徑的跨市場對照；兩個來源打架時，兩個都列出來，而不是悄悄挑一個。

Frameworks must be falsifiable框架必須可被證偽

Every model on this site states the conditions under which it would be wrong, before you reach the conclusion. Signals that failed a forward-frozen test are listed as failures instead of being deleted.站上每一個模型，都在結論之前先寫出「什麼情況代表它錯了」。前瞻凍結檢定沒通過的訊號，會被列為失敗，而不是被刪掉。

Nothing here is for sale站上沒有東西在賣

No listings, no referrals, no developer relationships, no lead capture. The scorecards rate projects the author holds no position in, which is the only reason a rating is worth reading.沒有物件、沒有轉介、沒有與發展商的合作、不收名單。評分卡評的是作者沒有部位的案子——這也是一份評分唯一值得看的理由。

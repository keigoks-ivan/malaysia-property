Bottom line總結 Credit is not constrained in the İstanbul housing market. It is absent. Mortgaged transactions were 10.8% of Turkish house sales in 2024 against 40.0% in 2013, and 13.4% in İstanbul against 45.8%. The nominal housing loan rate sits between 41% and 47%. Household credit fell from 19.4% of GDP in the third quarter of 2013 to 9.4% in 2024, and not by repayment: the nominal stock rose 5.7 times between the first quarter of 2022 and the fourth quarter of 2025. Inflation voided the instrument. Roughly six in seven Turkish house purchases now clear on cash, on inheritance or on a developer instalment plan.

This is the third distinct credit mechanism documented on this site, and the three are worth stating together. Bangkok is a market where the bank will not lend, with rejection rates of 40% to 50% on applications that were made. Dubai is a market that does not need to borrow, with 86% of purchases settling in cash by choice. İstanbul is a market where the instrument exists on paper and inflation has made it irrelevant. The three produce different price behaviour, different downside mechanics and different early-warning series, and confusing them is the most common error in cross-market property analysis.

The return has inverted. Rebased to the 2023 average, the new-tenant rent index stands at 322 against a house price index of 231; real prices are 18.6% below their January 2023 reading in İstanbul while real rents more than doubled between 2020 and 2025. The İstanbul gross yield went from 4.28% at the 2022 price peak to 6.09% in the second quarter of 2026. The mechanism is a lapsed statute: the 25% renewal cap introduced by Law 7409 in June 2022 and extended by Law 7456 expired on 1 July 2024 with no third extension, while new lettings ran near 80% a year. The macro conditions that produced all of this are set out in the companion Türkiye Macro × Property analysis. 伊斯坦堡住宅市場的信用並非受到限制，而是不存在。2024 年房貸成交佔土耳其房屋成交的 10.8%，2013 年為 40.0%；伊斯坦堡為 13.4%，2013 年為 45.8%。名目住宅貸款利率介於 41% 至 47%。家庭信用佔 GDP 比重自 2013 年第三季的 19.4% 降至 2024 年的 9.4%，其成因並非償還：名目餘額於 2022 年第一季至 2025 年第四季間增為 5.7 倍。通膨使該工具失效。土耳其現約每七筆購屋中有六筆以現金、繼承或建商分期方案完成。

此為本站記載的第三種信用機制，三者值得並列陳述。曼谷是銀行不放款的市場，已提出的申請退件率為 40% 至 50%。杜拜是不需要借款的市場，86% 的購置基於選擇而以現金交割。伊斯坦堡是工具在紙面上存在、而通膨使其失去意義的市場。三者產生不同的價格行為、不同的下行機制與不同的預警序列，混淆三者為跨市場房產分析中最常見的錯誤。

報酬來源已經反轉。以 2023 年平均為基期，新租租金指數為 322，房價指數為 231；伊斯坦堡實質房價低於 2023 年 1 月讀數 18.6%，同期實質租金於 2020 至 2025 年間增加逾一倍。伊斯坦堡毛收益率自 2022 年房價高點的 4.28% 升至 2026 年第二季的 6.09%。其機制為一項已失效的法律規定：2022 年 6 月第 7409 號法律所設、並由第 7456 號法律延長的 25% 續約調漲上限，於 2024 年 7 月 1 日到期且未第三度延長，同期新出租的租金年增接近 80%。促成上述情形的總體條件詳見對照的《土耳其總經 × 房產》。

Two facts have to be held together. Real İstanbul prices compounded at 4.9% a year across the full sixteen years, which is a genuine real return and is the strongest argument against extrapolating the current decline. Over the last three and a half years they have compounded at −5.8% a year. The market did not lose money over the long run; it repriced violently upward between 2019 and 2023 on a real funding rate that reached −74 percentage points, and it has been giving part of that back ever since the rate turned. The statutory position, verified against the consolidated text法律狀態，已對照彙編文本查證

The permanent rule is article 344 of the Turkish Code of Obligations, Law No. 6098: an agreement on the rent for a renewed lease term is valid provided it does not exceed the rate of change in the twelve-month average of the consumer price index for the preceding lease year, and where the parties have not agreed, the judge sets the rent subject to the same ceiling.   • The mortgaged share of transactions rises back above 20% for four consecutive quarters while the real policy rate stays negative. Breaks argument one: the instrument would have been suppressed by price rather than voided by inflation, and the standard credit-cycle framework would apply to Türkiye again.房貸成交佔比於實質政策利率為負的期間連續四季回升至 20% 以上。推翻論點一：該工具係遭價格壓抑而非遭通膨廢止，標準信用循環架構將重新適用於土耳其。
  • Inflation falls into the high teens without a currency event and the housing loan rate follows it below 25%. The credit channel reopens on a positive real rate, which is the disinflation-completes scenario and the one in which İstanbul property performs best.通膨於未發生貨幣事件的情況下降至十餘個百分點，且住宅貸款利率隨之降至 25% 以下。信用管道於正實質利率環境下重啟，此即反通膨完成情境，亦為伊斯坦堡房產表現最佳的情境。
  • İstanbul real house prices rise for four consecutive quarters while the real policy rate stays positive. The eleven-quarter decline would have ended without the rate turning, which means something outside this report's framework is setting the price.伊斯坦堡實質房價於實質政策利率維持為正的期間連續四季上升。十一季的跌勢於利率未反轉的情況下結束，代表設定價格者為本報告架構之外的因素。
  • The citizenship threshold is lowered and İstanbul foreign sales exceed 2,000 a month. That would restore the marginal buyer removed in June 2022, and it is the fastest-acting single change available to this market.公民身分門檻調降，且伊斯坦堡外資成交超過每月 2,000 件。該情形恢復 2022 年 6 月被移除的邊際買方，亦為本市場可得之作用最快的單一變動。

Falsified to the downside if若出現以下，向下證偽

  • The İstanbul gross yield compresses back below 5% while real prices resume rising. Breaks argument three: capital gain would be the return source again and the case for buying on the rent line would be removed at exactly the wrong point in the cycle.伊斯坦堡毛收益率壓縮回 5% 以下，而實質房價恢復上升。推翻論點三：資本利得重新成為報酬來源，而以租金線為承作基礎的理由將於循環中最不利的時點被移除。
  • The nominal house price index falls in any single quarter. It has not done so once in sixteen years. A first nominal decline would mean the inflation-hedge bid documented throughout this report had failed, and the correction would stop being slow.名目房價指數於任一單季下跌。該情形於十六年間未曾發生。首次名目下跌代表本報告全篇所載的通膨避險買盤已失效，修正將不再緩慢。
  • İstanbul new-let rents fall year on year for two consecutive quarters. The return is the rent line; a break there removes the only engine, since real capital growth has been negative for three and a half years. Growth has already decelerated from 175.0% to 35.4%.伊斯坦堡新租租金連續兩季年減。報酬來源為租金線，該線斷裂即移除唯一的報酬引擎，因實質資本利得已為負三年半。其成長率已自 175.0% 減速至 35.4%。
  • İstanbul-level construction permits are published and show the city taking a share of the national surge proportionate to its population. Part IV concludes that İstanbul supply risk is lower than the national permit count implies, on the evidence of falling first-hand sales. Province-level permit data would test that directly, and it is the largest single gap in this report.伊斯坦堡層級建照資料公布，並顯示該市於全國激增中所佔份額與其人口比重相當。第四部依一手成交下降的證據，判斷伊斯坦堡供給風險低於全國建照數所隱含者。省級建照資料可直接檢定該項判斷，亦為本報告最大的單一資料缺口。
What this report concludes本報告的結論

İstanbul is the third distinct credit mechanism documented on this site and the only one in which the instrument was removed by the price level rather than by a bank or by a preference. Mortgaged transactions were 10.8% of the Turkish market in 2024 against 40.0% in 2013, and household credit halved as a share of GDP while its nominal stock rose 5.7 times. The consequences are specific and they are not intuitive: there is no forced-seller channel, the nominal price index cannot fall, and a real correction of 17.9% has run for eleven quarters without producing a single quarter of nominal decline.伊斯坦堡為本站所記載的第三種信用機制，亦為唯一一個工具係遭物價水準而非遭銀行或偏好移除者。2024 年房貸成交佔土耳其市場的 10.8%，2013 年為 40.0%；家庭信用佔 GDP 比重減半，同期名目餘額增為 5.7 倍。其後果具體且非直覺：不存在強制賣方管道，名目房價指數無法下跌，且一次 17.9% 的實質修正歷經十一季而未產生任何一季的名目下跌。

The access rule is the one variable in this market whose effect can be measured cleanly, and it has been measured twice in opposite directions. The September 2018 loosening took monthly foreign sales from 4,159 to 6,134; the June 2022 tightening took İstanbul from 4,452 to 1,274 in a single month. The common account attributing the 2022 İstanbul market to Russian buyers is wrong on the transaction record: Russian buying peaked five months after the change and 83% of it was outside İstanbul. The flows the threshold removed were Iranian and Iraqi, and they have not been replaced.准入規則為本市場唯一一項其效果可被乾淨量測的變數，且已於兩個相反方向上各量測一次。2018 年 9 月的放寬使月頻外資成交自 4,159 件升至 6,134 件；2022 年 6 月的調高使伊斯坦堡於單月自 4,452 件降至 1,274 件。將 2022 年伊斯坦堡市場歸因於俄羅斯買方的通行說法，於成交紀錄上為錯誤：俄羅斯買盤的高點在該次變動後五個月，且其中 83% 位於伊斯坦堡以外。門檻所移除的流量為伊朗與伊拉克買方，且未被取代。

The return has inverted, and the mechanism is a lapsed statute rather than a market judgement. Real İstanbul rents and prices both peaked in 2023; since then real prices have fallen to 147.5 and real rents have risen to 206.5 on a 2018 = 100 base, and the gross yield has expanded from 4.28% to 6.09%. The 25% renewal cap expired on 1 July 2024 with no third extension while new lettings ran near 80% a year, and that wedge is now largely spent as new-let growth has decelerated to 35.4%. A position in this market is a position on the rent line, taken at a dollar entry price that is at an all-time high because the real exchange rate is. The macro conditions that decide all three arguments are set out in the companion Türkiye Macro × Property analysis.報酬來源已經反轉，其機制為一項已失效的法律規定，而非市場判斷。伊斯坦堡的實質租金與實質房價均於 2023 年見頂；以 2018＝100 為基期，其後實質房價降至 147.5，實質租金升至 206.5，毛收益率自 4.28% 擴張至 6.09%。25% 續約上限於 2024 年 7 月 1 日到期且未第三度延長，同期新出租租金年增接近 80%；隨新出租成長率減速至 35.4%，該落差現已大致耗盡。於本市場建立的部位即為租金線部位，且其進場時的美元價格處於歷史高點，因實質匯率正處歷史高位。決定上述三項論點的總體條件，詳見對照的《土耳其總經 × 房產》。

Bottom line總結 Turkish property has been priced by one variable for five years, and that variable is the real policy rate. Between November 2021 and June 2023 the central bank cut into accelerating inflation; the effective funding cost less consumer price inflation reached −74 percentage points in November 2022. Real house prices rose 168% in the four years to the second quarter of 2023. The stance then reversed to a peak policy rate of 50.00% in March 2024, the real rate crossed into positive territory in September 2024, and real prices have fallen in every quarter since the 2023 peak. The correction has run entirely through inflation: nominal prices have not declined in a single quarter over the whole sixteen-year series.

The second consequence of that inflation is structural rather than cyclical. Household credit fell from 19.4% of GDP in the third quarter of 2013 to 9.4% in 2024, and not by repayment — the nominal stock rose 5.7 times between the first quarter of 2022 and the fourth quarter of 2025. Price levels destroyed the real value of the debt faster than new lending replaced it. Mortgaged sales were 10.8% of Turkish house transactions in 2024 against 40.0% in 2013. The credit channel through which rate policy reaches house prices in most markets does not operate here at any material scale, which is set out transaction by transaction in the companion Istanbul Property Market Report.

The binding constraint is external, and it is deteriorating now. Reserves excluding gold fell from US$61.1bn in January 2026 to US$39.2bn in June 2026, a fall of US$21.9bn in five months to the lowest reading since the January 2021 trough, while the real effective exchange rate rose from 104.9 to 108.7 over the same months. Defending an appreciating real lira on a falling reserve stock is the configuration that broke in 2018 and again in 2021. We regard this as the single most important observable risk on this page, and it is not yet reflected in the general commentary on the market. 土耳其房產於近五年由單一變數定價，該變數為實質政策利率。2021 年 11 月至 2023 年 6 月間，央行於通膨加速期間持續降息；有效資金成本扣除消費者物價年增率後，於 2022 年 11 月達 −74 個百分點。至 2023 年第二季止的四年間，實質房價上漲 168%。政策立場其後反轉，政策利率於 2024 年 3 月升至 50.00% 的高點，實質利率於 2024 年 9 月轉正，實質房價自 2023 年高點起逐季下跌。該次修正完全經由通膨完成：於全部十六年的序列中，名目房價未曾出現單季下跌。

通膨的第二項後果屬結構性而非循環性。家庭信用佔 GDP 比重自 2013 年第三季的 19.4% 降至 2024 年的 9.4%，其成因並非償還：名目餘額於 2022 年第一季至 2025 年第四季間增為 5.7 倍。物價水準摧毀債務實質價值的速度，快於新增貸放的補充速度。2024 年土耳其房屋成交中使用房貸者佔 10.8%，2013 年為 40.0%。於多數市場中將利率政策傳導至房價的信用管道，於此地並不以具規模的方式運作，其逐筆成交的證據見對照的《伊斯坦堡住宅市場報告》。

真正的約束條件在對外帳，且正在劣化。外匯存底（不含黃金）自 2026 年 1 月的 611 億美元降至 2026 年 6 月的 392 億美元，五個月減少 219 億美元，為 2021 年 1 月谷底以來最低；同期實質有效匯率自 104.9 升至 108.7。以下降中的存底支撐升值中的實質里拉，即為 2018 年與 2021 年兩度失守的組合。我們認為此為本頁最重要的可觀察風險，且尚未反映於市場的一般評論。

Which rate is the policy rate哪一個才是政策利率

The distinction, stated so it can be checked此一區別的可查核陳述

The one-week repo rate is the announced policy rate and is what the central bank's own decision table records: 37.00% since 23 January 2026. The average cost of funding is what the central bank actually charged the banking system: it equals the announced rate exactly in calm periods, and sits above it when the bank funds off the upper band. It has been 40.00% from April to July 2026, three points above the announced rate. The OECD immediate rate is the overnight borrowing rate, not the policy rate, and reads 35.50%. The three coincide at 50.00, 50.00 and 47.00 in mid-2024, which is what identifies them.一週期附買回利率為公告政策利率，即央行決議表所載者：2026 年 1 月 23 日起為 37.00%。平均資金成本為央行實際向銀行體系收取的利率：於平穩期間與公告利率完全相等，於央行以利率走廊上緣供給資金時則高於公告利率。該項於 2026 年 4 至 7 月為 40.00%，高於公告利率 3 個百分點。OECD 即期利率為隔夜借款利率而非政策利率，讀數為 35.50%。三者於 2024 年年中分別為 50.00、50.00 與 47.00，該一致性即為辨識三者的依據。

The distinction is not academic. A real-rate calculation using the OECD series understates the current stance by 450 basis points against the average cost of funding, which is the difference between a modestly restrictive policy and a firmly restrictive one. This page uses the average cost of funding for the stance and the decision table for the narrative, and shows the OECD readings only as marked points.此一區別具實務意義。以 OECD 序列計算實質利率，相對於平均資金成本將低估當前政策緊縮程度達 450 個基點，該差距即為溫和緊縮與明確緊縮之別。本頁以平均資金成本衡量政策立場、以決議表敘述政策歷程，OECD 讀數僅以標記點呈現。

Any chart pairing the announced rate with house prices across that span is measuring the wrong variable.關於公告利率的另一項注意事項：2017 年至 2018 年 5 月間，一週期附買回實質上暫停使用，央行改以後期流動性窗口供給資金，公告政策利率因此於一年以上的期間低估實際利率。任何跨越該期間、將公告利率與房價並列的圖表，量測的是錯誤的變數。

Seventy years of Turkish inflation七十年的土耳其通膨

Türkiye has recorded double-digit January inflation in the large majority of the years for which the series exists, including a reading of 125.9% in January 1995 and 101.6% in January 1998. The fifteen years from 2003 to 2017, in which inflation ran between 4.9% and 10.6%, are the anomaly in this record rather than the baseline from which the current episode departed. The practical consequence for a property investor is that the inflation-hedging motive behind Turkish housing demand is not a response to a recent shock; it is a learned behaviour with a seventy-year sample behind it, and it does not disappear when a single disinflation succeeds.於序列存在的年份中，土耳其於絕大多數年度的 1 月錄得兩位數通膨，其中包括 1995 年 1 月的 125.9% 與 1998 年 1 月的 101.6%。2003 至 2017 年這十五年通膨介於 4.9% 至 10.6% 之間，於此紀錄中屬異常值，而非本輪通膨所偏離的基準。對房產投資人的實務意涵為：土耳其住宅需求背後的通膨避險動機，並非對近期衝擊的反應，而是具七十年樣本的習得行為，不會因單一次反通膨成功而消失。

The conclusions live in the interactions關鍵結論位於交互作用

  • Real funding rate × inflation-hedge demand. These are the two largest terms and they work against each other. A positive real rate gives a household a lira-denominated alternative to housing for the first time since 2019, so the hedging motive weakens exactly when the discount rate rises. That double effect is why the real price decline has been continuous rather than cyclical, and why it has run through eleven quarters without a single nominal fall.實質資金利率 × 通膨避險需求。兩者為式中最大的兩項，且方向相反。正實質利率使家戶自 2019 年以來首次擁有以里拉計價的住宅替代選項，避險動機因此於折現率上升的同一時點減弱。此一雙重效果即為實質房價跌勢呈連續而非循環形態的原因，亦為其歷經十一季而未出現任何一次名目下跌的原因。
  • Real exchange rate × external reserves. The real appreciation that removed the foreign-buyer discount is being financed from the reserve stock. If the reserve constraint binds, the adjustment is a step depreciation, which restores the foreign discount and raises inflation at the same time. The two paths out of the current configuration are therefore not symmetric: one restores foreign demand and destroys the real rate, the other holds the real rate and continues to drain the reserve.實質匯率 × 外匯存底。移除外資折讓的實質升值，其資金來源為存底。若存底約束成立，調整方式為階梯式貶值，該情形同時恢復外資折讓並推高通膨。當前配置的兩條出路因此並不對稱：一條恢復外資需求並摧毀實質利率，另一條維持實質利率並持續消耗存底。
  • Permitted supply × population growth. Permits rose 39.1% in 2025 to 1.19 million dwellings while population growth fell to 0.23% in 2024. The supply pipeline is being loaded against a household-formation base that is decelerating. The lag between a permit and a completion is two to four years, which places the arrival of that supply in 2027 to 2029.核准供給 × 人口成長。2025 年建照核發量年增 39.1% 至 119 萬戶，而人口成長率於 2024 年降至 0.23%。供給正對著一個減速中的家戶形成基礎累積。建照至完工的時間差為二至四年，該批供給的到達時點因此落於 2027 至 2029 年。

The Answer — Where, What, When結論篇 · 區位、產品、時點

Where區位

Not İstanbul on price momentum. The city's nominal index has risen 206% since December 2022 against Ankara's 301%, a gap of 95 percentage points in three and a half years, and its real index has fallen 18.6% from the January 2023 reading against a national 11.7%. İstanbul is the right market for rent, tenant depth and liquidity; it has been the wrong one for capital growth for three years running.不以價格動能為由選擇伊斯坦堡。該市名目指數自 2022 年 12 月起上漲 206%，安卡拉為 301%，三年半內差距 95 個百分點；其實質指數自 2023 年 1 月讀數下跌 18.6%，全國為 11.7%。伊斯坦堡為租金、租客深度與流動性的正確市場，惟已連續三年不是資本利得的正確市場。

What產品

Completed stock with a verifiable letting record, bought on the rent line. The gross yield has expanded from 4.28% at the 2022 price peak to 6.09% in the second quarter of 2026, and that expansion is the entire investment case. Off-plan and instalment structures carry the lira inflation risk the buyer believes the property is hedging, and they deliver into the 2027 to 2029 permit pipeline.具可查證出租紀錄的已完工物件，並以租金線為承作基礎。毛收益率自 2022 年房價高點的 4.28% 擴張至 2026 年第二季的 6.09%，該擴張即為投資論述的全部。預售與分期結構承擔買方認為該房產正在對沖的里拉通膨風險，且其交屋時點落於 2027 至 2029 年的建照供給區間。

When時點

The decision does not turn on the price index. It turns on the reserve series and the announced policy rate, both published and both moving before price does. A foreign buyer is additionally buying at a real exchange rate 39% above its January 2022 low, so the currency entry point is the worst in five years even where the property entry point is not.此一判斷不取決於房價指數，而取決於存底序列與公告政策利率，兩者均為公開資料，且均先於價格變動。外國買方另須考量進場時的實質匯率高於 2022 年 1 月低點 39%，故縱使房產的進場時點尚可，貨幣的進場時點為五年來最差。

What would change the answer什麼會改變這個答案

A step depreciation of the lira. It would restore the foreign-buyer discount that closed between January 2022 and June 2026, and it would do so at the same moment it forced the real rate negative and restarted the inflation-hedge bid. That single event flips three of the four terms in the equation at once, and the reserve series is where it becomes visible first.里拉的階梯式貶值。該事件將恢復 2022 年 1 月至 2026 年 6 月間消失的外資折讓，並於同一時點迫使實質利率轉負、重啟通膨避險買盤。單一事件即同時翻轉方程式四項中的三項，而存底序列為其最先顯現之處。

What this page concludes本頁的結論

Türkiye is the only market on this site where monetary policy alone explains most of the property price series, and it is the only one where the credit channel has been removed by inflation rather than by regulation or by preference. The real funding rate moved 82 percentage points in two years, and the real house price index moved with it in both directions. An investor who tracks one series here should track that one.土耳其為本站唯一一個貨幣政策單獨即可解釋房價序列多數變動的市場，亦為唯一一個信用管道係由通膨而非由法規或偏好所移除的市場。實質資金利率於兩年內移動 82 個百分點，實質房價指數於兩個方向上均隨之移動。於此市場僅追蹤單一序列者，應追蹤該序列。

The record does not support underwriting real capital growth from here. Real prices are 18.6% below their January 2023 reading in İstanbul and have fallen in every quarter for eleven quarters, entirely through inflation rather than through a nominal fall. Against that, real output per head rose 29% between 2020 and 2025, which is a genuine domestic income base and the strongest argument against extrapolating the decline. The return available now is the rent, at a gross yield that has expanded 181 basis points from the 2022 trough, and the mechanism behind that expansion is a lapsed statute rather than a market judgement.歷史紀錄不支持自此處起將實質資本利得納入承作假設。伊斯坦堡的實質房價低於 2023 年 1 月讀數 18.6%，並已連續十一季下跌，其全部成因為通膨而非名目下跌。相對地，實質人均產出於 2020 至 2025 年間上升 29%，此為真實存在的本地所得基礎，亦為反對將跌勢外推的最有力論據。現階段可取得的報酬為租金，其毛收益率自 2022 年谷底擴張 181 個基點，而該擴張背後的機制是一項已失效的法律規定，不是市場判斷。

The question that decides the next three years is not the price index. It is whether the reserve stock can continue to finance a real appreciation of the size seen since January 2022. That question is answered month by month in a published series, and the transaction-level consequences of each answer are set out in the companion Istanbul Property Market Report.決定未來三年的問題不是房價指數，而是存底能否持續支應自 2022 年 1 月以來規模的實質升值。該問題逐月由一項公開序列給出答案，各種答案於成交層面的後果詳見對照的《伊斯坦堡住宅市場報告》。

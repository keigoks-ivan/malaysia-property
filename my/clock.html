The Property Clock — Five Forces on Two Axes 房地產時鐘 · 五種力量、兩個軸

Structure × Flow (2×2)結構 × 資金流（2×2） Supply · Demand · Money · Population · Economy供給·需求·貨幣·人口·經濟 US pilot · 1975–2026美國試點 · 1975–2026 Updated Jul 2026更新至 2026年7月
A clean-sheet redesign推倒重來的設計 The old clock read the cycle off two ingredients. This one is rebuilt from the ground up around five forces — supply, demand, money, population, economy — collapsed onto two axes. The horizontal axis is Structure (slow): is housing fundamentally cheap and tight, with demographic support, or expensive and loose? The vertical axis is Flow (fast): is money and the economy pushing prices up right now, or pulling them down? Cross them and every quarter becomes a point in a plane — angle is where you sit in the cycle, distance is how stretched. Four corners: Base·Coiling, Primed·Expansion, Late·Melt-up, Correction·Downturn. All ten inputs are real published US series; the standardisation is causal (trailing window, no look-ahead), so the reading at any quarter used only data available then. Blunt caveat: this is a fresh reading built and shown on the same US history, so the corner statistics are descriptive, not an out-of-sample test — the forward test is the transplant to Taiwan and Malaysia next. 舊時鐘用兩樣原料判讀週期。這一座從頭重建，圍繞五種力量——供給、需求、貨幣、人口、經濟——壓進兩個軸。橫軸是結構（慢）：房子基本面是便宜又緊、有人口支撐，還是又貴又鬆？縱軸是資金流（快）：貨幣與經濟現在正把價格往上推，還是往下拉？兩軸一交叉，每一季就成為平面上的一個點——角度是你在週期裡的位置，離心距是拉伸程度。四個角：打底·蓄勢、蓄勢·擴張、晚週期·熔漲、修正·下行。十個輸入全是真實公佈的美國序列；標準化是因果的（滾動窗、無前視），所以任一季的讀數只用到當時已有的資料。直白但書：這是在同一段美國歷史上建立並展示的新判讀，所以各格統計是描述性、不是樣本外測試——前向測試是下一站移植到台灣與馬來西亞。

How to read it: a corner is a state, not a countdown. The honest signal is downside protection, not return-ranking: the two right-hand corners (cheap/tight) never printed a bad three years in this sample, while every negative outcome sits on the left. The cross tells you which tail you are exposed to.怎麼讀：一格是狀態，不是倒數。誠實的訊號是下檔保護，不是報酬排序：右半兩格（便宜緊）在此樣本裡從未打出糟糕的三年，而所有為負的結果都落在左半。交叉告訴你暴露在哪一條尾巴。

Four quadrants run counter-clockwise as the cycle turns: Base → Expansion → Melt-up → Downturn → Base. A gold comet-tail trails the last eight quarters; the faint ring is all quarters at once. How to read it: the hand only ever traces what already happened — this is a trajectory through history, not a forecast. Is this house prices predicting house prices? No — and the panel shows why. Neither axis contains price momentum. The Flow axis is money and macro: real mortgage-credit growth, the real 30-year rate, real GDP, unemployment, real disposable income. The Structure axis is physical and demographic: months-supply, homeowner vacancy, the household-formation-minus-completions gap, prime-age (25–54) population growth — plus one valuation ratio (price ÷ real income) that is the only place a price level enters at all, and it enters divided by income. Price YoY is drawn in the top layer purely as the thing being judged.這是用房價預測房價嗎？不是——面板直接展示原因。兩個軸都不含價格動能。資金流軸是貨幣與總經：實質房貸信貸成長、實質 30 年利率、實質 GDP、失業率、實質可支配所得。結構軸是實體與人口：月供給、自住空置率、家庭形成減完工缺口、壯年（25–54）人口成長——外加一條估值比（房價÷實質所得），這是價格水準唯一進入的地方，而且是除以所得後才進入。房價年增畫在上層，純粹作為被判讀的對象。

What the corners actually separate. On this nominal, boom-heavy US sample the medians do not fan out much — this is not a return-ranking machine and it would be dishonest to sell it as one. Where it separates cleanly is downside: the two structure-supportive corners (Expansion, Base) never printed a losing three years, while Correction·Downturn is the only corner with a negative lower quartile and holds the worst single outcome. The useful read is defensive — structure protects the downside, flow times the upside.四格真正分得開的是什麼。在這個名目、偏多頭的美國樣本上，各格中位並沒有拉得很開——這不是報酬排序機，把它當排序機賣並不誠實。它真正分得開的是下檔：兩個結構有支撐的格（擴張、打底）從未打出虧損的三年，而修正·下行是唯一下四分位為負的一格，也握有最差的單一結果。有用的讀法是防禦性的——結構保護下檔，資金流決定上檔的時機。

The Rule — Two Axes, Ten Inputs, Five Forces規則 · 兩軸、十輸入、五力量

X · Structure (slow)結構（慢）

  • Supply供給 — months-supply of new homes, homeowner vacancy (low = tight)新屋月供給、自住空置率（低＝緊）
  • Demand需求 — household formation minus completions (positive = under-built)家庭形成減完工（正＝供不應求）
  • Population人口 — prime-age 25–54 population growth壯年 25–54 人口成長
  • Valuation估值 — house price ÷ real disposable income (low = cheap)房價÷實質可支配所得（低＝便宜）

Y · Flow (fast)資金流（快）

  • Money貨幣 — real mortgage-credit growth, real 30-year rate (easing = up)實質房貸信貸成長、實質 30 年利率（寬鬆＝上）
  • Economy經濟 — real GDP growth, unemployment (falling = up), real disposable income實質 GDP 成長、失業率（下降＝上）、實質可支配所得
  • Each input becomes a causal z-score. Every series is standardised against its own trailing 10-year window (median and MAD) — so "cheap", "tight" or "easing" means relative to the last decade, not the 45-year level. Trailing (not full-sample) de-trends the secular drift in valuation and demographics, and it is causal: quarter t uses only data up to t.每個輸入化為因果 z 分數。每條序列都對自身的滾動 10 年窗（中位與 MAD）標準化——所以「便宜」「緊」「寬鬆」是相對近十年、不是相對 45 年的水準。用滾動窗（而非全樣本）能去掉估值與人口的長期漂移，且是因果的：第 t 季只用到 t 為止的資料。
  • Two axes = the average of their components. Structure X = mean of the four structural z-scores; Flow Y = mean of the five flow z-scores. The axes are then lightly smoothed (3-quarter trailing mean) so a cycle state does not flip on one noisy quarter — a structural choice, disclosed, not tuned to returns.兩個軸＝各自分項的平均。結構 X＝四個結構 z 的平均；資金流 Y＝五個資金流 z 的平均。兩軸再做輕度平滑（3 季滾動平均），讓一個週期狀態不會因單一噪音季而翻轉——這是結構性選擇、已揭露，不是對報酬調參。
  • The corner is the sign pair. X≥0 & Y≥0 = Primed·Expansion; X≥0 & Y<0 = Base·Coiling; X<0 & Y≥0 = Late·Melt-up; X<0 & Y<0 = Correction·Downturn. Angle and distance from the centre give the finer position shown on the clock.格＝符號組合。X≥0 且 Y≥0＝蓄勢·擴張；X≥0 且 Y<0＝打底·蓄勢；X<0 且 Y≥0＝晚週期·熔漲；X<0 且 Y<0＝修正·下行。離圓心的角度與距離，就是時鐘上更細的位置。
  • What is new, what is not. The old credit-×-tension 2×2 is retired for this reading. The five-force build is new; the discipline is the same — real published inputs, causal standardisation, honest labelling of a spent sample. The v2 twelve-market freeze on the framework page is untouched and still frozen, scored in 2029 on its own terms. Two rulesets coexist by design.什麼是新的、什麼不是。舊的信貸×張力 2×2 在此判讀中退役。五力量的建法是新的；紀律不變——真實公佈的輸入、因果標準化、對已花掉樣本的誠實標註。框架頁的 v2 十二市場凍結表原封不動、仍然凍結，2029 年照它自己的條件對答案。兩套規則刻意並存。

What This Clock Does and Does Not Claim這座鐘承諾什麼、不承諾什麼

  • Built and shown on the same US history — so it is descriptive, not out-of-sample. This is the caveat that matters most. The reading rule was designed on 1975–2026 US data and every corner statistic is computed on that same data, so the numbers describe the past; they are not evidence the rule works forward. The genuine out-of-sample test is the transplant to Taiwan and Malaysia, next in line.在同一段美國歷史上建立並展示——所以是描述性，不是樣本外。這是最要緊的但書。判讀規則是在 1975–2026 美國資料上設計的，而每一格統計都在同一段資料上計算，所以這些數字是在描述過去；不是規則能向前有效的證據。真正的樣本外測試，是移植到台灣與馬來西亞，下一站。
  • A position, not a prediction. The plane and the clock show 1979–2026 — things that already happened. Corners are rule-computed states; the framework is not validated as a timing or rotation model. It shows where the US stands, not where the next quarter goes.是位置，不是預測。平面與時鐘顯示 1979–2026——已經發生的事。四格是規則算出的狀態；框架未被驗證為擇時或輪動模型。它顯示美國站在哪，不承諾下一季往哪走。
  • Why the read differs from the old clock. The retired credit-only clock read 2026Q1 as a late-cycle push. Adding the real mortgage rate, the labour market and a valuation ratio pulls the read into Correction·Downturn: money is not actually easy, and fundamentals are not cheap. More inputs, a colder answer.為何與舊時鐘不同。退役的純信貸時鐘把 2026Q1 讀成晚週期推升。把實質房貸利率、勞動市場與一條估值比加進來，判讀被拉進修正·下行：貨幣其實不寬鬆，基本面也不便宜。輸入更多，答案更冷。
  • Known limits. Forward returns are nominal (a real-terms version would shift every median down by inflation but barely change the ranking). New-home months-supply and sales stand in for the whole market's supply and turnover. The valuation denominator is aggregate real disposable income, not a mortgage-serviceability measure. Every input and the full method are in the framework data.已知限制。前向報酬是名目（實質版會把每個中位往下移一個通膨、但幾乎不改排序）。新屋月供給與銷售代表整體市場的供給與周轉。估值分母是總體實質可支配所得，不是房貸負擔能力指標。每個輸入與完整方法都在框架資料裡。

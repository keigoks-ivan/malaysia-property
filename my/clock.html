The Property Clock — US Pilot, Fifty Years Replayed 房地產時鐘 · 美國試點，五十年重播

The strategy, and the score策略與成績 Strategy pivot: instead of running twelve markets shallowly, this clock is now built one market deep at a time. The rules were tuned on US data from 1975–1999 only, frozen, and then run exactly once over 2000–2025. The score, published as-is: coverage 87% (pass); the 2006 top was called two quarters early; the 2011–12 bottom was missed by three; phase separation is weak on the entry side but clean on the exit side — cooling quarters returned −7.9% median over the next three years against +16% to +40% for everything else. As calibrated, this is an exit instrument, not an entry timer. Other markets wait below until the ruleset earns its transplant. 策略轉向：不再十二個市場淺淺地跑，這座鐘改為一次做深一個市場。規則只用1975–1999 的美國資料調參，凍結後對 2000–2025 只跑一次。成績原樣公佈：覆蓋率 87%（過關）；2006 年頂部提前兩季叫出；2011–12 年底部晚了三季；相位分離在進場端偏弱、在出場端乾淨——降溫季往後三年中位報酬 −7.9%，對照其他所有相位的 +16% 到 +40%。以目前的校準，這是出場儀器，不是進場計時器。其他市場列在下方等待名單，等這套規則掙到移植資格。

Read the test half against history. The clock sat in overheat continuously from 2003Q4 to 2006Q2 — the structure that could never be tuned to fire in training lit up at national scale in the one episode that mattered, then flipped to cooling at 2006Q3, two quarters before the price peak (FHFA hard top 2007Q1) and stayed there through the crash. It re-entered primed only at 2013Q1, three quarters after the 2012Q2 bottom: it confirmed the recovery rather than anticipating it. Through 2016–2018 and 2021–2023 it showed overheat with benign outcomes — overheat is a state, not a countdown. In 2022–24 the FHFA index never actually turned (YoY fell from 17% to about 3%), and the clock printed cooling from 2023Q2. Today, 2026Q1 reads: no phase — zero tension components lit, credit rising, the same ignition-on-no-tension shape the v2 freeze flagged.拿測試那一半對歷史讀。時鐘從 2003Q4 到 2006Q2 連續處於過熱——訓練期怎麼調都點不亮的結構，在唯一要緊的那一段於全國尺度亮了，然後 2006Q3 翻成降溫，比價格頂早兩季（FHFA 硬頂 2007Q1），並一路維持到崩盤結束。它 2013Q1 才回到蓄勢，比 2012Q2 的底晚三季：它確認了復甦，而不是預判。2016–2018 與 2021–2023 顯示過熱但結果溫和——過熱是狀態，不是倒數計時。2022–24 FHFA 指數其實沒有轉折（年增率從 17% 降到約 3%），時鐘從 2023Q2 起打出降溫。今天，2026Q1 讀數：無相位——張力分項零個亮、信貸上行，正是 v2 凍結表標記過的「無張力點火」形狀。

Is this just house prices predicting house prices? No, and the panel now shows why. The tension score (middle layer) is built from four series that contain no price momentum: homeowner vacancy (Census HVS), months-supply of new homes (Census), the ten-year formation-minus-completions gap (Census/FRED), and a valuation ratio whose numerator is the only place the price level enters (against per-capita income, BEA). The hand (bottom layer) is mortgage credit from the Fed's Z.1, not prices. Price YoY itself enters exactly one rule — the overheat trigger — and is otherwise the thing being predicted, drawn in the top layer only so the phases can be judged against it.這是不是用房價預測房價？不是，面板現在直接展示原因。張力分數（中層）由四條不含價格動能的序列組成：自住空置率（普查局 HVS）、新屋月供給（普查局）、十年家庭形成減完工缺口（普查局／FRED），以及估值比——價格水準唯一進入分子的地方（分母是 BEA 人均所得）。指針（下層）是 Fed Z.1 的房貸信貸，不是價格。房價年增率本身只進一條規則（過熱觸發），其餘時候它是被預測的對象，畫在上層只是為了讓相位對著它接受檢驗。

One-shot verdict: a partial pass. The clock earned one specific, valuable property: when it says cooling, the next three years have been bad. It did not earn entry-timing authority. Per the strategy, the next step is transplanting the frozen ruleset to market #2, not re-tuning it on this data, which is now burned.一次性裁決：部分通過。這座鐘掙到了一項具體而值錢的性質：當它說降溫，接下來三年都不好過。它沒有掙到進場擇時的權威。按策略，下一步是把凍結規則移植到第二個市場，而不是在這份已燒掉的資料上重調。

The Frozen Ruleset凍結的規則

  • Tension score (0–4): homeowner vacancy in its bottom 20th percentile (trailing 10y) · months-supply bottom 20th percentile · 10-year formation-minus-completions gap positive · valuation (price ÷ per-capita disposable income) below its 10-year median.張力分數（0–4）：自住空置率處於自身十年最低 20 分位 · 月供給最低 20 分位 · 十年（家庭形成−完工）缺口為正 · 估值（房價÷人均可支配所得）低於十年中位。
  • Phases (precedence fixed): overheat = price YoY ≥70th percentile and valuation above its median; ignition = tension ≥2 and mortgage-credit growth rising (Test 6 turn rule); primed = tension ≥2, credit not rising; cooling = credit falling. Parameters K1=2, P_low=20, P_hi=70, K2=1.0, tuned only on 1975–1999; the 36-trial grid and objective are logged in the JSON.相位（優先序固定）：過熱＝價格年增 ≥70 分位且估值高於中位；引爆＝張力 ≥2 且房貸增速上行（Test 6 轉折規則）；蓄勢＝張力 ≥2、信貸未上行；降溫＝信貸下行。參數 K1=2、P_low=20、P_hi=70、K2=1.0，只在 1975–1999 調出；36 組網格與目標函數記錄在 JSON。

What This Clock Does and Does Not Claim這座鐘承諾什麼、不承諾什麼

  • Positions, not rotation. Phases are rule-computed states; the framework has not been validated as a rotation model. The chart shows where the US stands; it does not promise clockwise motion.呈現位置，不承諾順時針。相位是規則算出的狀態；框架未被驗證為輪動模型。圖上顯示美國站在哪裡，不承諾順時針前進。
  • Evidence grades, updated for the pilot. The credit hand remains the best-validated part (Test 6: led 8 of 10 turns, +2.5q median; pilot: drove the 2006 call). The cooling phase now has one out-of-sample validation (−7.9% median forward). Entry phases (primed and ignition) remain unproven for timing. The overheat rule has exactly one confirming episode.證據分級，隨試點更新。信貸指針仍是驗證最強的零件（Test 6：10 個轉折領先 8 個、中位 +2.5 季；試點：撐起 2006 那一叫）。降溫相位現在有一次樣本外驗證（前向中位 −7.9%）。進場相位（蓄勢與引爆）的擇時能力仍未證明。過熱規則只有一個確認事件。
  • The test set is burned. 2000–2025 US data was scored once, on 2026-07-12. Any future revision of these rules must declare test contamination; the next honest validation must come from a different market or a future period.測試集已燒掉。2000–2025 的美國資料在 2026-07-12 打過一次分。未來對這套規則的任何修訂都必須聲明測試污染；下一次誠實的驗證只能來自另一個市場，或未來的時間。

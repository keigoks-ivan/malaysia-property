A cleaner reading rule更乾淨的判讀規則 The single-signal machine — five phases, one of them a dead-centre "no phase" that swallowed too many quarters — is retired. In its place, one 2×2 read: credit direction (mortgage credit rising or falling) crossed with tension (the loaded-spring score — cheap and tight, or pricey and loose). Both axes were already validated; the cross gives every quarter a cell, no blanks (bar the early warm-up before credit data begins). Four corners: Primed·Recovery, Trough·Reset, Overheat·Late, Cooling·Downturn. Blunt honesty up front: the US test set was already spent on the old rule, so re-scoring the 2×2 on the same 1975–2026 US data is a descriptive re-calibration, not an out-of-sample test — the real validation moves to Taiwan next. Read the corners against history. The 2006–07 bubble ran as Overheat·Late (credit expanding into a pricey, loose market) right up to 2006Q2, then flipped to Cooling·Downturn at 2006Q3 as credit turned down — two quarters ahead of the FHFA hard top (2007Q1) — and stayed blue through the crash: the credit axis still catches the top. The COVID cycle is the cleanest full loop: Trough·Reset (2020Q1–Q3) → Primed·Recovery (2020Q4–2021Q1) → Overheat·Late (2021Q2–2022Q2) → Cooling·Downturn (2022Q3–2024Q4). What the corners actually separate. On this nominal, boom-heavy US sample the medians cluster in the mid-teens — the cross is not a return-ranking machine, and it would be dishonest to sell it as one. Where it does separate is downside: Cooling·Downturn holds the only clearly negative lower quartile and the worst single three-year outcome, while the two credit-rising corners rarely printed a bad three years. So the useful read is defensive — the corner tells you which tail you are exposed to. And the honest caveat stands above everything: these are descriptions of a spent US sample, not a forward test. The forward test is the transplant to Taiwan.四格真正分得開的是什麼。在這個名目、偏多頭的美國樣本上，中位都擠在十幾趴——這個交叉不是報酬排序機，把它當排序機賣是不誠實的。它真正分得開的是下檔：降溫·下行握有唯一明確為負的下四分位、以及最差的單一三年結果，而兩個信貸上行的格子幾乎不曾打出糟糕的三年。所以有用的讀法是防禦性的——格子告訴你暴露在哪一條尾巴。而那句誠實的但書凌駕一切：這些是對一份已花掉的美國樣本的描述，不是前向測試。前向測試是移植到台灣那一步。

The Rule — Two Validated Axes規則 · 兩個已驗證的軸

  • Y — tension (0–4, the loaded spring): homeowner vacancy in its bottom 20th percentile (trailing 10y) · months-supply bottom 20th percentile · 10-year formation-minus-completions gap positive · valuation (price ÷ per-capita disposable income) below its 10-year median. High = tension ≥ 2 (cheap and tight); low = below 2 (pricey and loose). Boundary K1=2 is unchanged from the frozen tuning — not a new knob.Y — 張力（0–4，上緊的彈簧）：自住空置率處於自身十年最低 20 分位 · 月供給最低 20 分位 · 十年（家庭形成−完工）缺口為正 · 估值（房價÷人均可支配所得）低於十年中位。高＝張力 ≥ 2（便宜又緊）；低＝低於 2（貴又鬆）。邊界 K1=2 沿用凍結時的調參——不是新旋鈕。
  • X — credit direction (FL1): mortgage credit (Fed Z.1) rising or falling, the same state series that led 8 of 10 price turns in the frozen work. Rising = left column, falling = right.X — 信貸方向（FL1）：房貸信貸（Fed Z.1）上行或下行，就是凍結研究裡領先 10 個價格轉折中 8 個的那條狀態序列。上行＝左欄，下行＝右欄。
  • The four corners: rising × high = Primed·Recovery; falling × high = Trough·Reset; rising × low = Overheat·Late; falling × low = Cooling·Downturn. Every quarter with credit data lands in exactly one — no precedence stack, no "no-phase" gap.四個角：上行 × 高＝蓄勢·復甦；下行 × 高＝打底·重置；上行 × 低＝過熱·晚週期；下行 × 低＝降溫·下行。每一個有信貸資料的季度剛好落一格——沒有優先序堆疊，也沒有「無相位」的缺口。
  • What changed, and what didn't. The old single-signal machine (overheat / ignition / primed / cooling / no-phase, with K2, P_low, P_hi) is retired for this reading — it left too many blank quarters and stacked fragile precedence. The two ingredients survive; only the combination is new. The v2 twelve-market freeze on the framework page is untouched and still frozen — it gets scored in 2029 on its own terms. Two rulesets coexist by design: v2 is a frozen bet, this 2×2 is the working reading tool.變了什麼、沒變什麼。舊的單訊號機（過熱／引爆／蓄勢／降溫／無相位，帶 K2、P_low、P_hi）在此判讀中退役——它留下太多空白季、又堆疊脆弱的優先序。兩樣原料都保留，只有組合方式是新的。框架頁的 v2 十二市場凍結表原封不動、仍然凍結——2029 年照它自己的條件對答案。兩套規則刻意並存：v2 是一注凍結的賭，這張 2×2 是實際在用的判讀工具。

The honest headline誠實的頭條 This dial is the visual output of the framework backtests: every position is computed by pre-registered rules from the frozen 2026 table, not drawn by opinion. And in July 2026 the mechanical answer is stark: no market qualifies for any running phase. Four markets are locked (US, JP, TW, MY), seven have insufficient inputs, and the UK sits one point short of the primed bar with credit already ignited. The most important thing this clock can say is that some markets do not go around. 這座鐘面是框架回測的視覺輸出層：每個位置都由預註冊規則從2026 凍結表算出，不是憑觀點畫的。而 2026 年 7 月的機械答案很直白：沒有任何市場符合任何運轉相位。四個市場被鎖住（US、JP、TW、MY），七個市場輸入不足，英國離蓄勢門檻差一分、信貸已點火。這座鐘最重要的一句話是：有些市場不走。

What This Clock Does and Does Not Claim這座鐘承諾什麼、不承諾什麼

  • Positions, not rotation. Every position is rule-computed, but the phase framework itself has not been historically validated as a rotation model. The dial shows where markets stand; it does not promise they move clockwise.呈現位置，不承諾順時針。每個位置都由規則算出，但相位框架本身未經歷史驗證為輪動模型。鐘面顯示市場站在哪裡，不承諾它們會順時針走。
  • The three parts carry different grades of evidence, stated plainly. The hand (credit) is the only link validated by the backtests: it led price at 8 of 10 turns, median +2.5 quarters (Test 6). The height (slow layer) has one moderate cross-sectional calibration in an easing window (ρ=+0.44) plus the capped-side rank result (0.75); its positive side is unproven. The speed ring (stickiness) is moderately validated (ρ=−0.68 excluding Japan, Test 2).三個零件的證據強度不同，如實分級。指針（信貸）是回測裡唯一驗證過的環節：10 個轉折裡 8 個領先價格、中位 +2.5 季（Test 6）。高度（慢層）只有放水期一次中等橫斷面校準（ρ=+0.44）加封頂端排名結果（0.75）；正面端未經證明。轉速環（黏性）為中度驗證（剔日本後 ρ=−0.68，Test 2）。
  • Missing data stays grey. Markets that fail the input threshold are greyed and not placed, per the frozen N/A matrix. Data cut-offs are noted per market in the table.缺資料就灰顯。未達輸入門檻的市場灰顯、不擺位，依凍結表的 N/A 矩陣。各市場資料截止日期標注於表內。

Update cadence: annual (each July, with the freeze-scoring cycle), plus ad-hoc updates on major events (a policy-ratchet strike, a credit-turn confirmation). This is not a live dashboard.

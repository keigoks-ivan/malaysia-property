The Property Clock — Five Forces on Two Axes 房地產時鐘 · 五種力量、兩個軸

A clean-sheet redesign推倒重來的設計 The old clock read the cycle off two ingredients. This one is rebuilt from the ground up around five forces — supply, demand, money, population, economy — collapsed onto two axes. The horizontal axis is Structure (slow): is housing fundamentally cheap and tight, with demographic support, or expensive and loose? The vertical axis is Flow (fast): is money and the economy pushing prices up right now, or pulling them down? Cross them and every quarter becomes a point in a plane — angle is where you sit in the cycle, distance is how stretched. Four corners: Base·Coiling, Primed·Expansion, Late·Melt-up, Correction·Downturn. All ten inputs are real published US series; the standardisation is causal (trailing window, no look-ahead), so the reading at any quarter used only data available then. Blunt caveat: this is a fresh reading built and shown on the same US history, so the corner statistics are descriptive, not an out-of-sample test — the forward test is the transplant to Taiwan and Malaysia next. 舊時鐘用兩樣原料判讀週期。這一座從頭重建，圍繞五種力量——供給、需求、貨幣、人口、經濟——壓進兩個軸。橫軸是結構（慢）：房子基本面是便宜又緊、有人口支撐，還是又貴又鬆？縱軸是資金流（快）：貨幣與經濟現在正把價格往上推，還是往下拉？兩軸一交叉，每一季就成為平面上的一個點——角度是你在週期裡的位置，離心距是拉伸程度。四個角：打底·蓄勢、蓄勢·擴張、晚週期·熔漲、修正·下行。十個輸入全是真實公佈的美國序列；標準化是因果的（滾動窗、無前視），所以任一季的讀數只用到當時已有的資料。直白但書：這是在同一段美國歷史上建立並展示的新判讀，所以各格統計是描述性、不是樣本外測試——前向測試是下一站移植到台灣與馬來西亞。
The transplant · Taiwan移植 · 台灣 This is the transplant. The same five-force engine — Structure (supply, demand, population, valuation) crossed with Flow (money, economy) — now runs on Taiwan. The price spine is the Sinyi house-price index (national, 2001–2026); the forces come from official Taiwan sources — DGBAS (real GDP, unemployment, CPI), the central bank (mortgage credit and rate), and the interior ministry (price-to-income, low-electricity vacancy, completions). Blunt caveat: Taiwan's usable history is far shorter than the US — readings begin only around 2012 once the trailing windows fill, so the corner base-rates rest on a small sample (~2 cycles) and read as indicative, not settled. Same engine, thinner evidence. 這是移植。同一套五力量引擎——結構（供給、需求、人口、估值）交叉資金流（貨幣、經濟）——現在跑台灣。價格主軸是信義房價指數（全國，2001–2026）；力量來自台灣官方來源——主計總處（實質 GDP、失業率、CPI）、中央銀行（房貸信貸與利率）、內政部（房價所得比、低度用電空置、完工量）。直白但書：台灣可用歷史遠短於美國——滾動窗填滿後，讀數約自 2012 年才開始，所以四格基準率建立在小樣本（約 2 個週期）上，視為指示性、非定論。同一引擎，證據較薄。
The home market · Malaysia主場 · 馬來西亞 The home market. The same five-force engine now runs on Malaysia. The price spine is the BIS nominal house-price index (1988–2026); the forces come from DOSM (real GDP, unemployment, CPI, income, population), Bank Negara (OPR, household credit) and NAPIC (unsold overhang, completions). Read this one differently: Malaysia is a welded market — nominal house prices have essentially never fallen in 38 years, so even the weakest corner still printed a positive three years. The clock here reads how fast, not up-or-down: "Downturn" means slowest growth and worst affordability, not a price fall. Short usable history (~2006 on), small samples — indicative. 主場。同一套五力量引擎現在跑馬來西亞。價格主軸是 BIS 名目房價指數（1988–2026）；力量來自 DOSM（實質 GDP、失業、CPI、所得、人口）、國家銀行（OPR、家庭信貸）與 NAPIC（餘屋、完工）。這一座要換個方式讀：馬來西亞是焊住型市場——名目房價 38 年來幾乎不曾下跌，所以連最弱的一格都還是正的三年。這裡的時鐘讀的是漲多快、不是漲或跌：「下行」代表漲最慢、可負擔性最差，不是價格下跌。可用歷史短（約 2006 起）、樣本小——指示性。
The full cycle · Japan完整週期 · 日本 The full cycle. The same five-force engine runs on Japan — the one market here with a real crash. The price spine is the BIS nominal house-price index (1955–2025, shown from 1975): prices roughly doubled into the 1991 bubble peak, then fell ~40% into 2005, recovering only partly under Abenomics. Forces from FRED/OECD (real GDP, unemployment, CPI, income), the World Bank (working-age population, falling since 1995), the BOJ (policy rate — 9% oil-shock highs to zero to negative), BIS (household credit) and e-Stat (housing starts, vacancy 空き家率). Long history (10-year windows, like the US); the demographic decline is a genuine structural headwind. This is the market where the clock's Downturn corner actually predicts falling prices. 完整週期。同一套五力量引擎跑日本——這裡唯一真正崩過盤的市場。價格主軸是 BIS 名目房價指數（1955–2025，圖從 1975 起）：價格漲到 1991 泡沫頂約翻倍，隨後跌約 40% 到 2005，Abenomics 下只部分回升。力量來自 FRED／OECD（實質 GDP、失業、CPI、所得）、世界銀行（工作年齡人口，1995 起下降）、日本央行（政策利率——油危機 9% 高點到零到負）、BIS（家庭信貸）與 e-Stat（住宅開工、空き家率）。歷史長（10 年窗，像美國）；人口下降是真實的結構逆風。這是時鐘的下行格真的會預示房價下跌的市場。

The Rule — Two Axes, Ten Inputs, Five Forces規則 · 兩軸、十輸入、五力量

X · Structure (slow)結構（慢）

  • Supply供給 — months-supply of new homes, homeowner vacancy (low = tight)新屋月供給、自住空置率（低＝緊）
  • Demand需求 — household formation minus completions (positive = under-built)家庭形成減完工（正＝供不應求）
  • Population人口 — prime-age 25–54 population growth壯年 25–54 人口成長
  • Valuation估值 — house price ÷ real disposable income (low = cheap)房價÷實質可支配所得（低＝便宜）

Y · Flow (fast)資金流（快）

  • Money貨幣 — real mortgage-credit growth, real 30-year rate (easing = up)實質房貸信貸成長、實質 30 年利率（寬鬆＝上）
  • Economy經濟 — real GDP growth, unemployment (falling = up), real disposable income實質 GDP 成長、失業率（下降＝上）、實質可支配所得
  • Each input becomes a causal z-score. Every series is standardised against its own trailing multi-year window (median and MAD) — so "cheap", "tight" or "easing" means relative to recent years, not the long-run level. Trailing (not full-sample) de-trends the secular drift in valuation and demographics, and it is causal: quarter t uses only data up to t.每個輸入化為因果 z 分數。每條序列都對自身的滾動多年窗（中位與 MAD）標準化——所以「便宜」「緊」「寬鬆」是相對近年、不是相對長期的水準。用滾動窗（而非全樣本）能去掉估值與人口的長期漂移，且是因果的：第 t 季只用到 t 為止的資料。
  • Two axes = the average of their components. Structure X = the mean of its structural z-scores; Flow Y = the mean of its flow z-scores. The axes are then lightly smoothed (3-quarter trailing mean) so a cycle state does not flip on one noisy quarter — a structural choice, disclosed, not tuned to returns.兩個軸＝各自分項的平均。結構 X＝其結構 z 的平均；資金流 Y＝其資金流 z 的平均。兩軸再做輕度平滑（3 季滾動平均），讓一個週期狀態不會因單一噪音季而翻轉——這是結構性選擇、已揭露，不是對報酬調參。
  • The corner is the sign pair. X≥0 & Y≥0 = Primed·Expansion; X≥0 & Y<0 = Base·Coiling; X<0 & Y≥0 = Late·Melt-up; X<0 & Y<0 = Correction·Downturn.   • What is new, what is not. The old credit-×-tension 2×2 is retired for this reading. The five-force build is new; the discipline is the same — real published inputs, causal standardisation, honest labelling of a spent sample.

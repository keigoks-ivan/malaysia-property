Malaysia's overhang has re-accelerated, and the improvement of 2022–2024 has reversed. NAPIC's full-year Property Market Report 2025 (released 19 March 2026) put completed-unsold residential stock at 30,471 units worth RM17.73 billion — up 31.6% year-on-year — and the first-quarter 2026 print rose further to 32,801 units, the highest since 3Q2022. After three consecutive years of falling inventory, the market has turned the wrong way at exactly the moment the 2024 construction-start wave begins to complete.

To read this correctly you have to read it in cycle, and the official NAPIC series now lets us do that precisely. Malaysia's overhang is not a one-off shock; it is the fourth act of a decade-long drama. Residential completed-unsold built from just 11,316 units in 2015 to 32,313 by 2018 as a wall of high-rise launches met a market cooled by the 2014 macroprudential package (the DIBS ban, tighter debt-service rules, the 35-year loan-tenure cap) and then taxed by the 2015 Goods & Services Tax. It kept climbing to an all-time peak of 36,863 units in 2021, when the pandemic froze absorption while completions kept arriving. It then drained to a 23,149-unit trough by 2024 — the Home Ownership Campaign, full stamp-duty waivers and a record-low 1.75% policy rate pulled forward years of demand — and is now refilling because developers, reading the 2021–2022 absorption surge as structural rather than borrowed, restarted the launch and construction pipeline in 2023–2024. The current overhang is, in large part, the lagged echo of that decision. The serviced-apartment line beneath it tells a quieter but more alarming version of the same story: SA overhang climbed almost without interruption from just 3,912 units in 2016 to a peak near 24,000 in 2021–2022, and has only partly receded since.

馬來西亞的餘屋（completed-unsold，已完工未售）再度惡化，2022 至 2024 年連三年的改善已經反轉。NAPIC 的全年《2025 房產市場報告》（2026 年 3 月 19 日發布）顯示，已完工未售住宅達 30,471 戶、市值 RM177.3 億，年增 31.6%；2026 年第一季更升到 32,801 戶，是 2022 年第三季以來最高。在連三年去化好轉之後，市場偏偏在 2024 年開工潮即將完工的時點，往錯的方向轉。

要正確解讀，必須放進「循環」裡看，而官方 NAPIC 序列現在讓我們能精確地這樣做。馬來西亞的餘屋不是一次性衝擊，而是一齣長達十年的戲的第四幕。住宅已完工未售從 2015 年僅 11,316 戶，堆到 2018 年的 32,313 戶：一整面牆的高層推案，撞上一個被 2014 年宏觀審慎措施（禁止 DIBS 利息補貼、收緊償債比、35 年貸款年限上限）冷卻、又被 2015 年消費稅（GST）課稅的市場。它持續攀升到 2021 年 36,863 戶的歷史高峰——疫情凍結了去化，完工卻照常到位。接著在 2024 年降到 23,149 戶谷底——擁屋計畫（HOC）、印花稅全免、1.75% 歷史低利率把好幾年的需求提前拉出來——如今又在回填，因為建商把 2021–2022 年的去化暴衝誤判為「結構性」而非「借來的」，於是在 2023–2024 年重啟推案與動工。今天的餘屋，很大程度上正是那個決定的遞延回聲。底下那條服務式公寓曲線，講的是同一個故事更安靜、卻更令人不安的版本：服務式公寓餘屋從 2016 年僅 3,912 戶，幾乎不間斷地攀到 2021–2022 年近 24,000 戶，此後也只部分回落。

The single most important analytical correction remains the serviced-apartment blind spot. When NAPIC reclassified serviced apartments (SA) from residential to commercial in 2016, the headline residential figure stopped capturing a fast-growing slice of the true completed-unsold problem. SA overhang has since climbed to 18,752 units at end-2025 and 19,263 units in 1Q2026 — and, for the first time, the value of unsold SA stock (RM16.52 billion) now exceeds unsold residential (RM16.37 billion). Add the two together and the wide-definition completed-unsold inventory is closer to 52,000 units. Treating residential overhang as the whole picture is the most common — and most expensive — error in Malaysian property research.

Against this sits a genuine paradox: Malaysia remains income-positive. Gross residential yields of 5.19% sit +2.44 points above the OPR of 2.75% (held by Bank Negara at the 7 May 2026 MPC, unchanged since the July 2025 cut), one of the few positive-carry property markets in Asia. The two facts are not contradictory once the mechanism is understood. Malaysian prices are structurally sticky downward — the Housing Development Act limits forced sales, developers have the balance-sheet capacity to hold rather than discount, and there is no distressed-resale culture — so the adjustment to oversupply shows up in volume and time-to-sell, not in price. Rents, meanwhile, have firmed on tight landed stock and resilient urban tenancy, widening the carry even as high-rise capital values stall.

理解這份數據最關鍵的修正，仍是 服務式公寓的統計盲點。NAPIC 在 2016 年把服務式公寓（SA）從住宅類改劃到商業類後，表面的住宅數字就不再涵蓋真實已完工未售中一塊快速膨脹的部分。服務式公寓餘屋此後升到 2025 年底的 18,752 戶、2026Q1 的 19,263 戶；而且首度出現未售服務式公寓的金額（RM165.2 億）超過未售住宅（RM163.7 億）。兩者相加，寬定義的已完工未售更接近 52,000 戶。把住宅餘屋當成全貌，是馬來西亞房地產研究最常見、也最昂貴的誤判。

但對照之下有個真實的矛盾：馬來西亞仍然「收租為正」。住宅毛收益率 5.19%，比 OPR 2.75%（BNM 於 2026 年 5 月 7 日 MPC 維持，自 2025 年 7 月降息後不變）高出 +2.44 個百分點，是亞洲少數靠租金就有正現金流的房市。理解了機制，這兩件事其實不矛盾。馬來西亞房價結構性向下僵固——《房屋發展法》（HDA）限制強制拍賣、建商有資產負債表能力「囤」而不降價、也沒有破產拋售文化——所以對供給過剩的調整，反映在成交量與去化時間，而不是價格。同時租金在有地物件稀缺與都會租賃需求支撐下走強，使利差擴大，即便高層的資本利得停滯。

The structural dichotomy is product-based, not geographic. Across all three major markets in this report — Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor Bahru — demand failure is almost exclusively a high-rise condominium and serviced-apartment problem; landed residential consistently clears with overhang rates in the single digits, because it is bought by owner-occupiers whose demand is indifferent to the investment cycle and whose supply cannot be rapidly expanded on scarce urban land. The investment conclusion is therefore stable even as the headline data worsens: avoid high-rise in oversupplied bands, favour landed in under-supplied corridors, and underwrite to the three catalysts — RTS Link commissioning, JS-SEZ enterprise deployment, and the Kuala Lumpur serviced-apartment planned-supply (NC) decision — that will determine whether the 2026–2028 completion wave self-corrects or compounds. The rest of this report builds that case from the supply mechanics outward.

真正的結構性分化在產品，不在地區。本報告涵蓋的三個主要市場——吉隆坡、檳城、新山——需求疲弱幾乎全部集中在高層公寓與服務式公寓；有地住宅的餘屋率始終是個位數，因為它的買家是自住客、需求不受投資循環左右，而供給又受限於稀缺的都會土地、無法快速擴張。因此即使表面數據惡化，投資結論依然不變：避開供給過剩價位帶的高層物件，鎖定供給不足走廊的有地住宅，並以三個催化劑來評估風險——新柔捷運（RTS Link）通車、JS-SEZ 招商落地、以及吉隆坡服務式公寓規劃供給（NC）的核准決定——這三者決定 2026 至 2028 年的完工潮會自我修正、還是繼續累積。本報告其餘部分，將從供給機制由內而外把這個論點建立起來。

02SUPPLY & THE OVERHANG ENGINE供給面與餘屋成因

The overhang is best understood as a time-lagged machine with locked-in consequences. Property supply does not respond to demand in real time: there is a two-to-three-year gap between the decision to start a project and the moment it completes and counts as inventory. A project commenced in 2024 cannot be un-built — the 106,236 units started that year (a 20.6% jump as developers chased the post-pandemic recovery) arrive between 2026 and 2028 regardless of what demand does in the interim. That wave is now landing: full-year 2025 completions reached 99,877 residential units, up 21.6% year-on-year, while absorption ran near its five-year average of about 85,000 units. When completions exceed absorption, the difference does not vanish; it accumulates as overhang. This is the engine, and it is mechanical.

餘屋最好理解成一台「有時間落差、後果已鎖定」的機器。房地產供給不會即時回應需求：從決定動工到完工計入庫存，中間有兩到三年的落差。2024 年動工的物件拆不掉——那年開工的 106,236 戶（年增 20.6%，建商追逐疫後復甦），無論期間需求如何，都會在 2026 到 2028 年陸續完工。這波正在落地：2025 全年住宅完工達 99,877 戶，年增 21.6%，而吸納量約在五年均值 85,000 戶上下。當完工量超過吸納量，差額不會消失，它會累積成餘屋。這就是引擎，而且是機械式的。

The developer response has been rational and swift, which is the necessary precondition for an eventual turn — but it acts on the wrong end of the pipe. New residential launches fell 14.9% in 2025 to 64,487 units, with a full-year sell-through of just 35.5%; the REHDA developer survey found only 41% of members planned launches in 2H2025 and optimism collapsed to 19% from 51%. The first quarter of 2026 extended the retreat — 9,112 units launched at an 11.5% take-up. Supply discipline is real and it is the seed of the next up-cycle. But it tightens future supply; the units already under construction will still complete on schedule. Because of the build lag, cutting launches in 2025 relieves the market in 2028–2029 — too late to soften the 2026–2027 completion peak. This is the cruelty of the property cycle: the cure arrives a full cycle after the disease.

建商的反應理性而迅速，是市場最終轉向的前提——但它作用在管線的錯誤一端。2025 年新住宅推案量年減 14.9% 至 64,487 戶，全年去化率僅 35.5%；REHDA 業者調查顯示只有 41% 會員打算在 2025 下半年推案，樂觀度從 51% 崩到 19%。2026 年第一季延續撤退——推案 9,112 戶、去化率 11.5%。供給端的紀律是真的，也是下一輪上行的種子。但它收緊的是未來供給；已在施工的，仍會如期完工。由於建造落差，2025 年砍推案，要到 2028–2029 年才紓解市場——來不及軟化 2026–2027 的完工高峰。這正是房市循環的殘酷：解藥總在病發整整一個循環之後才到。

The serviced-apartment loophole — origins of the blind spot服務式公寓漏洞——盲點的起源

The serviced-apartment segment is the structural core of the problem, and it exists because of a planning arbitrage. Built on commercial land but lived in as homes, SA units escape the rules that govern residential development: no Bumiputera quota, no affordable-housing quota, higher permitted plot ratios, and faster approvals. For a developer chasing margin on expensive urban land, that arbitrage is irresistible — which is why a decade of SA launches across KL, JB and Penang produced a wall of RM500k–RM1m product that bears only a loose relationship to local end-user demand. The 2016 reclassification then removed this entire category from the residential overhang statistic, so the official number understated reality precisely as the SA glut was forming. The national SA pipeline today — overhang plus under-construction plus planned — is roughly 83,383 units on NAPIC's FY2025 status tables, with 50,329 units still under construction that have not yet hit the inventory count. That under-construction block alone is larger than the entire residential and serviced-apartment overhang combined — it is next decade's oversupply, already poured.

服務式公寓是整個問題的結構核心，它的存在源自一場「規劃套利」。蓋在商業地上、卻當住宅住，服務式公寓繞開了管理住宅開發的規則：沒有土著配額、沒有可負擔房屋配額、容積率更高、審批更快。對在昂貴都會土地上追逐毛利的建商，這個套利難以抗拒——這也是為何過去十年吉隆坡、新山、檳城的服務式公寓推案，堆出一面牆的 RM50 至 100 萬產品，和本地自住需求只有鬆散關係。2016 年的重分類，又把這整個類別從住宅餘屋統計中移除，於是官方數字恰恰在服務式公寓供給過剩成形之際低估了現實。如今全國服務式公寓供給線——餘屋＋在建＋規劃——約 83,383 戶，其中 50,329 戶仍在施工、尚未計入庫存——光這個在建量，就比住宅加上服務式公寓的餘屋總和還大，是下個十年早已澆灌成形的供給過剩。

SA stock also clears differently — and worse — which is why concentration in this category is so dangerous. Commercially titled units are ineligible for first-home schemes, stamp-duty exemptions, 90% owner-occupier LTV and Bumiputera-quota absorption; they carry utility tariffs four-to-six times residential rates and stricter financing. The documented clearing cycle is 3–5 years versus 1–2 for residential. And the price problem compounds the structural one: roughly 85% of SA overhang is priced above RM500k, beyond the organic purchasing power of the local salaried market. That leaves clearance dependent on foreign and expatriate demand — exactly the buyer pool that the new 8% foreign stamp duty (doubled from 4% on 1 January 2026) is designed to discourage. The policy that protects local affordability and the policy that would clear the SA glut are, in this one segment, pulling in opposite directions.

服務式公寓的去化方式也不同、而且更差，這正是它高度集中為何危險。商業地契單位不適用首購補助、印花稅減免、自住 90% 貸款成數、土著配額吸收；水電費率是住宅的 4 至 6 倍、貸款條件更嚴。有紀錄的消化週期是 3 至 5 年，住宅只要 1 至 2 年。價格問題又疊加結構問題：約 85% 的服務式公寓餘屋定價在 RM50 萬以上，超出本地薪資族的實質購買力。去化於是高度依賴外籍與外派需求——偏偏這正是新的 8% 外國印花稅（2026 年 1 月 1 日由 4% 倍增）要勸退的客群。保護本地可負擔性的政策，與能清掉服務式公寓庫存的政策，在這個類別上正好背道而馳。

03DEMAND: INTENT WITHOUT ACCESS需求面：想買的人很多，能成交的不夠

Demand is more nuanced than either transaction volumes or overhang suggest, and its history explains why. Through the 2010s, transaction volume drifted sideways around 310,000–330,000 deals a year, dipped in the 2020 pandemic, then surged on the HOC-and-low-rate stimulus to a decade high of 420,545 all-sector transactions in 2024. In 2025 the count slipped slightly to 416,413 even as value rose to RM241.9 billion — the signature of a market in which prices and the upper segment are still rising while the broad volume base softens. Full-year 2025 residential transactions were 256,512 (−1.5%) worth RM108.27 billion. Intent is real; what fails is access.

需求比成交量或餘屋數字看到的更微妙，它的歷史說明了原因。整個 2010 年代，成交量在每年 31 萬至 33 萬件間橫盤，2020 年疫情下挫，接著在 HOC 與低利率刺激下暴衝，到 2024 年創下 420,545 件（全行業）的十年新高。2025 年件數小幅回落到 416,413，但總值升到 RM2,419 億——這正是「價格與高端仍漲、廣義量能轉弱」市場的特徵。2025 全年住宅成交 256,512 件（−1.5%）、總值 RM1,082.7 億。想買是真的，卡住的是「能不能買到」。

The clearest evidence of the access problem is launch take-up. In 3Q2025 only 5.1% of high-rise units sold in the launch window, against 22.5% for landed — a four-fold gap that maps onto financing profiles, not onto price alone. A sub-10% take-up is not disinterest; it is the financing system filtering most participants out before they can transact. The binding constraint is credit. The residential loan-approval rate recovered to roughly 44.8% by mid-2025 (from a 42.6% low), but more than half of applications are still declined, and a developer survey found 72% of buyers could not secure financing for the unit they wanted.

「買不到」最清楚的證據是新案去化。2025Q3 高層住宅首期只賣出 5.1%，有地住宅則有 22.5%——四倍的差距，對應的是貸款條件，而不只是價格。首期去化低於 10% 不是沒人要，而是貸款體系在他們成交之前，就把大多數人篩掉了。真正的約束是信貸。住宅貸款核准率到 2025 年年中回升到約 44.8%（自 42.6% 低點），但仍過半被拒；一份業者調查更顯示 72% 的買家無法為想要的單位取得貸款。

Causal chain · why credit binds因果鏈 · 信貸為何卡住

Household debt-to-GDP rose from ~86% in 2015, eased mid-cycle, and is back to 84.8% at end-2025 — among the highest in ASEAN. High existing leverage → debt-service ratios at the ceiling for M40 households → banks reject the marginal applicant → only equity-rich buyers transact → high-rise launches aimed at leveraged buyers don't clear → overhang. The 8% foreign stamp duty and SJKP guarantee act on the edges of this chain, not its core.家庭負債佔 GDP 自 2015 年約 86%，循環中段回落，2025 年底又回到 84.8%——東協最高之一。既有槓桿高 → M40 家庭償債比逼近上限 → 銀行拒絕邊際申請人 → 只有資產充裕者成交 → 針對槓桿買家的高層推案賣不掉 → 餘屋。8% 外國印花稅與 SJKP 保證只作用在這條鏈的邊緣，不是核心。

Policy has leaned against this, but at the margin. Budget 2026 doubled the SJKP mortgage-guarantee scheme to RM20 billion for about 80,000 buyers (now including gig and informal-income workers) and extended the full stamp-duty exemption on homes up to RM500k to end-2027. These widen access for the sub-RM500k buyer — useful, but aimed at exactly the segment that is least over-supplied. They do little for the RM500k-plus high-rise and SA inventory that constitutes the overhang. The result is a K-shaped market: genuine undersupply below RM300k where employment anchors exist; the healthiest take-up in the RM300k–500k band; and institutionally constrained demand above RM500k in high-rise formats. The secondary market's ~79% share of transactions is the direct expression of buyers voting with their feet for where value, location and financing access intersect — and that intersection is rarely a new high-rise launch.

政策有在對抗，但只在邊際。2026 年預算把 SJKP 貸款保證倍增到 RM200 億、覆蓋約 8 萬名買家（現納入零工與非正式收入族群），並把 RM50 萬以下住宅的印花稅全免延到 2027 年底。這些擴大了 RM50 萬以下買家的「可得性」——有用，但瞄準的恰恰是最不供給過剩的區段；對構成餘屋主體的 RM50 萬以上高層與服務式公寓庫存幫助有限。結果是一個 K 型市場：有就業支撐的地點，RM30 萬以下確實供給不足；RM30 至 50 萬去化最健康；RM50 萬以上的高層則被制度性卡死。二手市場佔成交約 79%，正是買家用腳投票、往「價值、地段、貸款可得性」交集靠攏的直接表現——而那個交集，很少是新推的高層案。

04VALUATION估值

Two frameworks give two conclusions, and holding both at once is the key to valuing this market. On income, Malaysia is cheap: a +2.44-point carry over the policy rate is rare in Asia, where Singapore, Hong Kong and most of Australia run negative real carry. On price level, it is stretched: the national House Price Index (base 2010=100) reached 233.1 in 2025 (+2.6%), at an average transacted price of RM502,922, and ticked higher again to 235.2 in 1Q2026 — against household incomes that have not kept pace, leaving the national median multiple around 4–5× income and materially higher in KL and Penang. The reconciliation is that the income case rests on rents (which are rising) and the price case rests on capital values (which are not), so the same asset can be cheap on yield and dear on price simultaneously.

The history matters here. Malaysian house-price growth ran hot in the 2010–2014 boom (high single digits to double digits annually), then decelerated sharply after the 2014–2015 cooling measures to a low-single-digit crawl that has held for a decade. The official NAPIC index makes the arc concrete: from 164.5 in 2015 to 233.1 in 2025 — about +42% nominal, or roughly 3.6% a year, front-loaded into 2015–2017 (+6–7% annually) and barely above 1% through the 2019–2021 trough. That deceleration is itself the slow-motion correction: rather than a price crash, Malaysia has run a decade of real-terms stagnation that gradually repaired affordability while never delivering the nominal pain that forces inventory to clear. The signal to watch is therefore not the annual headline but the turn: 3Q2025 recorded the first quarterly HPI decline since 3Q2021 (−1.46% QoQ), and volume-weighted Kuala Lumpur prices fell 4.3% year-on-year — dragged down by the oversupplied high-rise mix even as prime landed addresses rose. When prices are sticky, the adjustment shows up first in volume and in the quarterly sequential turn; that turn has now begun.

兩個框架、兩個結論，而同時握住兩者，正是評估這個市場的關鍵。看收租，馬來西亞便宜：比政策利率高 +2.44 個百分點的利差，在亞洲少見——新加坡、香港、澳洲多數地區都是負實質利差。看價格水準，則偏貴：全國房價指數（基期 2010=100）2025 年達 233.1（+2.6%）、平均成交價 RM502,922，2026Q1 再升到 235.2，所得卻沒跟上——全國房價所得比約 4 至 5 倍，吉隆坡與檳城明顯更高。調和之道在於：收租論點靠的是租金（在漲），價格論點靠的是資本利得（沒漲），所以同一資產可以「以收益看便宜、以價格看貴」並存。

這裡歷史很重要。馬來西亞房價在 2010–2014 年榮景火熱（年增高個位數到雙位數），2014–2015 年冷卻措施後急遽減速，進入維持十年的低個位數爬行。官方 NAPIC 指數把這條弧線具體化：從 2015 年的 164.5 到 2025 年的 233.1——名目約 +42%、年均約 3.6%，且集中在 2015–2017（年增 6–7%），在 2019–2021 谷底僅略高於 1%。這個減速本身就是慢動作的修正：馬來西亞沒有崩盤，而是走了十年的實質停滯，逐步修復可負擔性、卻始終沒有那種逼庫存出清的名目陣痛。因此要看的訊號不是年增表面，而是轉折：2025Q3 出現 2021Q3 以來首次季度房價下跌（−1.46% QoQ），以成交量加權的吉隆坡房價年跌 4.3%——被供給過剩的高層結構拖累，即便精華有地地段仍在漲。當房價僵固，調整會先反映在成交量與季度環比轉折；而這個轉折，現在已經開始。

05KUALA LUMPUR: A SERVICED-APARTMENT PROBLEM吉隆坡：一個服務式公寓的問題

Kuala Lumpur inverts the national narrative, and the inversion is a direct legacy of its 2012–2019 development history. During those years the city's prime districts — KLCC, Bukit Bintang, Conlay, the river-of-life corridor — absorbed a torrent of serviced-apartment and branded-residence launches, marketed heavily to foreign and investor buyers. That build-out is why KL's residential overhang today is low and still falling — just 2,055 units at end-2025, the lowest of any major market — while its real exposure sits in the SA category: 3,964 units of SA overhang, 21.1% of the national total, concentrated in a single federal territory.

The forward risk is larger than the current number, and the NAPIC supply tables show it is still growing. At end-2025 KL carried an SA pipeline of 9,619 units under construction plus 8,340 units planned (NC); by 1Q2026 those had risen to 11,754 under construction and 10,189 planned, even as the standing overhang sits below 4,000. KL's planned-SA queue is the largest of any state — roughly 58% of the national NC total at end-2025 and still climbing. Whether that pipeline proceeds to construction or is withdrawn is, quite literally, the single most consequential overhang decision for any KL investor. The logic is simple and binary: if it proceeds, KL's SA overhang plausibly climbs from ~4,000 toward 15,000+ by 2030, recreating in KL the glut Johor already lives with; if it is deferred, the existing overhang clears against genuine prime-location demand and the segment normalises. This is a policy switch, not a market variable — which is exactly why it should be monitored directly rather than forecast statistically.

吉隆坡與全國劇本相反，而這個反轉直接源自它 2012–2019 年的開發史。那幾年，城市的精華區——KLCC、武吉免登、Conlay、生命之河走廊——吸納了一波又一波服務式公寓與品牌住宅推案，大力行銷給外籍與投資型買家。正是這波建設，使吉隆坡今天的住宅餘屋低且仍在下降——2025 年底僅 2,055 戶，是各主要市場最低——而真正的曝險落在服務式公寓：餘屋 3,964 戶，佔全國 21.1%，集中在單一聯邦直轄區。

未來風險比當前數字更大，而 NAPIC 供給表顯示它還在成長。2025 年底吉隆坡的服務式公寓供給線有 9,619 戶在建、加上 8,340 戶規劃（NC）；到 2026Q1 已升到 在建 11,754 戶、規劃 10,189 戶，而現有餘屋還不到 4,000 戶。吉隆坡的規劃服務式公寓量是各州最大——2025 年底約佔全國 NC 的 58%，且仍在攀升。這條供給線到底會推進動工、還是被撤回，幾乎可以說是任何吉隆坡投資人最關鍵的單一餘屋決定。邏輯簡單而二元：若推進，吉隆坡服務式公寓餘屋很可能從約 4,000 戶爬到 2030 年 15,000 戶以上，在吉隆坡重演柔佛已承受的供給過剩；若延後，現有餘屋對著真實的精華地段需求去化，該區段恢復正常。這是一個政策開關，不是市場變數——正因如此，應該直接盯著、而不是用統計去預測。

Beneath the overhang story, the KL transaction market is strong at the top — and the bifurcation is the structural signature of the city. KL recorded 21,635 transactions in 2025 (+8.7%) worth RM23.81 billion (+36.6%), with above-RM1m deals making up 22.8% of first-half volume. Prime addresses rose even as the volume-weighted index fell 4.3%: KLCC +0.7%, Bangsar +3.4%, Mont’Kiara +6.2%, TTDI landed +6.3%. Two markets coexist in one city — equity-funded buyers transacting freely at the top, credit-dependent buyers locked out below — and the official index, which blends them, understates the prime trend and overstates the distress in good locations. The 8% foreign stamp duty cooled luxury demand into late-2025, and MRT3 (approved 2025, completion 2030–2031) is the next location-premium catalyst; the MRT1 and MRT2 precedents suggest stations capitalise a transit premium 12–18 months after the timeline firms, favouring currently-underserved alignments at Sentul, Wangsa Maju, Seputeh and Bukit Jalil.

在餘屋故事底下，吉隆坡的成交市場在高端很強——而這種分化正是這座城市的結構特徵。吉隆坡 2025 年成交 21,635 件（+8.7%）、總值 RM238.1 億（+36.6%），RM100 萬以上佔上半年量的 22.8%。即使成交量加權指數跌 4.3%，精華地段仍漲：KLCC +0.7%、Bangsar +3.4%、Mont’Kiara +6.2%、TTDI 有地 +6.3%。一座城市裡並存兩個市場——以資產自有資金的買家在高端自由成交、靠信貸的買家在下方被鎖在門外——而把兩者混在一起的官方指數，低估了精華趨勢、又高估了好地段的壓力。8% 外國印花稅在 2025 年底冷卻了豪宅需求；MRT3（2025 核准、2030–2031 完工）是下一個地段溢價催化劑——以 MRT1、MRT2 的前例看，站點通常在時間表確定後 12 至 18 個月把交通溢價反映到房價，利多目前較冷門的善南、旺沙瑪朱、士拍、武吉加里爾沿線。

It helps to see KL not as one market but as a ring. The prime core — KLCC, Bukit Bintang, Bangsar, Mont’Kiara, Damansara Heights — is branded, low-density or landed, and supply-constrained; this is where 2025 prices rose and prime rents climbed 7.5–7.6%, and where the SA overhang simply does not bind because the product is differentiated and the buyers are equity- or foreign-funded. The stress sits one ring out, in the city-fringe SA belts — Cochrane–Cheras, Old Klang Road, Jalan Ipoh, Sentul, Setapak — where most of the 3,964-unit SA overhang and the 9,619-unit under-construction block are concentrated: RM500k–800k investor product sold into thin local end-demand. Savills puts KL's all-category overhang at 7,143 units in 2025, down from 9,081 in 2024 — but that headline improvement is in completed stock, while the UC/NC pipeline quietly refills behind it.

把吉隆坡看成一個「環」會更清楚。精華核心——KLCC、武吉免登、Bangsar、Mont’Kiara、Damansara Heights——是品牌、低密度或有地、且供給受限；2025 年漲價、精華租金升 7.5–7.6% 的就是這裡，而服務式公寓餘屋在這裡根本不構成壓力，因為產品有差異化、買家靠自有資金或外籍資金。壓力在外一環，在市區邊緣的服務式公寓帶——Cochrane–Cheras、舊巴生路、Jalan Ipoh、Sentul、Setapak——3,964 戶服務式公寓餘屋與 9,619 戶在建量多半集中在此：RM50 至 80 萬的投資型產品，賣進稀薄的本地自住需求。Savills 估吉隆坡全類別餘屋 2025 年為 7,143 戶，低於 2024 年的 9,081——但這個表面改善是在已完工存量，背後的在建／規劃供給線正悄悄回填。

The forward catalyst is MRT3. The 39-km Circle Line, fully approved in 2025 for 2030–2031 completion, closes the loop around the inner city, and the MRT1 (2017) and MRT2 (2022–2023) precedents are clear: station-area values capitalised a transit premium of 10–20% once timelines firmed, strongest where prior transit was poorest. That points to the currently-underserved alignments — Sentul, Wangsa Maju, Seputeh, Bukit Jalil, Pandan — as the early-stage play: accumulate landed or low-density stock within 500 m of confirmed stations 18–24 months ahead of completion. The discipline is to keep the two catalysts separate: MRT3 re-rates well-located land, but it does not fix the fringe SA belts, where the binding constraint is financing, not transit. Note too that the 8% foreign stamp duty is a headwind for the foreign-heavy prime segment and barely touches the local-end fringe — so it slows the part of KL that works and does little for the part that does not.

未來催化劑是 MRT3。39 公里的環線 2025 年完全核准、2030–2031 完工，把內城繞成一圈；MRT1（2017）與 MRT2（2022–2023）的前例很清楚：時間表一旦確定，站點周邊把 10–20% 的交通溢價反映進房價，原本交通最差的地方漲最多。這指向目前較冷門的沿線——Sentul、Wangsa Maju、Seputeh、Bukit Jalil、Pandan——是早期布局點：在完工前 18–24 個月，鎖定確定站點 500 公尺內的有地或低密度物件。紀律是把兩個催化劑分開：MRT3 會把地段好的土地重新估值，但救不了邊緣服務式公寓帶——那裡的約束是貸款、不是交通。也要注意，8% 外國印花稅是外籍佔比高的精華區的逆風，卻幾乎碰不到本地自住的邊緣帶——所以它拖慢了吉隆坡「行得通」的那一塊，對「行不通」的那塊卻幾乎沒幫助。

Price tiers — the prime ladder價格層級——精華階梯

KL's prime market is a ladder, and knowing the rungs matters because the 8% foreign duty and the financing filter act on each differently. At the top, landed in TTDI transacts around RM1.70m and KLCC branded condos around RM1.48m; Bangsar sits near RM1.01m and Mont’Kiara around RM0.86m, against a KL-wide median closer to RM0.66m. The top of the ladder is supply-constrained and equity-funded, which is why it kept appreciating through 2025 while the volume-weighted index fell — but it is also the segment most exposed to the 8% foreign duty, because foreign buyers concentrate in exactly these addresses. The fringe SA belts sit a full tier below on price yet carry essentially all of the overhang. In KL, price level and distress are inversely correlated: the expensive product is scarce and wanted, the cheaper investor product is abundant and stuck.

吉隆坡的精華市場是一座階梯，認得清每一階很重要，因為 8% 外國印花稅與貸款篩選器，對每一階的作用都不同。最上層，TTDI 有地成交約 RM1.70m、KLCC 品牌公寓約 RM1.48m；Bangsar 近 RM1.01m、Mont’Kiara 約 RM0.86m，對比全吉隆坡中位約 RM0.66m。階梯頂端供給受限、靠自有資金，這是它在 2025 年成交量加權指數下跌時仍持續增值的原因——但它也是最受 8% 外國印花稅衝擊的區段，因為外籍買家正集中在這些地段。邊緣的服務式公寓帶在價格上低一整層，卻幾乎承載了所有餘屋。在吉隆坡，價格水準與去化壓力呈反向相關：貴的產品稀缺而搶手，便宜的投資型產品充裕而卡住。

Kuala Lumpur — playbook吉隆坡——操作策略

Buy: prime landed (TTDI, Bangsar, Damansara Heights, Bukit Tunku) for capital preservation and 7%-plus prime rental growth; and low-density or landed stock within 500 m of confirmed MRT3 stations in Sentul, Seputeh, Wangsa Maju and Bukit Jalil, accumulated 18–24 months before the 2030–2031 opening. Wait: branded prime condos where the 8% foreign duty is still being digested — entry should improve into 2026–2027 as foreign volume resets. Avoid: serviced apartments anywhere in the city-fringe belts, and any project in an area carrying the NC pipeline, where local end-demand cannot absorb the completion wave and the financing filter binds hardest. Hold thesis: KL is a capital-preservation and prime-rental market, not a yield chase — underwrite to location scarcity and the MRT3 timeline, not to the headline index.

買：精華有地（TTDI、Bangsar、Damansara Heights、Bukit Tunku）做資本保值與 7%+ 的精華租金成長；以及 MRT3 確定站點 500 公尺內的低密度或有地物件，於 2030–2031 通車前 18–24 個月分批進場（Sentul、Seputeh、Wangsa Maju、Bukit Jalil）。等：仍在消化 8% 外國印花稅的品牌精華公寓——隨外籍量能重設，2026–2027 進場條件應更好。避：市區邊緣帶的任何服務式公寓，以及位於 NC 供給線地區的任何案子——那裡本地自住需求無法吸收完工潮、貸款篩選最緊。持有論點：吉隆坡是資本保值與精華租金市場，不是高收益賭局——以地段稀缺與 MRT3 時間表評估，而非看表面指數。

06PENANG: THE INDUSTRIAL THESIS檳城：工業驅動的論點

Penang has quietly fallen out of the overhang top tier — residential completed-unsold of roughly 2,730–3,165 units, with landed clearing well and the stress confined to older high-rise. The reason is structural and decades-deep. Penang has been Malaysia's electronics heartland since the Bayan Lepas Free Trade Zone opened in 1972, and the current semiconductor super-cycle has supercharged that base: the state approved RM22.4 billion of manufacturing investment in 2025 (+29%), across 232 projects creating 24,633 jobs, anchored by Intel's ~RM12 billion advanced-packaging build (operational 2026), AMD, ASE and ViTrox. Unlike a speculative housing thesis, this is demand that originates outside the property cycle: each high-value job converts into rental and ownership demand that is indifferent to interest rates and sentiment.

The causal chain is what makes Penang different from JB. In Johor, supply ran ahead of a still-forming demand catalyst; in Penang, an entrenched employment base meets a land-constrained island where landed supply simply cannot expand quickly. Tight landed supply plus structural high-income demand equals durable price support — which is why Penang's overhang is modest and concentrated in the one product type (older high-rise) where it has no moat. Infrastructure extends the runway: the LRT Mutiara Line (under construction, target 2031) and the scaled-back Silicon Island reclamation (Phase 1 target 2031) will, over time, open new transit-served and industrial-adjacent corridors. The posture is straightforward — favour landed and transit-proximate stock on the island and in Batu Kawan; treat older, amenity-poor high-rise as the segment to avoid.

檳城已悄悄退出餘屋前段班——住宅已完工未售約 2,730 至 3,165 戶，有地去化良好，壓力集中在較舊的高層。原因是結構性的、扎根數十年。自 1972 年峇六拜自由貿易區開放以來，檳城就是馬來西亞的電子重鎮，而當前的半導體超級循環更為這個基礎注入強心針：檳州 2025 年核准製造業投資 RM224 億（+29%），共 232 個項目、創造 24,633 個職位，由 Intel 約 RM120 億的先進封裝廠（2026 投產）、AMD、ASE、ViTrox 領頭。與投機型購屋論點不同，這是源自房市循環之外的需求：每個高薪職位都轉化為對利率與情緒不敏感的租賃與自住需求。

讓檳城不同於新山的，是它的因果鏈。在柔佛，供給跑在一個尚在成形的需求催化劑之前；在檳城，是一個扎根的就業基礎，撞上一座土地受限、有地供給根本無法快速擴張的島。有地供給緊俏，加上結構性高所得需求，等於持久的價格支撐——這正是檳城餘屋不大、且只集中在唯一沒有護城河的產品（老舊高層）的原因。基建延長了續航：檳城輕軌 Mutiara 線（施工中、目標 2031）與縮編後的矽島填海（第一期目標 2031），會逐步打開新的捷運與工業鄰近走廊。姿態很清楚——優選島上與 Batu Kawan 的有地與近捷運物件；把老舊、機能差的高層視為要避開的區段。

Penang is really two markets, and the distinction drives the strategy. The island — George Town, Bayan Lepas, Tanjung Tokong — is land-constrained: landed stock is genuinely scarce and clears at a premium, and what overhang exists is confined to older, amenity-poor high-rise. The mainland — Seberang Perai and especially Batu Kawan — is the growth frontier: anchored by the Second Bridge and the Batu Kawan Industrial Park, Batu Kawan prices have converged toward southern-island levels as the semiconductor supply chain pushes employment across the strait. The investment read flips by geography: on the island, buy landed and transit-proximate and avoid ageing high-rise; on the mainland, Batu Kawan is the structural-growth bet on industrial adjacency rather than on the property cycle.

The demand mechanism is worth stating plainly, because it is also the risk. Penang housing demand is a derivative of the global semiconductor capex cycle — which is itself a derivative of AI, datacentre and supply-chain-reshoring spending. Each advanced-packaging line (Intel's ~RM12bn build, operational 2026) and each test-and-assembly expansion (AMD, ASE, ViTrox) employs thousands of engineers and technicians whose incomes sit well above the local median, converting directly into mid-market housing demand that is largely indifferent to interest rates. The risk is the same thesis inverted: semiconductors are cyclical, and a down-cycle — as in 2019 and 2023 — would soften exactly the demand that protects Penang. Infrastructure de-risks the timeline without removing the cyclicality: the LRT Mutiara Line (RM16.8bn, ~21 stations, target 2031) and the Silicon Island reclamation (Phase 1 2031) extend the runway and open new transit-served and industrial-adjacent corridors.

檳城其實是兩個市場，而這個區別主導了策略。島上——喬治市、峇六拜、丹絨道光——土地受限：有地物件真的稀缺、去化帶溢價，而僅有的餘屋集中在較舊、機能差的高層。對岸（威省）——尤其 Batu Kawan——是成長前緣：靠第二大橋與 Batu Kawan 工業園支撐，隨半導體供應鏈把就業推過海峽，Batu Kawan 房價已向島南水準靠攏。投資解讀依地理翻轉：在島上，買有地與近捷運、避開老舊高層；在對岸，Batu Kawan 是押注工業鄰近性的結構性成長，而非押房市循環。

需求機制值得講白，因為它同時也是風險。檳城的購屋需求，是全球半導體資本支出循環的衍生品——而後者又是 AI、資料中心與供應鏈回流支出的衍生品。每一條先進封裝產線（Intel 約 RM120 億、2026 投產）、每一次測試封裝擴產（AMD、ASE、ViTrox），都雇用數千名薪資遠高於在地中位數的工程師與技術員，直接轉化為對利率大致不敏感的中價位購屋需求。風險就是這個論點的反面：半導體是循環性產業，一個下行週期（如 2019、2023）會軟化的，正是保護檳城的那塊需求。基建降低時間風險、卻移除不了循環性：檳城輕軌 Mutiara 線（RM168 億、約 21 站、目標 2031）與矽島填海（第一期 2031）延長了續航，並打開新的捷運與工業鄰近走廊。

Infrastructure — the runway基礎建設——續航跑道

Penang's catalyst stack has shifted from speculative to confirmed, and each project extends the demand runway into the 2030s. The LRT Mutiara Line — RM16.8bn, roughly 21 stations across 29.5 km including a 5 km marine segment, targeted for 2031 — will for the first time give the island and Bayan Lepas a rapid-transit spine, repeating the station-premium pattern seen along KL's MRT lines. The Pan Island Link (RM8.88bn, at federal feasibility) would relieve the island's chronic road congestion. And the scaled-back Silicon Island reclamation (Phase 1, 2031) creates new industrial-adjacent land precisely where the semiconductor cluster needs expansion room. The read-through is the same as KL's MRT3: infrastructure does not rescue oversupplied stock, but it re-rates the land and well-located product along its alignment — here, Bayan Lepas, the island's south-west, and the Batu Kawan mainland.

檳城的催化劑組合已從「臆測」轉為「確定」，每個項目都把需求續航延伸到 2030 年代。檳城輕軌 Mutiara 線——RM168 億、約 21 站、橫跨 29.5 公里（含 5 公里海上段）、目標 2031——將首度為島嶼與峇六拜帶來捷運主幹，重演吉隆坡 MRT 沿線的站點溢價模式。泛島大道（PIL）（RM88.8 億，聯邦可行性評估中）可紓解島上長期的道路壅塞。而縮編後的矽島填海（第一期 2031）在半導體聚落最需要擴張空間之處，創造新的工業鄰近土地。其推論與吉隆坡 MRT3 相同：基建救不了供給過剩的存量，但會把沿線的土地與地段良好的產品重新估值——在此即峇六拜、島嶼西南、以及對岸 Batu Kawan。

Penang — playbook檳城——操作策略

Buy: landed on the island (George Town heritage-fringe, Tanjung Tokong, Bayan Lepas) where scarcity is structural and durable; and Batu Kawan on the mainland for the industrial-adjacency growth bet, entered before the LRT and Silicon Island timelines fully price in. Avoid: ageing, amenity-poor high-rise on the island — this is where Penang's modest overhang actually sits and it competes poorly against new transit-served stock. Size for the cycle: the thesis is levered to the semiconductor capex cycle, so hold horizons should span at least one full chip cycle (3–4 years) to ride through a 2019- or 2023-style down-leg. Hold thesis: Penang is an appreciation-and-scarcity market, not a yield market — gross yields on landed run a modest 3.5–4.5%, and the return comes from land scarcity plus the structural employment anchor, not cash flow.

買：島上有地（喬治市古蹟周邊、丹絨道光、峇六拜），稀缺是結構性且持久的；以及對岸 Batu Kawan，押注工業鄰近性的成長，在 LRT 與矽島時間表完全反映前進場。避：島上老舊、機能差的高層——檳城那一小撮餘屋正落在此，且難敵新的近捷運產品。依循環抓部位：論點槓桿於半導體資本支出循環，持有期應至少涵蓋一個完整晶片循環（3–4 年），以撐過 2019 或 2023 式的下行段。持有論點：檳城是增值與稀缺市場，不是收益市場——有地毛收益僅 3.5–4.5%，報酬來自土地稀缺加上結構性就業錨，而非現金流。

07JOHOR BAHRU: THE MOST COMPLEX MARKET新山：最複雜的市場

Johor carries Malaysia's most complex, multi-layered overhang and its strongest demand catalyst simultaneously — and both trace to the same history. The Iskandar Malaysia corridor, launched in 2006, drew a wave of large Chinese developers between 2013 and 2017 (Country Garden's Forest City, R&F Princess Cove, Greenland) who launched tens of thousands of high-rise and serviced-apartment units aimed at cross-border Chinese buyers. When Chinese capital controls tightened in 2017 and that buyer pool evaporated, Johor was left with the overhang legacy it still carries: residential overhang of 3,705 plus an SA overhang of 9,477 units — fully 50.5% of the entire national SA total — for a combined completed-unsold of ~14,705 units across all categories (rising to 15,470 by 1Q2026), the largest of any market in the country.

The catalyst side is where the second act differs from the first. This time the demand is anchored locally, not in offshore speculation. The JB–Singapore RTS Link is on track for December 2026 (around 65% complete, trial running since end-2025), and the Johor-Singapore SEZ drew a record RM110 billion of approved investment in 2025 with 24,000-plus jobs committed. The mechanism is concrete: an eight-minute rail crossing to Woodlands collapses the effective commute, making JB housing a genuine substitute for Singapore rents that run three-to-five times higher; and SEZ employment creates resident demand rather than empty investor units. The market is already pricing it — JB serviced-apartment transacted prices rose +20.8% to RM711 psf in 2025, with RTS-corridor Bukit Chagar trading RM1,000–1,500 psf at 6–8% gross yields.

柔佛同時背著馬來西亞最複雜的多層餘屋，與最強的需求催化劑——而兩者都源自同一段歷史。2006 年啟動的依斯干達特區，在 2013 至 2017 年吸引一波大型中資建商（碧桂園森林城市、富力公主灣、綠地），推出數以萬計、瞄準跨境中國買家的高層與服務式公寓。當 2017 年中國資本管制收緊、那個客群蒸發，柔佛就留下了它至今仍背負的餘屋遺產：住宅餘屋 3,705 戶，加上服務式公寓餘屋 9,477 戶——佔全國服務式公寓總量整整 50.5%——使全類別已完工未售合計達 約 14,705 戶（2026Q1 升至 15,470），全國各市場之最。

催化面，正是第二幕與第一幕不同之處。這一次需求錨定在本地，而非離岸投機。新柔 RTS Link 有望 2026 年 12 月通車（進度約 65%，2025 年底起試運行），柔佛-新加坡特區（JS-SEZ）2025 年吸引創紀錄的 RM1,100 億核准投資、逾 2.4 萬個職位承諾。機制很具體：八分鐘鐵路過境到兀蘭，壓縮了實際通勤，使新山住宅成為新加坡（租金高出三到五倍）的真實替代；而 SEZ 就業創造的是居住需求，不是空置的投資單位。市場已在反映——新山服務式公寓 2025 年成交價漲 +20.8% 至 RM711/呎，RTS 走廊 Bukit Chagar 成交 RM1,000–1,500/呎、毛收益 6–8%。

The forward logic splits the market into three distinct bets. RTS-adjacent high-rise above RM1,000 psf: the catalyst is largely priced — wait for better entry, because if RTS commissions on time the upside is already in the print, and if it slips you will buy the disappointment cheaper. Zone-1/2 landed (terraces RM400k–700k): accumulate — overhang pressure is minimal, owner-occupier demand is real and SEZ-reinforced, and 5–6.5% gross yields are achievable with genuine appreciation optionality. Peripheral high-rise and Forest City SA: avoid until SEZ absorption is demonstrable in occupancy and resale data, not brochures. The three signals that resolve the bet are NAPIC's quarterly Johor overhang, RTS construction milestones, and JS-SEZ enterprise registrations — watch them in that order, because the overhang number is the lagging truth, the RTS milestone is the coincident driver, and the SEZ registrations are the leading indicator.

未來邏輯把市場拆成三個不同的賭注。RTS 旁、每呎 RM1,000 以上的高層：催化劑大致已反映——等更好的價位，因為若 RTS 準時通車、上檔已在價格裡，若延誤、你能用更便宜的價格買進失望。第一／二區的有地（排屋 RM40 至 70 萬）：積極布局——餘屋壓力極低、自住需求真實且有 SEZ 強化、毛收益 5 至 6.5% 做得到，還帶真實的增值選擇權。外圍高層與森林城市服務式公寓：不碰，直到 SEZ 去化能在「入住率與轉售數據」（不是宣傳冊）上被驗證。解開賭局的三個訊號是：NAPIC 柔佛季度餘屋、RTS 施工里程碑、JS-SEZ 企業登記——按這個順序看，因為餘屋數字是落後的真相、RTS 里程碑是同步的驅動、SEZ 登記則是領先指標。

Johor rewards a zone-level read, because risk and catalyst concentrate differently across Iskandar's flagship areas. Zone A (JB City Centre / Bukit Chagar) is the direct RTS beneficiary; Zone B (Nusajaya / Iskandar Puteri) holds the Medini and EduCity build-out and part of the legacy overhang; Forest City sits outside the natural commute and carries the heaviest empty-tower legacy of the 2013–2017 Chinese-developer wave. The RTS economics are specific and large: an eight-minute crossing to Woodlands North, capacity around 10,000 passengers per hour per direction, at fares near S$5–7 — set against Singapore residential rents that run three-to-five times JB's. That arbitrage is why Bukit Chagar serviced apartments already trade RM1,000–1,500 psf at 6–8% gross yields while the broad Johor average sits at 5.5–6.25% and outer Iskandar Puteri nearer 4.6%.

柔佛值得做「分區」解讀，因為風險與催化劑在依斯干達各旗艦區的集中方式不同。A 區（新山市中心／Bukit Chagar）是 RTS 的直接受益者；B 區（Nusajaya／依斯干達公主城）有 Medini 與 EduCity 的開發、以及部分歷史餘屋；森林城市則落在自然通勤圈之外，背著 2013–2017 中資建商潮最沉重的空樓遺產。RTS 的經濟帳具體而龐大：八分鐘過境到兀蘭北、單向每小時運能約 10,000 人、票價約 S$5–7——對照新加坡住宅租金是新山的三到五倍。正是這個套利，使 Bukit Chagar 的服務式公寓已成交 RM1,000–1,500/呎、毛收益 6–8%，而柔佛大盤平均在 5.5–6.25%、外圍的依斯干達公主城則近 4.6%。

The bigger, slower catalyst is the Johor-Singapore SEZ, which drew a record RM110 billion of approved investment in 2025 (target RM140 billion for 2026) across nine flagship zones under a new incentive framework — concessional corporate tax, and the Forest City Special Financial Zone with 0% tax for single-family offices. The bull case is that SEZ employment converts the 9,477-unit SA overhang from empty investor stock into occupied dwellings for a cross-border workforce. The bear case is written into Forest City itself: a population estimated at 9,000–20,000 against a 700,000-person master plan shows how slowly absorption follows announcements. The discipline follows directly — underwrite JB to occupancy and resale data, not to brochures or approved-investment headlines. The leading signals are JS-SEZ enterprise registrations and, once the line opens, RTS ridership — not the RM110 billion figure, which measures committed intent rather than resident demand.

更大、更慢的催化劑是柔佛-新加坡特區（JS-SEZ），2025 年吸引創紀錄的 RM1,100 億核准投資（2026 目標 RM1,400 億），分布在九個旗艦區、採用新的獎勵框架——優惠企業稅、以及森林城市特別金融區（單一家族辦公室 0% 稅）。樂觀情境是：SEZ 就業把 9,477 戶服務式公寓餘屋，從空置投資存量轉成跨境勞動力入住的住宅。悲觀情境就寫在森林城市本身：人口估計僅 9,000–20,000，對比 70 萬人的總體規劃，顯示去化跟在公告後面有多慢。紀律因此很直接——以入住率與轉售數據評估新山，而不是宣傳冊或核准投資的標題數字。領先訊號是 JS-SEZ 企業進駐登記、以及通車後的 RTS 載客量——而不是 RM1,100 億這個數字，它衡量的是承諾意向、而非居住需求。

The overhang is a serviced-apartment problem餘屋本質是服務式公寓問題

Strip JB's overhang to its components and the diagnosis is unambiguous. Of roughly 14,705 completed-unsold units across all categories, serviced apartments account for 9,477 — about two-thirds — against just 3,705 residential and ~1,500 shops and SoHo. JB does not have a housing glut; it has a serviced-apartment glut, the direct legacy of the 2013–2017 cross-border investor wave. This shapes strategy, because the two clear on entirely different timelines and buyer pools: the residential overhang is small and clears against local owner-occupiers and SEZ in-migration, while the SA overhang is large, commercially titled, foreign-dependent, and now facing the 8% duty. The RTS is the one force that can convert empty SA stock into occupied dwellings — which is exactly why proximity to Bukit Chagar is the single most important variable in the JB serviced-apartment market, and why everything outside that corridor should be underwritten conservatively.

把新山的餘屋拆解到成分，診斷毫不含糊。全類別約 14,705 戶已完工未售中，服務式公寓佔 9,477 戶——約三分之二——住宅僅 3,705 戶、店舖與 SoHo 約 1,500 戶。新山沒有住宅供給過剩，它有的是服務式公寓供給過剩，是 2013–2017 跨境投資潮的直接遺產。這左右策略，因為兩者的去化時間與客群完全不同：住宅餘屋小、對著本地自住客與 SEZ 移入去化；服務式公寓餘屋大、商業地契、依賴外籍、如今還面對 8% 印花稅。RTS 是唯一能把空置服務式公寓轉成入住住宅的力量——這正是為何「臨近 Bukit Chagar」是新山服務式公寓市場最關鍵的單一變數，也是為何這條走廊以外的一切都該保守評估。

Johor Bahru — playbook新山——操作策略

Buy: Zone-1/2 landed — terraces and clusters in the RM400k–700k band (Bukit Indah, Adda Heights, Mount Austin) — for low overhang, real owner-occupier demand and 5–6.5% gross yields with SEZ-reinforced appreciation. Hold / watch: RTS-corridor Bukit Chagar high-rise — the 6–8% yield is attractive, but at RM1,000–1,500 psf the catalyst is largely priced, so wait for better entry or buy only with a long hold and an income focus. Avoid: Forest City and peripheral serviced apartments until occupancy and resale data — not approved-investment headlines — confirm absorption. Signals, in order: JS-SEZ enterprise registrations (leading), RTS construction milestones and, once live, ridership (coincident), and NAPIC's quarterly Johor SA overhang (lagging confirmation). Underwrite to data, not to brochures.

買：第一／二區有地——RM40 至 70 萬的排屋與 cluster（Bukit Indah、Adda Heights、Mount Austin）——餘屋低、自住需求真實、毛收益 5–6.5%，並有 SEZ 強化的增值。持有／觀望：RTS 走廊 Bukit Chagar 的高層——6–8% 收益吸引人，但在 RM1,000–1,500/呎下催化劑大致已反映，故等更好價位、或僅以長期持有與收益取向買進。避：森林城市與外圍服務式公寓，直到入住率與轉售數據（而非核准投資的標題）確認去化。訊號，依序：JS-SEZ 企業進駐登記（領先）、RTS 施工里程碑與通車後載客量（同步）、NAPIC 柔佛季度服務式公寓餘屋（落後確認）。以數據評估，而非宣傳冊。

08NATIONAL OVERHANG PICTURE全馬餘屋圖景

At the national level the overhang has three layers that must be analysed separately, because the same unit count carries very different risk depending on what and where it is. Layer one is the headline residential overhang, broadly spread but led by Perak on units (3,943) and by Johor on value (RM3.30 billion of unsold condo stock against Perak's RM1.24 billion for a comparable unit count). The divergence is the whole point: Perak's overhang is low-cost housing in a market with few new demand catalysts — structural, slow-clearing, but cheap and not systemically important. Johor's is pricey condominium product whose clearance depends on a cross-border catalyst still proving itself. Reading "units" alone would rank them similarly; reading value and product reveals they are entirely different risks.

全國層面，餘屋有三層，必須分開分析，因為同樣的戶數，依「是什麼、在哪裡」承載完全不同的風險。第一層是表面的住宅餘屋，分布廣，但以戶數論由霹靂領頭（3,943 戶）、以金額論由柔佛居首（RM33.0 億未售公寓，對比霹靂相近戶數卻僅 RM12.4 億）。這個差異正是重點：霹靂的餘屋是低價住宅、市場缺少新需求催化劑——結構性、去化慢，但便宜、非系統性重要。柔佛的是高價公寓，去化取決於一個仍在自我驗證的跨境催化劑。光看「戶數」會把兩者排得差不多；看金額與產品，才看出它們是完全不同的風險。

Layer two — the systematically ignored one — is the SA overhang, where concentration is extreme: Johor 50.5% (9,477 units), KL 21.1%, Selangor 13.2% together account for around 85% of the national total. This concentration is itself the risk. A diffuse overhang spread across many states clears against many independent local markets; a concentrated one depends on the fortunes of a single buyer pool — here, the foreign and cross-border demand that the new 8% stamp duty is actively discouraging. Layer three is the forward pipeline that turns today's problem into tomorrow's: Selangor's 18,379-unit SA under-construction block — the single largest in the country — and KL's planned-SA queue (8,340 units at end-2025, already up to 10,189 by 1Q2026) will complete into markets that already carry unsold SA stock. Stack all categories together and Johor's combined completed-unsold of ~14,705 units (rising to 15,470 by 1Q2026) stands as the most complex single-market overhang in Malaysia — roughly twice KL's ~7,150 and more than twice Selangor's ~6,730.

第二層——被系統性忽視的那層——是服務式公寓餘屋，集中度極高：柔佛 50.5%（9,477 戶）、吉隆坡 21.1%、雪蘭莪 13.2%，合計約佔全國 85%。這個集中本身就是風險。分散在多州的餘屋，對著許多獨立的在地市場去化；集中的餘屋，則繫於單一客群的命運——在此即新的 8% 印花稅正在勸退的外籍與跨境需求。第三層是把今天的問題變成明天的未來供給線：雪蘭莪 18,379 戶的服務式公寓在建量——全國最大單一在建量——與吉隆坡的規劃服務式公寓量（2025 年底 8,340 戶，2026Q1 已增至 10,189 戶），都會完工進入「現有服務式公寓餘屋已不少」的市場。把所有類別疊起來，柔佛合計約 14,705 戶（2026Q1 升至 15,470）的已完工未售，是馬來西亞單一市場最複雜的餘屋——約為吉隆坡（~7,150）的兩倍、雪蘭莪（~6,730）的兩倍多。

09INFRASTRUCTURE CATALYSTS基礎建設催化劑

Three infrastructure threads have moved from speculative to confirmed over the past 18 months, and their importance to the overhang thesis is that they are demand catalysts capable of absorbing supply that price alone cannot clear. The JB–Singapore RTS (target end-2026) is the fastest-acting: it is the only one already producing measurable rent and price effects, with the JB Sentral / Bukit Chagar corridor now carrying the highest yields in peninsular Malaysia. The mechanism — converting a multi-hour border crossing into an eight-minute commute — is the difference between an empty investor block and an occupied dormitory for Singapore's workforce.

The MRT3 Circle Line (approved 2025, completion 2030–2031) is the next KL location-premium catalyst, and its value is precedent-backed: MRT1 and MRT2 each lifted station-area prices once timelines firmed. The data-centre and FDI wave — Microsoft's USD2.2 billion (including a new Johor cloud region) and Google's USD2 billion — is the most structurally durable thread, because it creates high-income employment uncorrelated with the residential cycle; industrial property value rose 21.3% in 2025 on the back of it. The investment read is that infrastructure does not rescue oversupplied product directly — an empty SA tower near a future MRT station is still empty today — but it re-rates the land and the well-located stock around it, which is why proximity to confirmed alignments is now a first-order screen rather than a nice-to-have.

過去 18 個月，有三條基建線索從「說說而已」變成「確定」，它們對餘屋論點的重要性在於：它們是能吸收「價格本身清不掉的供給」的需求催化劑。新柔 RTS（目標 2026 年底）速效最強：它是唯一已產生可量測租金與價格效果的——JB Sentral／Bukit Chagar 走廊如今是馬來西亞半島收益率最高之處。機制——把數小時的過境變成八分鐘通勤——正是「空置投資大樓」與「新加坡勞動力入住宿舍」之間的差別。

MRT3 環線（2025 核准、2030–2031 完工）是下一個吉隆坡地段溢價催化劑，其價值有前例背書：MRT1、MRT2 都在時間表確定後把站點周邊房價拉升。資料中心與 FDI 浪潮——微軟 22 億美元（含柔佛新雲端區）、谷歌 20 億美元——是結構上最持久的一條，因為它創造與住宅循環無關的高薪就業；2025 年工業地產成交值因此年增 21.3%。投資解讀是：基建不會直接拯救供給過剩的產品——一棟臨近未來 MRT 站的空置服務式公寓大樓，今天仍是空的——但它會把周邊土地與地段良好的物件重新估值，這正是為何「臨近已確定路線」如今是第一層篩選，而非加分項。

10OUTLOOK & KEY RISKS展望與主要風險

The national overhang path through 2029 is the product of one fixed input and three moving ones. The fixed input is the completion curve: it is already poured in concrete by the 2024–2025 starts and cannot be changed by any decision taken now. The three moving inputs are all on the demand side, on different clocks. RTS commissioning is the fastest — every month of on-time progress releases pent-up Singapore-linked demand into Johor; every month of delay subtracts it. The JS-SEZ employment ramp works on an 18–36-month lag and determines whether the 2027–2028 Johor completion peak meets genuine resident demand or simply adds to overhang. The KL SA planned-pipeline decision is the longest-dated but the largest single swing factor — a binary policy switch on a planned-SA queue that stood at 8,340 units at end-2025 and had already grown to 10,189 by 1Q2026. Layered across all three is the 2026 demand headwind of the 8% foreign stamp duty, which bites the exact SA/luxury buyer pool the overhang depends on to clear.

全國餘屋到 2029 年的路徑，是「一個固定輸入＋三個變動輸入」的產物。固定輸入是完工曲線：它已被 2024–2025 年的開工澆灌成混凝土，任何現在的決定都改不了。三個變動輸入全在需求端，且各有不同時鐘。RTS 通車最快——每準時推進一個月，就把累積的新加坡相關需求釋放進柔佛；每延誤一個月，就減去它。JS-SEZ 就業擴張以 18 至 36 個月遞延運作，決定 2027–2028 年柔佛完工高峰對上的是真實居住需求、還是只是再添餘屋。吉隆坡服務式公寓規劃供給的核准決定時間最遠、卻是最大的單一擺動因子——一個 2025 年底為 8,340 戶、2026Q1 已增至 10,189 戶的規劃服務式公寓量的二元政策開關。橫跨三者之上的，是 2026 年 8% 外國印花稅這道需求逆風，它咬的正是餘屋去化所仰賴的服務式公寓／豪宅客群。

Translating those inputs into a forecast gives three scenarios. The base case (50%) chains as follows: the UC pipeline completes through 2026–2027, launches stay disciplined, RTS commissions roughly on time, and the KL NC pipeline is deferred rather than approved — residential overhang plateaus near 31,000–33,000 units through 2026 before declining toward 18,000 by 2029, while SA overhang stays above 17,000 through 2027 then eases toward 11,000. The bull case (25%) requires RTS on time, JS-SEZ above RM50 billion of deployed investment and stable rates; that combination drives overhang toward 10,000 residential and 6,500 SA by 2029. The bear case (25%) chains the failures together — RTS slips past 2027, the KL NC pipeline is approved, and the 8% duty plus a global risk-off chill foreign demand — pushing residential overhang to 38,000–40,000 by 2028 and SA toward 25,000.

The single most useful thing an investor can do is stop forecasting price and start watching one number. Because Malaysian prices are sticky, they are a lagging and noisy signal; the leading indicator is transaction volume. A sustained drop below 90,000 units per quarter would confirm the bear path is being entered — it would mean the financing filter is tightening faster than policy is loosening it, and that the completion wave is landing into a market that cannot absorb it. Conversely, volume holding above the five-year average through the 2026–2027 completion peak would confirm the base-to-bull path, in which the wave is absorbed and the cycle turns by 2028. Underwrite the product, not the average; favour landed and confirmed-transit-adjacent stock; and let the quarterly transaction print, not the headline price, tell you which scenario is unfolding.

把這些輸入轉成預測，得到三種情境。基本情境（50%）的因果鏈如下：在建供給於 2026–2027 完工、推案維持紀律、RTS 大致準時通車、吉隆坡 NC 供給線延後而非獲批——住宅餘屋在 2026 年高原在 31,000–33,000 戶，2029 年降向 18,000，服務式公寓餘屋 2027 年前維持 17,000 以上、之後緩降至約 11,000。樂觀情境（25%）需要 RTS 準時、JS-SEZ 落地投資逾 RM500 億、利率穩定；這個組合把 2029 年餘屋壓到 住宅 10,000、服務式公寓 6,500。悲觀情境（25%）把失敗串在一起——RTS 延到 2027 年後、吉隆坡 NC 供給線獲批、8% 稅加全球避險冷卻外籍需求——住宅餘屋在 2028 年推到 38,000–40,000、服務式公寓接近 25,000。

投資人能做的最有用的一件事，是停止預測價格、開始盯一個數字。因為馬來西亞房價僵固，它是落後又有雜訊的訊號；領先指標是成交量。季度成交持續低於 90,000 戶，就確認進入悲觀路徑——意味著貸款篩選收緊的速度，快過政策放鬆的速度，而完工潮正落進一個無法吸收它的市場。反之，若成交量在 2026–2027 完工高峰期間維持在五年均值之上，就確認基本到樂觀路徑——這波被吸收、循環在 2028 年轉向。以產品、而非平均值來評估風險；優選有地與已確定捷運鄰近的物件；並讓季度成交數據、而非表面房價，告訴你正在上演哪一種情境。

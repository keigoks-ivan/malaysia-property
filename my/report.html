Malaysia's overhang has re-accelerated, and the improvement of 2022–2024 has reversed. NAPIC's full-year Property Market Report 2025 (released 19 March 2026) put completed-unsold residential stock at 30,471 units worth RM17.73 billion — up 31.6% year-on-year — and the first-quarter 2026 print rose further to 32,801 units, the highest reading since 3Q2022. After three consecutive years of declining inventory, the market has turned the wrong way at exactly the moment the 2024 construction-start wave begins to complete.

馬來西亞的餘屋（completed-unsold，已完工未售）再度惡化，2022 至 2024 年連三年的改善已經反轉。NAPIC 的全年《2025 房產市場報告》（2026 年 3 月 19 日發布）顯示，已完工未售住宅達 30,471 戶、市值 RM177.3 億，年增 31.6%；2026 年第一季更升到 32,801 戶，是 2022 年第三季以來最高。在連三年去化好轉之後，市場偏偏在 2024 年開工潮即將完工的這個時點，往錯的方向轉。

The single most important analytical correction remains the serviced apartment blind spot. Because NAPIC reclassified serviced apartments (SA) from residential to commercial in 2016, the headline residential overhang systematically understates the true completed-unsold problem. SA overhang rose to 18,752 units at end-2025 and 19,263 units in 1Q2026 — and for the first time, the value of unsold SA stock (RM16.52 billion) now exceeds unsold residential (RM16.37 billion). Treating residential overhang as the whole picture is the most common error in Malaysian property research; the wide-definition completed-unsold inventory is closer to 52,000 units.

Against this sits a genuine paradox: Malaysia remains income-positive. Gross residential yields of 5.19% sit +2.44 points above the OPR of 2.75% (held by Bank Negara at the 7 May 2026 MPC), one of the few positive-carry property markets in Asia. The contradiction — record completed-unsold inventory in an income-positive market — is the defining feature of this cycle and the reason headline price stability masks acute, product-specific distress.

理解這份數據最關鍵的修正，仍然是 服務式公寓的統計盲點。由於 NAPIC 在 2016 年把服務式公寓（SA）從住宅類改劃到商業類，官方的「住宅餘屋」會系統性低估真實的已完工未售規模。服務式公寓餘屋在 2025 年底升到 18,752 戶、2026Q1 再到 19,263 戶；而且首度出現未售服務式公寓的金額（RM165.2 億）超過未售住宅（RM163.7 億）。把住宅餘屋當成完整數據，是馬來西亞房地產研究最常見的誤判；以寬定義計，已完工未售更接近 52,000 戶。

但對照之下卻有個真實的矛盾：馬來西亞仍然「收租為正」。住宅毛收益率 5.19%，比 OPR 2.75%（BNM 於 2026 年 5 月 7 日 MPC 維持）高出 +2.44 個百分點，是亞洲少數靠租金就有正現金流的房市之一。「在一個收租為正的市場裡，卻堆出史上最高的已完工未售庫存」——這個矛盾正是本輪循環的核心，也解釋了為何表面房價穩定，底下卻是劇烈、且高度集中在特定產品的去化壓力。

The structural dichotomy is product-based, not geographic. Across all three major markets in this report — Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor Bahru — demand failure is almost exclusively a high-rise condominium and serviced-apartment problem; landed residential consistently clears with overhang rates in the single digits. The investment conclusion is therefore stable even as the data worsens: avoid high-rise in oversupplied bands, favour landed in under-supplied corridors, and monitor the three catalysts — RTS Link commissioning, JS-SEZ enterprise deployment, and the Kuala Lumpur serviced-apartment planned-supply (NC) decision — that determine whether the 2026–2028 completion wave self-corrects or compounds.

真正的結構性分化在產品，不在地區。本報告涵蓋的三個主要市場——吉隆坡、檳城、新山——需求疲弱幾乎全部集中在高層公寓與服務式公寓；有地住宅的餘屋率始終是個位數，去化穩定。因此即使數據在惡化，投資結論依然不變：避開供給過剩價位帶的高層物件，鎖定供給不足走廊的有地住宅，並追蹤三個催化劑——新柔捷運（RTS Link）通車、JS-SEZ 招商落地、以及吉隆坡服務式公寓規劃供給（NC）的核准決定——這三者決定 2026 至 2028 年完工潮會自我修正，還是繼續累積。

02SUPPLY & THE OVERHANG ENGINE供給面與餘屋成因

The overhang is best understood as a time-lagged system with locked-in consequences. A project commenced in 2024 cannot be un-built: the 106,236 units started that year arrive between 2026 and 2028 regardless of demand. That wave is now landing — full-year 2025 completions reached 99,877 residential units, up 21.6% year-on-year. With absorption running near its five-year average of ~85,000 units, supply mechanically exceeds demand through 2027, and the unsold balance accumulates.

餘屋最好理解成一個「有時間落差、且後果已鎖定」的系統。2024 年動工的物件拆不掉：那年開工的 106,236 戶，無論需求如何，都會在 2026 到 2028 年陸續完工。這波現在正在落地——2025 全年住宅完工達 99,877 戶，年增 21.6%。而吸納量大約維持在五年均值 ~85,000 戶，供給在機械式地超過需求直到 2027 年，未售餘額因此持續累積。

The developer response has been rational and swift, which is the necessary precondition for an eventual turn. New residential launches fell 14.9% in 2025 to 64,487 units, with a full-year sell-through of just 35.5%; the REHDA developer survey found only 41% of members planned launches in 2H2025, with optimism collapsing to 19% from 51%. The first quarter of 2026 extended the pullback — 9,112 units launched at an 11.5% launch take-up. Supply discipline is real, but it acts on future inventory; the units already under construction will still complete.

建商的反應理性而迅速，這正是市場最終轉向的前提。2025 年新住宅推案量年減 14.9% 至 64,487 戶，全年去化率僅 35.5%；REHDA 業者調查顯示只有 41% 會員打算在 2025 下半年推案，樂觀度從 51% 崩到 19%。2026 年第一季延續收手——推案 9,112 戶、首期去化率 11.5%。供給端的紀律是真的，但它作用在未來的庫存；已經在蓋的，還是會完工。

The serviced-apartment segment is the structural core of the problem. Developers historically favoured the format because its commercial land classification exempts it from affordable-housing quotas — producing a sustained wave of RM500k–RM1m product across KL, JB and Penang that bears limited relationship to local demand. The national SA pipeline (overhang + under-construction + planned) is roughly 74,836 units, about 35.7% of all unsold supply, with 44,035 units still under construction. None of this appears in residential overhang statistics.

服務式公寓是整個問題的結構核心。建商長期偏好這種產品，因為商業地契可以繞開可負擔房屋配額——於是在吉隆坡、新山、檳城的 RM50 至 100 萬價位帶，堆出一大批和在地需求關係薄弱的供給。全國服務式公寓供給線（餘屋＋在建＋規劃）約 74,836 戶，約佔全部未售供給的 35.7%，其中 44,035 戶仍在施工。這些全都不會出現在住宅餘屋統計裡。

SA stock also clears differently — and worse. Commercially titled units are ineligible for first-home schemes, stamp-duty exemptions, 90% owner-occupier LTV and Bumiputera-quota absorption; they carry utility tariffs four-to-six times residential rates. The academic clearing cycle is 3–5 years versus 1–2 for residential, and roughly 85% of SA overhang is priced above RM500k — beyond the organic purchasing power of the local salaried market, leaving clearance dependent on foreign and expatriate demand precisely as the 8% foreign stamp duty bites.

服務式公寓的去化方式也不同、而且更差。商業地契的單位不適用首購補助、印花稅減免、自住 90% 貸款成數、土著配額吸收；水電費率還是住宅的 4 至 6 倍。學術紀錄的消化週期是 3 至 5 年，住宅只要 1 至 2 年；而且約 85% 的服務式公寓餘屋定價在 RM50 萬以上——超出本地薪資族的實質購買力，去化高度依賴外籍與外派需求，偏偏這時 8% 外國買家印花稅正在咬人。

03DEMAND: INTENT WITHOUT ACCESS需求面：想買的人很多，能成交的不夠

Demand is more nuanced than either transaction volumes or overhang suggest. Full-year 2025 residential transactions were 256,512 (−1.5%) worth RM108.27 billion; all-sector volume of 416,413 sat just below 2024's decade-high 420,525. Intent is real — what fails is access. The clearest evidence is launch take-up: in 3Q2025 only 5.1% of high-rise units sold in the launch window versus 22.5% for landed. A sub-10% take-up is not disinterest; it is the financing system filtering out most participants.

需求比成交量或餘屋數字看到的更微妙。2025 全年住宅成交 256,512 件（−1.5%）、總值 RM1,082.7 億；全行業成交 416,413 件，僅略低於 2024 年十年新高的 420,525 件。想買是真的——卡住的是「能不能買到」。最清楚的證據是新案去化：2025Q3 高層住宅首期只賣出 5.1%，有地住宅則有 22.5%。首期去化低於 10% 不是沒人要，而是貸款體系把大多數人篩掉了。

The binding constraint is credit. The residential loan-approval rate recovered to roughly 44.8% by mid-2025 (from a 42.6% low), but more than half of applications are still declined, and a developer survey found 72% of buyers could not secure financing. Household debt-to-GDP has risen to 84.8% — up from the 81.9% cited a year ago and among the highest in ASEAN — leaving debt-service ratios at their practical limit for M40 households. Budget 2026 doubled the SJKP guarantee to RM20 billion for ~80,000 buyers, but this addresses access at the margin, not the underlying affordability gap.

The result is a K-shaped market. Sub-RM300k product is genuinely undersupplied where employment anchors exist; the RM300k–500k band shows the healthiest take-up; above RM500k in high-rise and SA formats demand is institutionally constrained. The secondary market's ~79% share of transactions is the direct expression of buyers gravitating to where value, location and financing access intersect.

真正的約束是信貸。住宅貸款核准率到 2025 年年中回升到約 44.8%（自 42.6% 低點），但仍有過半申請被拒；一份業者調查更顯示 72% 的買家無法取得貸款。家庭負債佔 GDP 升至 84.8%——高於一年前引用的 81.9%，是東協最高之一——使 M40 家庭的償債比逼近極限。2026 年預算把 SJKP 信用保證倍增到 RM200 億、目標約 8 萬名買家，但這只是邊際改善「貸得到」，沒解決根本的可負擔缺口。

結果是一個 K 型市場。有就業支撐的地點，RM30 萬以下確實供給不足；RM30 至 50 萬去化最健康；RM50 萬以上的高層與服務式公寓則被制度性卡死。二手市場佔成交約 79%，正是買家往「價值、地段、貸款可得性」三者交集靠攏的直接表現。

04VALUATION估值

Two frameworks give two conclusions. On income, Malaysia is cheap: a +2.44pp carry over the policy rate is rare in Asia. On price level, it is stretched: the national House Price Index reached 233.1 in 2025 (+2.6%), an average transacted price of RM502,922, against incomes that have not kept pace. Crucially, 3Q2025 recorded the first quarterly HPI decline since 3Q2021 (−1.46% QoQ), and volume-weighted Kuala Lumpur prices fell 4.3% year-on-year — dragged down by the oversupplied high-rise segment even as prime landed rose.

兩個框架、兩個結論。看收租，馬來西亞便宜：比政策利率高 +2.44 個百分點的利差，在亞洲少見。看價格水準，則偏貴：全國房價指數 2025 年達 233.1（+2.6%）、平均成交價 RM502,922，所得卻沒跟上。關鍵是，2025Q3 出現 2021Q3 以來首次季度房價下跌（−1.46% QoQ），而以成交量加權的吉隆坡房價年跌 4.3%——被供給過剩的高層拖累，即便精華有地住宅仍在漲。

05KUALA LUMPUR: A SERVICED-APARTMENT PROBLEM吉隆坡：一個服務式公寓的問題

Kuala Lumpur inverts the national narrative. Its residential overhang is low and falling (~2,287 units by 3Q2025); KL's real exposure is the serviced apartment — 4,687 units of SA overhang (26.2% of the national total) plus a 9,415-unit planned (NC) pipeline that is 72.9% of the entire national NC queue. Whether those 9,415 units receive construction approval or are withdrawn is the single most consequential overhang decision for any KL investor — approval would push KL's SA overhang from ~4,700 toward 15,000+ by 2030.

吉隆坡和全國劇本相反。它的住宅餘屋低且還在下降（2025Q3 約 2,287 戶）；吉隆坡真正的曝險是服務式公寓——餘屋 4,687 戶（佔全國 26.2%），再加上 9,415 戶的規劃（NC）供給線，佔全國 NC 總量的 72.9%。這 9,415 戶到底會獲准動工、還是被撤回，是任何吉隆坡投資人最該盯的單一餘屋決定——若獲批，吉隆坡服務式公寓餘屋會從約 4,700 戶推升到 2030 年的 15,000 戶以上。

The transaction market, meanwhile, is strong at the top. KL recorded 21,635 transactions in 2025 (+8.7%) worth RM23.81 billion (+36.6%), with above-RM1m deals making up 22.8% of H1 volume. Prime addresses rose even as the volume-weighted index fell: KLCC +0.7%, Bangsar +3.4%, Mont’Kiara +6.2%, TTDI landed +6.3%. This bifurcation — record prime activity alongside a stuck loan-approval rate — is the structural signature of the KL market: equity transacts, credit cannot. The 8% foreign stamp duty cooled luxury demand into late-2025, and MRT3 (approved 2025, completion 2030–2031) is the next location-premium catalyst along Sentul, Wangsa Maju, Seputeh and Bukit Jalil.

另一方面，成交市場在高端很強。吉隆坡 2025 年成交 21,635 件（+8.7%）、總值 RM238.1 億（+36.6%），RM100 萬以上佔上半年量的 22.8%。即使成交量加權指數在跌，精華地段仍在漲：KLCC +0.7%、Bangsar +3.4%、Mont’Kiara +6.2%、TTDI 有地 +6.3%。這種分化——精華區成交創高，同時貸款核准率卡住——正是吉隆坡市場的結構特徵：有資產的在買，靠信貸的買不起。8% 外國印花稅在 2025 年底冷卻了豪宅需求；而 MRT3（2025 核准、2030–2031 完工）是下一個地段溢價催化劑，沿線的善南、旺沙瑪朱、士拍、武吉加里爾值得布局。

06PENANG: THE INDUSTRIAL THESIS檳城：工業驅動的論點

Penang has quietly fallen out of the overhang top tier — residential completed-unsold of roughly 2,730–3,165 units, with landed clearing well and the stress confined to older high-rise. The thesis here is demand-led, not supply-stressed: Penang approved RM22.4 billion of manufacturing investment in 2025 (+29%), across 232 projects creating 24,633 jobs, anchored by Intel's ~RM12bn advanced-packaging build (operational 2026), AMD, ASE and ViTrox. That employment base converts directly into housing demand that is structurally indifferent to the property cycle.

檳城已悄悄退出餘屋的前段班——住宅已完工未售約 2,730 至 3,165 戶，有地去化良好，壓力集中在較舊的高層。這裡的論點是需求驅動，而非供給受壓：檳城 2025 年核准製造業投資 RM224 億（+29%），共 232 個項目、創造 24,633 個職位，由 Intel 約 RM120 億的先進封裝廠（2026 投產）、AMD、ASE、ViTrox 等領頭。這個就業基礎會直接轉化為與房市循環無關的購屋需求。

07JOHOR BAHRU: THE MOST COMPLEX MARKET新山：最複雜的市場

Johor carries Malaysia's most complex, multi-layered overhang and its strongest demand catalyst simultaneously. On the stress side: residential overhang of 3,293 plus an SA overhang of 9,018 units — 50.4% of the entire national SA total — gives a combined completed-unsold of ~19,390 units across all categories, the largest in the country. On the catalyst side: the JB–Singapore RTS Link is on track for December 2026 (~65% complete, trial running since end-2025), and the Johor-Singapore SEZ drew a record RM110 billion of approved investment in 2025 (24,000+ jobs).

柔佛同時背著馬來西亞最複雜的多層餘屋，與最強的需求催化劑。壓力面：住宅餘屋 3,293 戶，加上服務式公寓餘屋 9,018 戶——佔全國服務式公寓總量 50.4%——使全類別已完工未售合計達 約 19,390 戶，全國最高。催化面：新柔（RTS Link）有望 2026 年 12 月通車（進度約 65%，2025 年底起試運行），柔佛-新加坡特區（JS-SEZ）2025 年吸引創紀錄的 RM1,100 億核准投資（逾 2.4 萬個職位）。

The investment posture is product- and zone-specific. RTS-adjacent high-rise above RM1,000 psf: wait for better entry — the catalyst is largely priced. Zone-1/2 landed (terraces RM400k–700k): accumulate — overhang pressure is minimal, owner-occupier demand is real, and gross yields of 5–6.5% are achievable. Peripheral high-rise and Forest City SA: avoid until SEZ absorption is demonstrable. Three signals to watch: NAPIC's quarterly Johor overhang, RTS construction milestones, and JS-SEZ enterprise registrations.

進場姿態要看產品與地區。RTS 旁、每呎 RM1,000 以上的高層：等更好的價位——催化劑大致已反映。第一／二區的有地住宅（排屋 RM40 至 70 萬）：積極布局——餘屋壓力極低、自住需求真實、毛收益 5 至 6.5% 做得到。外圍高層與森林城市服務式公寓：不碰，直到 SEZ 去化可被驗證。三個要追蹤的訊號：NAPIC 柔佛季度餘屋、RTS 施工里程碑、JS-SEZ 企業進駐登記。

08NATIONAL OVERHANG PICTURE全馬餘屋圖景

At the national level the overhang has three layers. Layer one is the headline residential overhang, broadly spread but led by Perak on units and by Johor on value (RM2.83 billion of unsold condo stock). Note the divergence: Perak's overhang is low-cost and structural; Johor's is pricey condominium product — the same unit count carries very different risk.

全國層面，餘屋有三層。第一層是表面的住宅餘屋，分布廣，但以戶數論由霹靂領頭、以金額論則由柔佛居首（RM28.3 億的未售公寓）。注意這個差異：霹靂的餘屋是低價、結構性的；柔佛的是高價公寓——同樣的戶數，風險天差地別。

Layer two — the systematically ignored one — is the SA overhang, where concentration is extreme: Johor 50.4%, KL 26.2%, Selangor 11.7% account for nearly nine-tenths of the national total. Layer three is the forward pipeline: Selangor's 18,116-unit SA under-construction block and KL's 9,415-unit NC queue will complete into markets that already carry unsold SA stock. Stacking all categories, Johor's combined completed-unsold of ~19,390 units is the most complex single-market overhang in Malaysia.

第二層——被系統性忽視的那層——是服務式公寓餘屋，集中度極高：柔佛 50.4%、吉隆坡 26.2%、雪蘭莪 11.7%，合計近全國的九成。第三層是未來供給線：雪蘭莪 18,116 戶的服務式公寓在建量、以及吉隆坡 9,415 戶的 NC 排隊量，都會完工進入「現有服務式公寓餘屋已經不少」的市場。把所有類別疊起來，柔佛合計約 19,390 戶的已完工未售，是馬來西亞單一市場最複雜的餘屋。

09INFRASTRUCTURE CATALYSTS基礎建設催化劑

Three infrastructure threads have moved from speculative to confirmed, with effects already visible in data. The JB–Singapore RTS (end-2026) is the fastest-acting — JB Sentral / Bukit Chagar corridor yields are the highest in peninsular Malaysia. The MRT3 Circle Line (approved 2025, completion 2030–2031) is the next KL location-premium catalyst. And the data-centre / FDI wave — Microsoft USD2.2bn (plus a new Johor cloud region), Google USD2bn — drove industrial property value up 21.3% in 2025, a demand stream uncorrelated with the residential cycle.

三條基建線索已從「說說而已」變成「確定」，且效果已在數據中浮現。新柔 RTS（2026 年底）速效最強——JB Sentral／Bukit Chagar 走廊的收益率是馬來西亞半島最高。MRT3 環線（2025 核准、2030–2031 完工）是下一個吉隆坡地段溢價催化劑。而資料中心／FDI 浪潮——微軟 22 億美元（加上柔佛新雲端區）、谷歌 20 億美元——在 2025 年帶動工業地產成交值年增 21.3%，這是與住宅循環無關的需求來源。

10OUTLOOK & KEY RISKS展望與主要風險

The national overhang path through 2029 turns on three variables on different clocks: RTS commissioning (fastest), the JS-SEZ employment ramp (18–36 month lag), and the KL SA planned-pipeline decision (longest-dated, largest single risk). Layered on top is a new 2026 demand headwind — the 8% foreign-buyer stamp duty — that bites the exact SA/luxury segment overhang depends on to clear.

The base case (50%) sees residential overhang plateauing near 31,000–33,000 units through 2026 before declining toward 18,000 by 2029 as the UC pipeline completes and starts stay disciplined; SA overhang stays above 17,000 through 2027, easing toward 11,000. The bull case (25%) — RTS on time, JS-SEZ above RM50bn, stable rates — drives overhang toward 10,000 residential / 6,500 SA by 2029. The bear case (25%) — RTS slips, KL NC pipeline approved, foreign demand chilled by the 8% duty — pushes residential overhang to 38,000–40,000 by 2028 and SA toward 25,000. The clearest leading indicator is not price but transaction volume: a sustained drop below 90,000 units per quarter would confirm the bear path.

全國餘屋到 2029 年的路徑，取決於三個不同時鐘的變數：RTS 通車（最快）、JS-SEZ 就業擴張（18–36 個月遞延）、以及吉隆坡服務式公寓規劃供給的核准決定（時間最遠、單一風險最大）。再疊上 2026 年新的需求逆風——8% 外國買家印花稅——它咬的正是餘屋去化所仰賴的服務式公寓／豪宅客群。

基本情境（50%）：住宅餘屋在 2026 年高原在 31,000–33,000 戶，隨在建供給完工、開工保持紀律，2029 年降向 18,000 戶；服務式公寓餘屋 2027 年前維持 17,000 戶以上，之後緩降至約 11,000。樂觀情境（25%）——RTS 準時、JS-SEZ 逾 RM500 億、利率穩定——2029 年餘屋壓到 住宅 10,000／服務式公寓 6,500。悲觀情境（25%）——RTS 延誤、吉隆坡 NC 獲批、8% 印花稅冷卻外籍需求——住宅餘屋在 2028 年達 38,000–40,000、服務式公寓接近 25,000。最清楚的領先指標不是價格，而是成交量：季度成交持續低於 90,000 戶，就確認進入悲觀路徑。

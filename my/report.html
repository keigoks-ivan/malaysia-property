Bottom line總結 Malaysia's residential overhang has turned higher again. Completed-but-unsold homes reached 32,801 units in the first quarter of 2026, the highest reading since the third quarter of 2022 and enough to unwind most of the absorption gains of the prior three years. Once serviced apartments are included, a category reclassified as commercial and excluded from the residential series since 2016, wide-definition unsold stock stands near 52,000 units, leaving the market under materially more pressure than the headline implies.

The defining feature of the market is that oversupply coexists with positive carry. Gross residential yields of 5.19% sit 2.44 points above the 2.75% policy rate, one of the few positive-carry housing markets remaining in Asia. The two are reconcilable: with the Housing Development Act limiting forced sales, developers would rather carry than cut, so the surplus shows up in transaction volumes and time-to-clear while prices barely move.

The distinction that matters is therefore product type; geography tells you much less. The clearance problem is concentrated in high-rise condominiums and serviced apartments, while landed housing continues to absorb. Underpinning that pattern is a structurally constrained income system, examined in full in the companion Macro × Property analysis. 馬來西亞的住宅餘屋再度轉升。2026 年第一季，已完工未售住宅升至 32,801 戶，創 2022 年第三季以來新高，過去三年的去化成果幾乎回吐殆盡。若將長期歸入商業、未計入住宅口徑的服務式公寓一併納入，廣義完工未售存量已逼近 52,000 戶，市場承壓程度高於官方數字所示。

值得注意的是，過剩與收益在此並存。全國住宅毛收益率 5.19%，較 2.75% 的隔夜政策利率高出 2.44 個百分點，是亞洲少數仍具正持有收益的住宅市場。兩者並不矛盾：在《房屋發展法》限制強制拍賣的結構下，發展商寧可持有也不降價，供過於求主要落在成交量與去化週期上，價格反而動得少。

因此，真正的分化在產品類別，地理區位反而其次。去化受阻者集中於高層公寓與服務式公寓，有地住宅的吸納相對穩健。這一格局的底層，是一個受結構性因素壓抑的收入體系，其完整機制見另文《總經 × 房產》分析。

I · Where the Market Stands第一部 · 市場現況

A decade-long cycle, now reversing十年週期，如今轉向

Exhibit 1 · National · 11-year history圖 1 · 全國 · 11年歷史

The overhang needs reading as a cycle, not a one-off shock. Inventory built steadily from 2016 as high-rise supply met a market cooled by macroprudential tightening and the 2015 GST, peaking at 36,863 residential units in 2021 when the pandemic froze absorption. It then drained to a 23,149-unit trough by 2024, as the Home Ownership Campaign and a 1.75% policy rate pulled demand forward. The renewed increase is the lagged consequence of developers restarting the pipeline in 2023–2024, having read the borrowed 2021–22 absorption surge as structural demand. The serviced-apartment series beneath it tells a parallel, quieter story, climbing from 3,912 units in 2016 to a plateau near 24,000 that has barely receded.餘屋要放在週期裡看，不是單一事件。庫存自 2016 年起穩步累積，高層供給遇上受宏觀審慎措施與 2015 年消費稅冷卻的市場，並在 2021 年疫情凍結去化時升抵 36,863 戶的住宅高點；其後因擁屋計畫與 1.75% 的政策利率提前釋放需求，至 2024 年回落至 23,149 戶的低點。此輪回升，是發展商將 2021 至 2022 年那波借來的去化誤判為結構性需求、於 2023 至 2024 年重啟推案的滯後結果。底層的服務式公寓走勢與之平行而更為隱蔽，自 2016 年的 3,912 戶一路攀升至接近 24,000 戶的高原，至今僅小幅回落。

The headline spread, however, flatters the product that actually dominates supply. Net of service charges, serviced-apartment utilities at four-to-six times residential rates, vacancy and the 8% entry duty, high-rise and serviced-apartment yields fall to roughly 2–3%, barely above and frequently below the policy rate. Prices: a decade of deceleration, not a correction房價：十年減速，不是修正

On yield the market is inexpensive; on price level it is stretched. The index has reached 235.2 against incomes that have not kept pace, leaving a national price-to-income multiple of roughly 4–5×. Yet annual growth has held in the low single digits for a decade, a slow real-terms adjustment that has gradually repaired affordability without forcing inventory to clear. The signal worth tracking is not the annual figure but the sequential turn: the third quarter of 2025 recorded the first quarterly decline since 3Q2021, and the volume-weighted Kuala Lumpur index fell 4.3% year-on-year. Set regionally, the direction is not isolated: Bangkok's new-condominium price index also turned negative in 2H2025, down 0.2% year-on-year in the third quarter, its second straight quarterly decline (REIC, a different measure). Kuala Lumpur's −4.3% cuts deeper, but a cooling high-rise market is the regional backdrop.以收益衡量，市場並不昂貴；以價格水準衡量，則偏高。指數已升抵 235.2，而所得未能同步，全國房價所得比約為 4 至 5 倍。然而年增率已在低個位數維持十年，屬於緩慢的實質調整，逐步修復可負擔性，卻未迫使庫存出清。值得追蹤的並非年度數字，而是季度轉折：2025 年第三季出現 2021 年第三季以來首次季度下跌，以成交量加權的吉隆坡指數則年減 4.3%。放在區域裡看，這個方向並不孤立：曼谷的新推公寓價格指數 2025 年下半年同樣轉負，第三季同比 −0.2%、連續第二季下跌（REIC，口徑不同）；吉隆坡的 −4.3% 更深，但高層市場降溫是區域共同的背景。

A single national index obscures a market pulling apart. The +2.44-point spread over the policy rate is striking, but the harder test is the rate a leveraged buyer actually pays. Measured against the owner-occupier mortgage rate, Kuala Lumpur is one of only three markets in twelve where rent still outruns the loan — with Tokyo and Dublin — while Sydney, Auckland, Toronto and Ho Chi Minh City sit deeply negative. II · The Supply Engine第二部 · 供給端的形成機制

Supply responds to demand with a two-to-three-year lag and cannot be recalled once committed. The 2026–2028 completion wave is therefore already determined, and today's restraint will not be felt until the end of the decade.供給對需求的反應有兩到三年的時間落差，且一旦投入便無法撤回。2026 至 2028 年的完工潮因此已經底定，而當前的供給收縮，要到本十年末才會顯現。

Completions outrun absorption完工量超過吸納量

Residential units · 2026E–2029E modelled from lagged starts · NAPIC, research estimates住宅戶數 · 2026E–2029E 依開工遞延推估 · NAPIC、研究估算

Residential supply has a long lag and little capacity to reverse once construction begins. Starts reached 106,236 units in 2024, up 20.6%, and that volume will complete between 2026 and 2028 regardless of how demand evolves in the interim. Completions rose to 99,877 units in 2025 (+21.6%) against annual absorption of roughly 85,000; the recurring gap between the two is what accumulates as overhang. A frame for the stock itself: 32,801 overhang units equal roughly 4.6 months of absorption, the wide ~52,000 about seven months, against US existing-home supply near 4.2 months at end-2025 (NAR). The measures are not fully comparable — Malaysia's overhang counts only developer-held completed-unsold stock, excludes secondary listings, and is under 1% of the standing stock. By international standards the total is not extreme; the pressure sits in concentration and product mismatch.住宅供給有很長的時間落差，一旦動工便難以回頭。2024 年全國開工量達 106,236 戶（年增 20.6%），這批存量無論後續需求如何，都將於 2026 至 2028 年陸續完工交付。2025 年完工量升至 99,877 戶（年增 21.6%），而市場一年的吸納能力約為 85,000 戶；兩者之間反覆出現的缺口，就是餘屋累積的來源。給存量本身一個參照系：32,801 戶餘屋約等於 4.6 個月的吸納量，廣義 ~52,000 戶約 7 個月；美國成屋市場 2025 年底的在售供給約 4.2 個月（NAR）。兩個口徑不完全可比：馬來西亞的餘屋只計發展商完工未售、不含二手掛牌，佔既有存量也不到 1%。以國際標準看總量不算極端，壓力在集中度與產品錯配。

Developers have responded. New launches fell 14.9% in 2025 to 64,487 units, full-year sell-through held at just 35.5%, and the first quarter of 2026 saw only 11.5% take-up. The restraint is genuine and is the seed of the next up-cycle, but it acts on future supply: launches cut in 2025 ease the market only from 2028 onward, and do nothing for the completion peak already concentrated in 2026–2027.發展商已作出回應。2025 年新推案量減少 14.9% 至 64,487 戶，全年銷售率僅 35.5%，2026 年第一季去化更只有 11.5%。這樣的收縮是真實的，也是下一輪上行的種子，但它作用於未來供給：2025 年所減的推案，要到 2028 年起才會紓解市場，對已集中於 2026 至 2027 年的完工高峰並無助益。

A second structural overhang — the Bumiputera quota第二個結構性餘屋 — 土著配額

Beneath the headline sits a second structural overhang. As property is a state matter, each state reserves 30% or more of new units for Bumiputera buyers at a mandated 5–15% discount, and unsold units return to the open market only with a lag. REHDA estimates these account for 40–50% of the national overhang — an industry figure, not an official one — with roughly 77% priced RM300,000–500,000 and about 72% unsold for 36 months or more. The serviced-apartment glut (above RM500,000, foreign-dependent) and the quota overhang (below RM500,000, release-constrained) are thus two distinct mechanisms feeding a single headline.表面數字之下，還有第二個結構性餘屋。由於房地產屬州權事項，各州為土著買家保留 三成以上的新單位，並規定 5% 至 15% 的折扣，未售出者須隔一段時間方能釋回公開市場。REHDA 估計這類單位佔全國餘屋的 四至五成（業界估算，不是官方數字），其中約 77% 定價在 RM30 萬至 50 萬、約 72% 已滯銷 36 個月以上。服務式公寓的過剩（RM50 萬以上、仰賴外籍）與配額餘屋（RM50 萬以下、受釋出機制制約），因此是餵養同一個表面數字的兩套不同機制。

Expressed in ringgit, the ceiling is explicit. Policy works only at the margin. The SJKP guarantee was doubled to RM20 billion and the stamp-duty exemption extended to RM500,000, both widening access in the least over-supplied band. An 8% duty is not one cost but three. On prime landed, where capital appreciation runs near 6% a year, the levy is grown back in roughly 16 months — a rounding error against a multi-year hold. Demand — the engine's last warm stretch需求的十年形狀 — 引擎最後的熱段

The demand engine runs hot for most of the decade, then downshifts. Malaysia adds roughly 116,000 new households a year through 2030, a Gen Z cohort of nearly 9 million enters home-buying age over the next ten-to-twenty years, and urbanisation is targeted from 75% toward 85%. But the counterweights are already visible: a fertility rate of 1.6 and an aged society arriving around 2044 mean the engine begins to downshift in the mid-2030s. Now take the number apart. RM7,017 is a household figure, so it moves on three things at once: the price of each salary, the number of earners each household sends to work, and the composition of the household itself. The second is easy to miss. Labour-force participation hit 70.6% in 2024, the highest on record (DOSM Labour Force Survey) — meaning part of recent household income growth came from more people going out to work, not from each job paying more. Looking forward, the same fact hides an engine. Nearly seven in ten of those outside the labour force are women (about 4.86 million in 2024). On the Atlas method, GNI per capita went from US$11,700 in 2023 to US$11,650 in 2024 — down, not up, because the three-year Atlas average absorbs the weak ringgit — while the threshold keeps moving (US$13,935 for FY26, US$14,375 for FY27). The gap is roughly US$2,300–2,700, and the July 2026 classification missed again. A decade of data shows no convergence: the gap narrowed to about US$1,800 in 2019, then widened back out. The policy toolbox lifts the floor, not the middle政策工具箱：抬得動地板，抬不動中間

Policy is not idle; the question is which layer it reaches. The minimum wage went from RM900 in 2013 to RM1,700 in 2025 (effective February, universal from August) — a cumulative +55% across 2019–2025, against +19% for median household income across 2019–2024. Policy lifts the floor, and the B40 feel it. It does not lift the middle: M40 wages are priced by the market, and the buyer of an above-RM500,000 home lives in that middle.政策不是沒在動，問題是動在哪一層。最低工資從 2013 年的 RM900 一路調到 2025 年的 RM1,700（2025 年 2 月生效、8 月起全面適用），2019 至 2025 年累計 +55%；同期（2019 至 2024 年）中位家庭收入累計只有 +19%。政策抬得動地板，B40 有感；抬不動中間：M40 的薪資由市場定價，而 RM50 萬以上住宅的目標買家，就住在中間這一層。

Two other tools need their scale stated. The SSPA civil-service revision is the largest on record — +8% in December 2024 (4% for top management) and +7% in January 2026 (3% at the top), covering about 1.6 million staff — and it will push the 2026 median up as a one-off; read the 2026 HIS with that component stripped out, and note the funding still draws on a treasury with a 12% revenue base. One more mechanism before the projection: why has approval never crossed half even in years when income grew? Because between a rising income and a home loan stand three gates, and any one of them can stop an applicant alone. The first is the down payment — a ~1% savings rate and 61% unable to raise RM1,000 mean this gate answers to the stock of savings, not the flow of income; a pay rise takes years to fill it. The second is debt-service capacity that is already spoken for: the 41% median DSR on new loans counts all debt, and in household debt cars take 13.5% and personal financing 12.4% (BNM, 2024). In a country where public transport covers little, a young household usually carries a car loan before it applies for a mortgage — the housing loan gets whatever capacity is left. The third is documentation: informal income that a bank cannot see. So the chain "income up → approval breaks 50%" is not automatic; it works only if incomes rise while non-housing debt does not rise with them. The closing frame總結框架

The completion wave is cyclical and will turn with the three catalysts; the demand base against which it clears is not. Beneath the cycle sits a structurally capped buyer pool — median income RM7,017, the top 20% taking about 46%, a savings rate near 1%. Demographics is the one structural variable in Malaysia's favour: it is the youngest of the major Asian economies and will not reach an aged society until around 2044–48, so the household-formation floor lasts longer here than across its greying neighbours.完工潮屬景氣性，將隨三項催化因素轉向；但它所對應去化的需求基礎，並非如此。週期之下，坐落著一個受結構封頂的買盤：收入中位數 RM7,017、最高 20% 佔約 46%、儲蓄率近 1%。人口結構是少數對馬來西亞有利的結構變數：它是主要亞洲經濟體中最年輕者，約於 2044 至 2048 年才邁入高齡社會，因此其家庭形成的支撐，比逐漸老化的鄰國延續更久。

The binding constraint is income, not age. The durable bull case is not a faster cycle or a cheaper policy rate but the income system itself improving. The discipline that follows is to underwrite the product rather than the average, to favour landed and confirmed-transit-adjacent stock, and to let the quarterly residential trend identify which scenario is unfolding.

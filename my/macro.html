Bottom line一句話結論 2025 was the pivot year. After three years on hold, BNM cut the OPR to 2.75% — a pre-emptive insurance move, with growth still above 5% and inflation under 2%. But the easing lands in a market where household leverage is near its ceiling (84.8% of GDP) and Kuala Lumpur's average price is actually falling. Cheaper money meets demand that is structurally concentrated — Johor's SEZ, data centres and record FDI — rather than broad-based. 2025年是轉向年。國行按兵三年後把OPR降到2.75%，屬預防性的保險降息，當時經濟仍站穩5%以上、通膨壓在2%以下。但這波寬鬆，落在一個家庭槓桿逼近上限（佔GDP 84.8%）、吉隆坡均價反而下跌的市場。便宜的錢遇到的需求高度集中（柔新特區、數據中心、創紀錄FDI），不是全面普漲。

Malaysia's wealth has always come out of the ground. Stuck Below the High-Income Line卡在高收入門檻前

GDP per capita, nominal US$, 2024 (World Bank)人均GDP（名目美元），2024（世界銀行）

Put plainly: Malaysia reached upper-middle income in 1996; nearly thirty years on, GDP per capita is about US$11,900 — below China, a third of South Korea, a sixth of Singapore, and roughly US$2,000 short of the World Bank's high-income line (official target: 2030). It has the skeleton of an upper-middle economy but hasn't grown the high-income muscle. The points below are why it keeps falling short — and why that matters for property.話說白一點：馬來西亞1996年就跨進中高收入，將近三十年了，人均GDP約1.19萬美元，還是卡在中國之下、只有南韓的三分之一、新加坡的六分之一，離世界銀行的高收入門檻還差約兩千美元（官方目標2030年達標）。它有一具中高收入國家的骨架，卻一直長不出高收入國家的肌肉。底下這幾條，才是它一直跨不過去的真正原因，也是它跟房市的關係所在。

The Root: It Deindustrialised Too Soon病根：它太早去工業化

Manufacturing as a share of GDP, % (World Bank). Peaked while still middle-income.製造業佔GDP比重，%（世界銀行）。在還是中等收入時就見頂。

Start at the root. The textbook path to high income runs through deep industrialisation — make ever more valuable things, pay ever-higher wages — and only then shift to services. Taiwan and Korea crossed into high income (1987/1995) while keeping a manufacturing base near a quarter to a third of GDP. Malaysia did the opposite: manufacturing peaked at ~30.9% of GDP in 1999 and has slid to ~22.5% — it began deindustrialising while still upper-middle income. Economists call this premature deindustrialisation (Rodrik; KRI). The high-productivity engine that should have built a broad high-wage middle class turned down early — and everything downstream inherits that.從病根看起。教科書上邁向高收入的路，是先深化工業——做愈來愈值錢的東西、付愈來愈高的薪水——之後才轉向服務業。台灣與南韓跨進高收入（1987／1995）時，仍保有佔GDP約四分之一到三分之一的製造業。馬來西亞反過來：製造業佔GDP在1999年見頂約30.9%，之後一路滑到約22.5%——它在還只是中高收入時就開始去工業化。經濟學家叫這個過早去工業化（Rodrik；Khazanah研究院）。那具本該撐起廣大高薪中產的高生產力引擎，提早熄火了——而下游的一切，都繼承了這個病根。

Why Growth Keeps Falling Short為什麼一直跨不過去

Five structural mechanics behind the plateau — mostly policy choices, not the cycle卡住的五個結構性原因，多半是政策選擇，不是景氣

  • Wages lag output. Labour's share of GDP is just 33.6% (2024), far below the ~50% of advanced economies and short of the government's own (missed) 40% target. The median citizen salary was only RM2,793/month in 2024. Output grows; pay doesn't keep up — and that sits directly on mortgage affordability.薪資跟不上產出。勞動報酬只佔GDP 33.6%（2024），遠低於先進國的約五成，連政府自訂、已跳票的40%目標都沒到。2024年公民月薪中位數才RM2,793。產出長大了，工資卻沒同步，這直接壓住房貸可負擔性。
  • Locked into the low-value end. In its one world-class industry, Malaysia owns chip back-end assembly & test (~13% globally) but barely any design or fabrication; semiconductors are ~65% of E&E exports, much of it thin-margin contract work for foreign firms.卡在價值鏈低端。在它唯一世界級的產業裡，馬來西亞拿的是晶片後段封測（全球約13%），設計與晶圓製造幾乎缺席；半導體佔電子電機出口約65%，做的多是替外商代工、利潤很薄。
  • Cheap foreign labour delays the upgrade. Migrant workers are ~15% of the documented workforce (more counting the undocumented), holding the wage floor down and blunting firms' incentive to automate and move up.廉價外勞拖住升級。外勞約佔有證勞動力一成五（連同無證更高），壓低了工資底盤，也削弱了企業自動化、往高值轉型的誘因。
  • The skills gap is on the demand side. It isn't a shortage of graduates but of high-skill jobs: 88.7% of fresh graduates are absorbed into semi/low-skill roles, and mismatched graduates earn ~49% less — the "gaji cukup makan" (just-enough-to-get-by) reality.技能錯配在需求端。不是大學生不夠，是高技能職位不夠：88.7%的新鮮人被中低技能職位吸納，學用錯配的大學生少賺約49%，這就是民間講的「gaji cukup makan」（薪水剛好夠吃）。
  • A thin treasury. Tax revenue is only ~12% of GDP; since GST was scrapped in 2018 the state leans on a narrower SST, while government debt nears its 65% ceiling and the oil cushion shrinks (Petronas's 2026 dividend cut to RM20bn). Limited firepower to fund the R&D, education and upgrade it needs.國庫太薄。稅收只佔GDP約12%；2018年取消GST後，靠的是較窄的SST；政府債務又逼近65%上限、石油紅利在縮水（國油2026年股息砍到RM200億）。要砸錢做研發、教育、升級，子彈很有限。

The property read: when wages are structurally held down, prices can't ride local owner-occupier demand very far. The real upside comes from the specific places where high-value industry — chips, data centres — actually lands.對房市的意思：當薪資被結構壓著，房價就很難靠本地自住剛需大漲；真正的增量，來自把高值產業（晶片、數據中心）落地的特定地點。

Here is the mechanism in one chart. Over 2010–2024 real GDP grew +82%, but the real median wage rose only +43% — barely half the pace (World Bank, 2026). Labour's share of output is stuck at 33.6% versus ~50% in advanced economies, and value-add per Malaysian worker (RM99k) is now about a fifth of Singapore's — a gap that has widened since 2010 while China has caught up. The problem isn't jobs; unemployment is ~3%. It is that the high-paying work sits in a thin frontier of export and MNC firms — E&E is ~40% of exports, ~60% of that in Penang — not across the broad economy.一張圖把機制說完。2010到2024年，實質GDP長了82%，實質薪資中位數卻只漲43%，不到一半（世界銀行，2026）。勞動報酬佔產出卡在33.6%，對比先進國約五成；每名馬來西亞工人的產值（RM9.9萬）只有新加坡的約五分之一，而且這道差距從2010年以來還在擴大，中國則已追平。問題不在缺工作——失業率僅約3%——而在高薪工作只集中在一小撮出口與跨國企業的前沿（電子電機佔出口約四成、其中約六成在檳城），沒有擴散到整體經濟。

The property read: when output grows but wages don't, owner-occupier purchasing power can't track asset prices. Growth shows up in GDP and in the value of capital — including property — but not in the paycheque that has to service the mortgage. That wedge is the affordability squeeze, three sections down.對房市的意思：當產出長大、薪水沒跟上，自住買家的購買力就追不上資產價格。成長體現在GDP、體現在資本（包括房子）的價值上，卻沒體現在那張要拿去繳房貸的薪水條上。這道楔子，就是後面三段要講的可負擔squeeze。

Imports the Bottom, Exports the Top進口底層，出口頂層

Imports the bottom — the cheap-labour floor進口底層 — 廉價勞動的地板

  • ~2.1m documented foreign workers (Oct 2025, −13% YoY) + an estimated 1.2–3.5m undocumented — together ~15%+ of the workforce約210萬有證外勞（2025年10月，年減13%）＋估計120–350萬無證——合計佔勞動力一成五以上

Exports the top — the brain-drain ceiling出口頂層 — 人才外流的天花板

  • ~1.86m Malaysians live abroad (World Bank, 2026); more than half in skilled jobs — Malaysia is a "net skills exporter"約186萬馬來西亞人在海外（世界銀行，2026），逾半從事高技能工作——馬來西亞是「淨技能輸出國」

  • Only 13–18% of patents at the local office are filed by residents — inventive capacity is leaking out, not building up本地專利只有13–18%由本國居民申請——創新能量在外流，而不是累積

In between sits the squeeze. Malaysia produces graduates faster than it produces graduate-level jobs: 36% of tertiary workers are in roles below their qualifications, taking a 49% wage penalty — and the World Bank is explicit that this is a demand-side problem (too few high-skill jobs, not too many graduates). High-skilled roles are just 30% of employment, against 65% in Singapore. So the country imports the bottom of the labour market and exports the top, while the state occupies the middle — and the human capital that would move the structure up the value chain never accumulates. That is the trap closing on itself.夾在中間的，是那道擠壓。馬來西亞生產大學生的速度，快過它生產大學生等級的工作：36%的高教勞工，做的是低於學歷的職位，少賺49%——世界銀行講得很白，這是需求面問題（高技能職位太少，不是大學生太多）。高技能職位只佔就業30%，新加坡是65%。於是這個國家進口勞動市場的底層、出口頂層，而中間由國家機器佔據——那些本該把結構往價值鏈上游推的人力資本，始終累積不起來。這就是陷阱閉合在自己身上。

The property read: the labour market that can't manufacture high incomes is the same one that can't manufacture broad homeowners. Organic owner-occupier demand stays thin, so the market leans on the skilled minority who stayed, plus foreign and investor money — concentrated exactly where the surviving high-value jobs are.對房市的意思：那個製造不出高收入的勞動市場，也製造不出廣大的有殼族。自住剛需始終偏薄，於是市場靠的是留下來的那一小撮高技能者，加上外資與投資客的錢——而且集中在那些倖存的高值工作所在之處。

A State-Heavy Economy國家之手很重的經濟

Part of the plateau is institutional. Malaysia's six government investment funds (EPF, Khazanah, PNB and peers) manage over RM1.8 trillion — roughly the size of the entire economy — and hold about 26% of the local stock market; government-linked companies are ~40% of Bursa by value. The 1971 affirmative-action goal of 30% Bumiputera corporate equity is still unmet at ~18% and has been re-anchored to 2035. A large, redistributive state stabilises the economy, but it also crowds out private risk-taking and slows deep reform — which is why the income gap is as much a political choice as an economic one.天花板有一部分是制度造成的。馬來西亞六大政府投資基金（EPF、Khazanah、PNB等）管理逾1.8兆令吉，規模約等於整個經濟體，並持有本地股市約26%的市值；政府連結公司（GLC）更佔馬股約四成。1971年留下的土著持股30%平權目標，至今只到約18%，已順延到2035年。一個龐大、講求再分配的國家機器能穩住經濟，卻也排擠了民間冒險的空間、讓深層改革走得很慢。所以這道收入差距，與其說是經濟問題，不如說也是政治選擇。

But the tailwind is fading: the fertility rate has fallen to 1.6, well below replacement; citizen growth is just +0.7%; and Malaysia crosses into an "aged society" around 2044. Malaysia isn't one housing market; it is a dual one, and the dividing line is ethnicity. Seen from the world, Malaysia's chip story is a value-chain story. It runs about 13% of the planet's semiconductor back-end — the assembly, test and packaging at the tail of the line — and is the 6th-largest chip exporter. But that is the bottom of the smile above. The value is captured at the ends: chip design and IP (~85% gross margin, almost all US/UK), the tools (ASML alone makes 100% of EUV machines), and leading-edge fabrication (Taiwan makes 92% of the world's sub-10nm logic) — while back-end runs on ~15% margins and is barely 10% of a finished chip's value. Malaysia is essentially absent from the high-value rungs: no leading-edge fab, no world-class design house, no EDA. And even its 13% is owned by Intel, Infineon and Micron — Malaysia captures the jobs; foreign firms keep the margin and the IP. It does a large share of the world's chip work and keeps a sliver of the value — and the niche is contestable, with Vietnam, India and China underbidding the same low-margin back-end.從世界的角度看，馬來西亞的晶片故事，其實是一個價值鏈故事。它做了全球約13%的半導體後段——產線最末端的封裝、測試——也是全球第6大晶片出口國。但這是上面那條微笑曲線的最低點。價值被兩端吃走：晶片設計與IP（毛利率約85%，幾乎全在美英）、設備（光ASML就壟斷100%的EUV曝光機）、以及先進晶圓製造（台灣包辦全球92%的10奈米以下邏輯晶片）——而後段毛利率只有約15%，僅佔一顆成品晶片價值的一成。馬來西亞在高價值環節幾乎缺席：沒有先進晶圓廠、沒有世界級設計公司、沒有EDA。而且那13%還是Intel、Infineon、Micron的——馬來西亞拿到的是工作機會，外商留住利潤與IP。它做了世界一大塊晶片的工，卻只分到一小片值——而且這個位置可被取代，越南、印度、中國都在低價搶同一塊低毛利後段。

The one thing working in Malaysia's favour is geography as neutrality. As the US–China chip war splits the supply chain, Malaysia is the neutral ground where firms diversifying out of China and Taiwan land — Intel's RM30bn advanced-packaging build in Penang, Infineon's €7bn power-chip fab in Kulim, SPIL/ASE's RM6bn in Batu Kawan. But neutrality is a tightrope. Malaysia leans on US technology and the China market at once (China is its #1 trading partner); since July 2025 it needs a strategic-trade permit to move advanced AI chips, under direct US pressure over transshipment to China; and while its mainstream back-end mix is, for now, spared the US tariffs, a 25% AI-chip tariff (Jan 2026) and a pending mid-2026 review keep the risk live. The "Switzerland of semiconductors" is also standing in the crossfire.唯一站在馬來西亞這邊的，是「中立」這個地理位置。當美中晶片戰把供應鏈一切為二，馬來西亞成了企業從中國、台灣分散時的中立落腳點——Intel在檳城RM300億的先進封裝廠、Infineon在居林70億歐元的功率晶片廠、日月光／矽品在峇都加灣的RM60億。但中立是走鋼索。馬來西亞同時靠美國技術和中國市場（中國是它第一大貿易夥伴）；2025年7月起，要移動先進AI晶片得申請戰略貿易許可，背後是美國對「轉運到中國」的直接施壓；而它主流的後段出口組合雖然目前還躲過美國關稅，2026年1月的25% AI晶片關稅、加上年中將至的複審，都讓風險懸著。這個「半導體界的瑞士」，其實也站在火線上。

Malaysia knows it is stuck — which is what the 2024 National Semiconductor Strategy is for: ≥RM500bn of investment, 60,000 engineers, and a push up into design and advanced packaging, the higher-value rungs the AI era is opening right next to its back-end strength. But the climb is, so far, aspirational. Only ~RM63bn (~13% of the goal) is secured; local firms own ~0% of advanced-packaging IP; the flagship Arm-IP deal is under a corruption probe; and the binding constraint is people — Malaysia graduates ~5,000 engineers a year against a ~50,000 need while losing ~15% of its skilled talent a year, mostly to Singapore (which pays roughly 1.5× more). The brain drain and the value-chain trap are the same wound: you cannot climb a chain whose top keeps emigrating.馬來西亞自己也知道卡住了——這正是2024年國家半導體策略（NSS）的用意：至少RM5,000億投資、6萬名工程師，以及往設計與先進封裝爬升——那是AI時代在它後段強項旁邊打開的高價值階。但這趟攀爬，到目前為止還是「願景」。只籌到約RM630億（約目標的13%）；本地公司握有的先進封裝IP幾近於零；旗艦的Arm IP案還陷入貪腐調查；而真正的瓶頸是人——馬來西亞一年只產出約5,000名工程師，需求卻約5萬，同時每年流失約15%的高技能人才，多半流向新加坡（薪水約高1.5倍）。人才外流和價值鏈陷阱，是同一道傷口：當一條鏈的頂端不斷往外移民，你就爬不上去。

Four Loops That Keep It Stuck四道讓它卡住的迴圈

Put the pieces together and you don't get a list — you get four self-reinforcing loops. The World Bank frames it not as a "vicious cycle" but as a broken virtuous one: workers keep building qualifications, but the economy creates too few good jobs to absorb them, so the loop that should link capability to opportunity never turns.把這些拼起來，你得到的不是一張清單，而是四道自我強化的迴圈。世界銀行不把它叫「惡性循環」，而是一個斷掉的良性循環：勞工不斷累積學歷，經濟卻創造不出足夠的好工作來吸納他們，於是那個本該把「能力」接上「機會」的循環，始終轉不起來。

1 · The cheap-labour loop1 · 廉價勞動迴圈

Cheap migrant labour → weak incentive to automate → low productivity → low wages → still-cheap labour. Note: the official diagnosis; the automation link isn't yet proven for Malaysia.廉價外勞 → 自動化誘因弱 → 生產力低 → 薪資低 → 勞動力仍便宜。註：這是官方診斷；自動化連結在馬國尚未被實證。

2 · The fiscal–structural loop2 · 財政—結構迴圈

Low value-add → low incomes → thin tax base (~12%) → too little R&D/education → stays low value-add. The firmest-evidenced loop — the spine of the trap.低值 → 收入低 → 稅基薄（約12%）→ 研發／教育投入不足 → 繼續低值。證據最扎實的一道，是整個陷阱的脊椎。

3 · The brain-drain loop3 · 人才外流迴圈

Low high-skill wages → skilled emigrate (1.86m) → human-capital shortage → can't move up the chain → high-skill wages stay low. The top leaks out.高技能薪資低 → 人才外流（186萬）→ 人力資本短缺 → 爬不上價值鏈 → 高技能薪資仍低。頂層持續流失。

4 · The redistributive-state loop4 · 再分配國家迴圈

GLC + NEP dominance → crowds out private & foreign capital → weak private dynamism → more reliance on the state. Note: crowding-out is firm-level proven; the full chain is contested.GLC＋新經濟政策主導 → 排擠民間與外資 → 民間活力弱 → 更依賴國家。註：排擠效應有公司層級實證；整條鏈仍有爭議。

Loops 1 and 2 are the economic engine; loop 3 is the human-capital leak; loop 4 is the political-economy lock that holds it all in place. Together: the country imports the bottom of the labour market, exports the top, and lets the state occupy the middle — and the structure that produces a thin treasury keeps reproducing itself.迴圈1與2是經濟引擎；迴圈3是人力資本的漏洞；迴圈4是把一切固定住的政治經濟之鎖。合起來：這個國家進口勞動市場的底層、出口頂層、讓國家機器佔據中間——而那個製造出薄弱國庫的結構，不斷地自我複製。

The 1969 Contract1969 年的那份合約

How a race riot became a housing policy: the NEP as the country's founding bargain一場種族衝突如何變成一套房市政策：新經濟政策，這個國家的立國協議

On 13 May 1969, days after an election that shook Malay political primacy, Kuala Lumpur burned. Out of the violence came a settlement that still governs the country: the New Economic Policy of 1971. Its terms were simple and have never really lapsed. Malay political leadership and state-led redistribution, in exchange for racial peace. Property became the single largest instrument of that bargain: Bumiputera quotas and discounts, Malay Reserve Land, government-linked developers. Part 2 covered how these mechanisms work; this section is about why they exist.1969 年 5 月 13 日，一場撼動馬來人政治主導地位的大選過後幾天，吉隆坡陷入火海。從那場流血裡走出來的，是一份至今仍在運作的協議：1971 年的新經濟政策（NEP）。條款很簡單：馬來人掌握政治領導、國家主導再分配，換取族群之間的和平。房地產成了這份協議最主要的執行載體，土著配額與折扣、馬來保留地、政府連結發展商，都是從這裡來的。機制在第二篇講過，這一節講它為什麼存在。

The bargain came with a number: 30% Bumiputera corporate equity by 1990. It was never hit. Official measures put it at 18.4% in 2020 and around 17–18% today; on a strict listed-company basis it is closer to 7% (ISEAS / Lee Hwok Aun). So the deadline keeps moving. The PuTERA35 blueprint re-anchors the target to 2035 and adds a new one, 70% of high-skilled jobs for Bumiputera. Fifty-five years on, the contract still has at least another decade to run.這份協議帶著一個數字：1990 年前達成土著企業股權 30%。它從來沒達標。官方口徑是 2020 年 18.4%、目前約 17–18%；若嚴格只看上市公司，更接近 7%（ISEAS／Lee Hwok Aun）。於是期限一延再延，目標如今寫進 PuTERA35 藍圖、重新錨定到 2035 年，還疊上一個新目標：高技能職位的 70% 要給土著。五十五年過去，這份協議至少還要再跑十年。

The Geometry of an Unequal Vote一票不等值的選舉幾何

Voters per federal seat — why the rules tilt toward the countryside每個國會席位的選民數，以及規則為什麼向鄉區傾斜

Malaysia's constitution allows rural seats to carry far more weight than urban ones, and the gap has become extreme. Igan, a rural seat in Sarawak, has 28,290 voters; Bangi, on Selangor's urban fringe, has more than 303,000. One vote in Igan therefore weighs about 10.5 votes in Bangi. Undi18 and automatic registration only sharpened the imbalance: the electoral roll expanded by roughly a third to 21 million, and the number of seats holding over 100,000 voters jumped from 35 to 85 (eight now exceed 200,000). The new voters piled up in the cities, which deepened the tilt rather than correcting it.馬來西亞的憲法，默許鄉區席位承載遠比都市席位更重的分量，而這道落差已經走到極端。砂拉越的鄉區席位伊干（Igan），只有 28,290 名選民；坐落在雪蘭莪都市邊緣的班基（Bangi），選民卻超過 303,000。換算下來，伊干的一票，約等於班基的 10.5 票。Undi18 與自動登記，只讓失衡更尖銳：選民冊擴大約三分之一、來到 2,100 萬人，選民超過 10 萬的席位從 35 席暴增到 85 席（其中 8 席已超過 20 萬）。而新增的選民大多堆在城市，讓這道傾斜更深，沒有修正它。

The chain runs straight from here. Rural, majority-Malay seats are structurally over-weighted → any workable governing coalition must first win the countryside → the NEP-and-subsidy structure becomes electorally untouchable → and so the urban middle class and foreign buyers end up funding a system that answers to someone else. The property read: taxes rise on cities and foreigners first, for a plain reason. The seats that decide who governs are rural, and rural seats pay almost no property tax. That ordering is unlikely to reverse in the foreseeable future.對房市的意思：加稅的順序總是城市與外國人優先，原因很直接：決定政權的席位在鄉區，而鄉區幾乎不繳房產稅。這個順序在可見的將來不會反過來。

The Three-Way Auction for the Malay Vote馬來票的三方拍賣

From 61 years of one-party rule to a hung parliament, and what the 2026 Johor landslide means從 61 年一黨獨大到懸峙國會，以及 2026 年柔佛州選的意義

For 61 years one coalition governed unbroken (Barisan Nasional, 1957–2018). Since then the system has cycled through an upset (2018), a backroom coup (the 2020 "Sheraton Move"), and a hung parliament with an Islamist "green wave" (2022). The most recent data point is the July 2026 Johor state election, and it reversed the trend sharply: Barisan, contesting alone, took 48 of 56 seats (UMNO 36 of 37), Perikatan Nasional lost every seat it contested, and Pakatan Harapan won eight.有 61 年，這個國家由單一聯盟不間斷地執政（國民陣線，1957–2018）。之後八年連續換了幾種局面：2018 年變天、2020 年「喜來登政變」的後門奪權、2022 年懸峙國會加伊斯蘭「綠潮」。最近的一筆是 2026 年 7 月的柔佛州選，方向明顯反轉：單獨出戰的國陣拿下 56 席中的 48 席（巫統 37 席中拿 36 席），國盟（PN）掛零，希盟（PH）只拿到 8 席。

Read structurally, the Malay vote is now split three ways among UMNO, PAS and Bersatu, and a three-way split produces competitive outbidding: each party has to prove it protects Malays hardest. That ratchet pushes policy deeper into the NEP and never back out. The non-Malay vote, locked to PH/DAP with nowhere else to go, gets taken for granted. Johor cuts both ways. The collapse of the green wave genuinely lowers the risk of harder Islamisation, but the winner was a solo UMNO, so the live question before GE16 is the balance of power inside the ruling coalition itself.從結構讀，馬來票如今在巫統、伊斯蘭黨、土團之間三分，而三方分裂就會出現競價式喊價：每一方都得證明自己最保護馬來人。這個棘輪只會把政策往 NEP 更深處推，不會往回。非馬來票被鎖在希盟／行動黨、無處可去，於是被視為理所當然。柔佛這一役要分兩面看：綠潮崩盤確實降低了更激進伊斯蘭化的風險，但贏家是單飛的巫統，所以 GE16 之前真正的變數，是執政聯盟內部的權力平衡。

The property read: whoever ends up governing, the policy stance toward urban asset owners barely moves. What changes is mainly who administers it.對房市的意思：無論最後由誰執政，對城市資產持有者的政策方向都差不多，會變的主要是執行的人。

Three Pens Write the Rules三支筆共寫規則

Land is a state matter, and the palace is not just ceremonial土地是州權事項，而王室並不只是儀式性的

Under the Federal Constitution, land is a state matter, and that single line splits the market into a dozen regulatory regimes. Foreign-purchase thresholds, state consent letters, and the size of the Bumiputera quota and discount are all set state by state. Penang gates island landed homes at around RM3 million, Kuala Lumpur sets its foreign floor near RM1 million, and Johor writes its own concessions inside the special economic zone. At the level of rules there is no single "Malaysian property market"; there are thirteen state versions of it.依《聯邦憲法》，土地是州權事項。光是這一條，就把市場切成十來套規則：外國人購屋門檻、州同意書、土著配額與折扣的比例，全部一州一套。檳城把島上的有地住宅門檻設在約 RM300 萬，吉隆坡的外國人門檻定在約 RM100 萬，柔佛則在經濟特區裡自訂優惠。就規則層面而言，「馬來西亞房市」其實是十三個州各一版。

The second pen is the palace, and in Johor it is not ceremonial. The state's ruler, Sultan Ibrahim, Malaysia's current King (Agong, 2024–2028), holds an effective 21.9% stake in CGPV, the master developer of Forest City, through Esplanade Danga 88 (99.9% owned), as reported by The Edge. Forest City itself was rescued by policy: designated a Special Financial Zone in 2024, with nine family offices approved by June 2026. The rescue and the royal commercial interest point the same way. The crown's constitutional role has also widened; the King personally arbitrated the formation of government after both the 2020 Sheraton Move and the 2022 hung parliament.第二支筆是王室，而在柔佛，它一點都不儀式性。柔佛蘇丹依布拉欣（馬來西亞現任國家元首，2024–2028）透過持股 99.9% 的 Esplanade Danga 88，在森林城市總發展商 CGPV 握有實質 21.9% 股權（據 The Edge 報導）。森林城市本身則是被政策救回來的：2024 年獲劃為特別金融區（SFZ），到 2026 年 6 月已批出 9 家家族辦公室。政策救援與王室的商業利益，指向同一個方向。王室的憲政角色也在擴大，2020 年喜來登政變與 2022 年懸峙國會之後，兩度由國家元首親自裁決組閣。

The property read: the rules on any given unit are co-written by three pens. Federal finance sets stamp duty, RPGT and income tax; the state government sets thresholds, quotas and consent letters; and on land and the largest projects, the palace's position matters too. Due diligence that only reads the federal layer misses two of the three.對房市的意思：你買的每一戶，規則由三支筆共寫。聯邦財政管印花稅、RPGT 與所得稅；州政府管門檻、配額與同意書；土地與大型項目，還要看王室的態度。只查聯邦這一層的盡職調查，等於漏掉三分之二。

The list supports three working rules. The half-life of a policy is about one political cycle. Anything that touches foreigners or the consumption tax tends to get reversed. And anything that touches the NEP core has never moved.這張清單可以整理出三條規律：政策的半衰期約等於一個政治週期；凡是碰外國人或消費稅的，多半會反覆；碰 NEP 核心的，則從來沒動過。

The property read: a ten-year hold will span two or three governments, and therefore two or three sets of rules. "The rules won't change" is the biggest unpriced assumption in buying KL. Note also that the rules most certain to stay, the quotas and the reserved land, are the ones that hold prices down.對房市的意思：十年持有期會跨過兩到三屆政府，也就是兩到三套規則。「規則不會變」是買進吉隆坡最大的一個未定價假設。另外要記得：最確定不會變的那幾條規則（配額與保留地），正好是壓著房價的那幾條。

Follow the money and the two-sidedness resolves. On the federal side, the 2026 budget pencils in RM10.3 billion from stamp duty and RM2.4 billion from RPGT (up from RM10.1bn and RM2.3bn in 2025), and the line keeps being revised upward. So the mechanism splits by level. When the federation is short of cash, it raises taxes on foreigners first, because that costs no votes. When a state is short, it leans on land appreciation and conversion premiums, which is why no state lets land get cheap. Reintroducing the GST is the one change that could loosen this ratchet, and that is why GST is a property question as much as a fiscal one.於是機制按層級一分為二。聯邦缺錢時，先對外國人加稅，因為這不會流失選票；州缺錢時，靠土地增值與轉換 premium，所以它不會讓地變便宜。能鬆開這個棘輪的只有重啟 GST，這也是為什麼 GST 既是財政議題，也是房市議題。

The property read: fiscal structure pushes the state to protect prices and volumes while raising the cost on foreign buyers. In practice that limits how far this market can fall, and also how fast it is allowed to rise.對房市的意思：財政結構讓國家傾向保價、保量，同時把成本轉到外國買家身上。實際效果是這個市場跌不深，但也漲不快。

The Verdict — Three Tradeable Political Laws裁決：三條可以拿來交易的政治規律

Turning all of the above into rules you can actually position around把上面這一切，收斂成三條你真的能拿來布局的規則

  • 1 · Rule risk has a direction. The same fiscal pressure produces opposite moves for different buyers: one-way tightening on foreigners (stamp duty 4%→8%, REIT withholding 10%→30%, MM2H now tied to a compulsory property purchase) and one-way loosening for local first-home buyers (the stamp-duty exemption extended repeatedly, the SJKP loan guarantee doubled). The buyer's passport is a good predictor of which way the next change goes.1 · 規則風險有方向。同一股財政壓力，對不同買家走出相反的兩條路：對外國人單向收緊（印花稅 4%→8%、REIT 扣繳 10%→30%、MM2H 綁上強制購房），對本國首購族單向放鬆（印花稅豁免一延再延、SJKP 貸款擔保翻倍）。要預測下一次改動的方向，看買家的護照通常就夠了。
  • 2 · Rule risk has layers. The NEP core (quotas, reserved land, GLCs) is constitutional-grade and does not move; consumption taxes (GST/SST) are electoral-grade and reverse with the cycle; foreigner clauses are fiscal-grade and swing with the deficit. The longer the hold, the more the bet rests on the immovable layer alone, and that layer is the one that caps the market.2 · 規則風險有分層。NEP 核心（配額、保留地、GLC）是憲法級的，動不了；消費稅（GST／SST）是選舉級的，隨週期反覆；外國人條款是財政級的，隨赤字擺盪。持有期越長，押的就越只剩那個動不了的層，而那一層正好是把市場封頂的。
  • 3 · The political cycle is a property clock. The twelve months before an election are the sweetener window (exemptions, infrastructure announcements, subsidies); the twelve-to-eighteen months after are the tax window (the first foreigner-tax template appeared after GST was scrapped in 2018, and the 4% legislated after the 2022 election doubled to 8% by Budget 2026). With GE16 most likely late-2026 to early-2027, the pre-election sweeteners for buyers are already on their way. A foreign buyer should treat the first post-election Budget as the point where rule risk comes due.3 · 政治週期是一座房產時鐘。大選前 12 個月是甜頭窗口（豁免、基建宣布、補貼），選後 12 到 18 個月是加稅窗口（2018 年廢 GST 後首度出現外國人稅的雛型，2022 年選後立的 4%，到 2026 年預算翻倍成 8%）。GE16 大概率落在 2026 年底到 2027 年初，選前給買家的甜頭已經在路上；外國人則應該把選後第一份預算案，當成規則風險兌現的時點。

The fiscal repair is real: the deficit is being walked from 5.5% toward 3.5–3.8%, diesel and RON95 subsidies are being targeted, and the widened SST should raise around RM25.5 billion a year. But what is being repaired is the fiscal position, not the structural lock, so the low-volatility, low-mean equilibrium stays. What improves is the tail risk, as the odds of a ratings downgrade or a ringgit crisis recede. Overall, politics will not break this market, but it does decide how the growth is shared: the industrial corridors get it, the national index does not, and locals carry a much lighter entry cost than foreigners. That arrangement has held since 1969, and there is no near-term reason to expect it to change.財政修復是真的在走：赤字正從 5.5% 往 3.5–3.8% 收、柴油與 RON95 補貼在目標化、擴圍後的 SST 預計一年多收約 RM255 億。但被修的是財政部位，不是那把結構鎖，所以「低波動、低均值」的均衡不變，改善的是尾部風險：評級遭降、令吉危機的機率在下降。整體來說，政治不會讓這個市場崩掉，但它決定成長的分配：產業走廊拿得到，全國大盤拿不到；本國人負擔較輕，外國人要先付一筆明顯更高的進場成本。這個格局從 1969 年延續至今，短期內看不到改變的理由。

The property takeaway對房產的結論

The key move is to read the property market as the output of the system, not an accident of the cycle. Because the traps are self-reinforcing, their property signature is an equilibrium: growth doesn't fully reach wages → wages are unevenly distributed → after necessities and debt, almost nothing is left to save → so effective buying power is a minority of households, concentrated by class, ethnicity and geography, sitting on a quota-divided dual market and a demographic engine on a timer. The near-term easing is real, but it mostly lifts those already qualified. That is why Malaysian property rewards picking the right location, segment and timing — not buying the national index, which is structurally capped until one of the loops actually breaks.關鍵的一步，是把房市讀成這套系統的產物，而不是景氣的意外。正因為這些陷阱會自我強化，它們在房市上的印記是一種「均衡」：成長到不了薪水 → 薪水分配不均 → 扣掉必需品與債務後幾乎存不下來 → 所以真正的購買力只在少數家庭手上，被階層、族群與地理三重集中，又坐在一個被配額切成兩半的雙軌市場、和一具裝了計時器的人口引擎上。短期降息是真的，但它主要抬升本來就過得了關的人。這就是為什麼馬來西亞房產獎勵的是選對地點、產品與時機，而不是買進「全國大盤」——在四道迴圈真正斷掉其中一道之前，大盤在結構上是被封頂的。

Locations: the corridors地點：哪些走廊

  • Penang: island landed around George Town, Tanjung Tokong and Bayan Lepas, plus Batu Kawan on the mainland. The case is a fifty-year electronics cluster that anchors employment; the state approved RM22.4bn of manufacturing investment in 2025 (see the Market Report's Penang section).檳城：島上有地（喬治市周邊、丹絨道光、峇六拜）與對岸的 Batu Kawan。理由是五十年的電子業聚落撐住就業，2025 年核准製造業投資 RM224 億（詳見市場報告檳城節）。
  • Johor: landed terraces in the RM400–700k band at Bukit Indah, Adda Heights and Mount Austin, backed by genuine owner-occupier demand plus SEZ job spillover. RTS-corridor high-rise (Bukit Chagar) yields 6–8%, but prices already reflect the link; wait for a pullback.柔佛：一二區有地排屋（Bukit Indah、Adda Heights、Mount Austin，RM40 到 70 萬帶），背後是真實自住盤加特區就業外溢。RTS 走廊高層（Bukit Chagar）收益 6–8%，但價格已大致反映利多，等回檔再考慮。
  • Kuala Lumpur: prime landed in TTDI, Bangsar and Damansara Heights, or low-density within 500m of confirmed MRT3 stations (Sentul, Seputeh, Bukit Jalil), positioned 18–24 months before the line opens.吉隆坡：精華有地（TTDI、Bangsar、Damansara Heights），或 MRT3 確定站點 500 公尺內的低密度（Sentul、Seputeh、Bukit Jalil），在通車前 18 到 24 個月布局。
  • Selangor: industrial property on the data-centre and logistics corridors (industrial rose 21.3% nationally in 2025), or the same exposure held indirectly through listed vehicles.雪蘭莪：數據中心與物流走廊的工業地產（2025 年全國工業地產漲 21.3%），或改用金融載體間接持有。
  • Avoid: serviced apartments on KL's city fringe, anything sitting on the NC supply line, outer-Johor high-rise, and Forest City until occupancy and resale data exist.迴避：吉隆坡市區邊緣的服務式公寓、任何落在 NC 供給線上的項目、柔佛外圍高層，以及森林城市（等入住率與轉售數據出來再說）。

Products: rank by the rent line產品：先看租金曲線

  • Landed before high-rise. New-launch sell-through runs 22.5% for landed against 5.1% for high-rise, and reasonable appreciation assumptions are ~6% versus 1.5–3% (Market Report, charts 11 and 19).有地優先於高層。首銷去化率 22.5% 對 5.1%，合理的增值假設約 6% 對 1.5–3%（市場報告圖 11、圖 19）。
  • Pick yield over stories. Roughly three quarters of a ten-year return comes from compounded rent (Market Report, part 7). A target of 5.5%+ gross with a deep tenant pool beats an appreciation narrative; the Mont'Kiara and Bangsar expat belt runs 4.6–5.4%, and room-let or near-employment product can run higher.挑租金，少聽增值故事。十年報酬約七成半來自租金複利（市場報告第七部）。與其挑增值題材，不如挑毛租 5.5% 以上、租客池深的標的；Mont'Kiara／Bangsar 級的外派租務帶約 4.6–5.4%，分租與近就業區可以更高。
  • Foreigners have a cheaper wrapper. The 8% entry duty and 30% rental withholding do not apply to listed vehicles: M-REITs and listed developers express the same corridor view, and industrial REITs carry the data-centre demand directly. Run the numbers in the REITs vs Property calculator.外國人有更便宜的載體。8% 進場稅與 30% 租金扣繳，不適用於上市載體：M-REIT 與上市發展商可以表達同一個走廊觀點，工業型 REIT 更直接承接數據中心需求。試算見 REIT vs 房產計算器。
  • Skip commercial-title serviced apartments as passive investments. Structurally the slowest appreciation, plus commercial assessment rates, commercial utilities and a thin resale pool (the Market Report's part 4 has the full breakdown). Running one yourself as a short-stay operation is the exception.別把商業地契服務式公寓當被動投資。增值結構性最慢，門牌稅、水電與轉售池全弱（市場報告第四部有完整拆解）；能自己經營短租的是例外。

Timing: three clocks時機：三個時鐘

  • The political clock. The 12 months before an election bring policy sweeteners; historically the tax rises land 12–18 months after. Foreigners should stay clear of the first post-GE16 Budget.政治時鐘。選前 12 個月是政策甜頭期；歷史上加稅都落在選後 12 到 18 個月。外國人避開 GE16 選後的第一份預算案。
  • The supply clock. The 2026–28 completion wave is a buyer's window; the 2029–31 completion trough is the first seller's window (Market Report, part 7).供給時鐘。2026 到 28 年的完工潮是買方議價窗；2029 到 31 年的完工谷，是第一個賣方窗（市場報告第七部）。
  • The tax clock. RPGT falls to zero for citizens from year 6, so plan exits after year 6; for foreigners it drops to 10% from year 6, so hold at least across that line. Aim the exit at 2033–35 rather than 2036 and beyond, when the demographic engine has already downshifted.稅務時鐘。本國人第 6 年起 RPGT 歸零，出場排在第 6 年之後；外國人第 6 年起降到 10%，持有期至少要跨過這條線。出場規劃放在 2033 到 35 年，不要押人口引擎已降檔的 2036 年之後。

Combinations where all three clocks line up are rare. The clearest one available now: enter in the 2026–27 pre-election window while the completion wave keeps buyers in charge, hold past year 6, and plan the exit around 2033.三個時鐘同時對齊的組合不多。目前看得到最順的一條路徑：在 2026 到 27 年的選前窗、趁完工潮還是買方市場時進場，持有跨過第 6 年，出場規劃在 2033 年前後。

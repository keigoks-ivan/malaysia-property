Bottom line一句話結論 2025 was the pivot year. After three years on hold, BNM cut the OPR to 2.75% — a pre-emptive insurance move, with growth still above 5% and inflation under 2%. But the easing lands in a market where household leverage is near its ceiling (84.8% of GDP) and Kuala Lumpur's average price is actually falling. Cheaper money meets demand that is structurally concentrated — Johor's SEZ, data centres and record FDI — rather than broad-based. 2025年是轉向年。國行按兵三年後把OPR降到2.75%，屬預防性的保險降息，當時經濟仍站穩5%以上、通膨壓在2%以下。但這波寬鬆，落在一個家庭槓桿逼近上限（佔GDP 84.8%）、吉隆坡均價反而下跌的市場。便宜的錢遇到的需求高度集中（柔新特區、數據中心、創紀錄FDI），不是全面普漲。

Malaysia's wealth has always come out of the ground. Stuck Below the High-Income Line卡在高收入門檻前

GDP per capita, nominal US$, 2024 (World Bank)人均GDP（名目美元），2024（世界銀行）

Put plainly: Malaysia reached upper-middle income in 1996; nearly thirty years on, GDP per capita is about US$11,900 — below China, a third of South Korea, a sixth of Singapore, and roughly US$2,000 short of the World Bank's high-income line (official target: 2030). It has the skeleton of an upper-middle economy but hasn't grown the high-income muscle. The points below are why it keeps falling short — and why that matters for property.話說白一點：馬來西亞1996年就跨進中高收入，將近三十年了，人均GDP約1.19萬美元，還是卡在中國之下、只有南韓的三分之一、新加坡的六分之一，離世界銀行的高收入門檻還差約兩千美元（官方目標2030年達標）。它有一具中高收入國家的骨架，卻一直長不出高收入國家的肌肉。底下這幾條，才是它一直跨不過去的真正原因，也是它跟房市的關係所在。

The Root: It Deindustrialised Too Soon病根：它太早去工業化

Manufacturing as a share of GDP, % (World Bank). Peaked while still middle-income.製造業佔GDP比重，%（世界銀行）。在還是中等收入時就見頂。

Start at the root. The textbook path to high income runs through deep industrialisation — make ever more valuable things, pay ever-higher wages — and only then shift to services. Taiwan and Korea crossed into high income (1987/1995) while keeping a manufacturing base near a quarter to a third of GDP. Malaysia did the opposite: manufacturing peaked at ~30.9% of GDP in 1999 and has slid to ~22.5% — it began deindustrialising while still upper-middle income. Economists call this premature deindustrialisation (Rodrik; KRI). The high-productivity engine that should have built a broad high-wage middle class turned down early — and everything downstream inherits that.從病根看起。教科書上邁向高收入的路，是先深化工業——做愈來愈值錢的東西、付愈來愈高的薪水——之後才轉向服務業。台灣與南韓跨進高收入（1987／1995）時，仍保有佔GDP約四分之一到三分之一的製造業。馬來西亞反過來：製造業佔GDP在1999年見頂約30.9%，之後一路滑到約22.5%——它在還只是中高收入時就開始去工業化。經濟學家叫這個過早去工業化（Rodrik；Khazanah研究院）。那具本該撐起廣大高薪中產的高生產力引擎，提早熄火了——而下游的一切，都繼承了這個病根。

Why Growth Keeps Falling Short為什麼一直跨不過去

Five structural mechanics behind the plateau — mostly policy choices, not the cycle卡住的五個結構性原因，多半是政策選擇，不是景氣

  • Wages lag output. Labour's share of GDP is just 33.6% (2024), far below the ~50% of advanced economies and short of the government's own (missed) 40% target. The median citizen salary was only RM2,793/month in 2024. Output grows; pay doesn't keep up — and that sits directly on mortgage affordability.薪資跟不上產出。勞動報酬只佔GDP 33.6%（2024），遠低於先進國的約五成，連政府自訂、已跳票的40%目標都沒到。2024年公民月薪中位數才RM2,793。產出長大了，工資卻沒同步，這直接壓住房貸可負擔性。
  • Locked into the low-value end. In its one world-class industry, Malaysia owns chip back-end assembly & test (~13% globally) but barely any design or fabrication; semiconductors are ~65% of E&E exports, much of it thin-margin contract work for foreign firms.卡在價值鏈低端。在它唯一世界級的產業裡，馬來西亞拿的是晶片後段封測（全球約13%），設計與晶圓製造幾乎缺席；半導體佔電子電機出口約65%，做的多是替外商代工、利潤很薄。
  • Cheap foreign labour delays the upgrade. Migrant workers are ~15% of the documented workforce (more counting the undocumented), holding the wage floor down and blunting firms' incentive to automate and move up.廉價外勞拖住升級。外勞約佔有證勞動力一成五（連同無證更高），壓低了工資底盤，也削弱了企業自動化、往高值轉型的誘因。
  • The skills gap is on the demand side. It isn't a shortage of graduates but of high-skill jobs: 88.7% of fresh graduates are absorbed into semi/low-skill roles, and mismatched graduates earn ~49% less — the "gaji cukup makan" (just-enough-to-get-by) reality.技能錯配在需求端。不是大學生不夠，是高技能職位不夠：88.7%的新鮮人被中低技能職位吸納，學用錯配的大學生少賺約49%，這就是民間講的「gaji cukup makan」（薪水剛好夠吃）。
  • A thin treasury. Tax revenue is only ~12% of GDP; since GST was scrapped in 2018 the state leans on a narrower SST, while government debt nears its 65% ceiling and the oil cushion shrinks (Petronas's 2026 dividend cut to RM20bn). Limited firepower to fund the R&D, education and upgrade it needs.國庫太薄。稅收只佔GDP約12%；2018年取消GST後，靠的是較窄的SST；政府債務又逼近65%上限、石油紅利在縮水（國油2026年股息砍到RM200億）。要砸錢做研發、教育、升級，子彈很有限。

The property read: when wages are structurally held down, prices can't ride local owner-occupier demand very far. The real upside comes from the specific places where high-value industry — chips, data centres — actually lands.對房市的意思：當薪資被結構壓著，房價就很難靠本地自住剛需大漲；真正的增量，來自把高值產業（晶片、數據中心）落地的特定地點。

Here is the mechanism in one chart. Over 2010–2024 real GDP grew +82%, but the real median wage rose only +43% — barely half the pace (World Bank, 2026). Labour's share of output is stuck at 33.6% versus ~50% in advanced economies, and value-add per Malaysian worker (RM99k) is now about a fifth of Singapore's — a gap that has widened since 2010 while China has caught up. The problem isn't jobs; unemployment is ~3%. It is that the high-paying work sits in a thin frontier of export and MNC firms — E&E is ~40% of exports, ~60% of that in Penang — not across the broad economy.一張圖把機制說完。2010到2024年，實質GDP長了82%，實質薪資中位數卻只漲43%，不到一半（世界銀行，2026）。勞動報酬佔產出卡在33.6%，對比先進國約五成；每名馬來西亞工人的產值（RM9.9萬）只有新加坡的約五分之一，而且這道差距從2010年以來還在擴大，中國則已追平。問題不在缺工作——失業率僅約3%——而在高薪工作只集中在一小撮出口與跨國企業的前沿（電子電機佔出口約四成、其中約六成在檳城），沒有擴散到整體經濟。

The property read: when output grows but wages don't, owner-occupier purchasing power can't track asset prices. Growth shows up in GDP and in the value of capital — including property — but not in the paycheque that has to service the mortgage. That wedge is the affordability squeeze, three sections down.對房市的意思：當產出長大、薪水沒跟上，自住買家的購買力就追不上資產價格。成長體現在GDP、體現在資本（包括房子）的價值上，卻沒體現在那張要拿去繳房貸的薪水條上。這道楔子，就是後面三段要講的可負擔squeeze。

Imports the Bottom, Exports the Top進口底層，出口頂層

Imports the bottom — the cheap-labour floor進口底層 — 廉價勞動的地板

  • ~2.1m documented foreign workers (Oct 2025, −13% YoY) + an estimated 1.2–3.5m undocumented — together ~15%+ of the workforce約210萬有證外勞（2025年10月，年減13%）＋估計120–350萬無證——合計佔勞動力一成五以上

Exports the top — the brain-drain ceiling出口頂層 — 人才外流的天花板

  • ~1.86m Malaysians live abroad (World Bank, 2026); more than half in skilled jobs — Malaysia is a "net skills exporter"約186萬馬來西亞人在海外（世界銀行，2026），逾半從事高技能工作——馬來西亞是「淨技能輸出國」

  • Only 13–18% of patents at the local office are filed by residents — inventive capacity is leaking out, not building up本地專利只有13–18%由本國居民申請——創新能量在外流，而不是累積

In between sits the squeeze. Malaysia produces graduates faster than it produces graduate-level jobs: 36% of tertiary workers are in roles below their qualifications, taking a 49% wage penalty — and the World Bank is explicit that this is a demand-side problem (too few high-skill jobs, not too many graduates). High-skilled roles are just 30% of employment, against 65% in Singapore. So the country imports the bottom of the labour market and exports the top, while the state occupies the middle — and the human capital that would move the structure up the value chain never accumulates. That is the trap closing on itself.夾在中間的，是那道擠壓。馬來西亞生產大學生的速度，快過它生產大學生等級的工作：36%的高教勞工，做的是低於學歷的職位，少賺49%——世界銀行講得很白，這是需求面問題（高技能職位太少，不是大學生太多）。高技能職位只佔就業30%，新加坡是65%。於是這個國家進口勞動市場的底層、出口頂層，而中間由國家機器佔據——那些本該把結構往價值鏈上游推的人力資本，始終累積不起來。這就是陷阱閉合在自己身上。

The property read: the labour market that can't manufacture high incomes is the same one that can't manufacture broad homeowners. Organic owner-occupier demand stays thin, so the market leans on the skilled minority who stayed, plus foreign and investor money — concentrated exactly where the surviving high-value jobs are.對房市的意思：那個製造不出高收入的勞動市場，也製造不出廣大的有殼族。自住剛需始終偏薄，於是市場靠的是留下來的那一小撮高技能者，加上外資與投資客的錢——而且集中在那些倖存的高值工作所在之處。

A State-Heavy Economy國家之手很重的經濟

Part of the plateau is institutional. Malaysia's six government investment funds (EPF, Khazanah, PNB and peers) manage over RM1.8 trillion — roughly the size of the entire economy — and hold about 26% of the local stock market; government-linked companies are ~40% of Bursa by value. The 1971 affirmative-action goal of 30% Bumiputera corporate equity is still unmet at ~18% and has been re-anchored to 2035. A large, redistributive state stabilises the economy, but it also crowds out private risk-taking and slows deep reform — which is why the income gap is as much a political choice as an economic one.天花板有一部分是制度造成的。馬來西亞六大政府投資基金（EPF、Khazanah、PNB等）管理逾1.8兆令吉，規模約等於整個經濟體，並持有本地股市約26%的市值；政府連結公司（GLC）更佔馬股約四成。1971年留下的土著持股30%平權目標，至今只到約18%，已順延到2035年。一個龐大、講求再分配的國家機器能穩住經濟，卻也排擠了民間冒險的空間、讓深層改革走得很慢。所以這道收入差距，與其說是經濟問題，不如說也是政治選擇。

But the tailwind is fading: the fertility rate has fallen to 1.6, well below replacement; citizen growth is just +0.7%; and Malaysia crosses into an "aged society" around 2044. Malaysia isn't one housing market; it is a dual one, and the dividing line is ethnicity. Seen from the world, Malaysia's chip story is a value-chain story. It runs about 13% of the planet's semiconductor back-end — the assembly, test and packaging at the tail of the line — and is the 6th-largest chip exporter. But that is the bottom of the smile above. The value is captured at the ends: chip design and IP (~85% gross margin, almost all US/UK), the tools (ASML alone makes 100% of EUV machines), and leading-edge fabrication (Taiwan makes 92% of the world's sub-10nm logic) — while back-end runs on ~15% margins and is barely 10% of a finished chip's value. Malaysia is essentially absent from the high-value rungs: no leading-edge fab, no world-class design house, no EDA. And even its 13% is owned by Intel, Infineon and Micron — Malaysia captures the jobs; foreign firms keep the margin and the IP. It does a large share of the world's chip work and keeps a sliver of the value — and the niche is contestable, with Vietnam, India and China underbidding the same low-margin back-end.從世界的角度看，馬來西亞的晶片故事，其實是一個價值鏈故事。它做了全球約13%的半導體後段——產線最末端的封裝、測試——也是全球第6大晶片出口國。但這是上面那條微笑曲線的最低點。價值被兩端吃走：晶片設計與IP（毛利率約85%，幾乎全在美英）、設備（光ASML就壟斷100%的EUV曝光機）、以及先進晶圓製造（台灣包辦全球92%的10奈米以下邏輯晶片）——而後段毛利率只有約15%，僅佔一顆成品晶片價值的一成。馬來西亞在高價值環節幾乎缺席：沒有先進晶圓廠、沒有世界級設計公司、沒有EDA。而且那13%還是Intel、Infineon、Micron的——馬來西亞拿到的是工作機會，外商留住利潤與IP。它做了世界一大塊晶片的工，卻只分到一小片值——而且這個位置可被取代，越南、印度、中國都在低價搶同一塊低毛利後段。

The one thing working in Malaysia's favour is geography as neutrality. As the US–China chip war splits the supply chain, Malaysia is the neutral ground where firms diversifying out of China and Taiwan land — Intel's RM30bn advanced-packaging build in Penang, Infineon's €7bn power-chip fab in Kulim, SPIL/ASE's RM6bn in Batu Kawan. But neutrality is a tightrope. Malaysia leans on US technology and the China market at once (China is its #1 trading partner); since July 2025 it needs a strategic-trade permit to move advanced AI chips, under direct US pressure over transshipment to China; and while its mainstream back-end mix is, for now, spared the US tariffs, a 25% AI-chip tariff (Jan 2026) and a pending mid-2026 review keep the risk live. The "Switzerland of semiconductors" is also standing in the crossfire.唯一站在馬來西亞這邊的，是「中立」這個地理位置。當美中晶片戰把供應鏈一切為二，馬來西亞成了企業從中國、台灣分散時的中立落腳點——Intel在檳城RM300億的先進封裝廠、Infineon在居林70億歐元的功率晶片廠、日月光／矽品在峇都加灣的RM60億。但中立是走鋼索。馬來西亞同時靠美國技術和中國市場（中國是它第一大貿易夥伴）；2025年7月起，要移動先進AI晶片得申請戰略貿易許可，背後是美國對「轉運到中國」的直接施壓；而它主流的後段出口組合雖然目前還躲過美國關稅，2026年1月的25% AI晶片關稅、加上年中將至的複審，都讓風險懸著。這個「半導體界的瑞士」，其實也站在火線上。

Malaysia knows it is stuck — which is what the 2024 National Semiconductor Strategy is for: ≥RM500bn of investment, 60,000 engineers, and a push up into design and advanced packaging, the higher-value rungs the AI era is opening right next to its back-end strength. But the climb is, so far, aspirational. Only ~RM63bn (~13% of the goal) is secured; local firms own ~0% of advanced-packaging IP; the flagship Arm-IP deal is under a corruption probe; and the binding constraint is people — Malaysia graduates ~5,000 engineers a year against a ~50,000 need while losing ~15% of its skilled talent a year, mostly to Singapore (which pays roughly 1.5× more). The brain drain and the value-chain trap are the same wound: you cannot climb a chain whose top keeps emigrating.馬來西亞自己也知道卡住了——這正是2024年國家半導體策略（NSS）的用意：至少RM5,000億投資、6萬名工程師，以及往設計與先進封裝爬升——那是AI時代在它後段強項旁邊打開的高價值階。但這趟攀爬，到目前為止還是「願景」。只籌到約RM630億（約目標的13%）；本地公司握有的先進封裝IP幾近於零；旗艦的Arm IP案還陷入貪腐調查；而真正的瓶頸是人——馬來西亞一年只產出約5,000名工程師，需求卻約5萬，同時每年流失約15%的高技能人才，多半流向新加坡（薪水約高1.5倍）。人才外流和價值鏈陷阱，是同一道傷口：當一條鏈的頂端不斷往外移民，你就爬不上去。

Four Loops That Keep It Stuck四道讓它卡住的迴圈

Put the pieces together and you don't get a list — you get four self-reinforcing loops. The World Bank frames it not as a "vicious cycle" but as a broken virtuous one: workers keep building qualifications, but the economy creates too few good jobs to absorb them, so the loop that should link capability to opportunity never turns.把這些拼起來，你得到的不是一張清單，而是四道自我強化的迴圈。世界銀行不把它叫「惡性循環」，而是一個斷掉的良性循環：勞工不斷累積學歷，經濟卻創造不出足夠的好工作來吸納他們，於是那個本該把「能力」接上「機會」的循環，始終轉不起來。

1 · The cheap-labour loop1 · 廉價勞動迴圈

Cheap migrant labour → weak incentive to automate → low productivity → low wages → still-cheap labour. Note: the official diagnosis; the automation link isn't yet proven for Malaysia.廉價外勞 → 自動化誘因弱 → 生產力低 → 薪資低 → 勞動力仍便宜。註：這是官方診斷；自動化連結在馬國尚未被實證。

2 · The fiscal–structural loop2 · 財政—結構迴圈

Low value-add → low incomes → thin tax base (~12%) → too little R&D/education → stays low value-add. The firmest-evidenced loop — the spine of the trap.低值 → 收入低 → 稅基薄（約12%）→ 研發／教育投入不足 → 繼續低值。證據最扎實的一道，是整個陷阱的脊椎。

3 · The brain-drain loop3 · 人才外流迴圈

Low high-skill wages → skilled emigrate (1.86m) → human-capital shortage → can't move up the chain → high-skill wages stay low. The top leaks out.高技能薪資低 → 人才外流（186萬）→ 人力資本短缺 → 爬不上價值鏈 → 高技能薪資仍低。頂層持續流失。

4 · The redistributive-state loop4 · 再分配國家迴圈

GLC + NEP dominance → crowds out private & foreign capital → weak private dynamism → more reliance on the state. Note: crowding-out is firm-level proven; the full chain is contested.GLC＋新經濟政策主導 → 排擠民間與外資 → 民間活力弱 → 更依賴國家。註：排擠效應有公司層級實證；整條鏈仍有爭議。

Loops 1 and 2 are the economic engine; loop 3 is the human-capital leak; loop 4 is the political-economy lock that holds it all in place. Together: the country imports the bottom of the labour market, exports the top, and lets the state occupy the middle — and the structure that produces a thin treasury keeps reproducing itself.迴圈1與2是經濟引擎；迴圈3是人力資本的漏洞；迴圈4是把一切固定住的政治經濟之鎖。合起來：這個國家進口勞動市場的底層、出口頂層、讓國家機器佔據中間——而那個製造出薄弱國庫的結構，不斷地自我複製。

The economy decides how big the pie is. Politics decides who is allowed to eat — and in Malaysia that decision was taken in 1969, and is still being enforced.經濟決定這塊餅有多大，政治決定誰有資格吃——而在馬來西亞，這個決定 1969 年就下了，至今仍在執行。

The 1969 Contract1969 年的那份合約

How a race riot became a housing policy — the NEP as the country's founding bargain一場種族衝突，如何變成一套房市政策 —— 新經濟政策，這個國家的立國契約

On 13 May 1969, days after an election that shook Malay political primacy, Kuala Lumpur burned. Out of the violence came a settlement that still governs the country: the New Economic Policy of 1971. Its terms were simple and have never really lapsed — Malay political leadership and state-led redistribution, in exchange for racial peace. Property became the single largest instrument of that bargain. Bumiputera quotas and discounts, Malay Reserve Land and government-linked developers are not features bolted onto the housing market; they are the NEP written into the title deed. (Part 2 covered the mechanism — here we trace the politics behind it.)1969 年 5 月 13 日，一場撼動馬來人政治主導地位的大選過後幾天，吉隆坡陷入火海。從那場流血裡走出來的，是一份至今仍統治著這個國家的協議：1971 年的新經濟政策（NEP）。它的條款很簡單，而且從未真正失效——馬來人掌握政治領導、國家主導再分配，換取族群之間的和平。房地產，成了這份契約最主要的載體。土著配額與折扣、馬來保留地、政府連結發展商，都不是後來才栓上房市的零件，而是把 NEP 直接寫進了地契。（機制在第二篇講過，這裡追的是它背後的政治。）

The bargain came with a number — 30% Bumiputera corporate equity by 1990. It was never hit. Official measures put it at 18.4% in 2020 and around 17–18% today; on a strict listed-company basis it is closer to 7% (ISEAS / Lee Hwok Aun). So the deadline keeps moving. The target has now been re-anchored to 2035 under the PuTERA35 blueprint, which layers on a fresh goal — 70% of high-skilled jobs going to Bumiputera. Fifty-five years on, the contract still has at least another decade to run.這份契約帶著一個數字——1990 年前達成土著企業股權 30%。它從來沒達標。官方口徑是 2020 年 18.4%、目前約 17–18%；若嚴格只看上市公司，更接近 7%（ISEAS／Lee Hwok Aun）。於是期限一延再延。目標如今被重新錨定到 2035 年、寫進 PuTERA35 藍圖，還疊上一個新目標——高技能職位的 70% 要給土著。五十五年過去，這份合約至少還要再跑十年。

The Geometry of an Unequal Vote一票不等值的選舉幾何

Voters per federal seat — why the rulebook tilts toward the countryside每個國會席位的選民數 —— 為什麼規則向鄉區傾斜

Malaysia's constitution allows rural seats to carry far more weight than urban ones, and the gap has become extreme. Igan, a rural seat in Sarawak, has 28,290 voters; Bangi, on Selangor's urban fringe, has more than 303,000. One vote in Igan therefore weighs about 10.5 votes in Bangi. Undi18 and automatic registration only sharpened the imbalance: the electoral roll expanded by roughly a third to 21 million, and the number of seats holding over 100,000 voters jumped from 35 to 85 (eight now exceed 200,000). The new voters piled up in the cities — deepening the tilt, not correcting it.馬來西亞的憲法，默許鄉區席位承載遠比都市席位更重的分量，而這道落差已經走到極端。砂拉越的鄉區席位伊干（Igan），只有 28,290 名選民；坐落在雪蘭莪都市邊緣的班基（Bangi），選民卻超過 303,000。換算下來，伊干的一票，約等於班基的 10.5 票。Undi18 與自動登記，只讓失衡更尖銳：選民冊擴大約三分之一、來到 2,100 萬人，選民超過 10 萬的席位從 35 席暴增到 85 席（其中 8 席已超過 20 萬）。而新增的選民，全堆在城市——把這道傾斜加深，而不是修正。

The weight of one vote — voters per federal seat, Igan vs Bangi (EC; Fulcrum). A vote in Igan is worth ~10.5 in Bangi.一票的重量 —— 每個國會席位的選民數，伊干 對 班基（選委會；Fulcrum）。伊干的一票，約值班基的 10.5 票。

The chain runs straight from here. Rural, majority-Malay seats are structurally over-weighted → any workable governing coalition must first win the countryside → the NEP-and-subsidy structure becomes electorally untouchable → and so the urban middle class and foreign buyers end up as the system's payers, not the people it is built to serve. (Sources: Fulcrum; Penang Institute; Malaysiakini Kini News Lab.)這條鏈，從這裡一路直下。鄉區、馬來人佔多數的席位在結構上被超額加權 → 任何能執政的聯盟都必須先贏下鄉區 → NEP 與補貼結構在選舉上變得動不得 → 於是都市中產與外國買家，最後成了這套系統的付費方，而不是它服務的對象。（來源：Fulcrum、Penang Institute、Malaysiakini Kini News Lab）

The property read: "tax cities and foreigners first" is not a coincidence of any one budget. It falls straight out of the geometry of the vote — the seats that decide who holds power are the seats that pay the least property tax.對房市的意思：「加稅先加城市與外國人」，不是哪一份預算案的偶然，而是選票幾何學算出來的必然——決定政權的那些席位，恰好是繳房產稅最少的那些。

The Three-Way Auction for the Malay Vote馬來票的三方拍賣

From 61 years of one-party rule to a hung parliament — and what the 2026 Johor landslide really means從 61 年一黨獨大，到懸峙國會 —— 以及 2026 年柔佛大勝，真正的意思

For 61 years one coalition governed unbroken (Barisan Nasional, 1957–2018). Then the dam broke: the 2018 upset, the 2020 "Sheraton Move" backroom coup, a hung parliament and an Islamist "green wave" in 2022, and — most recently — the July 2026 Johor state election, where the pendulum swung hard back. Barisan, contesting alone, took 48 of 56 seats (UMNO 36 of 37); Perikatan Nasional was wiped out at zero; Pakatan Harapan managed just eight.有 61 年，這個國家由單一聯盟不間斷地執政（國民陣線，1957–2018）。然後堤壩潰了：2018 年的變天、2020 年「喜來登政變」的後門奪權、2022 年的懸峙國會與伊斯蘭「綠潮」，以及最近一次——2026 年 7 月的柔佛州選，鐘擺狠狠盪了回來。單獨出戰的國陣，拿下 56 席中的 48 席（巫統 37 席中拿 36 席）；國盟（PN）全軍覆沒、掛零；希盟（PH）只拿到 8 席。

Read structurally, the Malay vote is now split three ways — UMNO, PAS and Bersatu — and a three-way split means competitive outbidding: each party racing to prove it protects Malays hardest. That ratchet only ever pushes policy deeper into the NEP, never back out. Meanwhile the non-Malay vote is locked to PH/DAP with nowhere else to go, so it is taken for granted. Johor cuts both ways: the collapse of the green wave genuinely lowers the risk of harder Islamisation — but the winner was a solo UMNO, so the live question before GE16 is no longer left-versus-right; it is the balance of power inside the ruling coalition itself.從結構讀，馬來票如今三分——巫統、伊斯蘭黨、土團——而三方分裂意味著競價式喊價：每一方都在搶著證明自己最保護馬來人。這個棘輪，只會把政策往 NEP 更深處推，不會往回。與此同時，非馬來票被鎖在希盟／行動黨，無處可去，於是被視為理所當然。柔佛這一役是雙面的：綠潮崩盤，確實降低了更激進伊斯蘭化的風險——但贏家是單飛的巫統，所以 GE16 之前真正的問題，已經不是「左右之爭」，而是執政聯盟內部的權力平衡。

The property read: whoever ends up governing, the policy function pointed at the "urban asset class" barely changes. What changes is only who gets to collect.對房市的意思：無論最後由誰執政，對準「城市資產階級」的那條政策函數幾乎不變，變的只是換誰來收。

Three Pens Write the Rules三支筆共寫規則

Land is a state matter — and the palace is not just ceremonial土地是州權事項 —— 而王室，並不只是儀式性的

Under the Federal Constitution, land is a state matter — and that single line fractures the market into a dozen rulebooks. Foreign-purchase thresholds, state consent letters, and the size of the Bumiputera quota and discount are all set state by state. Penang gates island landed homes at around RM3 million; Kuala Lumpur sets its foreign floor near RM1 million; Johor writes its own concessions inside the special economic zone. Buying "Malaysian property" really means buying into whichever state's rulebook you happen to be standing in.依《聯邦憲法》，土地是州權事項——而光是這一條，就把市場裂成十來套規則。外國人購屋門檻、州同意書、土著配額與折扣的比例，全部一州一套。檳城把島上的有地住宅門檻設在約 RM300 萬；吉隆坡的外國人門檻定在約 RM100 萬；柔佛則在經濟特區裡自寫優惠。買「馬來西亞房產」，其實是買進你當下所站的那一州的規則本。

The second pen is the palace, and in Johor it is not ceremonial. The state's ruler, Sultan Ibrahim — Malaysia's current King (Agong, 2024–2028) — holds, through Esplanade Danga 88 (99.9% owned), an effective 21.9% stake in CGPV, the master developer of Forest City (as reported by The Edge). Forest City itself went from unsellable orphan to anointed flagship: designated a Special Financial Zone in 2024, with nine family offices approved by June 2026. The policy rescue and the royal commercial interest point the same way. And the crown's constitutional role is widening more broadly — the King personally arbitrated the formation of government after both the 2020 Sheraton Move and the 2022 hung parliament.第二支筆是王室，而在柔佛，它一點都不儀式性。柔佛蘇丹依布拉欣——馬來西亞現任國家元首（2024–2028）——透過持股 99.9% 的 Esplanade Danga 88，在森林城市總發展商 CGPV 握有實質 21.9% 股權（據 The Edge 報導）。而森林城市本身，從賣不動的棄兒，變成被欽點的旗艦：2024 年獲劃為特別金融區（SFZ），到 2026 年 6 月已批出 9 家家族辦公室。政策救援與王室的商業利益，指向同一個方向。而王室的憲政角色，也在更廣的層面擴張——2020 年喜來登政變與 2022 年懸峙國會之後，兩度都由國家元首親自裁決組閣。

The property read: every unit you buy has its rules co-written by three pens — federal finance (stamp duty, RPGT, income tax), the state government (thresholds, quotas, consent letters), and the palace (the invisible backing behind land and the biggest deals). On the contract, you only ever get to read the first signature.對房市的意思：你買的每一戶，規則都由三支筆共寫——聯邦財政（印花稅、RPGT、所得稅）、州政府（門檻、配額、同意書）、以及王室（土地與最大宗交易背後那道隱形背書）。而這份合約上，你只讀得到第一個簽名。

A Record of Reversals政策反覆的實績清單

What actually happened to the last decade's flagship policies過去十年那些旗艦政策，後來到底怎麼了

The clearest evidence for how much of this is contingent is the track record itself. Six flagship policies, six reversals:要衡量這一切有多少是「可變的」，最硬的證據就是紀錄本身。六個旗艦政策，六次反覆：

  • GST: introduced 2015 → zero-rated and abolished within weeks of the 2018 election (one of the few times any country has scrapped a working VAT) → back under debate for 2026.GST：2015 年開徵 → 2018 年大選後幾週內歸零、廢除（全球極少數把運作中的消費稅整個廢掉的案例）→ 2026 年重回辯論桌。
  • KL–Singapore HSR: signed 2016 → cancelled in 2021 (with compensation paid to Singapore) → revived in talks from 2024.新隆高鐵（HSR）：2016 年簽約 → 2021 年取消（並賠償新加坡）→ 2024 年起重啟談判。

  • Foreign stamp duty: a single 4% rate legislated in 2024 → doubled to 8% for 2026. Two years, twice the rate.外國人印花稅：2024 年立單一 4% 稅率 → 2026 年加倍到 8%。兩年，翻一倍。
  • REIT withholding tax: the 10% preferential rate sunsets in 2026 (reverting to resident progressive rates / 30% for non-residents).REIT 扣繳稅：10% 優惠稅率 2026 年落日（回到居民累進稅率／非居民 30%）。
  • Forest City: political orphan → rescued as a Special Financial Zone in 2024.森林城市：政治棄兒 → 2024 年以特別金融區之名獲救。

Three regularities fall out of the list. The half-life of a policy is about one political cycle. Anything that touches foreigners or the consumption tax will be reversed. And anything that touches the NEP core never moves at all.從這張清單，掉出三條規律。政策的半衰期，約等於一個政治週期。凡是碰到外國人、或碰到消費稅的，必然反覆。而凡是碰到 NEP 核心的，則永遠不動。

The property read: a ten-year hold will span two or three governments — which means two or three rulebooks. "The rules won't change" is the single biggest unpriced assumption in buying KL. And the only rules guaranteed not to change — the quotas and the reserved land — are precisely the ones holding prices down.對房市的意思：一個十年持有期，會跨過兩到三屆政府——也就是兩到三套規則。「規則不會變」，是買進吉隆坡最大的一個未定價假設。而唯一保證不會變的規則（配額與保留地），恰好就是壓著房價的那些。

Follow the money and the two-sidedness resolves. On the federal side, the 2026 budget pencils in RM10.3 billion from stamp duty and RM2.4 billion from RPGT (up from RM10.1bn and RM2.3bn in 2025), and the line keeps being revised upward. A treasury that collects only ~12% of GDP in tax, has cut the Petronas dividend to a nine-year-low RM20 billion, and watched the RON95 fuel subsidy spike to RM3.2 billion in a single month cannot afford to let this stream shrink. The states are even more exposed: quit rent and land-conversion premiums are among the few own-source revenues a state government controls, so no state can allow land values to crater.跟著錢走，這個「兩面性」就解開了。聯邦這一邊，2026 年預算把印花稅收入編列在 RM103 億、RPGT 編在 RM24 億（比 2025 年的 RM101 億與 RM23 億再高），而且這條線年年上修。一個稅收只佔 GDP 約 12% 的國庫，剛把國油股息砍到九年低點 RM200 億、又眼看 RON95 燃油補貼單月暴增到 RM32 億，它付不起讓這條收入線縮水的代價。州政府暴露得更深：地稅（quit rent）與土地轉換 premium，是州政府少數能自主掌握的財源，所以沒有哪個州敢讓地價崩。

So the mechanism splits by level. When the federation is short of cash, it turns the one valve that costs no votes — the foreigner. When a state is short, it leans on land appreciation and conversion premiums — which is why it will never let land get cheap. Reintroducing the GST is the only switch that could loosen the whole ratchet, which is exactly why it is simultaneously a fiscal story and a property story.於是機制按層級一分為二。當聯邦缺錢，它就去擰那一個不流失選票的閥門——外國人。當州缺錢，它就靠土地增值與轉換 premium——所以它永遠不會讓地變便宜。重啟 GST，是唯一能鬆開整個棘輪的開關，這也正是為什麼它同時是一個財政議題，也是一個房市議題。

The property read: the state is structurally built to protect price and protect volume while squeezing the foreigner. The downside is politically protected; the upside is politically locked.對房市的意思：這個國家在結構上，就是要「保價、保量、擠外國人」。下檔有政治保護，上檔被政治鎖死。

The Verdict — Three Tradeable Political Laws裁決：三條可以拿來交易的政治規律

Turning all of the above into rules you can actually position around把上面這一切，收斂成三條你真的能拿來布局的規則

  • 1 · Rule risk has a direction. The same fiscal pressure exits two opposite doors: one-way tightening on foreigners (stamp duty 4%→8%, REIT withholding 10%→30%, MM2H bound to a compulsory property purchase) and one-way loosening for local first-home buyers (the stamp-duty exemption extended again and again, the SJKP loan guarantee doubled). Read the buyer's passport and you already know which way the next change points.1 · 規則風險有方向。同一股財政壓力，從兩個相反的門走出去：對外國人單向收緊（印花稅 4%→8%、REIT 扣繳 10%→30%、MM2H 綁上強制購房），對本國首購族單向放鬆（印花稅豁免一延再延、SJKP 貸款擔保翻倍）。看一眼買家的護照，你就已經知道下一次改動會朝哪個方向。
  • 2 · Rule risk has layers. The NEP core — quotas, reserved land, GLCs — is constitutional-grade and immovable; consumption taxes (GST/SST) are electoral-grade and reverse with the cycle; foreigner clauses are fiscal-grade and swing with the deficit. The longer your hold, the more you are forced to bet only on the immovable layer — which is the same as accepting the capped equilibrium it produces.2 · 規則風險有分層。NEP 核心——配額、保留地、GLC——是憲法級的、動不了；消費稅（GST／SST）是選舉級的、隨週期反覆；外國人條款是財政級的、隨赤字擺盪。你持有得越久，就越只能押在那個動不了的層——而這等於接受它所產生的、被封頂的均衡。
  • 3 · The political cycle is a property clock. The twelve months before an election are the sweetener window (exemptions, infrastructure announcements, subsidies); the twelve-to-eighteen months after are the tax window (the foreigner-tax template born after GST was scrapped in 2018; the 4% of the 2022 post-election period doubling to 8% by Budget 2026). With GE16 most likely late-2026 to early-2027, the pre-election sweeteners for buyers are already on their way — but a foreigner should treat the first post-election Budget as the expiry date on rule risk.3 · 政治週期，就是一座房產時鐘。大選前 12 個月是甜頭窗口（豁免、基建宣布、補貼）；選後 12 到 18 個月是加稅窗口（2018 年廢 GST 後長出的外國人稅祖型；2022 年選後那個 4%，到 2026 年預算翻倍成 8%）。GE16 大概率落在 2026 年底到 2027 年初，選前給買家的甜頭已經在路上——但外國人應該把選後第一份預算案，當成規則風險的到期日。

One honest qualification to end on. The fiscal repair is real — the deficit is being walked from 5.5% toward 3.5–3.8%, diesel and RON95 subsidies are being targeted, and the widened SST is set to raise around RM25.5 billion a year. But what is being repaired is the fiscal position, not the structural lock. So the "low-volatility, low-mean" equilibrium does not change; what changes is that the tail risk — a ratings downgrade, a ringgit crisis — is receding. Which leaves one sentence: politics will not kill this market; it only decides the distribution. The corridors get the growth, the index does not; locals get in, foreigners pay a toll at the gate — and that sentence is simply the 1969 contract, re-quoted for property in 2026.最後，補一句誠實的話。財政修復是真的在走——赤字正從 5.5% 往 3.5–3.8% 收、柴油與 RON95 補貼正在目標化、擴圍後的 SST 預計一年多收約 RM255 億。但被修的是財政部位，不是那把結構鎖。所以「低波動、低均值」的均衡不會變，變的是尾部風險——評級遭降、令吉危機——正在退潮。於是只剩一句話：政治不會殺死這個市場，它只決定分配。走廊分得到成長，大盤分不到；本國人進得了場，外國人在門口先繳一道過路費——而這句話，不過就是 1969 年那份合約，在 2026 年替房市重新報出的價。

Six pairwise products — mechanisms no single chapter can see六組兩兩相乘 —— 任何單一章節都看不到的機制

1 · Demography × Income — the dividend, discounted by half1 · 人口 × 收入 —— 被折現一半的紅利

Demography supplies the volume; the income structure cuts the price. A ~9m Gen Z cohort is entering the market — but 88.7% of fresh graduates land in semi/low-skill jobs, and the mismatched earn ~49% less. So the same cohort that guarantees demand floods the affordable segment while everything above RM500k stays as thin as before. The dividend isn't cancelled — it is discounted by half before it ever reaches the market.人口給的是「量」，收入結構砍的是「價」。約 900 萬的 Z 世代正在進場——但 88.7% 的新鮮人進了中低技能職位、學用錯配者少賺約 49%。於是同一批保證了需求的人，把可負擔段的需求灌爆，RM50 萬以上卻依舊薄。紅利沒有被取消——它是在抵達市場之前，先被薪資結構折現了一半。

2 · Demography × Politics — renewing the 1969 contract2 · 人口 × 政治 —— 替 1969 合約續約

The Bumiputera share runs 70.5% → 79.4% by 2060, on a stark fertility gap (Malay 1.9 vs Chinese 0.9). The quota market therefore expands automatically with the population, the dual market deepens rather than fades, and the rural vote geometry hardens with it. 3 · Income × Politics — the key isn't economic3 · 收入 × 政治 —— 鑰匙不在經濟這邊

Part 2's four loops are policy choices, and loop 4 — the political-economy lock — is what holds loops 1–3 in place. Breaking the income ceiling means dismantling the NEP core, and Part 3 showed why that is electorally impossible. So the precondition for any "income breakout" scenario is not economic — it is political. That is the single most important sentence in this model.第二篇的四道迴圈是政策選擇，而第四道——政治經濟之鎖——正是把前三道固定住的那道。要打破收入天花板，就得拆 NEP 核心，而第三篇已經講明那在選舉上不可能。所以「收入突破劇本」的先決條件不是經濟性的，是政治性的。這是整個模型裡最重要的一句話。

4 · Fiscal × Demography — 8% is a signpost, not a terminus4 · 財政 × 人口 —— 8% 是路標，不是終點

Around 2044 Malaysia turns "aged", lifting health and retirement spending structurally — against a treasury still collecting only ~12% of GDP. 5 · Supply × Politics — supply is itself a political tool5 · 供給 × 政治 —— 供給本身就是政治工具

"Market supply and demand" explains perhaps half the volume — the other half is allocated.在這個市場，供給不是市場的回應，而是一支政策槓桿。配額決定誰買得到；NC 審批開關決定吉隆坡服務式公寓的命運；13MP 的百萬戶，壓住 RM50 萬以下段位的天花板。「市場化供需」在這裡大概只解釋一半的量——另一半是被分配出來的。

The property read: any single chapter, read alone, misleads. Demography alone says buy everything; income alone says buy nothing; politics alone says stay away. The property takeaway對房產的結論

The key move is to read the property market as the output of the system, not an accident of the cycle. Because the traps are self-reinforcing, their property signature is an equilibrium: growth doesn't fully reach wages → wages are unevenly distributed → after necessities and debt, almost nothing is left to save → so effective buying power is a minority of households, concentrated by class, ethnicity and geography, sitting on a quota-divided dual market and a demographic engine on a timer. The near-term easing is real, but it mostly lifts those already qualified. That is why Malaysian property rewards picking the right location, segment and timing — not buying the national index, which is structurally capped until one of the loops actually breaks.關鍵的一步，是把房市讀成這套系統的產物，而不是景氣的意外。正因為這些陷阱會自我強化，它們在房市上的印記是一種「均衡」：成長到不了薪水 → 薪水分配不均 → 扣掉必需品與債務後幾乎存不下來 → 所以真正的購買力只在少數家庭手上，被階層、族群與地理三重集中，又坐在一個被配額切成兩半的雙軌市場、和一具裝了計時器的人口引擎上。短期降息是真的，但它主要抬升本來就過得了關的人。這就是為什麼馬來西亞房產獎勵的是選對地點、產品與時機，而不是買進「全國大盤」——在四道迴圈真正斷掉其中一道之前，大盤在結構上是被封頂的。

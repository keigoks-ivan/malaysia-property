The Regime Shift當前格局轉變
The 2025 Easing Pivot2025年的降息轉向

After holding the Overnight Policy Rate at 3.00% for nearly three years, Bank Negara delivered its first cut in five years in July 2025, trimming the OPR by 25bp to 2.75%. This was an insurance cut, not a distress signal: growth was still running above 5%, inflation sat comfortably below 2%, and the move was framed as pre-emptive support against external headwinds — chiefly the 2025 US tariff shock and a softening export pulse. BNM has held at 2.75% through May 2026 and signalled a neutral stance.

So the cycle has turned a corner. Borrowing costs are easing into a market where the headline house-price index is already at a record, yet the picture beneath it is split: the completed-unit overhang has swung back up +31.6%, and Kuala Lumpur's average transacted price is actually falling (−4.3% YoY). Cheaper money is meeting a supply problem, not a shortage. The structural demand story — Johor's SEZ, the RTS Link, the data-centre build-out — is real, but it is concentrated, and policy has simultaneously doubled the foreign-buyer stamp duty to 8%.

國行（BNM）把隔夜政策利率（OPR）按在3.00%將近三年後，2025年7月終於出手，五年來首度降息25個基點至2.75%。這一刀是預防性的保險式降息，不是危機訊號：當時經濟增長仍站穩5%以上，通膨舒服地壓在2%以下，國行也明言此舉旨在提前防範外部逆風，主要針對2025年美國關稅衝擊與走弱的出口動能。直到2026年5月，OPR都維持在2.75%，立場轉為中性。

週期由此轉了個彎。資金成本開始走低，房價總指數雖已創歷史新高，底下卻是兩極分化：完工滯銷量（overhang）回升31.6%，吉隆坡平均成交價反而下跌（年減4.3%）。便宜的錢遇上的是供給過剩，不是供給短缺。柔佛特區、新柔捷運（RTS Link）、數據中心這條結構性需求主線確實存在，但高度集中；同一時間，政策又把外國買家印花稅一口氣調高到8%。

Monetary Cycle × House Prices貨幣週期 × 房價
What to watch: House prices lag policy by 12–18 months. The 2020–21 OPR floor (1.75%) plus the M2 surge pre-loaded the 2022–24 recovery; the July-2025 cut to 2.75% now feeds into 2026–27. But note the gap between the OPR (2.75%) and the average lending rate (4.56%) — the spread stays wide, so the transmission to mortgage affordability is partial.
重點觀察：房價通常落後政策約12至18個月。2020至2021年OPR探底（1.75%）疊加M2激增，提前墊高了2022至2024年那波回升；2025年7月降到2.75%，效果要等2026至2027年才會浮現。但要留意，OPR（2.75%）與平均貸款利率（4.56%）的利差仍偏寬，傳導到實際房貸可負擔性只到位了一部分。

Real Economy × House Prices實體經濟 × 房價
What to watch: Growth is resilient (Q1 2026 GDP +5.4%, jobless rate ~3.0%, a multi-year low), which is the real floor under prices. But housing-loan growth has slowed to ~6% — well below the 2010–13 boom (14%) — so this is income-and-jobs support, not a credit-fuelled melt-up. Watch the 2026 export normalisation as 2025's tariff front-loading unwinds.
重點觀察：經濟韌性很強（2026 Q1 GDP +5.4%、失業率約3.0%、多年新低），這才是房價的真正底盤。但房貸成長已放緩到約6%，遠低於2010至2013年榮景的14%，所以這是就業與收入在撐盤，不是信貸狂熱。要盯著2026年出口正常化：2025年因關稅而提前出貨的動能將會回吐。

The Affordability Ceiling — Household Leverage可負擔性的天花板：家庭槓桿
Why it caps the cycle: Household debt hit 93.2% of GDP in the 2020 COVID slump (denominator effect) and has since normalised to ~84.8% — but that is still high by regional standards, and housing loans are ~61% of it. With the median debt-service ratio on new loans at 41%, the marginal buyer is stretched. This is the structural reason Malaysian prices grind rather than spike, even when rates fall. The offset: household financial assets are ~2.1× total debt and impaired-loan ratios sit near 1.1%, so the leverage is heavy but well-collateralised.
為何它封住週期上緣：家庭債務在2020年COVID衰退時衝到GDP的93.2%（分母效應），之後回落到約84.8%，但以區域標準仍偏高，其中房貸約佔六成。新批貸款的中位數償債比（DSR）達41%，邊際買家已被拉得很緊。這正是馬來西亞房價即使降息也多是磨升、難以暴衝的結構性原因。另一面是：家庭金融資產約為總負債的2.1倍，不良貸款比率僅約1.1%，槓桿雖重但抵押充足。

The Supply Warning — Completed Overhang供給警訊：完工滯銷
The standout negative: Unsold completed homes were being worked down steadily to a 2024 low of 23,149 units (RM13.9bn) — then jumped +31.6% to 30,471 units (RM17.7bn) in 2025. Roughly 47% is condos/apartments, and ~92% of Klang Valley overhang is high-rise. Separately, the serviced-apartment glut (counted as commercial, not in these bars) is concentrated in Johor (~9,000 units). The read: demand is real but selective — affordable landed clears, while high-rise and pricey stock sticks.
數據裡最刺眼的負面：完工未售住宅原本一路去化，到2024年降到23,149戶（RM139億）的低點，2025年卻暴增31.6%，回到30,471戶（RM177億）。其中約47%是公寓/共管式住宅，巴生谷的滯銷更有約92%是高層產品。另外，服務式公寓（歸類為商業、不含在本圖）的庫存集中在柔佛，約9,000戶。解讀是：需求真實但挑剔，可負擔的有地住宅去得動，高層與高價產品則卡住。

The KL paradox: the national index is at a record, but KL's average transacted price is falling — a mix shift toward affordable stock plus genuine high-rise oversupply at the top end. Yet the floor is firm: rents are rising +6.1%, yields near 4.9% beat the deposit rate, and the digital-economy build-out (Microsoft's "Malaysia West" cloud region, AWS Cyberjaya) is adding skilled jobs across Greater KL. Knight Frank still projects +3–5% a year through 2028 for prime pockets (KLCC, Bangsar, Mont Kiara) on thin new supply. The MRT3 Circle Line is the next big TOD catalyst, but with construction now slipping to 2027 and operation to ~2028+, that uplift is a medium-term, not a today, story. Net: a stock-pickers' market, not a beta market.

吉隆坡的矛盾：全國指數創高，吉隆坡的平均成交價卻在跌。一部分是成交結構往可負擔產品傾斜，一部分是高端高層真的供過於求。但底盤很穩：租金年增6.1%、回報率近4.9%高於定存，數位經濟落地（微軟「Malaysia West」雲端區、AWS賽城）正為大吉隆坡增加高技能就業。萊坊（Knight Frank）仍預估精華地段（KLCC、孟沙、滿家樂）在新供給稀缺下到2028年每年漲3%至5%。MRT3環線是下一個TOD大催化劑，但動工已順延到2027年、通車約在2028年後，這份增值屬於中期題材，不是當下行情。一句話：這是選股的市場，不是買大盤的市場。

Structural Demand — Where the Money Is Going結構性需求：資金往哪裡去
The map matters: Malaysia drew a record RM426.7bn of approved investment in 2025 (+11%), and it is highly concentrated. Johor alone took RM110bn — the most of any state — on the back of the Johor-Singapore SEZ and the data-centre cluster, with Selangor and KL next. The three property markets in scope capture roughly 60% of national approved investment. This is the structural counterweight to the overhang.
地圖很關鍵：馬來西亞2025年核准投資創下RM4,267億新高（+11%），而且高度集中。光柔佛就拿下RM1,100億，居各州之冠，靠的是柔新特區（JS-SEZ）與數據中心群聚；其次是雪蘭莪與吉隆坡。本站關注的三個市場合計約佔全國核准投資的六成。這是對沖滯銷壓力的結構性力量。

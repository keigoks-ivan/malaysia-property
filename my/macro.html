Bottom line一句話結論 2025 was the pivot year: money got cheaper, growth held (GDP +5.4%) and the price index hit a record — yet the completed-home overhang swung back up +32% and Kuala Lumpur's average price actually fell. Cheaper money is meeting an oversupply, not a shortage. The structural demand — Johor's SEZ, data centres, record FDI — is real, but it is concentrated in Johor. 2025年是轉向年：錢變便宜、經濟還穩（GDP +5.4%）、房價指數創高，但完工滯銷回升32%，吉隆坡均價反而下跌。便宜的錢遇到的是供給過剩，不是短缺。結構性需求（柔新特區、數據中心、創紀錄FDI）真實，卻高度集中在柔佛。

Rates × House Prices利率週期 × 房價

OPR & average lending rate vs the house-price index, 2008–2026OPR 與平均貸款利率 對 房價指數，2008–2026

  • Jul 2025: BNM's first cut in five years, to 2.75% — a pre-emptive insurance move against the US-tariff shock, not a recession signal.2025年7月：國行五年來首度降息至2.75%，是預防性的保險降息（防美國關稅），不是衰退訊號。
  • House prices lag rates by 12–18 months, so this cut mainly feeds into 2026–2027.房價落後利率約12到18個月，這刀主要反映在2026到2027年。
  • The gap between the OPR (2.75%) and the average mortgage rate (4.56%) stays wide — only part of the cut reaches buyers.OPR（2.75%）與平均房貸利率（4.56%）的利差仍寬，降息紅利只有一部分傳到買家。
  • The index hit a record in 2025 (avg 233, Q4 236.7) — headline strong, but the market is split underneath.房價指數2025年創高（年均233、Q4達236.7），總體強，但底下分化。

Completed Overhang — the Supply Warning完工滯銷 · 供給警訊

Unsold completed residential — units & RM value, 2019–2025完工未售住宅 · 戶數與金額，2019–2025

  • Unsold completed homes jumped +31.6% in 2025 to 30,471 units (RM17.7bn) — the clearest negative in the data.完工未售2025年暴增31.6%至30,471戶（RM177億），是數據裡最明確的警訊。
  • ~47% are condos; ~92% of Klang Valley overhang is high-rise; Johor holds ~9,000 serviced-apartment units on top.約47%是公寓；巴生谷滯銷九成是高層；柔佛另有約9,000戶服務式公寓庫存。
  • Kuala Lumpur's average price actually fell −4.3% YoY — the national soft spot, oversupplied at the top end.吉隆坡均價反而年減4.3%，是全國最弱、高端供過於求。
  • Demand is selective: affordable landed homes clear, while high-rise and pricey stock sticks.需求很挑：可負擔的有地住宅賣得動，高層與高價產品卡著。

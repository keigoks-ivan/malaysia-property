Bottom line一句話結論 2025 was the pivot year. After three years on hold, BNM cut the OPR to 2.75% — a pre-emptive insurance move, with growth still above 5% and inflation under 2%. But the easing lands in a market where household leverage is near its ceiling (84.8% of GDP) and Kuala Lumpur's average price is actually falling. Cheaper money meets demand that is structurally concentrated — Johor's SEZ, data centres and record FDI — rather than broad-based. 2025年是轉向年。國行按兵三年後把OPR降到2.75%，屬預防性的保險降息，當時經濟仍站穩5%以上、通膨壓在2%以下。但這波寬鬆，落在一個家庭槓桿逼近上限（佔GDP 84.8%）、吉隆坡均價反而下跌的市場。便宜的錢遇到的需求高度集中（柔新特區、數據中心、創紀錄FDI），不是全面普漲。

Part 2 · The Structural Economy經濟結構篇 · 為什麼這影響房價

Somewhere on the road from commodities to chips, Malaysia's economy got stuck — and a stuck income story is, quietly, a capped house-price story.從商品走到晶片這條路，馬來西亞的經濟走到一半卡住了；而收入卡住，房價其實也就有了一道隱形的天花板。

Malaysia's wealth has always come out of the ground. In the 1800s, rubber and tin pulled different peoples onto the peninsula and drew today's demographic and political map; later, palm oil and petroleum filled the treasury and bred a dependence on commodities. This century the bet is electronics and semiconductors. The trouble is that, somewhere on that road, the economy stalled.馬來西亞的財富，一直是從土地裡長出來的。十九世紀的橡膠與錫，把不同族群帶上這片半島，也鋪下了今天的人口與政治格局；後來是棕油與石油，撐起國庫，也養出對大宗商品的依賴。進入這個世紀，國家把賭注壓在電子與半導體上。問題是，從商品到晶片的這條路，它走到一半卡住了。

Stuck Below the High-Income Line卡在高收入門檻前

GDP per capita, nominal US$, 2024 (World Bank)人均GDP（名目美元），2024（世界銀行）

Put plainly: Malaysia reached upper-middle income in 1996; nearly thirty years on, GDP per capita is about US$11,900 — below China, a third of South Korea, a sixth of Singapore, and roughly US$2,000 short of the World Bank's high-income line (official target: 2030). It has the skeleton of an upper-middle economy but hasn't grown the high-income muscle. The points below are why it keeps falling short — and why that matters for property.話說白一點：馬來西亞1996年就跨進中高收入，將近三十年了，人均GDP約1.19萬美元，還是卡在中國之下、只有南韓的三分之一、新加坡的六分之一，離世界銀行的高收入門檻還差約兩千美元（官方目標2030年達標）。它有一具中高收入國家的骨架，卻一直長不出高收入國家的肌肉。底下這幾條，才是它一直跨不過去的真正原因，也是它跟房市的關係所在。

Why Growth Keeps Falling Short為什麼一直跨不過去

Five structural mechanics behind the plateau — mostly policy choices, not the cycle卡住的五個結構性原因，多半是政策選擇，不是景氣

  • Wages lag output. Labour's share of GDP is just 33.6% (2024), far below the ~50% of advanced economies and short of the government's own (missed) 40% target. The median citizen salary was only RM2,793/month in 2024. Output grows; pay doesn't keep up — and that sits directly on mortgage affordability.薪資跟不上產出。勞動報酬只佔GDP 33.6%（2024），遠低於先進國的約五成，連政府自訂、已跳票的40%目標都沒到。2024年公民月薪中位數才RM2,793。產出長大了，工資卻沒同步，這直接壓住房貸可負擔性。
  • Locked into the low-value end. In its one world-class industry, Malaysia owns chip back-end assembly & test (~13% globally) but barely any design or fabrication; semiconductors are ~65% of E&E exports, much of it thin-margin contract work for foreign firms.卡在價值鏈低端。在它唯一世界級的產業裡，馬來西亞拿的是晶片後段封測（全球約13%），設計與晶圓製造幾乎缺席；半導體佔電子電機出口約65%，做的多是替外商代工、利潤很薄。
  • Cheap foreign labour delays the upgrade. Migrant workers are ~15% of the documented workforce (more counting the undocumented), holding the wage floor down and blunting firms' incentive to automate and move up.廉價外勞拖住升級。外勞約佔有證勞動力一成五（連同無證更高），壓低了工資底盤，也削弱了企業自動化、往高值轉型的誘因。
  • The skills gap is on the demand side. It isn't a shortage of graduates but of high-skill jobs: 88.7% of fresh graduates are absorbed into semi/low-skill roles, and mismatched graduates earn ~49% less — the "gaji cukup makan" (just-enough-to-get-by) reality.技能錯配在需求端。不是大學生不夠，是高技能職位不夠：88.7%的新鮮人被中低技能職位吸納，學用錯配的大學生少賺約49%，這就是民間講的「gaji cukup makan」（薪水剛好夠吃）。
  • A thin treasury. Tax revenue is only ~12% of GDP; since GST was scrapped in 2018 the state leans on a narrower SST, while government debt nears its 65% ceiling and the oil cushion shrinks (Petronas's 2026 dividend cut to RM20bn). Limited firepower to fund the R&D, education and upgrade it needs.國庫太薄。稅收只佔GDP約12%；2018年取消GST後，靠的是較窄的SST；政府債務又逼近65%上限、石油紅利在縮水（國油2026年股息砍到RM200億）。要砸錢做研發、教育、升級，子彈很有限。

The property read: when wages are structurally held down, prices can't ride local owner-occupier demand very far. The real upside comes from the specific places where high-value industry — chips, data centres — actually lands.對房市的意思：當薪資被結構壓著，房價就很難靠本地自住剛需大漲；真正的增量，來自把高值產業（晶片、數據中心）落地的特定地點。

Here is the mechanism in one chart. Over 2010–2024 real GDP grew +82%, but the real median wage rose only +43% — barely half the pace (World Bank, 2026). Labour's share of output is stuck at 33.6% versus ~50% in advanced economies, and value-add per Malaysian worker (RM99k) is now about a fifth of Singapore's — a gap that has widened since 2010 while China has caught up. The problem isn't jobs; unemployment is ~3%. It is that the high-paying work sits in a thin frontier of export and MNC firms — E&E is ~40% of exports, ~60% of that in Penang — not across the broad economy.一張圖把機制說完。2010到2024年，實質GDP長了82%，實質薪資中位數卻只漲43%，不到一半（世界銀行，2026）。勞動報酬佔產出卡在33.6%，對比先進國約五成；每名馬來西亞工人的產值（RM9.9萬）只有新加坡的約五分之一，而且這道差距從2010年以來還在擴大，中國則已追平。問題不在缺工作——失業率僅約3%——而在高薪工作只集中在一小撮出口與跨國企業的前沿（電子電機佔出口約四成、其中約六成在檳城），沒有擴散到整體經濟。

The property read: when output grows but wages don't, owner-occupier purchasing power can't track asset prices. Growth shows up in GDP and in the value of capital — including property — but not in the paycheque that has to service the mortgage. That wedge is the affordability squeeze, three sections down.對房市的意思：當產出長大、薪水沒跟上，自住買家的購買力就追不上資產價格。成長體現在GDP、體現在資本（包括房子）的價值上，卻沒體現在那張要拿去繳房貸的薪水條上。這道楔子，就是後面三段要講的可負擔squeeze。

A State-Heavy Economy國家之手很重的經濟

Part of the plateau is institutional. Malaysia's six government investment funds (EPF, Khazanah, PNB and peers) manage over RM1.8 trillion — roughly the size of the entire economy — and hold about 26% of the local stock market; government-linked companies are ~40% of Bursa by value. The 1971 affirmative-action goal of 30% Bumiputera corporate equity is still unmet at ~18% and has been re-anchored to 2035. A large, redistributive state stabilises the economy, but it also crowds out private risk-taking and slows deep reform — which is why the income gap is as much a political choice as an economic one.天花板有一部分是制度造成的。馬來西亞六大政府投資基金（EPF、Khazanah、PNB等）管理逾1.8兆令吉，規模約等於整個經濟體，並持有本地股市約26%的市值；政府連結公司（GLC）更佔馬股約四成。1971年留下的土著持股30%平權目標，至今只到約18%，已順延到2035年。一個龐大、講求再分配的國家機器能穩住經濟，卻也排擠了民間冒險的空間、讓深層改革走得很慢。所以這道收入差距，與其說是經濟問題，不如說也是政治選擇。

Who Can Actually Buy — Income & Its Distribution誰真的買得起 — 收入與它的分配

Share of total income vs share of households, by group (DOSM HIS 2022)各族群佔總所得 對 佔家庭數（DOSM 家庭收入調查 2022）

The median household earns RM7,017/month (2024) — but the mean is RM9,155, a 30% gap that tells you the distribution is top-heavy. The top 20% of households (T20, ≥RM12,681/mo) capture ~46% of all income; the bottom 40% (B40, ≤RM5,858/mo) get just 16%. The Gini has eased to 0.39 (2024, a 50-year low) yet has sat in a 0.39–0.41 band for a decade — and surveys undercount the very top. So the realistic buyer pool for new urban stock is the T20 plus the upper M40 — a minority of households, not the median family.家庭收入中位數是每月RM7,017（2024），但平均數是RM9,155，差三成——這個落差就告訴你分配是頭重腳輕的。最高20%家庭（T20，月入≥RM12,681）拿走約46%的總所得；最低40%（B40，≤RM5,858）只分到16%。吉尼係數雖降到0.39（2024，五十年低點），卻已在0.39–0.41區間卡了十年，而且調查還低估了最頂層。所以新都會產品真正的買家池，是T20加上M40的上半——是少數家庭，不是那個中位數家庭。

Median household income by state, RM/month (DOSM 2024; Kelantan 2022)各州家庭收入中位數，RM/月（DOSM 2024；吉蘭丹為2022）

And that minority is concentrated by geography and ethnicity. A median KL household earns RM10,805 and Selangor RM10,726 — roughly three times Kelantan's ~RM3,600. By ethnicity, median income runs Chinese RM8,933 > Bumiputera RM7,964 > Indian RM6,555. Effective housing demand therefore pools tightly in Klang Valley, Johor and Penang — the same places that hold the high-paying jobs, the investment and the bulk of transaction volume. The rest of the country has thin effective-demand pools, however cheap money gets.而這群少數，還被地理和族群進一步集中。吉隆坡家庭收入中位數RM10,805、雪蘭莪RM10,726，約是吉蘭丹（約RM3,600）的三倍。依族群，中位數是華裔RM8,933＞土著RM7,964＞印裔RM6,555。所以真正的購屋需求，緊緊集中在巴生谷、柔佛與檳城——也就是高薪工作、投資與成交量集中的地方。國家其他地方，無論錢變多便宜，有效需求池都很薄。

This is the demand-side ceiling that mirrors the leverage ceiling in Part 1. About 67% of household spending goes to necessities (DOSM 2024); the household savings rate is just ~1% of disposable income; and the share of Malaysians who couldn't raise RM1,000 in an emergency has jumped to 61%, from 47% in 2021 (BNM). With buffers that thin and a median debt-service ratio already at 41% on new loans, down-payment formation is near-zero and there is no margin for a rate rise, a repair, or a vacant month.這是需求面的天花板，正好對應第一篇的槓桿天花板。家庭支出約67%花在必需品（DOSM 2024）；家庭儲蓄率只有可支配所得的約1%；而「臨時湊不出RM1,000」的馬來西亞人比例，已從2021年的47%跳到61%（BNM）。緩衝這麼薄、新貸款的償債比中位數又已到41%，頭期款幾乎攢不出來，更沒有餘裕去扛升息、修繕、或空置一個月。

The property read: cheaper money lifts the ceiling for households that already qualify — it does little for the majority whose binding constraint is the deposit and the buffer, not the interest rate. This is why an OPR cut feeds the upper segment and investor demand faster than it broadens first-home ownership.對房市的意思：便宜的錢，幫得到的是本來就過得了關的家庭——對多數人沒什麼用，因為他們真正的綁手綁腳，是頭期款和緩衝，不是利率。這也是為什麼降息會比較快流向中高端與投資需求，而不是擴大首購自住。

The Affordability Squeeze買不起的鴻溝

Cumulative growth 2010–2023: house prices vs median household income (KRI)2010–2023累計漲幅：房價 對 家庭收入中位數（KRI）

This is where the wage story hits housing. Between 2010 and 2023, house prices rose +99% while median household income rose only +34%. Malaysia's median house-price-to-income multiple now sits around 4.2–5.1× — the global benchmark for "affordable" is 3.0, so the market is rated "seriously unaffordable." Home-ownership is 78% nationally but only 61% in KL and 72% in Selangor: the hot urban cores are where buying is hardest. The squeeze is structural, not cyclical — and it is exactly why so much demand sits in the affordable segment while pricey high-rise stock lingers.薪資的故事，就是在這裡撞上房子。2010到2023年間，房價漲了99%，家庭收入中位數卻只漲34%。馬來西亞的房價所得比中位數如今約在4.2到5.1倍，而國際上「可負擔」的標準是3.0倍，等於被歸類為「嚴重負擔不起」。全國自住率78%，但吉隆坡只有61%、雪蘭莪72%：愈是熱門的都會核心，愈買不起。這道鴻溝是結構性的、不是景氣性的，也正好解釋了為什麼需求大量集中在可負擔產品，高價高層卻去化緩慢。

The KL math, in ringgit用令吉算一遍：吉隆坡

A median-priced KL home (~RM794k, NAPIC) on a 90% loan needs about RM10,550/month to service at a prudent 30% debt-service ratio — almost exactly the RM10,805 a median KL household earns. In other words, the median KL family can buy the median KL home only by stretching to a 35-year loan and a 35–40% DSR. On the classic "3× annual income" rule the affordable price is ~RM389k — roughly half the market. The squeeze is arithmetic, not sentiment.一間吉隆坡中位數價位的房子（約RM79.4萬，NAPIC），貸九成，要在穩健的30%償債比下供得起，每月約需RM10,550——幾乎等於吉隆坡家庭收入中位數的RM10,805。換句話說，那個中位數家庭要買中位數的房子，只能靠拉到35年期、35–40%的償債比硬撐。按經典的「年收入3倍」標準，可負擔價位約RM38.9萬，大概是市價的一半。這道squeeze是算出來的，不是情緒。

Demography is the long-run engine under housing — and it is shifting. The population is still growing (34.1m) and urbanising toward an 85% target, and an estimated ~116,000 new households a year through 2030 are the structural floor under demand. But the tailwind is fading: the fertility rate has fallen to 1.6, well below replacement; citizen growth is just +0.7%; and Malaysia crosses into an "aged society" around 2044. The read for property: near-term household formation and urbanisation still support demand — especially affordable, well-located homes — but the demographic dividend that once guaranteed ever-rising demand is quietly closing.人口是房市最長線的引擎，而它正在轉向。人口還在成長（3,410萬）、也還在往85%的都市化目標走，估計到2030年前每年約新增11.6萬個家庭，這是需求的結構性底盤。但順風正在減弱：生育率已跌到1.6，遠低於替代水準；公民人口只成長0.7%；馬來西亞約在2044年邁入高齡社會。對房市的解讀：短期內，家庭形成與都市化仍撐著需求，尤其是可負擔、地段好的房子；但那個曾經保證需求只漲不跌的人口紅利，正在悄悄關上。

The Bumiputera Market — a Structural Layer土著市場 — 一道結構性分層

Ethnic composition, % of population, 2010 → 2060 (projection)族群結構，佔人口比重，2010 → 2060（推估）

Malaysia isn't one housing market; it is a dual one, and the dividing line is ethnicity. Bumiputera are 70.5% of citizens (2025) and their share is rising — DOSM projects 79.4% by 2060 — while the Chinese share, historically the dominant property-investor cohort, falls toward 14.8%, driven by a stark fertility gap (Malay TFR 1.9 vs Chinese 0.9). The buyer base is steadily shifting toward the group the policy regime is built around.馬來西亞不是一個房市，而是兩個，而那條分界線是族群。土著佔公民70.5%（2025），比重還在上升——DOSM推估2060年達79.4%——而歷來房產投資主力的華裔，比重將降到約14.8%，背後是懸殊的生育率（馬來人總和生育率1.9 對 華裔0.9）。買方結構，正穩定地往政策所環繞的那個族群移動。

Property is a state matter, so every state runs a Bumiputera layer: a 5–15% discount on new launches (median ~7%) and a 30%+ quota of units reserved for Bumi buyers. Two consequences ripple through the market. First, unsold Bumi-quota units are a structural source of overhang — REHDA has estimated they make up ~40–50% of it (an industry estimate, not an official figure) — released to the open market only with a lag. Second, Malay Reserve Land — between ~12% and ~38% of the peninsula depending on definition, and still growing — is permanently locked out of open-market transactions, tightening developable land exactly where it is concentrated. Bumi lots also carry a thinner resale buyer pool and weaker liquidity.房地產是州權事項，所以每個州都有一層土著機制：新建案5–15%折扣（中位數約7%），以及保留給土著買家的30%以上配額。兩個後果在市場裡擴散。其一，賣不掉的土著配額單位，是滯銷餘屋的結構性來源——REHDA估計約佔四到五成（業界估算，非官方數字）——而且要隔一段時間才釋出到公開市場。其二，馬來保留地——依定義約佔半島12%到38%，而且還在增加——被永久鎖在公開交易之外，在它集中的州屬把可開發土地硬生生收緊。土著單位的轉售買家池也較薄、流動性較弱。

The property read: the quota-and-discount system shapes segment pricing, mechanically feeds the overhang the Market Report tracks, and means a Bumi lot and a non-Bumi lot in the same project are not the same asset. A rising Bumiputera majority makes this layer more, not less, central over time.對房市的意思：配額與折扣機制會牽動分層定價、機械性地餵養市場報告追蹤的滯銷餘屋，也意味著同一個案裡的土著單位與非土著單位，並不是同一種資產。而土著比重持續上升，會讓這道分層隨時間變得更核心，而不是更邊緣。

The Ringgit — a Long Slide, a Recent Bounce令吉 · 長貶之後的回彈

USD/MYR, approx. year-end (higher = weaker ringgit). Pegged at 3.80 in 1998–2005.美元兌令吉，約年底值（數字越高＝令吉越弱）。1998–2005釘住3.80。

The ringgit is the thermometer of all this. Before the 1997 crisis a dollar bought ~2.5 ringgit; afterwards it was pegged at 3.80 for seven years, then slid for a decade, hitting a 4.80 low in Feb 2024. In 2025, with capital returning, record exports and tighter fiscal policy, it rebounded to ~4.05 — a four-year high. A weak ringgit long made Malaysian property "cheap" for foreigners, a quiet demand support; but the 8% foreign stamp duty (2026) plus a firmer ringgit are shrinking that discount.令吉是這套結構的溫度計。1997年金融風暴前，一美元只換約2.5令吉；風暴後一度釘在3.80長達七年，之後一路走貶，2024年2月最弱見4.80。2025年隨外資回流、出口創高、財政紀律改善，令吉強彈回約4.05，是四年新高。弱令吉長期讓馬來西亞房產對外國買家「便宜」，是一股需求支撐；但2026年起外國印花稅倍增到8%、加上令吉回穩，這個折扣正在縮小。

The Silicon Bet — Where the Money Goes半導體賭注 · 資金往哪裡去

Approved investment by state, 2025 (RM bn) — a record RM426.7bn nationally, highly concentrated2025各州核准投資（RM十億）—— 全國創高RM4,267億，高度集中

Malaysia isn't standing still. The 2024 National Semiconductor Strategy targets at least RM500bn of investment (phase one) and 60,000 trained engineers, pushing its ~13% share of global chip back-end higher. With the data-centre wave, this structural investment is concentrated in Johor, Penang, Selangor and KL — Johor alone took RM110bn of 2025's record approvals. The property read is direct: demand isn't a nationwide tide, it follows the chips and the servers into specific corridors.馬來西亞並沒有坐以待斃。2024年推出的國家半導體策略（NSS）喊出第一階段至少5,000億令吉投資、培訓六萬名工程師，要把全球封測（後段）約13%的市佔再往上推。配上數據中心熱潮，這波結構性投資高度集中在柔佛、檳城、雪蘭莪與吉隆坡；光柔佛就拿走2025年核准額中的1,100億。對房市的啟示很直接：需求不是全國普漲，而是跟著晶片和伺服器走，落在特定走廊。

The property takeaway對房產的結論

Follow the chain: growth doesn't fully reach wages → wages are unevenly distributed → after necessities and debt, almost nothing is left to save → so effective buying power is a minority of households, concentrated by class, ethnicity and geography, sitting on a quota-divided dual market and a demographic engine that is slowly cooling. The near-term easing is real, but it mostly lifts those already qualified. That is why Malaysian property rewards picking the right location, segment and timing — not buying the national index.順著這條鏈看：成長到不了薪水 → 薪水分配不均 → 扣掉必需品與債務後幾乎存不下來 → 所以真正的購買力只在少數家庭手上，而且被階層、族群與地理三重集中，又坐在一個被配額切成兩半的雙軌市場、和一具正在降溫的人口引擎上。短期降息是真的，但它主要抬升本來就過得了關的人。這就是為什麼馬來西亞房產獎勵的是選對地點、產品與時機，而不是買進「全國大盤」。

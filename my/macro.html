Bottom line一句話結論 2025 was the pivot year. After three years on hold, BNM cut the OPR to 2.75% — a pre-emptive insurance move, with growth still above 5% and inflation under 2%. But the easing lands in a market where household leverage is near its ceiling (84.8% of GDP) and Kuala Lumpur's average price is actually falling. Cheaper money meets demand that is structurally concentrated — Johor's SEZ, data centres and record FDI — rather than broad-based. 2025年是轉向年。國行按兵三年後把OPR降到2.75%，屬預防性的保險降息，當時經濟仍站穩5%以上、通膨壓在2%以下。但這波寬鬆，落在一個家庭槓桿逼近上限（佔GDP 84.8%）、吉隆坡均價反而下跌的市場。便宜的錢遇到的需求高度集中（柔新特區、數據中心、創紀錄FDI），不是全面普漲。

Monetary Cycle × House Prices貨幣週期 × 房價

  • Jul 2025: first cut in five years to 2.75% — pre-emptive insurance against the US-tariff shock, not a recession signal.2025年7月：五年來首度降息至2.75%，是防美國關稅衝擊的預防性保險降息，不是衰退訊號。
  • House prices lag policy by 12–18 months, so this cut mainly feeds 2026–2027 — just as the 2020–21 OPR floor pre-loaded the 2022–24 run-up.房價落後政策約12到18個月，這刀主要反映在2026到2027年，正如2020–21年的低利率墊出了2022–24那波漲勢。
  • The OPR (2.75%) vs average mortgage rate (4.56%) spread stays wide — only part of the cut reaches the buyer.OPR（2.75%）與平均房貸利率（4.56%）的利差仍寬，降息紅利只有一部分傳到買家。
  • Inflation sits under 2% and M2 growth is ~4% — easing without credit overheating.通膨壓在2%以下、M2成長約4%，是沒有信貸過熱的寬鬆。

The Affordability Ceiling可負擔性的天花板

Household debt as a share of GDP, 2013–2025家庭債務佔GDP比重，2013–2025

  • Debt peaked at 93.2% of GDP in the 2020 COVID slump and has normalised to ~84.8% — but that is still high by regional standards.家庭債務在2020年衰退時衝到GDP的93.2%，之後回到約84.8%，但以區域標準仍偏高。
  • Housing loans are ~61% of household debt and the median debt-service ratio on new loans is 41% — the marginal buyer is stretched, which caps mortgage-driven upside even as rates fall.房貸約佔家庭債務六成，新批貸款的償債比中位數達41%，邊際買家被綁得很緊，就算降息也封住房貸驅動的上行。
  • The offset: household financial assets are ~2.1× total debt and the impaired-loan ratio is ~1.1% — heavy leverage, but well-collateralised.另一面是：家庭金融資產約為總負債的2.1倍，不良貸款比率僅約1.1%，槓桿雖重但抵押扎實。

Part 2 · The Structural Economy經濟結構篇 · 為什麼這影響房價

Somewhere on the road from commodities to chips, Malaysia's economy got stuck — and a stuck income story is, quietly, a capped house-price story.從商品走到晶片這條路，馬來西亞的經濟走到一半卡住了；而收入卡住，房價其實也就有了一道隱形的天花板。

Malaysia's wealth has always come out of the ground. In the 1800s, rubber and tin pulled different peoples onto the peninsula and drew today's demographic and political map; later, palm oil and petroleum filled the treasury and bred a dependence on commodities. This century the bet is electronics and semiconductors. The trouble is that, somewhere on that road, the economy stalled.馬來西亞的財富，一直是從土地裡長出來的。十九世紀的橡膠與錫，把不同族群帶上這片半島，也鋪下了今天的人口與政治格局；後來是棕油與石油，撐起國庫，也養出對大宗商品的依賴。進入這個世紀，國家把賭注壓在電子與半導體上。問題是，從商品到晶片的這條路，它走到一半卡住了。

The Middle-Income Ceiling中等收入的天花板

GDP per capita, nominal US$, 2024 (World Bank)人均GDP（名目美元），2024（世界銀行）

The "middle-income trap" is nowhere clearer than here. Malaysia reached upper-middle income in 1996; nearly thirty years on, GDP per capita is about US$11,900 — below China, a third of South Korea, a sixth of Singapore. It has the skeleton of an upper-middle economy but can't grow high-income muscle, sitting roughly US$2,000 short of the World Bank's high-income line. The cause isn't a mystery, it's choices: R&D is ~1% of GDP versus 5% in Korea, exports run over 70% of GDP with electronics ~40% of them, GLCs are ~40% of the stock market, and skilled talent keeps leaking out.經濟學家講的「中等收入陷阱」，在馬來西亞身上看得最清楚。它1996年就跨進中高收入，將近三十年過去，人均GDP約1.19萬美元，卡在中國之下、只有南韓的三分之一、新加坡的六分之一。它有一具中高收入國家的骨架，卻一直長不出高收入國家的肌肉，距世界銀行的高收入門檻還差約兩千美元。卡住的原因不神秘，是選擇：研發只佔GDP約1%，南韓是5%；出口佔GDP逾七成，電子電機又佔了四成；政府連結公司（GLC）佔馬股約四成市值；人才還在持續外流。

The Ringgit — a Long Slide, a Recent Bounce令吉 · 長貶之後的回彈

USD/MYR, approx. year-end (higher = weaker ringgit). Pegged at 3.80 in 1998–2005.美元兌令吉，約年底值（數字越高＝令吉越弱）。1998–2005釘住3.80。

The ringgit is the thermometer of all this. Before the 1997 crisis a dollar bought ~2.5 ringgit; afterwards it was pegged at 3.80 for seven years, then slid for a decade, hitting a 4.80 low in Feb 2024. In 2025, with capital returning, record exports and tighter fiscal policy, it rebounded to ~4.05 — a four-year high. A weak ringgit long made Malaysian property "cheap" for foreigners, a quiet demand support; but the 8% foreign stamp duty (2026) plus a firmer ringgit are shrinking that discount.令吉是這套結構的溫度計。1997年金融風暴前，一美元只換約2.5令吉；風暴後一度釘在3.80長達七年，之後一路走貶，2024年2月最弱見4.80。2025年隨外資回流、出口創高、財政紀律改善，令吉強彈回約4.05，是四年新高。弱令吉長期讓馬來西亞房產對外國買家「便宜」，是一股需求支撐；但2026年起外國印花稅倍增到8%、加上令吉回穩，這個折扣正在縮小。

Concept · The Middle-Income Trap概念深掘 · 中等收入陷阱

When wages grow too high to beat low-cost rivals but technology isn't high enough to compete with advanced economies, a country stalls in between. Escaping it takes productivity and innovation — which require sustained investment in R&D, education and institutional reform, not just cheap credit or another building boom.當一個國家的薪資已高到拚不過低成本對手，技術又還沒高到能跟先進國競爭，就會卡在中間。要突圍，靠的是生產力與創新，而那需要長期投資研發、教育與制度改革，不是靠便宜的信貸或再蓋一波房子就能解決。

The Silicon Bet — Where the Money Goes半導體賭注 · 資金往哪裡去

Approved investment by state, 2025 (RM bn) — a record RM426.7bn nationally, highly concentrated2025各州核准投資（RM十億）—— 全國創高RM4,267億，高度集中

Malaysia isn't standing still. The 2024 National Semiconductor Strategy targets at least RM500bn of investment (phase one) and 60,000 trained engineers, pushing its ~13% share of global chip back-end higher. With the data-centre wave, this structural investment is concentrated in Johor, Penang, Selangor and KL — Johor alone took RM110bn of 2025's record approvals. The property read is direct: demand isn't a nationwide tide, it follows the chips and the servers into specific corridors.馬來西亞並沒有坐以待斃。2024年推出的國家半導體策略（NSS）喊出第一階段至少5,000億令吉投資、培訓六萬名工程師，要把全球封測（後段）約13%的市佔再往上推。配上數據中心熱潮，這波結構性投資高度集中在柔佛、檳城、雪蘭莪與吉隆坡；光柔佛就拿走2025年核准額中的1,100億。對房市的啟示很直接：需求不是全國普漲，而是跟著晶片和伺服器走，落在特定走廊。

The property takeaway對房產的結論

Stack the cycle on the structure: a near-term easing tailwind, a long-term income ceiling, and demand that is real but concentrated. So Malaysian property is a market for picking the right location and product — not one where buying anything rides a national tide.把週期面疊在結構面上看：短期有降息順風，長期有收入天花板，需求真實卻集中。所以馬來西亞房產是一個「選對地點與產品」的市場，不是「隨便買進就跟著大盤上漲」的市場。

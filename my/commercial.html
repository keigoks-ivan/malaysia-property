Key takeaways關鍵摘要

  • Shop overhang rose to 7,023 units in Q1 2026, its highest reading since the Q1 2023 peak of 7,367 and the third consecutive quarterly rise from the Q2 2025 trough of 5,699 — arriving in the same year national shop transaction volume declined for the first time since the post-pandemic recovery, which together is the report's central topping signal.店屋餘屋在 2026 年第一季升至 7,023 戶，是 2023 年第一季高點 7,367 戶以來的新高，也是自 2025 年第二季低點 5,699 戶起連續第三季走升——同一年全國店屋成交量更是自疫後回穩以來首度下滑，兩者合看，是本報告最核心的見頂訊號。
  • Office occupancy is scope-dependent, not contradictory: 72.34% private-only (Q1 2026) vs 78.1% private+government (FY2025) are two different, both-official NAPIC series. The gap is near-zero in KL and Selangor but 32 points in Putrajaya, driven almost entirely by near-full government buildings.辦公室入駐率是口徑問題，不是矛盾：72.34% 僅私人（2026Q1）對 78.1% 私人＋政府（2025年）是兩個都官方、但口徑不同的 NAPIC 序列。KL 與雪蘭莪的落差近乎為零，布城卻高達 32 個百分點，幾乎全部來自近乎滿租的政府樓。
  • Johor's private-only office occupancy, 55.15%, is the national outlier — well below KL (71.87%), Selangor (71.47%) and Penang (77.31%).柔佛僅私人口徑辦公室入駐率 55.15%，是全國異數——遠低於 KL（71.87%）、雪蘭莪（71.47%）與檳城（77.31%）。
  • Q4 2025→Q1 2026 absorption (126,850 sm) outpaced new supply (61,360 sm) with vacant space also falling in absolute terms — not a first occurrence over the full Q1 2020–Q1 2026 history (it has happened in a number of earlier quarters, most sharply in the 2022–2023 recovery), but the largest such reading with supply itself genuinely positive, not merely small or negative. It also follows immediately after the series' deepest single-quarter contraction, Q4 2024→Q1 2025 (occupied space fell 283,340 sm against a 491,320 sm stock contraction) — three quarters of positive readings since that trough, not yet enough to call a confirmed turn.2025Q4 到 2026Q1，淨吸納量（12.69萬平方米）跑贏新增供給（6.14萬平方米），空置空間也同步以絕對值下降——這在 2020Q1–2026Q1 的完整歷史上並非首見（過去多個季度都曾發生，2022–2023 復甦期尤其明顯），但這是供給本身確實為正值（不是靠供給基期很小甚至為負墊出來）的讀數中規模最大的一次。它也緊接在全序列單季跌幅最深的區間之後——2024Q4 到 2025Q1（入駐空間減少 28.334 萬平方米，存量本身收縮 49.132 萬）——自那次谷底以來已連三段區間轉正，但還不足以判定是已確認的轉折。
  • The three commercial-title pipelines carry very different supply weight — serviced apartments 40.9% of stock incoming, SOHO 23.2%, shops just 3.8% — yet shops are the one sub-sector where overhang keeps climbing back toward its 2023 peak, which points the explanation toward demand and financing, not a construction wave.三條商業地契供給線的份量差異極大——服務式公寓即將供給佔存量 40.9%、SOHO 23.2%、店屋僅 3.8%——但餘屋持續朝 2023 年高點回升的偏偏是店屋，這把解釋方向指向需求與融資端，不是營建潮。
  • 2025's named capital-markets transactions show retail capital concentrating outside KL — Johor's Paradigm Mall (Johor Bahru) alone sold for RM1.157 billion, while KL's only listed shopping-complex sale is a 2024-vintage deal worth RM34.0 million — while office capital remains KL-heavy (9 of 20 major sales, ~82% of listed value).2025 年具名資本市場交易顯示，零售資金正流向 KL 以外——單是柔佛的 Paradigm Mall（新山）就賣出 RM11.57 億，KL 唯一列入的商場交易卻是一筆 2024 年、僅 RM3,400 萬的舊案——辦公室資金則仍集中在 KL（20 筆大額交易中佔 9 筆，約佔列示總值 82%）。
  • An individual investor has exactly one directly-investable commercial sub-sector — shops, with title, tenant and rent roll they can personally verify. Everything else (offices, malls, industrial/data-centre space) trades at institutional scale and is better accessed through a REIT.個人投資人只有一個能真正直接投資的商業次板塊——店屋，有地契、有租客、有租金流可親自查核。其餘（辦公室、商場、工業／資料中心用地）皆屬機構級交易規模，更適合透過 REIT 曝險。

Market View市場觀點

Every category NAPIC measures either improved marginally or is quietly deteriorating — nothing moved sharply in either direction this quarter. The exception is shops, where three consecutive quarters of rising overhang and 2025's first transaction-volume decline together read as more than noise. That is where this report's underwriting caution concentrates.NAPIC 量測的每個類別，這一季不是小幅改善、就是悄悄惡化——沒有任何一個方向劇烈移動。唯一的例外是店屋：連三季餘屋走升，加上 2025 年首見的成交量下滑，兩者合看已經不只是雜訊。這正是本報告盡調紀律最集中的地方。

Bottom line總結 Malaysia's commercial property is not one market but three tiers of accessibility, and the tier a product sits in matters more than its headline yield. Offices, shopping complexes and industrial data-centre space are institutional assets in practice: strata offices and mall units trade in thin, illiquid pools and the only sensible individual entry is an M-REIT unit. Shophouses are the one commercial sub-sector an individual investor can actually underwrite directly, complete with title, tenant and rent roll — which is why this report spends most of its length there. And that segment is currently flashing a warning: unsold completed shops reached 7,023 units in the first quarter of 2026, the highest reading since the Q1 2023 peak of 7,367, worsening for three consecutive quarters, arriving in the same year that national shop transaction volume turned negative for the first time since the data series recovered from the pandemic while transaction value kept rising — the classic shape of a topping market. Serviced apartments and commercial-title SOHOs, already covered in depth in the companion Market Report, remain the segment to avoid as passive investments. 馬來西亞的商業不動產不是單一市場，而是三層不同可及性的市場；一項產品落在哪一層，比它表面的殖利率更重要。辦公樓、購物中心與工業資料中心用地，實際上都是機構級資產：分層辦公室與商場單位的交易池薄且流動性差，個人合理的入場方式只有 M-REIT 一種。店屋則是唯一一類個人投資人能真正直接盡調的商業次板塊——有地契、有租客、有租金流可查——這也是本報告篇幅最集中於此的原因。而這個板塊此刻正亮著警示燈：2026 年第一季已完工未售店屋升至 7,023 戶，是 2023 年第一季高點 7,367 戶以來的新高，且已連三季惡化，同一年全國店屋成交量更是自疫後數據回穩以來首度轉負，成交值卻續漲——這是市場見頂的典型形狀。服務式公寓與商業地契 SOHO，已在另文《市場報告》深入處理，作為被動投資，結論仍是迴避。

I · Definitions & Scale第一部 · 口徑與規模

What "commercial title" covers「商業地契」涵蓋什麼

NAPIC's commercial universe spans five product types with almost nothing in common except the land title: shops (single- to two-and-a-half-storey shophouses through pre-war units), SOHO (small-office/home-office strata units), serviced apartments (residential-format towers built on commercial land), purpose-built offices (PBO — dedicated office towers), and shopping complexes (SC — malls with a single ownership structure). Each has its own supply cycle, its own buyer pool and, as this report sets out, its own answer to whether an individual should touch it at all.NAPIC 的商業世界涵蓋五種產品，彼此除了地契性質之外幾乎沒有共通點：店屋（一至二層半連棟店屋，含戰前老屋）、SOHO（小型辦公／居家辦公分層單位）、服務式公寓（蓋在商業用地上、卻做住宅格局的高樓）、純辦公樓（PBO，單一用途的辦公大樓）、以及購物中心（SC，單一產權結構的商場）。五者各有各的供給週期、各有各的買家群，而如本報告將展開的，各自對「個人該不該碰」也有不同的答案。

The grey zone already covered elsewhere已在別處深講的灰色地帶

Serviced apartments sit on commercial land but are marketed and lived in as residential units — Malaysia's most consequential zoning grey zone, and the subject of a full treatment in the companion Market Report (overhang, price bands, clearance timelines). It is referenced here only for scale: unsold completed SA stock reached 19,263 units worth RM16.52 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rising for a third straight quarter from the Q2 2025 low of 17,883 (itself the softest reading since Q1 2020's 16,942) — still well below the Q3 2021 series peak of 24,346, but the direction has turned.服務式公寓蓋在商業用地上，卻以住宅之姿銷售與居住——這是馬來西亞影響最大的分區灰色地帶，已在另文《市場報告》有完整處理（餘屋、價位帶、去化週期）。此處僅作規模參照：2026 年第一季，已完工未售的服務式公寓升至 19,263 戶，市值 RM165.2 億，自 2025 年第二季 17,883 戶的低點（該讀數本身已是 2020 年第一季 16,942 戶以來最低）起已連三季走升；雖仍遠低於 2021 年第三季 24,346 戶的序列高點，但方向已經轉了。

Price-band detail, transcribed for the first time from NAPIC's Table 8 rather than approximated, sharpens the picture further. Nationally, 81.5% of unsold SA stock sits above RM500,000 and 22.9% above RM1 million — close to, and now confirming, the "about 80%" estimate used elsewhere on this site. The state split does the real explanatory work: Kuala Lumpur's SA overhang is 43.1% above RM1 million (average unsold price RM1.13 million), Johor's single largest band is RM800,000–900,000 alone (3,006 of its 9,972 unsold units), and Penang's average unsold SA price — RM1.30 million — is the highest of any state measured.價位帶細節首次直接自 NAPIC Table 8 逐格轉錄、而非估算，讓圖像更清晰。全國未售服務式公寓中，81.5% 定價在 RM50 萬以上、22.9% 在 RM100 萬以上——與本站其他地方「約八成」的估算相近，如今算是坐實。真正有解釋力的是州別拆解：吉隆坡的服務式公寓餘屋有 43.1% 落在 RM100 萬以上（未售均價 RM113 萬），柔佛單一最大價位帶集中在 RM80–90 萬（9,972 戶未售中就有 3,006 戶落在此帶），檳城的未售均價——RM130 萬——則是所有州別中最高。

In 2025, Malaysia recorded 46,649 commercial-sector transactions worth RM58.70 billion (NAPIC, all commercial sub-sectors). Shops alone accounted for 19,551 of those deals — 42% of the total, and more than shopping complexes, offices and industrial combined could ever produce, because those trade as whole buildings rather than individual units. The point is structural, not incidental: for a person writing a cheque rather than a REIT manager allocating a fund, the commercial property market in Malaysia is, in practical terms, the shophouse market. Everything else in this report exists to explain why, and what the alternative looks like when a shophouse is not the answer.2025 年，馬來西亞全商業類交易共 46,649 件，總值 RM587 億（NAPIC，涵蓋全部商業次板塊）。單店屋一項就佔了 19,551 件——佔總數 42%，比購物中心、辦公室與工業加總起來能貢獻的件數都多，原因是那幾類多以整棟交易，不是個別單位。這一點是結構性的，不是巧合：對一個親自簽支票的個人投資人、而非替基金配置的 REIT 經理人而言，馬來西亞的商業房產市場，實際上就是店屋市場。本報告接下來的篇幅，都在解釋為什麼會是這樣，以及當店屋不是答案時，替代方案長什麼樣子。

The three commercial sub-sectors an individual might actually touch carry very different weights of supply still to arrive. Serviced apartments have 198,348 units in the incoming-supply pipeline against an existing stock of 484,591 — 40.9% more units already committed on top of what stands today (derived: 198,348 ÷ 484,591). SOHO carries 17,444 units against 75,329 existing, 23.2%. Shops carry 21,564 against 560,624 — just 3.8%. These are not three versions of the same risk. The shop problem, developed at length in Part IV, is clearing an existing pile with almost nothing new joining the queue. The SA problem, covered in the companion Market Report, is the opposite: a wave that has barely started to arrive. The 50,329 SA units already under construction cited there are a slice of this same 198,348-unit pipeline.個人投資人真正可能碰到的三個商業次類別，未來要迎接的供給份量完全不同。服務式公寓的即將供給有 198,348 戶，對照既有存量 484,591 戶——等於在現有基礎上，再加掛 40.9% 的戶數（derived：198,348 ÷ 484,591）。SOHO 的即將供給 17,444 戶，對存量 75,329 戶，佔 23.2%。店屋則是 21,564 戶對 560,624 戶，僅 3.8%。這三個不是同一種風險的三種版本。店屋的問題，第四部會詳細展開，是既有存量去化不動、幾乎沒有新的隊伍加入排隊；服務式公寓的問題，另文《市場報告》已處理，恰恰相反——是一波幾乎還沒真正抵達的浪。另文提到、已在興建中的 50,329 戶服務式公寓，正是這 198,348 戶供給線裡的一部分。

Market View市場觀點

Five products share a land-title category and nothing else. Scale alone explains why this report weights shops so heavily: they generate 42% of all commercial deals by count, and carry the thinnest incoming-supply pipeline of the three individually-accessible sub-sectors — which is what makes the overhang trend in Part IV worth the attention it gets.五種產品共享的只有地契類別，別無其他。單看規模，就能解釋本報告為何如此側重店屋：它以件數計貢獻了全部商業交易的 42%，而在三個個人可及的次板塊裡，即將供給又是三者中最薄的一條——這正是第四部的餘屋走勢值得如此關注的原因。

Office occupancy is sticky in a way retail and industrial are not, and that stickiness cuts both directions. Leases typically run three to six years, so a tenant's decision to leave shows up in the vacancy figures twelve to eighteen months after it is made, not the quarter it happens — occupancy is a lagging read on sentiment, not a live one. And once a building starts losing tenants to a newer address, the loss tends to compound rather than reverse: ESG and green-building certification is functioning as a binary qualification threshold for large corporate and MNC tenants, not a continuous discount an older tower can price its way around. The market context beneath the number is a bifurcation. Klang Valley overall office vacancy stood at 15.2% and KL prime-grade vacancy at 22.1% as of the first quarter of 2026 (Knight Frank, 1Q2026), with a further 4.61 million sq ft of new supply due in 2026–27. Office occupancy is not a market you can buy a floor of and expect to sell — the pool is too thin, and the newest towers are eating the oldest one's tenants.辦公室入駐率不是一個你買一層樓就能指望脫手的市場——買賣池太薄，而且最新的大樓，正在吃掉最舊那棟的租客。

The conclusion for an individual is unambiguous. Strata office floors trade in a shallow secondary market, carry commercial financing terms, and are directly exposed to the flight-to-quality dynamic described above — an individually-owned floor in an ageing tower is precisely the asset losing tenants to TRX. Exposure belongs in a REIT with an office book, such as KLCC REIT, not in a strata title.對個人而言，結論很明確。分層辦公樓層的二手交易池淺、融資條件走商業貸款，且直接暴露在上述「品質出走」的動態裡——一層個人持有的舊樓辦公室，正是那個正在把租客輸給 TRX 的資產。要曝險，該透過持有辦公資產的 REIT，例如 KLCC REIT，而不是分層產權本身。

Why a 3.8% pipeline still pushes overhang back toward its peak為什麼一條僅 3.8% 的供給線，仍能把餘屋推回逼近高點

Put the pieces of this part together and the paradox resolves into three separate mechanisms, not one. On the demand side, e-commerce keeps eroding the case for street-level retail space, and small-business credit — the segment most likely to lease or buy a shop lot — remains tight; a shop's value depends on someone wanting to run a business out of it, and that pool has been shrinking gradually for years. On the product side, the price-band and geography findings above point the same direction: new supply concentrates in township-scale schemes pricing above RM1 million, exactly the band buyers are avoiding, while the corridors buyers actually want — established, footfall-proven streets — have little new stock to sell because they were built out long ago. On the financing side, commercial-title lending runs shorter tenures, lower loan-to-value and higher rates than residential, shops carry no first-home scheme support of any kind, and the foreign buyer's stamp duty of 8% (effective January 2026) removes one of the few pools of capital that might have cleared the top of the price-band distribution. None of these three is dramatic on its own. Together, they are enough to keep pushing overhang back toward its 2023 peak, quarter after quarter, on a pipeline too small to blame.把本部的線索放在一起，這個悖論會拆解成三個各自獨立的機制，不是一個。需求端，電商持續侵蝕臨街零售空間的存在理由，而最可能承租或購買一間店屋的小型企業信貸，依然偏緊——店屋的價值終究取決於有沒有人想在裡面做生意，而這群人已經緩慢萎縮了好幾年。產品端，前面的價格帶與地理發現指向同一個方向：新供給集中在定價 RM100 萬以上的 township 級建案，正是買家在迴避的那一帶，而買家真正想要的走廊——人流已獲驗證的成熟街道——反而幾乎沒有新貨可賣，因為早就蓋滿了。融資端，商業地契的貸款年期更短、成數更低、利率更高，店屋完全沒有任何首購方案支持，而 2026 年 1 月生效的 8% 外籍買家印花稅，又拿掉了少數本可能吃掉價位帶頂端的資金池之一。這三個機制，單獨看都不算戲劇性。加在一起，卻足以讓餘屋一季接一季地推回逼近 2023 年的高點——即便供給線小到怪不到它頭上。

Market View市場觀點

Shop overhang is this report's central topping signal: the highest reading since the 2023 peak, three straight quarters of deterioration, and 2025's first transaction-volume decline all landing together, on a supply pipeline too thin to explain any of it. The cause sits on the demand and financing side, not the construction side — which means waiting for supply to slow will not fix it.店屋餘屋是本報告最核心的見頂訊號：創 2023 年高點以來新高、連三季惡化、2025 年首見的成交量下滑，三者同時到齊，而供給線薄到解釋不了任何一項。原因在需求與融資端，不在營建端——代表等供給放緩，並不會解決問題。

The absence of an official metric here is not the same absence as the one in the shop market. Shops lack a price index but still generate an overhang count, because NAPIC's Table 8 is built around unit sales — a shop can be individually titled, unsold, and counted. SA (40.9%) and SOHO (23.2%) carry pipelines large enough to overwhelm current absorption rates; shops (3.8%) face almost none, which is precisely why their overhang cannot be blamed on over-construction.服務式公寓（40.9%）與 SOHO（23.2%）的供給線大到足以壓過目前的吸納速度；店屋（3.8%）幾乎沒有新供給，這正是為什麼它的餘屋不能怪罪於過度營建。

Rate environment — OPR 2.75%利率環境——OPR 2.75%

Commercial-title lending already runs shorter tenures and lower LTV than residential; any OPR increase would compress an already-thin buyer pool for shops and strata offices further before it affects REIT-held whole-building assets, which finance on different, typically longer-dated terms.商業地契貸款的年期本就比住宅更短、成數更低；OPR 若上升，會先壓縮店屋與分層辦公室原本就淺的買家池，才輪到 REIT 持有的整棟資產——後者的融資條件不同，通常年期更長。

State-level divergence州別分歧

Johor's office occupancy (55.15%) and shop-overhang volatility, alongside Penang's retail occupancy (70.55%) and highest-in-country SA overhang pricing, mean a national-average read can miss the state where an individual's actual property sits.柔佛的辦公室入駐率（55.15%）與店屋餘屋的劇烈波動，加上檳城的零售入駐率（70.55%）與全國最高的服務式公寓餘屋定價，代表看全國均值，可能漏掉個人實際持有物業所在的那個州。

Data-centre policy tightening資料中心政策收緊

2026's approval limits, Johor's 18-month water-cooled pause and mandatory green-power sourcing are cooling signals in a segment individuals cannot access directly except through REITs such as Axis REIT — the risk is indirect but real for that exposure.2026 年的審批限制、柔佛 18 個月水冷暫緩、強制綠電採購，都是這個板塊的降溫訊號，個人無法直接接觸，只能透過 Axis REIT 這類 REIT 曝險——風險是間接的，但對這份曝險而言真實存在。

Market View市場觀點

None of the base-case judgments above are forecasts with a stated probability — they are directional reads built from Parts I–VI, meant to be checked against the falsifiable triggers each quarter. Shop overhang extending its already-confirmed three-quarter rise to a fourth in Q2 2026 is the single trigger most likely to matter first.以上基本情境判斷都不是附機率的預測——它們是根據第一至第六部建立的方向性判讀，用來每季對照可證偽觸發點檢核。店屋餘屋若在 2026 年第二季把已確認的連三季升勢延伸到第四季，是最可能第一個觸發、也最重要的一個訊號。

VIII · Conclusion — Three Tiers of Actionability第八部 · 結論：可操作性三層

Everything above collapses into one filter: can an individual investor actually underwrite this product directly, or does it only make sense wrapped in a fund? Sorted that way, Malaysia's commercial property market splits cleanly into three tiers.以上所有內容，都收斂成同一道篩子：個人投資人真的能親自盡調這項產品，還是只有包成基金才說得通？照這個標準排列，馬來西亞的商業房產市場乾淨地分成三層。

Shop entry checklist, built from the price-band evidence店屋進場檢核清單，從價位帶證據推導

  • Avoid the RM1 million-plus band in new township schemes — it is where 23.3% of the national overhang sits, and Selangor's evidence shows the same band clearing far slower than everything beneath it.避開新 township 建案裡 RM100 萬以上的價位帶——全國 23.3% 的餘屋卡在這裡，雪蘭莪的證據顯示同一帶去化明顯慢過其下所有價位帶。
  • Favour established corridors with proven footfall over newer developments — the mismatch between where shops sell and where they sit unsold is a location story more than a price story.優先選人流已獲驗證的成熟街廓，而非較新的建案——成交地點與滯銷地點的落差，講的是地段，不是價格。
  • Require an actual rent roll and verified tenant covenant, not vacant possession — with no price index, the rent stream is the only cash-flow evidence available to underwrite against.要求真實租金流與已驗證的租客履約，不是空置交屋——沒有價格指數，租金流是唯一能拿來盡調的現金流證據。
  • Cross-check against three to five comparable transactions on the same street before agreeing a price — the only substitute for an index this market does not have.議價前用同條街三到五筆可比成交交叉核對——這是這個市場沒有指數之下，唯一的替代方案。
  • Treat Stratified-title units as a different asset class from ground-floor shophouses — they carry more of the strata-liquidity risk covered in Parts II and III than the land-title advantage that makes shops attractive in the first place.把 Stratified 分層產權單位當成與地面層店屋不同的資產類別——它承擔的是第二部、第三部講的分層流動性風險，而不是店屋原本吸引人的地契優勢。
① Buy directly① 可直接買

Shops. Title, tenant and rent roll an individual can verify. But entry now needs discipline: overhang just hit its highest reading since the 2023 peak (7,023, 1Q26) after three worsening quarters, and 2025's volume-down/value-up transaction pattern is a topping signal, not a green light.店屋。地契、租客、租金流，個人都能親自查核。但現在進場需要紀律：餘屋剛升至2023 年高點以來新高（7,023，2026Q1）、已連三季惡化，2025 年量縮值升的成交型態是見頂訊號，不是進場燈號。

Selection discipline: location (footfall, parking, corridor continuity) over asking price; verified tenant covenant, not vacant possession; underwrite from comparable transactions on the same street, since no price index exists to check your own math against.選案紀律：地段（人流、停車、街廓連續性）優先於開價；要有已驗證的租客履約，不是空置交屋；用同條街的可比成交來盡調，因為沒有價格指數可以核對自己的判斷。

② REIT only② 只能透過 REIT

Offices, shopping complexes, industrial/data-centre space — all institutional-scale by transaction structure and tenant relationships. An individual strata lot underperforms the whole-building product it competes against.辦公室、購物中心、工業／資料中心用地——就交易結構與租戶關係而言，全屬機構等級。個人持有的分層單位，表現輸給與其競爭的整棟持有產品。

REIT dividends fall under the YA2026 reform: residents now pay their marginal tax rate on REIT income (no longer a flat 10%); non-residents pay 30% (up from 10%). Model the after-tax numbers in the REITs vs Property calculator.REIT 配息適用 YA2026 新制：居民須按邊際稅率課 REIT 所得（不再是固定 10%）；非居民從 10% 升至 30%。稅後數字可用 REITs vs 房產計算器試算。

③ Avoid as passive investment③ 別碰（被動投資）

Commercial-title serviced apartments and SOHOs. Structurally the slowest appreciation, commercial utility and assessment rates, and a thin resale pool — the full breakdown is in the companion Market Report and Macro × Property analyses; this report does not re-litigate it.商業地契的服務式公寓與 SOHO。結構性增值最慢、商業水電與門牌稅、轉售池薄——完整拆解見另文《市場報告》與《總經 × 房產》，本篇不重複論證。

Running one yourself as an active short-stay operation is the stated exception, not the passive-investment case this tier is scoring.若是自己主動經營短租，是既有的例外情況，不在本層評估的被動投資之列。

The closing frame總結框架

Malaysia's commercial property market rewards asking the accessibility question before the yield question. Shops are the one sub-sector where an individual's own diligence is the whole game, and that game has just gotten harder: overhang at its highest since the 2023 peak, transaction volume down for the first time in the recovery era, value still rising underneath it. Everything institutional — office, mall, industrial — is better owned through the REIT structures built to manage it, priced daily, taxed under the YA2026 regime, and never mistaken for something a strata title could replicate.馬來西亞的商業房產市場，獎勵的是先問「碰不碰得到」，再問殖利率。店屋是唯一一個「個人自己的盡調就是全部戰局」的次板塊，而這場戰局眼下正變得更難：餘屋升至 2023 年高點以來新高，成交量自疫後回穩以來首度下滑，總值卻仍在其下續漲。所有機構級的部分——辦公室、商場、工業——都更適合透過為此而生的 REIT 結構持有：天天有報價、依 YA2026 新制課稅，而且永遠不會被誤認為分層產權能複製得來的東西。

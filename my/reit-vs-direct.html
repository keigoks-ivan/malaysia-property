The method — equal-outlay comparison方法論 · 等額出資比較法 A fair fight needs equal money in. So the REIT path mirrors every cash outflow of the property path: the same total at Year 0 (down payment + all buy-in fees goes into REITs), and whenever the property bleeds a negative-cash-flow year, the REIT path tops up the same amount. Property surpluses roll into a fixed-deposit side-pocket; REIT dividends are reinvested after tax (DRIP). At the end: property = net sale proceeds (after loan, selling costs and RPGT) + the deposit pot; REIT = portfolio value minus a one-off sell fee (no capital-gains tax). Terminal net worth and IRR are reported for both. 公平比較需要投入相同的錢。因此 REIT 路徑鏡射實體路徑的每一筆現金流出：Year 0 投入同額（頭期款＋全部買入費用同額買 REIT），實體每逢淨現金流為負的年度，REIT 路徑同年同額加碼。實體的正現金流滾入定存側袋；REIT 配息稅後再投入（DRIP）。期末：實體＝房屋淨售款（扣餘貸、賣出成本與 RPGT）＋定存累積；REIT＝持倉市值扣一次性賣出費（無資本利得稅）。兩路徑各輸出期末淨值與 IRR。

Four forces that decide the winner決定勝負的四個關鍵

🏛️ YA2026: the REIT 10% shield is goneYA2026：REIT 10% 保護傘落日

Until 2025, M-REIT distributions to individuals were taxed at a final 10% withholding. From YA2026 that sunset kicks in: residents fold REIT income into progressive tax at their marginal rate (so a 30%-bracket local now pays 30%, not 10%), and non-residents jump to 30% (from 10%). Flip the "Old 10%" toggle above to see exactly how much this reform costs 2025 年以前，M-REIT 配息給個人採 10% 最終扣繳。自 YA2026 起落日：居民須將 REIT 所得併入綜所稅按邊際稅率課（30% 級距的本國人現在課 30%，不再是 10%），非居民則從 10% 跳到 30%。切上方「舊制 10%」對照，看這次稅改實際成本

🌏 The foreigner triple-tax外國人三重稅

A non-resident buying KL bricks faces three compounding hits: an 8% flat MOT stamp duty at entry (Budget 2026, vs a local's ~3%), rental income taxed at a flat 30% with no relief band, and RPGT that never falls below 10% — even after 6+ years. The shorter the hold, the more the 8% entry fee alone can swamp any gain. ⛰️ The RPGT Year-6 cliff (locals)RPGT 第 6 年懸崖（本國人）

For citizens & PRs, RPGT is 15% at Year 5 but 0% from Year 6. Selling one year too early can hand the government a five-figure cheque for nothing. ⚖️ Leverage only fires above the mortgage rate槓桿要在房貸利率之上才點得著

A mortgage pays off only when the property appreciates faster than the loan costs — so the break-even for leverage is simply the mortgage rate itself. Above it, every extra turn of LTV multiplies the gain; below it, leverage multiplies the bleed. That mechanism, not any tax, is often the real reason a leveraged KL condo trails the REIT — the same down payment could control more bricks, but only pays off once growth clears the borrowing cost. 房貸只有在房價漲得比借錢成本快時才划算——所以槓桿的損益分歧點就是房貸利率本身。高於它，每加一分 LTV 都放大獲利；低於它，槓桿放大失血。這個機制（跟稅無關）常常才是槓桿買 KL 公寓輸給 REIT 的真正原因——同一筆頭期款確實能控制更多磚頭，但要等房價漲幅越過借錢成本才划算。

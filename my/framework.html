The Framework Backtest — Five Factors, Twelve Markets 通用框架回測 · 五因子 × 十二市場

2010–20242010–2024 Ranks only, no point estimates只測排序，不測點位 Updated Jul 2026更新至 2026年7月
Bottom line一句話結論 We generalised this site's Malaysia analysis into a five-factor framework and ran four falsifiable tests on twelve markets with real data. The most valuable finding is a failure: money variables alone have no power to rank 2010–2024 house-price growth across markets (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.15, and the real-rate sign comes out wrong). The framework survives only in its full form, where institutions and supply multiply the money term — and the tests below check that this is not just an excuse. 我們把本站的馬來西亞分析一般化成五因子框架，用真實數據對十二個市場跑了四個可證偽測試。最有價值的發現是一個「不成立」：單靠貨幣變數，完全排不出 2010–2024 各市場的房價漲幅順序（Spearman ρ 約 0.15，實際利率甚至方向相反）。框架只有在完整形式下成立：制度與供給必須乘在貨幣項上。而下面的測試，就是在檢驗這句話不只是藉口。

Method, Declared Up Front方法宣言，先講清楚

  • Ranks and patterns only. We test whether variables order the twelve markets correctly (Spearman rank correlation), never point estimates.只測排序與型態。我們檢驗變數能不能排對十二個市場的順序（Spearman 排名相關），不做點位預測。
  • Rules defined before application. Index rules, drawdown windows and sample rules were fixed before the correlations were read; the one mid-course rule change (a drawdown window artifact) is disclosed with both variants stored.打分規則先定義後套用。指數換算、回撤視窗與樣本規則在讀相關係數之前就定好；唯一一次中途改規則（回撤視窗假象）已揭露，兩套數字都保存。
  • Real data or nothing. Every number carries a source (BIS, FRED, World Bank, IMF, official statistics first). Where a clean figure could not be verified, the market is excluded from that test and the exclusion is shown. Nothing is interpolated.只用實際資料。每個數字都帶出處（BIS、FRED、世界銀行、IMF、官方統計優先）。查不到乾淨數字的市場，從該測試剔除並揭露，絕不內插。
  • Check us. All raw values, sources, rules and exclusions are in data/framework-backtest.json.歡迎核對。所有原始數值、來源、規則與剔除清單都在 data/framework-backtest.json。

Test 1 · Money vs the Price Ranking — It FailsTest 1 · 貨幣 vs 房價排名 —— 不成立

This is the headline result of the whole study, and it is a negative one: money alone explains almost nothing about which markets rose most.這是整份研究的頭條結果，而且是個否定的結果：單看貨幣，幾乎解釋不了哪個市場漲得多。

Method and Result方法與結果

Hypothesis (written before computing): markets with faster broad-money growth and lower average real policy rates should rank higher on 2010–2024 nominal house-price growth. Result: Spearman ρ between money growth and price growth is +0.147 (n=12). Between the average real policy rate and price growth it is +0.188 (n=10) — the hypothesis needs a negative sign, so this is not weak support, it is the wrong direction. A composite score, z(money growth) − z(real rate), reaches only +0.224.假設（計算前寫定）：廣義貨幣增速越快、平均實際政策利率越低的市場，2010–2024 名目房價漲幅排名應該越前。結果：貨幣增速對房價漲幅的 Spearman ρ 是 +0.147（n=12）；平均實際利率對房價漲幅是 +0.188（n=10）。假設要求負號，所以這不是「支持力道弱」，是方向錯了。複合分數 z(貨幣增速)−z(實際利率) 也只有 +0.224。

The scatter shows why. Korea had the second-fastest money growth and nearly the lowest price growth (macroprudential caps did the filtering). Malaysia had the highest average real policy rate in the sample (+0.79%) and still posted near-top price growth. Hong Kong had the loosest real money conditions and only mid-table growth. Whatever ranked these markets, it was not the monetary dial.散點圖說明了原因。南韓的貨幣增速全樣本第二快，房價漲幅卻幾乎墊底（宏觀審慎上限做了過濾）。馬來西亞的平均實際政策利率全樣本最高（+0.79%），漲幅卻在最前段。香港的實質貨幣條件最鬆，漲幅只在中段。真正替這些市場排序的，不是貨幣那顆旋鈕。

What this does and does not mean. It kills the standalone claim that "money sets the water level" across markets. It is consistent with the full framework, where credit filters, supply and institutions multiply the money term. But we state the risk honestly: once multipliers are allowed, almost any outcome can be rationalised, and the framework becomes hard to falsify. That is exactly why Tests 2 to 4 exist — each checks one multiplier against an independent prediction, on its own data.這代表什麼、不代表什麼。它否定了「貨幣決定水位」作為單獨命題在跨市場層面的成立。它與框架的完整形式相容：信貸過濾器、供給與制度乘在貨幣項上。但風險要誠實講：一旦允許乘數，幾乎任何結果都能被合理化，框架就變得難以被證偽。這正是 Test 2 到 4 存在的理由：每一個都用獨立資料，檢驗一個乘數的一項獨立預測。

Test 2 · Downside Stickiness in 2022–23Test 2 · 2022–23 緊縮下的下檔黏性

The Natural Experiment一場天然實驗

The 2022–23 global tightening hit every market with roughly the same shock. Prediction (written first): the higher the floating-rate share of the mortgage stock, the deeper the nominal drawdown. Drawdown rule: maximum nominal peak-to-trough on the BIS quarterly index, window 2021Q1–2024Q4. One disclosed rule change: our first window rule ("peak = max within 2021–2023") hid the drawdown of any market that fell and then recovered to new highs inside the window (Australia), so we switched to standard max-drawdown; both variants are stored in the JSON.2022–23 的全球緊縮，用大致相同的力道打到每個市場。預測（先寫）：房貸存量的浮動利率佔比越高，名目回撤越深。回撤規則：BIS 季度指數在 2021Q1–2024Q4 視窗內的最大峰谷。一次已揭露的規則變更：第一版視窗規則（「峰＝2021–2023 窗內最大值」）會把窗內先跌後創高的市場（澳洲）判成零回撤，所以改用標準最大回撤；兩套數字都存在 JSON 裡。

Result: supported, with a disclosed sample rule. On the eight markets with a numeric floating share, ρ = −0.429. Japan, however, never applied the treatment: the BOJ did not tighten in 2022–23, so a 74%-floating market saw no rate shock. Excluding Japan on that pre-stated ground, ρ = −0.679 (n=7) — a clear ranking in the predicted direction. The extremes behave: the US (4.7% floating) barely dipped, Hong Kong (all floating) fell 27%.結果：成立，附帶一條已揭露的樣本規則。八個有數值浮動佔比的市場，ρ = −0.429。但日本根本沒有接受「處置」：BOJ 在 2022–23 沒有緊縮，所以一個 74% 浮動的市場沒有經歷利率衝擊。依這條事先陳述的理由剔除日本後，ρ = −0.679（n=7），排序方向清楚符合預測。兩個極端都對：美國（浮動 4.7%）幾乎沒跌，香港（幾乎全浮動）跌了 27%。

The Malaysia residual. Malaysia is annotated, not scored: its mortgages are predominantly floating (SBR is pegged 1:1 to the OPR) but no official stock percentage is published. On the prediction, a floating-rate market that got 125bp of hikes should have fallen visibly. Its maximum drawdown was −0.2%. That gap is the institutional stickiness the Malaysia pages describe: the Housing Development Act structure and developer holding behaviour absorb the shock in volumes and unsold stock instead of prices.馬來西亞殘差。馬來西亞是註記點，不入計分：它的房貸幾乎全浮動（SBR 與 OPR 一比一連動），但沒有官方存量佔比可用。照預測，一個吃了 125 個基點升息的浮動利率市場應該明顯下跌，它的最大回撤卻是 −0.2%。這道缺口，就是本站馬來西亞各頁描述的制度性黏性：《房屋發展法》結構與發展商的持有行為，把衝擊吸收在成交量與滯銷餘屋裡，而不是價格裡。

Test 3 · Corridor vs Index, Inside CountriesTest 3 · 國內離散：走廊 vs 大盤

Three Real Pairs三組真實配對

Prediction: industrial corridors explain dispersion inside a country, in both directions — they overshoot up and they overshoot down. Two pairs have clean same-source series; the Malaysian pair is disclosed as partial.預測：產業走廊解釋一國之內的離散，而且是雙向的：上行超漲，下行也超跌。兩組配對有乾淨的同源序列；馬來西亞那組以「部分」揭露。

  • Austin vs the US (Zillow ZHVI, computed from the raw file): Austin rose +71.2% from Feb 2020 to its June 2022 peak, then fell −19.1% to Dec 2024, while the national index never drew down more than ~2%. Net result 2019–2024: Austin +40.7%, US +46.9% — after the round trip, the corridor underperforms the index it was supposed to beat.Austin 對 美國大盤（Zillow ZHVI，原始檔自算）：Austin 從 2020 年 2 月到 2022 年 6 月的高點漲了 +71.2%，之後跌 −19.1% 到 2024 年 12 月；全國指數同期回撤不到約 2%。2019–2024 淨結果：Austin +40.7%、美國 +46.9%。一趟來回之後，走廊反而輸給它本來要贏的大盤。
  • Hsinchu vs Taipei (JCIC mortgage data, same source, 2015Q3–2024Q3): the science-park corridor rose +103% (Hsinchu County; Hsinchu City +71%) while the capital-city core rose +7%. A fourteen-to-one gap inside one country, produced by one industry.新竹 對 台北（聯徵中心房貸資料，同一來源，2015Q3–2024Q3）：科學園區走廊漲了 +103%（新竹縣；新竹市 +71%），首都核心只漲 +7%。同一個國家裡十四倍的差距，由一個產業製造出來。
  • Johor corridor vs Malaysia national — partial. The national index rose +14.8% over 2019–2024 (BIS/NAPIC); the JB RTS corridor printed +20.8% in 2025 alone (NAPIC, via the Market Report). The bases differ (five-year vs single-year), so this pair is shown in the table only, not charted, until the state-level NAPIC series is assembled.柔佛走廊 對 馬來西亞大盤——部分。全國指數 2019–2024 漲 +14.8%（BIS／NAPIC）；新山 RTS 走廊光 2025 一年就 +20.8%（NAPIC，見市場報告）。兩者口徑不同（五年 對 單年），所以這組只列表、不入圖，等州級 NAPIC 序列補齊。

Result: supported, with the honest half attached. Corridors do explain intra-country dispersion. But Austin shows the same mechanism running in reverse: corridor exposure is leverage on an industry, not a one-way ticket. That cuts directly against reading Johor's corridor as guaranteed outperformance.結果：成立，並附上誠實的另一半。走廊確實解釋國內離散。但 Austin 展示了同一個機制反著跑的樣子：走廊曝險是對單一產業的槓桿，不是單程票。這直接打在「柔佛走廊必然跑贏」這種讀法上。

What the Framework Says About Malaysia這個框架對馬來西亞說了什麼

Put Malaysia back into the twelve-market coordinate system and it is a genuine outlier three times over. It posted near-top price growth (+125%) with the sample's highest average real policy rate — money cannot explain its rise. It carries a floating-rate mortgage stock yet recorded a −0.2% maximum drawdown through the global tightening — markets cannot explain its downside either. And its foreign-buyer tax sits early on a ratchet whose later steps (Singapore's 60%, outright bans) are documented in this table. Every one of those anomalies points at the same place: institutions, not money, set both the ceiling and the floor. That is the Malaysia thesis of this site, now located on a map instead of asserted.把馬來西亞放回十二市場座標系，它是三重異常值。它用全樣本最高的平均實際政策利率，跑出前段的漲幅（+125%），貨幣解釋不了它的上行。它的房貸幾乎全浮動，卻在全球緊縮裡只有 −0.2% 的最大回撤，市場邏輯也解釋不了它的下檔。而它的外資稅，正站在一條棘輪的早段，後面的階梯（新加坡的 60%、直接禁購）都寫在上面那張表裡。三個異常指向同一個地方：定住天花板和地板的都是制度，不是貨幣。這就是本站的馬來西亞論點，只是現在它被放在一張地圖上，而不是用說的。

Limitations, Stated Plainly限制，照實列出

  • The supply column is empty. The equation's supply-elasticity term was never scored: published estimates were only available to us as charts, not verifiable tables, so no market has a supply value. Test 1's failure could partly be supply doing the ranking — we cannot show that yet.供給欄是空的。式子裡的供給彈性項從頭到尾沒被計分：既有估計我們只取得圖表、沒有可核對的數值表，所以沒有任何市場有供給值。Test 1 的不成立，有一部分可能正是供給在排序，這一點目前無法展示。
  • Small sample, mixed vintages. Twelve markets is enough for ranks, not for significance claims. Several cells cover shorter periods (SG money to 2020, CA to 2023, NZ 12 of 15 years) and floating shares are a 2022 snapshot. All flagged in the table and the JSON.樣本小、口徑混齡。十二個市場夠做排序，不夠做顯著性宣告。若干欄位期間較短（新加坡貨幣到 2020、加拿大到 2023、紐西蘭 15 年中的 12 年），浮動佔比是 2022 年的快照。表格與 JSON 均有標註。
  • Narrative overfit risk. A framework with multipliers can rationalise anything after the fact. Our defence is procedural, not rhetorical: rules before results, disclosed exclusions, a headline test we let fail in public, and raw data at data/framework-backtest.json.敘事過擬合風險。帶乘數的框架，事後什麼都能圓。我們的防線是程序性的，不是修辭性的：規則先於結果、剔除全揭露、頭條測試當眾放它不成立、原始數據放在 data/framework-backtest.json。

The Framework Backtest — Five Factors, Twelve Markets 通用框架回測 · 五因子 × 十二市場

2010–20242010–2024 Ranks only, no point estimates只測排序，不測點位 Updated Jul 2026更新至 2026年7月
Bottom line一句話結論 We generalised this site's Malaysia analysis into a five-factor framework and ran four falsifiable tests on twelve markets with real data. The most valuable finding is a failure: money variables alone have no power to rank 2010–2024 house-price growth across markets (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.15, and the real-rate sign comes out wrong). The framework survives only in its full form, where institutions and supply multiply the money term — and the tests below check that this is not just an excuse. 我們把本站的馬來西亞分析一般化成五因子框架，用真實數據對十二個市場跑了四個可證偽測試。最有價值的發現是一個「不成立」：單靠貨幣變數，完全排不出 2010–2024 各市場的房價漲幅順序（Spearman ρ 約 0.15，實際利率甚至方向相反）。框架只有在完整形式下成立：制度與供給必須乘在貨幣項上。而下面的測試，就是在檢驗這句話不只是藉口。

Method, Declared Up Front方法宣言，先講清楚

  • Ranks and patterns only. We test whether variables order the twelve markets correctly (Spearman rank correlation), never point estimates.只測排序與型態。我們檢驗變數能不能排對十二個市場的順序（Spearman 排名相關），不做點位預測。
  • Rules defined before application. Index rules, drawdown windows and sample rules were fixed before the correlations were read; the one mid-course rule change (a drawdown window artifact) is disclosed with both variants stored.打分規則先定義後套用。指數換算、回撤視窗與樣本規則在讀相關係數之前就定好；唯一一次中途改規則（回撤視窗假象）已揭露，兩套數字都保存。
  • Real data or nothing. Every number carries a source (BIS, FRED, World Bank, IMF, official statistics first). Where a clean figure could not be verified, the market is excluded from that test and the exclusion is shown. Nothing is interpolated.只用實際資料。每個數字都帶出處（BIS、FRED、世界銀行、IMF、官方統計優先）。查不到乾淨數字的市場，從該測試剔除並揭露，絕不內插。
  • Check us. All raw values, sources, rules and exclusions are in data/framework-backtest.json.歡迎核對。所有原始數值、來源、規則與剔除清單都在 data/framework-backtest.json。

Test 1 · Money vs the Price Ranking — It FailsTest 1 · 貨幣 vs 房價排名 —— 不成立

This is the headline result of the whole study, and it is a negative one: money alone explains almost nothing about which markets rose most.這是整份研究的頭條結果，而且是個否定的結果：單看貨幣，幾乎解釋不了哪個市場漲得多。

Method and Result方法與結果

Hypothesis (written before computing): markets with faster broad-money growth and lower average real policy rates should rank higher on 2010–2024 nominal house-price growth. Result: Spearman ρ between money growth and price growth is +0.147 (n=12). Between the average real policy rate and price growth it is +0.188 (n=10) — the hypothesis needs a negative sign, so this is not weak support, it is the wrong direction. A composite score, z(money growth) − z(real rate), reaches only +0.224.假設（計算前寫定）：廣義貨幣增速越快、平均實際政策利率越低的市場，2010–2024 名目房價漲幅排名應該越前。結果：貨幣增速對房價漲幅的 Spearman ρ 是 +0.147（n=12）；平均實際利率對房價漲幅是 +0.188（n=10）。假設要求負號，所以這不是「支持力道弱」，是方向錯了。複合分數 z(貨幣增速)−z(實際利率) 也只有 +0.224。

The scatter shows why. Korea had the second-fastest money growth and nearly the lowest price growth (macroprudential caps did the filtering). Malaysia had the highest average real policy rate in the sample (+0.79%) and still posted near-top price growth. Hong Kong had the loosest real money conditions and only mid-table growth. Whatever ranked these markets, it was not the monetary dial.散點圖說明了原因。南韓的貨幣增速全樣本第二快，房價漲幅卻幾乎墊底（宏觀審慎上限做了過濾）。馬來西亞的平均實際政策利率全樣本最高（+0.79%），漲幅卻在最前段。香港的實質貨幣條件最鬆，漲幅只在中段。真正替這些市場排序的，不是貨幣那顆旋鈕。

What this does and does not mean. It kills the standalone claim that "money sets the water level" across markets. It is consistent with the full framework, where credit filters, supply and institutions multiply the money term. But we state the risk honestly: once multipliers are allowed, almost any outcome can be rationalised, and the framework becomes hard to falsify. That is exactly why Tests 2 to 4 exist — each checks one multiplier against an independent prediction, on its own data.這代表什麼、不代表什麼。它否定了「貨幣決定水位」作為單獨命題在跨市場層面的成立。它與框架的完整形式相容：信貸過濾器、供給與制度乘在貨幣項上。但風險要誠實講：一旦允許乘數，幾乎任何結果都能被合理化，框架就變得難以被證偽。這正是 Test 2 到 4 存在的理由：每一個都用獨立資料，檢驗一個乘數的一項獨立預測。

Test 2 · Downside Stickiness in 2022–23Test 2 · 2022–23 緊縮下的下檔黏性

The Natural Experiment一場天然實驗

The 2022–23 global tightening hit every market with roughly the same shock. Prediction (written first): the higher the floating-rate share of the mortgage stock, the deeper the nominal drawdown. Drawdown rule: maximum nominal peak-to-trough on the BIS quarterly index, window 2021Q1–2024Q4. One disclosed rule change: our first window rule ("peak = max within 2021–2023") hid the drawdown of any market that fell and then recovered to new highs inside the window (Australia), so we switched to standard max-drawdown; both variants are stored in the JSON.2022–23 的全球緊縮，用大致相同的力道打到每個市場。預測（先寫）：房貸存量的浮動利率佔比越高，名目回撤越深。回撤規則：BIS 季度指數在 2021Q1–2024Q4 視窗內的最大峰谷。一次已揭露的規則變更：第一版視窗規則（「峰＝2021–2023 窗內最大值」）會把窗內先跌後創高的市場（澳洲）判成零回撤，所以改用標準最大回撤；兩套數字都存在 JSON 裡。

Result: supported, with a disclosed sample rule. On the eight markets with a numeric floating share, ρ = −0.429. Japan, however, never applied the treatment: the BOJ did not tighten in 2022–23, so a 74%-floating market saw no rate shock. Excluding Japan on that pre-stated ground, ρ = −0.679 (n=7) — a clear ranking in the predicted direction. The extremes behave: the US (4.7% floating) barely dipped, Hong Kong (all floating) fell 27%.結果：成立，附帶一條已揭露的樣本規則。八個有數值浮動佔比的市場，ρ = −0.429。但日本根本沒有接受「處置」：BOJ 在 2022–23 沒有緊縮，所以一個 74% 浮動的市場沒有經歷利率衝擊。依這條事先陳述的理由剔除日本後，ρ = −0.679（n=7），排序方向清楚符合預測。兩個極端都對：美國（浮動 4.7%）幾乎沒跌，香港（幾乎全浮動）跌了 27%。

The Malaysia residual. Malaysia is annotated, not scored: its mortgages are predominantly floating (SBR is pegged 1:1 to the OPR) but no official stock percentage is published. On the prediction, a floating-rate market that got 125bp of hikes should have fallen visibly. Its maximum drawdown was −0.2%. That gap is the institutional stickiness the Malaysia pages describe: the Housing Development Act structure and developer holding behaviour absorb the shock in volumes and unsold stock instead of prices.馬來西亞殘差。馬來西亞是註記點，不入計分：它的房貸幾乎全浮動（SBR 與 OPR 一比一連動），但沒有官方存量佔比可用。照預測，一個吃了 125 個基點升息的浮動利率市場應該明顯下跌，它的最大回撤卻是 −0.2%。這道缺口，就是本站馬來西亞各頁描述的制度性黏性：《房屋發展法》結構與發展商的持有行為，把衝擊吸收在成交量與滯銷餘屋裡，而不是價格裡。

Three Real Pairs三組真實配對

Prediction: industrial corridors explain dispersion inside a country, in both directions — they overshoot up and they overshoot down. Two pairs have clean same-source series; the Malaysian pair is disclosed as partial.預測：產業走廊解釋一國之內的離散，而且是雙向的：上行超漲，下行也超跌。兩組配對有乾淨的同源序列；馬來西亞那組以「部分」揭露。

  • Austin vs the US (Zillow ZHVI, computed from the raw file): Austin rose +71.2% from Feb 2020 to its June 2022 peak, then fell −19.1% to Dec 2024, while the national index never drew down more than ~2%. Result: supported, with the honest half attached. Corridors do explain intra-country dispersion. But Austin shows the same mechanism running in reverse: corridor exposure is leverage on an industry, not a one-way ticket. That cuts directly against reading Johor's corridor as guaranteed outperformance.結果：成立，並附上誠實的另一半。走廊確實解釋國內離散。但 Austin 展示了同一個機制反著跑的樣子：走廊曝險是對單一產業的槓桿，不是單程票。這直接打在「柔佛走廊必然跑贏」這種讀法上。

Test 5 · The State Machine, BacktestedTest 5 · 狀態分類回測

Could pre-declared rules have sorted markets into "primed" and "capped" in advance? We scanned 176 market-years. The answer is narrow: the rules almost never cry wolf, but they also almost never bark.事先寫死的規則，能不能提前把市場分成「蓄勢」與「封頂」？我們掃了 176 個市場年。答案很窄：這套規則幾乎不誤報，但也幾乎不出聲。

Method方法

Four "primed" rules (volume compression, cheap carry, balance-sheet room, cheap valuation) and three "capped" rules (high inventory, heavy household debt, stretched valuation) were written down with fixed thresholds before any result was computed, then applied annually to eight markets over judgment years 2000–2021. Primed = at least 3 of 4 S-rules with at most 1 C-rule; capped = at least 2 C-rules with at most 1 S-rule. Forward returns are the next 3 and 5 years of the same nominal indices used in Test 1. Missing inputs score zero and are disclosed, never patched: markets with fewer than three computable S-rules (NZ, JP, MY) can never reach "primed" at all. Full rules, series and sources are in the JSON.四條「蓄勢」規則（量的壓縮、便宜的 carry、資產負債表空間、估值不貴）與三條「封頂」規則（高庫存、家庭債務沉重、估值拉伸），在計算任何結果之前就以固定門檻寫死，然後逐年套用在八個市場、判定年 2000–2021。蓄勢＝四條 S 規則至少中三條且 C 至多一條；封頂＝C 至少兩條且 S 至多一條。前向報酬用 Test 1 同一套名目指數的後續三年與五年。缺的輸入計零分並揭露、絕不補值：可計算 S 規則不足三條的市場（紐西蘭、日本、馬來西亞）根本到不了「蓄勢」。完整規則、序列與來源都在 JSON。

Forward nominal returns by state (boxes: min / Q1 / median / Q3 / max). Primed n=5, neutral n=121, capped n=49 (3-year horizon).各狀態的前向名目報酬（箱形：最小值／Q1／中位數／Q3／最大值）。蓄勢 n=5、中性 n=121、封頂 n=49（三年期）。

What the 176 Market-Years Say176 個市場年說了什麼

  • As a false-positive filter: strong. Only 5 primed calls in 176 market-years, all in the US 2011–2016 window, and every one delivered (+16% to +23% over 3 years, +31% to +43% over 5). Zero primed calls came in below +10%. The primed box is the narrowest in the chart, and entirely positive.作為偽陽性過濾器：強。176 個市場年裡只發出 5 次蓄勢判定，全部落在美國 2011–2016 這個窗口，而且每一次都兌現（三年 +16% 到 +23%、五年 +31% 到 +43%）。沒有任何一次蓄勢判定低於 +10%。蓄勢是圖中最窄的箱形，而且全部為正。
  • The one systemic bust was flagged. The only market-years where all three C-rules fired together are US 2005, 2006 and 2007, and they preceded the only systemic crash in the sample: 3-year forward returns of −15%, −27% and −24%.唯一的系統性崩盤被標到了。全樣本裡三條 C 規則同時觸發的市場年只有美國 2005、2006、2007，而它們正好排在樣本中唯一一次系統性崩盤之前：後續三年 −15%、−27%、−24%。
  • As an upside finder: weak. Median 3-year returns barely separate (primed 17.9% vs neutral 17.4%), and 35 neutral market-years delivered 3-year gains of 30% or more that the rules never caught, including the US in 2019–2020 and Taiwan in 2019–2020. Recall is 5 out of 176.作為找大漲的工具：弱。三年中位數幾乎沒有分離（蓄勢 17.9% 對 中性 17.4%），而且有 35 個中性市場年後續三年漲了 30% 以上、規則全數漏接，包括 2019–2020 的美國與 2019–2020 的台灣。召回率是 176 分之 5。
  • The capped bucket has a systematic flaw, reported as found. 33 of 49 capped calls still returned +10% or more over 3 years. The culprit is the C2 level trigger (household debt ≥80% of GDP): it stamps chronically leveraged compounders (Australia, New Zealand, Canada) as capped for decades while they kept rising. As written, "capped" means a worse downside tail (Q1 of +2.4% vs neutral's +7.7%), not falling prices. The threshold was not adjusted after seeing this.封頂桶有一個系統性缺陷，照查獲原樣報告。49 次封頂判定裡有 33 次後續三年仍漲了 +10% 以上。禍首是 C2 的水平觸發（家庭債務 ≥GDP 的 80%）：它把慢性高槓桿的複利市場（澳洲、紐西蘭、加拿大）連續幾十年蓋上封頂章，而那些市場一路續漲。照規則字面，「封頂」的意思是下檔尾部更差（Q1 為 +2.4%，中性為 +7.7%），不是價格會跌。看到結果後，門檻沒有被回調。

The methodological lesson, stated as a boundary and not a claim. Compressing this site's qualitative framework into four quantifiable rules loses most of its inputs: the supply-gap stock, demographic peaks, visible catalysts and institutional filters that the qualitative checklist leans on were never quantified here. That richer input set has not been tested; the honest position is that its predictive value remains unverified, not that it has been demonstrated. What this backtest does establish is narrower: the quantifiable core almost never issues a false primed call, and the full three-rule capped signal preceded the one systemic bust in the sample.方法論教訓，寫成邊界而不是能力。把本站的質性框架壓縮成四條可量化規則，會丟掉它大部分的輸入：質性檢查清單所依賴的供給缺口存量、人口高峰、可見催化劑與制度過濾器，在這裡從未被量化。那套更豐富的輸入還沒有被檢驗過；誠實的說法是它的預測力尚未驗證，而不是已被證明。這次回測真正確立的東西比較窄：可量化的核心幾乎不發出錯誤的蓄勢訊號，而三條封頂規則齊發的訊號，出現在樣本中唯一一次系統性崩盤之前。

Conditioning on the Liquidity Regime用流動性 regime 條件化

2020–2024 was a historic liquidity anomaly, so we condition on it rather than delete it. The regime variable is mechanical and was fixed in advance: the 12-month change in G3 central-bank total assets (Fed, ECB, BOJ via FRED; the PBoC and BoE are not on FRED, so G3 is used and disclosed), converted to US dollars and divided by combined G3 nominal GDP. Above +5% is an easing year, below −2% a tightening year. The series marks 2008, 2011, 2016, 2020 and 2021 as easing and 2022–2024 as tightening. Two honest notes: the rule was expected to also catch 2009 and 2012–13 and does not (the Fed was flat across 2009 year-end to year-end, and ECB contraction offset QE3 in 2012–13), and the USD conversion embeds currency-valuation effects. Thresholds were not adjusted after seeing this.2020–2024 是史上級的流動性特例，所以我們用「條件化」處理它，而不是刪掉它。Regime 變數是機械式的、事先定死：G3 央行總資產（Fed、ECB、BOJ，經 FRED；PBoC 與 BoE 不在 FRED 上，故用 G3 並揭露）的 12 個月變動，換算美元後除以 G3 名目 GDP 合計。高於 +5% 算放水年、低於 −2% 算抽水年。序列標出的放水年是 2008、2011、2016、2020、2021，抽水年是 2022–2024。兩個誠實註記：這條規則原本預期也會抓到 2009 與 2012–13，結果沒有（Fed 資產在 2009 年底對年底大致持平，而 2012–13 的 ECB 縮表抵銷了 QE3）；美元換算內含匯率估值效應。看到結果後，門檻沒有被調整。

Two things move once the regime is held fixed. The primed-versus-neutral separation gets clearer inside normal windows (22.3 vs 16.6). And the capped bucket turns out to be harmless in calm conditions (median +20.6%) — its damage is concentrated where windows cross regimes (Q1 of −1.2%): in this sample, busts happen around regime shifts, not in calm years. Note also that every judgment year from 2017 to 2021 has its forward window soaked in the 2020–2024 storm. On US 2019 specifically, the regime frame gives the correct wording of the miss: the framework should not be asked to predict a global liquidity flood, but it should be able to rank who rises most when the flood arrives.把 regime 固定住之後，有兩件事變了。蓄勢對中性的分離在正常窗裡變清楚（22.3 對 16.6）。而封頂桶在平靜年份其實無害（中位 +20.6%），它的殺傷力集中在跨 regime 的窗（Q1 為 −1.2%）：在這個樣本裡，崩盤發生在 regime 轉換的前後，不在平靜年。另外注意：2017 到 2021 的每一個判定年，前向窗都整段泡在 2020–2024 的風暴裡。關於 US 2019，regime 框架給出這次漏判的正確表述：框架不該被要求預測全球放水，但它該給得出「放水來時誰漲最多」的排序。

The same flood, different boats. As a first test of that requirement: across the twelve markets, the 2019Q4–2021Q4 nominal gain (NZ +46%, US +28%, AU +28% … HK +3.6%, MY +3.1%) ranks best on five-year momentum of the 25–39 first-buyer cohort (UN WPP 2024): Spearman ρ = +0.44, ahead of broad-money growth (+0.29) and the real policy rate (−0.12, right sign, negligible). New Zealand tops both lists; Japan, Hong Kong and Thailand sit at the bottom of both. Taiwan is the clear wrong-way point (cohort shrinking, prices +22%), which points at a money-substitute demand channel these slow variables do not carry. This is the first calibration of the framework's slow layer: moderate, not strong, and reported as found.同一場水，不同的船。作為對這個要求的第一次檢驗：十二個市場 2019Q4–2021Q4 的名目漲幅（紐西蘭 +46%、美國 +28%、澳洲 +28%……香港 +3.6%、馬來西亞 +3.1%），用25–39 歲首購年齡層的五年動能（UN WPP 2024）排序效果最好：Spearman ρ = +0.44，高於廣義貨幣增速（+0.29）與實際政策利率（−0.12，方向對、幅度可忽略）。紐西蘭在兩張榜單都是第一；日本、香港、泰國在兩張都墊底。台灣是明顯的反向點（年齡層在縮、房價 +22%），指向這組慢變量沒有涵蓋的貨幣替代需求管道。這是框架慢層的第一次校準：中等，不強，照查獲原樣報告。

Test 6 · Turn Sequencing — Credit Leads; Volume Is the TurnTest 6 · 轉折次序：信貸領先，量本身就是轉折

At the verifiable turning points in this sample, mortgage credit turned at or before the price in 8 of 10 cases, a median of 2.5 quarters early. If you build a property clock, the hand is credit.在樣本裡可驗證的轉折點上，房貸信貸有 10 次裡 8 次在價格之前或同時轉向，中位領先 2.5 季。如果要做一座房地產時鐘，指針是信貸。

Method方法

Turns were dated by rules fixed in advance, on quarterly nominal indices. A hard top is a local maximum followed by a fall of at least 8% within 12 quarters; a hard bottom is the mirror image; a soft top is the quarter where price growth first drops below 2% year-on-year after running above 8% within the previous 8 quarters. Volume turns are sign changes in the year-on-year of a 4-quarter moving average, held for at least 2 quarters; credit turns are local peaks and troughs of mortgage-stock growth. Leads are signed distances to the nearest same-direction turn within 12 quarters — the first implementation matched only non-negative leads, a bias toward confirming the hypothesis, and was corrected before any result was read. Markets: US and UK carry the full chain; Taiwan (official MOI index, from 2012Q3), Malaysia and New Zealand carry turn dating only, because their quarterly volume and credit series could not be verified (New Zealand's REINZ volumes were unavailable; the US volume series is Census new-home sales, a disclosed substitution for the NAR history FRED no longer carries). Everything is in the JSON.轉折用事先定死的規則在季度名目指數上標定。硬頂＝局部最高點、其後 12 季內回落至少 8%；硬底是鏡像；軟頂＝價格年增率在前 8 季內曾高於 8%、首次跌破 2% 的那一季。量的轉折＝成交量 4 季移動平均的年增率變號並維持至少 2 季；信貸轉折＝房貸餘額年增率的局部峰谷。領先是帶正負號的距離：取 12 季內最近的同向轉折。第一版實作只配對非負的領先，是一個偏向證實假設的錯誤，在讀任何結果之前就修正並揭露。市場：美國與英國有完整三鏈；台灣（內政部官方指數，2012Q3 起）、馬來西亞、紐西蘭只做轉折標定，因為季度量與信貸序列查證不到（紐西蘭 REINZ 成交量取不到；美國量序列用普查局新屋銷售，是 FRED 已無 NAR 歷史後的替代，已揭露）。全部細節在 JSON。

How this reconciles with "watch the volume" in the Malaysia pages. It does not contradict it; it locates it. In sticky, institutionally damped markets, the top does not arrive as a price decline at all: it arrives as volume and momentum disappearing while the price barely moves. There, volume is not a leading indicator of the price — volume is the turn itself. The price series never confirms, which is exactly why the Market Report tells a Malaysian reader to underwrite on transactions and overhang rather than wait for the index to speak.這與馬來西亞各頁「看量」的指引如何調和。不是矛盾，而是定位。在黏性、被制度阻尼的市場，頂部根本不以價格下跌的形式到來：它以量與增速消失、價格幾乎不動的形式到來。在那裡，量不是價格的領先指標——量本身就是轉折。價格序列永遠不會給確認，這正是市場報告要馬來西亞的讀者盯成交量與餘屋、而不是等指數開口的原因。

Zero Hard Turns Is a Finding, Not a Defect零硬轉折是發現，不是缺陷

Run one set of rules over five markets and the sticky ones simply produce no events. Taiwan's official index has zero hard tops and zero hard bottoms in its entire history; the 2014–16 episode registers only as a soft top (2015Q4), after which the price sat within 1% for two years while annual transactions collapsed by a third. Malaysia has gone 27 years without a single 8% drawdown since the Asian Financial Crisis, and its 2012–13 deceleration took roughly 24 quarters to travel from over 8% growth to under 2% — too gradual for the soft-top rule to fire at all. This silence is the strongest evidence of institutional damping in the whole study, and it matches the Test 2 stickiness grouping.同一套規則掃五個市場，黏性市場乾脆不產生事件。台灣的官方指數全史零硬頂、零硬底；2014–16 那一段只留下一個軟頂（2015Q4），之後價格兩年動不到 1%，而年成交量崩掉三分之一。馬來西亞自亞洲金融風暴以來27 年沒有出現過一次 8% 回撤，2012–13 的減速花了約 24 季才從年增 8% 以上走到 2% 以下，漸進到連軟頂規則都無法觸發。這種沉默是整份研究裡制度阻尼最強的證據，而且與 Test 2 的黏性分組一致。

What the Framework Says About Malaysia這個框架對馬來西亞說了什麼

Put Malaysia back into the twelve-market coordinate system and it is a genuine outlier three times over. It posted near-top price growth (+125%) with the sample's highest average real policy rate — money cannot explain its rise. It carries a floating-rate mortgage stock yet recorded a −0.2% maximum drawdown through the global tightening — markets cannot explain its downside either. And its foreign-buyer tax sits early on a ratchet whose later steps (Singapore's 60%, outright bans) are documented in this table. Every one of those anomalies points at the same place: institutions, not money, set both the ceiling and the floor. That is the Malaysia thesis of this site, now located on a map instead of asserted.把馬來西亞放回十二市場座標系，它是三重異常值。它用全樣本最高的平均實際政策利率，跑出前段的漲幅（+125%），貨幣解釋不了它的上行。它的房貸幾乎全浮動，卻在全球緊縮裡只有 −0.2% 的最大回撤，市場邏輯也解釋不了它的下檔。而它的外資稅，正站在一條棘輪的早段，後面的階梯（新加坡的 60%、直接禁購）都寫在上面那張表裡。三個異常指向同一個地方：定住天花板和地板的都是制度，不是貨幣。這就是本站的馬來西亞論點，只是現在它被放在一張地圖上，而不是用說的。

Limitations, Stated Plainly限制，照實列出

  • The supply column is empty. The equation's supply-elasticity term was never scored: published estimates were only available to us as charts, not verifiable tables, so no market has a supply value. Test 1's failure could partly be supply doing the ranking — we cannot show that yet.供給欄是空的。式子裡的供給彈性項從頭到尾沒被計分：既有估計我們只取得圖表、沒有可核對的數值表，所以沒有任何市場有供給值。Test 1 的不成立，有一部分可能正是供給在排序，這一點目前無法展示。
  • Small sample, mixed vintages. Twelve markets is enough for ranks, not for significance claims. Several cells cover shorter periods (SG money to 2020, CA to 2023, NZ 12 of 15 years) and floating shares are a 2022 snapshot. All flagged in the table and the JSON.樣本小、口徑混齡。十二個市場夠做排序，不夠做顯著性宣告。若干欄位期間較短（新加坡貨幣到 2020、加拿大到 2023、紐西蘭 15 年中的 12 年），浮動佔比是 2022 年的快照。表格與 JSON 均有標註。
  • Narrative overfit risk. A framework with multipliers can rationalise anything after the fact. Our defence is procedural, not rhetorical: rules before results, disclosed exclusions, a headline test we let fail in public, and raw data at data/framework-backtest.json.敘事過擬合風險。帶乘數的框架，事後什麼都能圓。我們的防線是程序性的，不是修辭性的：規則先於結果、剔除全揭露、頭條測試當眾放它不成立、原始數據放在 data/framework-backtest.json。

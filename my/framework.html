The Compass, Backtested — And the Evidence Behind It 羅盤回測 · 以及它背後的證據

2010–20242010–2024 Ranks only, no point estimates只測排序，不測點位 Updated Jul 2026更新至 2026年7月
Bottom line一句話結論 This page backtests the Property Clock. Its instrument is the Compass — where prices are headed, read from two forces: house-price momentum and the direction of credit. The headline test leads: do the compass's four cells rank the next three years of house-price returns in the order declared before looking at any outcome? They do in the US, Malaysia and Japan; they break in Taiwan, and that break is a short, one-directional sample, not a broken mechanism. Everything below the compass is the evidence it is built from the right forces — seven falsifiable tests on twelve markets, including the instructive failure that forced the design: money variables alone cannot rank 2010–2024 house-price growth across markets (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.15, and the real-rate sign comes out wrong). The framework survives only where credit, supply and institutions multiply the money term. 這一頁在回測房地產時鐘。它的儀器是羅盤——房價往哪去，讀自兩股力：房價動能，與信貸的方向。頭條測試先上場：羅盤的四個象限，能不能把未來三年的房價報酬排進「看任何結果之前就宣告」的順序？在美國、馬來西亞與日本成立；在台灣不成立，而那個不成立是樣本短、幾乎單向，不是機制壞了。羅盤以下的一切，是證明它建立在對的力上的證據——十二個市場上的七個可證偽測試，包含那個逼出整個設計的「不成立」：單靠貨幣變數，排不出 2010–2024 各市場的房價漲幅順序（Spearman ρ 約 0.15，實際利率甚至方向相反）。框架只有在信貸、供給與制度乘上貨幣項時才成立。

The Core Backtest · The Compass核心回測 · 羅盤

The ordering holds in the US, Malaysia and Japan — the market that actually crashed. It breaks in Taiwan, and the reason is a short, one-directional sample, not a wrong mechanism.排序在美國、馬來西亞與日本——真正崩過盤的那個市場——都成立。它在台灣不成立，原因是樣本短、幾乎單向，不是機制錯了。

Method方法

Momentum × credit, four cells, tested against forward 3-year house-price returns across the four Clock markets.動能 × 信貸，四個象限，用四個時鐘市場的三年前向房價報酬去檢驗。

Prediction, declared before reading any outcome: forward 3-year nominal house-price returns should rank the compass's four cells Fuelled ≥ Draining ≥ Reflating ≥ Starved. Momentum (trailing price z, smoothed) is the dominant axis; credit direction (trailing real credit-impulse z) is the secondary tie-break. This is theory stated up front, not a fit — the cells and their forward-return medians already existed in the Clock's own data (compass-us.json and its TW/MY/JP siblings, built by scripts/build_compass.py); this test only orders them against the declared prediction.預測，在看任何結果之前先宣告：三年前向名目房價報酬應該把羅盤的四個象限排成有燃料 ≥ 資金退 ≥ 資金回流 ≥ 斷炊。動能（平滑後的落後價格 z）是主軸；信貸方向（落後的實質信貸衝量 z）是次要的平手判定。這是先講的理論，不是套出來的：這些象限與各自的前向報酬中位數早就存在時鐘自己的資料裡（compass-us.json 及其台／馬／日姊妹檔，由 scripts/build_compass.py 產生）；這次測試只是拿它們去對那條宣告的排序打分。

Two measurements, computed by scripts/backtest_compass.py directly from the compass JSON — no raw series refetched. First, the four cells' realized 3-year-forward medians (full quarterly history per cell) checked against the six pairwise inequalities the declared ordering implies. Second, the sharper test: a Spearman rank correlation between each quarter's declared cell rank (fuelled=4 best … starved=1 worst) and that quarter's realized forward return, over every quarter with a non-null forward value (warmup quarters excluded). Verdict thresholds were fixed before any market was read: ρ≥0.30 holds, 0.10–0.30 weak, below 0.10 breaks.兩項量測，都由 scripts/backtest_compass.py 直接從羅盤 JSON 算出，沒有重新抓任何原始序列。第一，四個象限各自的三年前向報酬中位數（每個象限的完整季度歷史），拿去對照宣告排序隱含的六組兩兩不等式。第二，也是較嚴格的測試：每一季「宣告的象限排名」（有燃料=4 最佳……斷炊=1 最差）與該季「實際前向報酬」之間的 Spearman 排名相關，涵蓋所有前向值非空的季度（暖機季排除）。裁決門檻在看任何市場結果之前就定死：ρ≥0.30 成立、0.10–0.30 偏弱、低於 0.10 不成立。

Where it holds, and why it isn't sharp. In the US and Japan, starved correctly sits at the bottom (the min of the four cells in both), but fuelled and reflating swap places — by 0.4pp in the US (19.4 vs 19.8, on n=70 vs a thin n=19), by 3.7pp in Japan (6.0 vs 9.7, n=64 vs n=21). Reflating is the rarest cell in both markets and the swap is a thin-cell artifact, not a reversal: momentum still separates the top from the bottom cleanly, and the quarter-level ρ (0.58 and 0.40) confirms the ordering is real, just not razor-sharp at the top. Malaysia is the cleanest hold on the top half (fuelled > draining > both bottom cells, ρ=0.71) with its own near-miss at the bottom — reflating and starved swap (8.2 vs 11.7, n=7 vs n=24), both thin, both close.成立的地方，以及為什麼不夠銳利。在美國與日本，斷炊都正確落在最底（兩個市場的四象限最小值），但有燃料與資金回流互換位置——美國差 0.4 個百分點（19.4 對 19.8，n=70 對薄薄的 n=19），日本差 3.7 個百分點（6.0 對 9.7，n=64 對 n=21）。資金回流在兩個市場都是最罕見的象限，這個互換是薄象限的雜訊，不是反轉：動能仍然乾淨地把頂與底分開，季度層級的 ρ（0.58 與 0.40）確認排序是真的，只是頂端不夠銳利。馬來西亞在上半場最乾淨（有燃料 > 資金退 > 底下兩格，ρ=0.71），自己的小失誤在底部——資金回流與斷炊互換（8.2 對 11.7，n=7 對 n=24），兩個都薄、也都接近。

Where it breaks. Taiwan's starved cell realized +34.1%, the single highest median of any cell in any market — the exact opposite of what "starved" is supposed to mean. The reason is visible in Test 6: Taiwan's official index has zero hard tops and zero hard bottoms in its entire history, a 2001–2026 sample that is short and nearly one-directional. A momentum-down, credit-down quarter in a market that has never really turned down is just the pause before the next leg of one long bull run, not a regime with its own downside distribution. ρ=0.08 on n=74 says the same thing with a number: essentially no relationship. This is reported as a sample problem, not evidence against the mechanism — but it is reported, not smoothed over. Australia joins Taiwan in this break — added to the panel after this test's ordering was already published, AU's starved cell (+22.3%, n=44) sits well above its fuelled cell (+16.1%, n=34), the same no-crash signature, and ρ=−0.16 on n=118 confirms it; see the hold-out section below for the full disclosure.不成立的地方。台灣的斷炊象限實現報酬是 +34.1%，是所有市場、所有象限裡最高的一個中位數——正好是「斷炊」這個名字該有的意思的反面。原因在 Test 6 看得到：台灣的官方指數全史零硬頂、零硬底，2001–2026 的樣本又短又幾乎單向。一個動能向下、信貸向下的季度，落在一個從未真正下跌過的市場裡，只是下一段長多頭的中場休息，不是一個有自己下檔分佈的獨立體制。ρ=0.08、n=74，用數字說了同一件事：幾乎沒有關係。這被記為樣本問題，不是機制被推翻的證據——但它照樣被記下來，沒有被抹平。澳洲在這一點上跟台灣同一國——這個市場是在本測試的排序已經公佈之後才加進面板的，它的斷炊象限（+22.3%，n=44）明顯高於有燃料象限（+16.1%，n=34），一樣的「沒真跌過」訊號，ρ=−0.16、n=118 也確認了這件事；完整揭露見下方樣本外一節。

The Gate Is the Walk-Forward Test真正的門檻是前向走查測試

Full-period fit answers the wrong question — it lets a candidate be chosen with the benefit of the whole history, including the future. The walk-forward test asks the question that matters: at each selection date, using only quarters that had already matured (their 3-year forward window closed), would the process have picked the winning candidate and applied it going forward? Amendment 1 — the Metric Itself Had a Bug修正說明一：指標本身有問題

The first run, scored under the walk-forward composite exactly as specified in the frozen protocol, came back FAIL — with the US degrading by −0.13, past the −0.05 disqualifying line. Before that number was published, a review of the mechanics found the composite was concatenating raw scores from whichever candidate won at each selection date. Candidates sit on incommensurable scales: the baseline is a rank from 1 to 4, C1/C2/C4 are continuous z-sums. Method方法

Protocol frozen 2026-07-14, before any evaluation run: scripts/compass_v3_protocol.md. Full per-market detail: data/compass-backtest-v3.json.協定於 2026-07-14 凍結，在任何評測跑之前：scripts/compass_v3_protocol.md。逐市場完整細節：data/compass-backtest-v3.json。

Two binary targets, declared before any 2×2 table was computed. T_ABS (absolute loss): bad=1 iff the forward 3-year nominal return is negative — a straight loss, no benchmark needed. T_REL (relative weakness): bad=1 iff the forward return falls below the bottom quartile of the market's own matured history, where the quartile at each quarter uses only outcomes that had already resolved twelve quarters earlier (u ≤ t−12) — an expanding, look-ahead-safe cutoff, never the full-sample quartile computed with hindsight. The warning under test is W_PRIM = the compass's momentum cell reads starved (no fuel) and its own supply-glut flag is lit, in the same quarter — no fuel, plus a glut on top.兩個二元標的，在算出任何 2×2 表之前就宣告。T_ABS（絕對虧損）：三年前向名目報酬為負即 bad=1——單純虧損，不需要對照基準。T_REL（相對弱勢）：前向報酬落在該市場自身已成熟歷史的最低四分位以下即 bad=1，而每一季的四分位切點只用十二季前已經揭曉的結果（u ≤ t−12）計算——一個擴張、不偷看未來的切點，絕不是事後算出的全樣本四分位。被檢驗的警報是 W_PRIM＝羅盤動能格讀到斷炊（沒燃料）且自身的供給過剩旗標同一季亮燈——沒燃料，外加餘屋過剩。

A market only earns an absolute-loss verdict if it clears an applicability gate: at least 8 matured quarters of realized loss in its own history, disclosed either way. The protocol's own stated prior expected the US and Japan to clear that gate and Taiwan and Malaysia not to — the sample disagreed with itself on one side of that prior, and the disagreement is reported below, not smoothed over. Where more than one market qualifies, effect sizes are pooled with a Mantel–Haenszel odds ratio, stratified by market — no fitted model, stratified counts only. Pass bars were fixed before the first table ran: ABS claim needs odds ratio ≥3 with ≥8 warning-quarters and precision at least twice the base rate, in both the US and Japan, plus pooled MH OR ≥3; REL claim needs odds ratio ≥2 in at least 3 of the 4 markets, plus pooled MH OR ≥2.5; two conditioners were pre-declared and would only be adopted if either raised the odds ratio by ≥50% over the plain warning without recall falling below two-thirds of the plain warning's own recall, in both the US and Japan. AU confirms the instrument's boundary rather than extending it. The Finding Under the Finding — Monotone Series Defeat Both Bases發現底下的發現 —— 單調序列讓兩個基準都失效

The re-run also partly overturned the audit's own framing, and that belongs on the record as much as the verdict does. The audit's second exhibit was JP 2023Q3 — vacancy at 13.6%, essentially the all-time high of 13.8% — flagged as the trailing basis's fatigue failure. The expanding basis misses it too: the expanding z reads −0.71, short of the −1 trigger, because Japan's vacancy has risen near-monotonically for fifty years, so the whole-history median has drifted up alongside the chronic glut it was supposed to measure the glut against. That is the general lesson: for a monotonically deteriorating series, any relative basis fatigues — trailing re-bases in six years, expanding re-bases over decades, but both re-base. Catching an "absolute glut" would require an external anchor — a level that means glut in every market — and no such cross-market standard exists for series as different as a Japanese survey rate and a Malaysian overhang count. So the trailing basis stays, and the reason is stated exactly: not because it is conceptually right — the audit's complaint about that stands — but because it empirically dominates the alternative on the only busts available to test against. That tension is now on the page, which is where a frozen protocol says it belongs.重跑同時也部分推翻了審計自己的問題設定，而這件事跟裁決一樣屬於紀錄的一部分。審計的第二件展示品是日本 2023Q3——空置率 13.6%，幾乎就是史上最高的 13.8%——被標為滾動基準的疲乏失靈案例。結果擴張基準同樣漏掉它：擴張 z 讀 −0.71，不到 −1 的觸發線，因為日本的空置率五十年來近乎單調上升，整段歷史的中位數跟著那個「本來要拿來對照餘屋」的慢性餘屋一起往上漂。這就是一般化的教訓：對一條單調惡化的序列，任何相對基準都會疲乏——滾動基準六年就重新定錨，擴張基準要幾十年，但兩者都在重新定錨。要抓「絕對的餘屋過剩」，需要一個外部錨——一個在每個市場都代表過剩的水位——而對日本的調查比率和馬來西亞的餘屋戶數這種天差地遠的序列，這種跨市場標準並不存在。所以滾動基準留了下來，理由照實寫明：不是因為它在概念上是對的——審計對這一點的投訴依然成立——而是因為在僅有的可供檢驗的崩盤上，它在實證上壓過了替代方案。這個張力如今就寫在頁面上，凍結協定說它就該在這裡。

The Seven Tests Behind It背後的七個測試

Why the compass sits on momentum and credit and nothing else on the predictive axis — seven falsification tests on twelve markets, including the failure that forced the design.羅盤的預測軸為何只放動能與信貸、不放別的——十二個市場上的七個證偽測試，包含那個逼出這個設計的「不成立」。

Test 1 · Money vs the Price Ranking — It FailsTest 1 · 貨幣 vs 房價排名 —— 不成立

This is the failure that shaped the whole design, and it is a negative one: money alone explains almost nothing about which markets rose most.這是形塑了整個設計的那個「不成立」，而且是個否定的結果：單看貨幣，幾乎解釋不了哪個市場漲得多。

Method and Result方法與結果

Hypothesis (written before computing): markets with faster broad-money growth and lower average real policy rates should rank higher on 2010–2024 nominal house-price growth. Result: Spearman ρ between money growth and price growth is +0.147 (n=12). Between the average real policy rate and price growth it is +0.188 (n=10) — the hypothesis needs a negative sign, so this is not weak support, it is the wrong direction. A composite score, z(money growth) − z(real rate), reaches only +0.224.假設（計算前寫定）：廣義貨幣增速越快、平均實際政策利率越低的市場，2010–2024 名目房價漲幅排名應該越前。結果：貨幣增速對房價漲幅的 Spearman ρ 是 +0.147（n=12）；平均實際利率對房價漲幅是 +0.188（n=10）。假設要求負號，所以這不是「支持力道弱」，是方向錯了。複合分數 z(貨幣增速)−z(實際利率) 也只有 +0.224。

The scatter shows why. Korea had the second-fastest money growth and nearly the lowest price growth (macroprudential caps did the filtering). Malaysia had the highest average real policy rate in the sample (+0.79%) and still posted near-top price growth. Hong Kong had the loosest real money conditions and only mid-table growth. Whatever ranked these markets, it was not the monetary dial.散點圖說明了原因。南韓的貨幣增速全樣本第二快，房價漲幅卻幾乎墊底（宏觀審慎上限做了過濾）。馬來西亞的平均實際政策利率全樣本最高（+0.79%），漲幅卻在最前段。香港的實質貨幣條件最鬆，漲幅只在中段。真正替這些市場排序的，不是貨幣那顆旋鈕。

What this does and does not mean. It kills the standalone claim that "money sets the water level" across markets. It is consistent with the full framework, where credit filters, supply and institutions multiply the money term. But we state the risk honestly: once multipliers are allowed, almost any outcome can be rationalised, and the framework becomes hard to falsify. That is exactly why Tests 2 to 4 exist — each checks one multiplier against an independent prediction, on its own data.這代表什麼、不代表什麼。它否定了「貨幣決定水位」作為單獨命題在跨市場層面的成立。它與框架的完整形式相容：信貸過濾器、供給與制度乘在貨幣項上。但風險要誠實講：一旦允許乘數，幾乎任何結果都能被合理化，框架就變得難以被證偽。這正是 Test 2 到 4 存在的理由：每一個都用獨立資料，檢驗一個乘數的一項獨立預測。

Test 2 · Downside Stickiness in 2022–23Test 2 · 2022–23 緊縮下的下檔黏性

The Natural Experiment一場天然實驗

The 2022–23 global tightening hit every market with roughly the same shock. Prediction (written first): the higher the floating-rate share of the mortgage stock, the deeper the nominal drawdown. Drawdown rule: maximum nominal peak-to-trough on the BIS quarterly index, window 2021Q1–2024Q4. One disclosed rule change: our first window rule ("peak = max within 2021–2023") hid the drawdown of any market that fell and then recovered to new highs inside the window (Australia), so we switched to standard max-drawdown; both variants are stored in the JSON.2022–23 的全球緊縮，用大致相同的力道打到每個市場。預測（先寫）：房貸存量的浮動利率佔比越高，名目回撤越深。回撤規則：BIS 季度指數在 2021Q1–2024Q4 視窗內的最大峰谷。一次已揭露的規則變更：第一版視窗規則（「峰＝2021–2023 窗內最大值」）會把窗內先跌後創高的市場（澳洲）判成零回撤，所以改用標準最大回撤；兩套數字都存在 JSON 裡。

Result: supported, with a disclosed sample rule. On the eight markets with a numeric floating share, ρ = −0.429. Japan, however, never applied the treatment: the BOJ did not tighten in 2022–23, so a 74%-floating market saw no rate shock. Excluding Japan on that pre-stated ground, ρ = −0.679 (n=7) — a clear ranking in the predicted direction. The extremes behave: the US (4.7% floating) barely dipped, Hong Kong (all floating) fell 27%.結果：成立，附帶一條已揭露的樣本規則。八個有數值浮動佔比的市場，ρ = −0.429。但日本根本沒有接受「處置」：BOJ 在 2022–23 沒有緊縮，所以一個 74% 浮動的市場沒有經歷利率衝擊。依這條事先陳述的理由剔除日本後，ρ = −0.679（n=7），排序方向清楚符合預測。兩個極端都對：美國（浮動 4.7%）幾乎沒跌，香港（幾乎全浮動）跌了 27%。

The Malaysia residual. Malaysia is annotated, not scored: its mortgages are predominantly floating (SBR is pegged 1:1 to the OPR) but no official stock percentage is published. On the prediction, a floating-rate market that got 125bp of hikes should have fallen visibly. Its maximum drawdown was −0.2%. Test 5 · The State Machine, BacktestedTest 5 · 狀態分類回測

Could pre-declared rules have sorted markets into "primed" and "capped" in advance? We scanned 176 market-years. The answer is narrow: the rules almost never cry wolf, but they also almost never bark.事先寫死的規則，能不能提前把市場分成「蓄勢」與「封頂」？我們掃了 176 個市場年。答案很窄：這套規則幾乎不誤報，但也幾乎不出聲。

Method方法

Four "primed" rules (volume compression, cheap carry, balance-sheet room, cheap valuation) and three "capped" rules (high inventory, heavy household debt, stretched valuation) were written down with fixed thresholds before any result was computed, then applied annually to eight markets over judgment years 2000–2021. Primed = at least 3 of 4 S-rules with at most 1 C-rule; capped = at least 2 C-rules with at most 1 S-rule. Forward returns are the next 3 and 5 years of the same nominal indices used in Test 1. Missing inputs score zero and are disclosed, never patched: markets with fewer than three computable S-rules (NZ, JP, MY) can never reach "primed" at all. Full rules, series and sources are in the JSON.四條「蓄勢」規則（量的壓縮、便宜的 carry、資產負債表空間、估值不貴）與三條「封頂」規則（高庫存、家庭債務沉重、估值拉伸），在計算任何結果之前就以固定門檻寫死，然後逐年套用在八個市場、判定年 2000–2021。蓄勢＝四條 S 規則至少中三條且 C 至多一條；封頂＝C 至少兩條且 S 至多一條。前向報酬用 Test 1 同一套名目指數的後續三年與五年。缺的輸入計零分並揭露、絕不補值：可計算 S 規則不足三條的市場（紐西蘭、日本、馬來西亞）根本到不了「蓄勢」。完整規則、序列與來源都在 JSON。

  • As a false-positive filter: strong. Only 5 primed calls in 176 market-years, all in the US 2011–2016 window, and every one delivered (+16% to +23% over 3 years, +31% to +43% over 5). Zero primed calls came in below +10%. The primed box is the narrowest in the chart, and entirely positive.作為偽陽性過濾器：強。176 個市場年裡只發出 5 次蓄勢判定，全部落在美國 2011–2016 這個窗口，而且每一次都兌現（三年 +16% 到 +23%、五年 +31% 到 +43%）。沒有任何一次蓄勢判定低於 +10%。蓄勢是圖中最窄的箱形，而且全部為正。
  • The one systemic bust was flagged. The only market-years where all three C-rules fired together are US 2005, 2006 and 2007, and they preceded the only systemic crash in the sample: 3-year forward returns of −15%, −27% and −24%.唯一的系統性崩盤被標到了。全樣本裡三條 C 規則同時觸發的市場年只有美國 2005、2006、2007，而它們正好排在樣本中唯一一次系統性崩盤之前：後續三年 −15%、−27%、−24%。

  • The capped bucket has a systematic flaw, reported as found. 33 of 49 capped calls still returned +10% or more over 3 years. Conditioning on the Liquidity Regime用流動性 regime 條件化

2020–2024 was a historic liquidity anomaly, so we condition on it rather than delete it. The regime variable is mechanical and was fixed in advance: the 12-month change in G3 central-bank total assets (Fed, ECB, BOJ via FRED; the PBoC and BoE are not on FRED, so G3 is used and disclosed), converted to US dollars and divided by combined G3 nominal GDP. Above +5% is an easing year, below −2% a tightening year. The series marks 2008, 2011, 2016, 2020 and 2021 as easing and 2022–2024 as tightening. Two honest notes: the rule was expected to also catch 2009 and 2012–13 and does not (the Fed was flat across 2009 year-end to year-end, and ECB contraction offset QE3 in 2012–13), and the USD conversion embeds currency-valuation effects. Test 6 · Turn Sequencing — Credit Leads; Volume Is the TurnTest 6 · 轉折次序：信貸領先，量本身就是轉折

At the verifiable turning points in this sample, mortgage credit turned at or before the price in 8 of 10 cases, a median of 2.5 quarters early. If you build a property clock, the hand is credit.在樣本裡可驗證的轉折點上，房貸信貸有 10 次裡 8 次在價格之前或同時轉向，中位領先 2.5 季。如果要做一座房地產時鐘，指針是信貸。

Method方法

Turns were dated by rules fixed in advance, on quarterly nominal indices. A hard top is a local maximum followed by a fall of at least 8% within 12 quarters; a hard bottom is the mirror image; a soft top is the quarter where price growth first drops below 2% year-on-year after running above 8% within the previous 8 quarters. Volume turns are sign changes in the year-on-year of a 4-quarter moving average, held for at least 2 quarters; credit turns are local peaks and troughs of mortgage-stock growth. Leads are signed distances to the nearest same-direction turn within 12 quarters — the first implementation matched only non-negative leads, a bias toward confirming the hypothesis, and was corrected before any result was read. Markets: US and UK carry the full chain; Taiwan (official MOI index, from 2012Q3), Malaysia and New Zealand carry turn dating only, because their quarterly volume and credit series could not be verified (New Zealand's REINZ volumes were unavailable; the US volume series is Census new-home sales, a disclosed substitution for the NAR history FRED no longer carries). Everything is in the JSON.轉折用事先定死的規則在季度名目指數上標定。硬頂＝局部最高點、其後 12 季內回落至少 8%；硬底是鏡像；軟頂＝價格年增率在前 8 季內曾高於 8%、首次跌破 2% 的那一季。量的轉折＝成交量 4 季移動平均的年增率變號並維持至少 2 季；信貸轉折＝房貸餘額年增率的局部峰谷。領先是帶正負號的距離：取 12 季內最近的同向轉折。第一版實作只配對非負的領先，是一個偏向證實假設的錯誤，在讀任何結果之前就修正並揭露。市場：美國與英國有完整三鏈；台灣（內政部官方指數，2012Q3 起）、馬來西亞、紐西蘭只做轉折標定，因為季度量與信貸序列查證不到（紐西蘭 REINZ 成交量取不到；美國量序列用普查局新屋銷售，是 FRED 已無 NAR 歷史後的替代，已揭露）。全部細節在 JSON。

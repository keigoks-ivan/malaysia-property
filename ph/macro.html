Bottom line總結 The Philippines is the mirror image of the ageing ASEAN economies. The population is still growing, real income per head has compounded faster in each successive decade — 2.84% in the 2000s, 3.03% in the 2010s, 4.91% from 2020 to 2025 — and the external balance sheet has been rebuilt from an 85.9%-of-GDP debt crisis in 1986 to 30.3% in 2025. None of the structural decline that defines Thailand is present here.

The problem is elsewhere, and it is specific. Bank lending against Metro Manila residential property broke in 2020 and has not been repaired. Approved mortgage counts in the capital fell from 21,925 in 2019 to 11,041 in 2025, a decline of 49.6%, while the rest of the country rose 15.8% over the same period. The past-due ratio on residential real-estate loans doubled from 5.74% at end-2019 to 12.20% at end-2020 and, six years later, is still 8.83% against a 2013–2017 baseline near 3.4%. Commercial-property delinquency, by contrast, returned to normal. The damage is concentrated in exactly one place: residential credit in the capital.

Two things fill the gap and both are cash rather than credit. Remittances reached US$39.6 billion in 2025 and, because the peso lost a third of its value against the dollar over thirteen years, the same dollars now buy far more Philippine property than they did. The second is foreign capital, which the 1987 Constitution confines to condominium units under a 40% collective ceiling. Neither channel runs through a loan officer, which is why the market has not cleared through price. Those mechanics are tested against the transaction data in the companion Metro Manila Property Market Report. 菲律賓與東協高齡化經濟體構成鏡像。人口仍在成長，實質人均所得的年均複合成長率逐個十年加速：2000 年代 2.84%、2010 年代 3.03%、2020 至 2025 年 4.91%；對外資產負債表已自 1986 年外債佔 GDP 85.9% 的債務危機重建至 2025 年的 30.3%。界定泰國處境的各項結構性衰退，於此均不存在。

問題在他處，且範圍明確。針對大馬尼拉住宅的銀行貸放於 2020 年中斷，其後未曾修復。首都的房貸核准件數自 2019 年的 21,925 件降至 2025 年的 11,041 件，減幅 49.6%；同期全國其餘地區增加 15.8%。銀行住宅類不動產放款的逾期比率自 2019 年底的 5.74% 升至 2020 年底的 12.20%，六年後仍為 8.83%，而 2013 至 2017 年的基線約 3.4%。相對地，商用類不動產的逾期率已回到正常水準。劣化集中於單一位置：首都的住宅授信。

填補該缺口的有兩項來源，且均屬現金而非授信。匯款於 2025 年達 396 億美元；由於披索兌美元十三年間貶值三分之一，同一筆美元現可購入的菲律賓房地產顯著增加。第二項為外國資本，其範圍由 1987 年憲法限定於受 40% 集合上限約束的公寓單位。兩者均不經由授信審查，此為市場未透過價格出清的成因。上述機制與成交資料的對照，詳見《大馬尼拉住宅市場報告》。

Philippine headline inflation ran at 2.42% in February 2026, then 4.07% in March and 7.23% in April, easing to 6.36% in June. The move is both level and base: the national index rose from 131.2 to 136.5 between February and April 2026, while the comparison months of April to June 2025 were themselves falling. Against a policy rate of 4.25% to 4.75% through that window, the derived real policy rate moved from +4.30% in July 2025 to −2.73% in April 2026, a swing of seven points in nine months. The 2022 precedent sets the expected duration2022 年的前例界定了持續期間

This is not the first negative real rate in the series. Inflation peaked at 8.68% in January 2023 and the real policy rate bottomed at −3.59% in June 2022. The response was 450 basis points of tightening between May 2022 and October 2023, and the real rate did not return to positive territory until 2023. Every other market on this site is analysed with the BIS credit-to-households series, which is the standard cross-country measure of household leverage. The Philippines is not in that database. The two candidate identifiers, QPHHAM770A and QPHPAM770A, both return no series; this was confirmed against the FRED search interface and is a coverage gap at BIS, not a retrieval failure. Part 3 · The Politics — Who Sets the Rules政治篇 · 規則的制定者

In Thailand the foreign-ownership limit is a statute, so a parliament could move it. In the Philippines it is a constitutional article, so nothing short of amending the constitution can.在泰國，外資持有上限訂於法律，國會即可修改；在菲律賓，該限制訂於憲法條文，非經修憲不能變動。

The foreign-ownership limit is constitutional, which places it beyond both administration and legislation外資持有限制的位階在憲法，因此行政與立法均無法移動

The 1987 Constitution, Article XII, Sections 7 and 81987 年憲法第十二條第 7、8 項

Section 7 prohibits the transfer of private land to anyone not qualified to acquire public land, that is, to any individual who is not a Philippine citizen or any corporation that is not at least 60% Filipino-owned, with succession the only exception. Section 8 creates a narrow further exception for natural-born citizens who have lost their citizenship. The restriction on land is therefore in the constitutional text, not in a statute.第 7 項禁止將私有土地移轉予不具取得公地資格者，即非菲律賓籍個人或菲籍持股未達 60% 之法人，繼承為唯一例外；第 8 項為喪失菲律賓籍之天生公民另設一項範圍狹窄的例外。土地持有限制因而載於憲法本文，而非法律。

The Condominium Act, RA 4726, Section 5公寓法（RA 4726）第 5 條

The 40% ceiling operates at the level of the condominium corporation that holds the common areas, not at the level of the individual unit transaction. That legal structure is why a foreign buyer can hold a condominium certificate of title at all. Section 5 makes any transfer that would take the corporation past the ceiling void, not merely refusable.40% 上限運作於持有共有部分之區分所有權公司層級，而非個別單位交易層級。此一法律結構即為外國買方得以持有公寓所有權狀（CCT）的原因。第 5 條規定，凡將使該公司外資比例超過上限之移轉一律無效，而非僅得予駁回。

How the 40% is counted, RA 4726 Section 6(c)40% 的計算基礎，RA 4726 第 6(c) 條

The statutory default is one equal share per unit, that is, a headcount of units rather than a floor-area weighting. The section expressly permits the master deed to provide otherwise, and floor-area weighting is understood to be the common commercial practice. This page treats the statutory default as verified and the practice as unverified, because no case-by-case check of master deeds was performed.法定預設為每單位一等分，即按戶數計而非按樓地板面積加權。該條明文允許主契約另為約定，而以樓地板面積加權一般理解為業界慣例。本頁將法定預設列為已驗證，將實務慣例列為未驗證，因未逐案查核主契約。

Two separate long-lease regimes兩套互不相同的長期租地制度

Presidential Decree 471 of 1974 allows a general foreign lessee 25 years renewable for 25, fifty in total, for any purpose. Republic Act 7652 of 1993 allows 50 years renewable for 25, seventy-five in total, but only for qualified investments meeting a minimum investment threshold in industrial estates, export processing, agro-industry or tourism projects. An individual buying a home falls under the first, not the second, and the two are frequently conflated.1974 年第 471 號總統令允許一般外國承租人 25 年並得續約 25 年，合計五十年，不限用途。1993 年投資人租賃法（RA 7652）允許 50 年並得續約 25 年，合計七十五年，惟僅適用於工業區、加工出口、農工企業或觀光專案中達最低投資額門檻之合格投資。個人購屋適用前者而非後者，兩制常被混用。

The practical consequence is that the entire liberalisation channel available in Thailand does not exist here. A Thai government could raise the condominium quota to 75% with an act of parliament, which is why the proposal recurs there and why every valuation that assumed it has been wrong. In the Philippines the 40% ceiling on condominium corporations exists because the corporation holds land, and the 60% Filipino floor on land-holding corporations is in Article XII. Raising the condominium ceiling requires a constitutional amendment, not a bill. For an investor the correct posture is therefore not to monitor legislative sentiment but to price the current article and treat any change as outside the investable horizon.實務結果為：泰國所具備的整條自由化管道於此並不存在。泰國政府得以國會立法將公寓配額提高至 75%，此即該提案於當地反覆出現、且所有將其納入假設的估值均被證明錯誤的原因。在菲律賓，區分所有權公司的 40% 上限之所以存在，係因該公司持有土地，而持有土地之法人須有 60% 菲籍持股的規定載於第十二條。提高公寓上限須經修憲，而非立法。對投資人而言，正確立場並非追蹤立法動向，而是以現行憲法條文定價，並將任何變動視為可投資期間以外的事項。

Three tradeable political rules三條可交易的政治規律

One — price the constitutional article, not the reform debate. The condominium ceiling is downstream of Article XII. Charter change has been discussed in Philippine politics for decades without altering the economic provisions. A valuation that assumes a higher foreign quota is assuming a constitutional amendment, and should be labelled as such rather than as policy risk.其一 —— 以憲法條文定價，不以改革討論定價。公寓上限為第十二條的下游規定。修憲議題於菲律賓政治中已討論數十年，其經濟條款未曾變動。將更高外資配額納入假設的估值，實際上是在假設一次修憲，應據此標示，而非列為政策風險。

Two — the executive can remove a tenant class faster than any market can reprice. Executive Order 74 was signed on 5 November 2024 with a compliance deadline of 31 December 2024. Three — the binding constraint here is a bank's credit committee, not a ministry. The variables that moved this market between 2019 and 2026 were the approval count and the delinquency ratio, neither of which is set by policy. Watch the BSP quarterly releases, not the legislative calendar.其三 —— 本市場的實質約束來自銀行的授信委員會，而非部會。2019 至 2026 年間驅動本市場的變數為核准件數與逾期比率，兩者均非由政策設定。相對於泰國（一道移民命令即於一夜之間改變外資買方門檻），追蹤菲律賓政治動向的邊際效益偏低。應追蹤菲律賓央行的季度發布，而非立法議程。

The conclusions live in the interactions關鍵結論位於交互作用

  • E enters twice with opposite signs. A weaker peso raises R × E for the domestic remittance buyer and lowers the dollar value of the same asset for the foreign holder. Between 2013Q1 and 2026Q1 the nominal index rose 152.1% in pesos and 74.0% in dollars; over the same period peso remittances rose roughly twice as fast as dollar remittances. The two parties to the same transaction read the currency in opposite directions, which is why domestic and foreign demand have not turned together.E 以相反符號進入方程式兩次。披索走弱使本地匯款買方的 R × E 提高，同時降低外國持有人所持同一資產的美元價值。2013Q1 至 2026Q1 期間，名目指數以披索計上漲 152.1%，以美元計上漲 74.0%；同期披索計匯款的成長率約為美元計的兩倍。同一筆交易的兩造對匯率的解讀方向相反，此即本地與外資需求未同步反轉的成因。
  • C × product mix is the binding interaction. Condominium is 87% of NCR approvals and is the only title a foreign buyer may hold. The credit channel that halved is therefore the same channel that finances the foreign-accessible product's domestic resale market. A foreign buyer's exit price depends on a Filipino borrower's mortgage approval, and that approval count is at half its 2019 level.C × 產品結構為具約束力的交互作用。公寓佔 NCR 核准件數 87%，且為外國買方唯一可持有的產權形式。因此，腰斬的授信通道同時是為外資可及產品的本地二手市場提供融資的通道。外國買方的出場價格取決於本地借款人的房貸核准，而該核准件數僅為 2019 年水準的一半。
  • R × S is why the market has not cleared through price. Vacancy near 25% would normally force nominal price declines. It has not, because remittance-funded cash demand does not require the credit channel to function and is not disciplined by a valuation. High vacancy and a rising nominal index can coexist for as long as the cash channel holds, and the real index in Part 1 shows what that combination produces: three years of flat purchasing power under a rising headline number.R × S 為市場未透過價格出清的成因。接近 25% 的空置率通常將迫使名目價格下跌，惟此處並未發生，因匯款支撐的現金需求不以授信通道運作為前提，亦不受鑑價約束。高空置率與上升的名目指數得以並存，其持續期間取決於現金管道是否維持；第一部的實質指數已呈現該組合的結果：在上升的表面數字之下，購買力三年持平。
  • r × leverage separates the two buyer types cleanly. A negative real policy rate is favourable to a hard asset, but the Philippine borrower cannot access it: mortgage rates moved 48 basis points while policy moved 275. The Answer — Where, What, When結論篇 · 區位、產品、時點

Locations: the capital is where the credit broke, and it is also the only place a foreigner can buy區位：授信中斷之處即為首都，亦為外國人唯一能買之處

The geographic split in this dataset is unusually clean. Mortgage approvals outside NCR rose 15.8% between 2019 and 2025 while NCR fell 49.6%; national population compounded at 1.55% a year over the last census interval while NCR managed 0.93% and lost share. On both the credit and the demographic reading, the growth is in the provinces. But the constitutional structure sends foreign capital in the opposite direction. A foreign buyer may hold a condominium unit and may not hold land, and condominium supply outside the capital is thin, so the product that is legally available and the geography that is economically growing do not overlap. The correct posture is not to resolve that contradiction but to state it: a foreign position in Philippine residential property is a position in NCR condominium, and NCR condominium is the segment whose domestic financing channel is running at half its 2019 volume. Anyone who is not constrained by the ownership rules — a Filipino citizen or a former natural-born citizen under Article XII Section 8 — faces a materially different opportunity set from the one this page describes.本資料集的地理分野異常清晰。2019 至 2026 年間，NCR 以外地區的房貸核准件數增加 15.8%，NCR 則減少 49.6%；最近一個普查間隔內，全國人口年均複合成長 1.55%，NCR 為 0.93% 且佔比下降。無論自信貸端或人口端讀取，成長均在地方。惟憲法結構將外國資本導向相反方向。外國買方得持有公寓單位而不得持有土地，首都以外的公寓供給稀薄，故法律上可及的產品與經濟上成長的地理並不重疊。正確立場並非解決此一矛盾，而是明確陳述之：外資於菲律賓住宅的部位即為 NCR 公寓部位，而 NCR 公寓正是本地融資通道量能僅存 2019 年一半的分部。不受持有規則約束者（菲律賓公民，或第十二條第 8 項所指之原天生公民）所面對的機會集合，與本頁所描述者有實質差異。

Products: rank by whether the exit buyer needs a mortgage產品：依出場買方是否需要房貸排序

The ranking principle in this market is not quality and not location. It is whether the eventual resale requires a bank to approve someone. A unit that will be resold to a remittance-funded cash buyer sits behind a channel that grew 111% in peso terms between 2013 and 2025 and has not declined in twenty-one years. A unit that will be resold to a mortgage applicant sits behind a channel that halved in the capital and carries an 8.83% past-due ratio. The same building can contain both. Average household size gives the practical filter: 3.83 persons in NCR and 3.35 in Makati, against 4.12 nationally, which is the demographic case for the smaller unit rather than the family-sized one. On leasehold structures, the applicable regime for an individual is Presidential Decree 471 at twenty-five years renewable for twenty-five, not the seventy-five-year investor lease under RA 7652, and the two are routinely conflated in marketing material. Verifying which regime a specific arrangement falls under is a legal question that precedes any pricing question.本市場的排序原則既非品質亦非區位，而在於未來的轉售是否需要銀行核准某人。將轉售予匯款支撐之現金買方的單位，其背後管道於 2013 至 2025 年間以披索計成長 111%，且二十一年間未曾衰退；將轉售予房貸申請人的單位，其背後管道於首都腰斬，並帶有 8.83% 的逾期比率。同一棟建物可同時包含兩者。平均家戶規模提供實務篩選：NCR 3.83 人、馬卡蒂 3.35 人，全國為 4.12 人，構成偏向小坪數而非家庭房型的人口學論據。關於租賃制結構，適用於個人的制度為第 471 號總統令的 25 年並得續約 25 年，而非 RA 7652 之七十五年投資人租賃，兩者於行銷資料中經常被混用。確認特定安排所適用的制度屬法律問題，其順序先於任何定價問題。

Timing: three clocks, and the fastest one has already turned時機：三個時鐘，最快的那個已經反轉

Fast clock — the real policy rate, months快時鐘 —— 實質政策利率，以月計

CPI went from 2.42% to 7.23% in two months and the derived real rate from +4.30% to −2.73% in nine. The policy rate has already been raised twice, in April and June 2026. The 2022 precedent resolved in roughly eighteen months, which places the observable checkpoint in late 2027.CPI 年增率兩個月內自 2.42% 升至 7.23%，推導的實質利率九個月內自 +4.30% 降至 −2.73%。政策利率已於 2026 年 4 月與 6 月兩度調升。2022 年的前例約於十八個月內完成，據此可觀察的檢核點落在 2027 年底。

Medium clock — the residential loan book, two to four years中時鐘 —— 住宅貸放組合，二到四年

The past-due ratio has fallen every year since 2020 and is still at 8.83%. At the observed rate of improvement it reaches the pre-2018 baseline no earlier than the end of the decade. This is the clock that determines when the capital's approval count can recover, and it is the one to track quarterly.逾期比率自 2020 年起逐年下降，現仍為 8.83%。依已觀察到的改善速度，其回到 2018 年前基線的時點不早於本十年結束。此為決定首都核准件數何時得以回升的時鐘，應逐季追蹤。

Slow clock — the constitution, indefinite慢時鐘 —— 憲法，無明確期限

The 40% ceiling is downstream of Article XII and cannot be moved by an administration or a legislature. There is no date on this clock, and any model that assumes one is assuming a constitutional amendment. Price the current article.40% 上限為第十二條的下游規定，行政部門與立法機關均無法移動。此一時鐘沒有日期，任何為其設定日期的模型，實際上是在假設一次修憲。應以現行條文定價。

The currency runs across all three匯率變數橫跨上述三者

The peso lost 33.6% of its dollar value over thirteen years, a drag of roughly three points a year on a foreign holder's return, while the real effective rate ended where it began. For a cross-border position this is the first-order variable, and it is also the term that amplifies domestic remittance demand.披索十三年間損失 33.6% 的美元價值，對外國持有人的報酬構成每年約三個百分點的拖累，而實質有效匯率則回到起點。就跨境部位而言，此為第一階變數，同時亦為放大國內匯款需求的變數。

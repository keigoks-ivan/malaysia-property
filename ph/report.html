Bottom line總結 A foreign buyer in Manila is not choosing among the properties on the market. The 1987 Constitution removes land from the choice set entirely, and what remains is one product — the condominium unit — under a statutory 40% foreign cap that operates at the level of the condominium corporation, with no central register that would let anyone check how much of that cap a given building has already used.

Inside that narrow channel the record is weaker than the headline suggests. The BIS index for Makati rose 219% in nominal terms between the first quarter of 2008 and the first quarter of 2026, and 70% after inflation. Measured from 2013, the first year with a peso exchange-rate series in this dataset, the same index returns +152% in nominal pesos, +66% after Philippine inflation, and +74% in nominal US dollars, because the peso fell 31% against the dollar over the period.

The demand channel that produces the official price index is contracting. NCR recorded 2,620 bank-financed residential purchases in the first quarter of 2026 against 6,494 in the fourth quarter of 2019; over the same period the rest of the country moved from 7,025 to 6,383, having peaked at 8,758 one quarter earlier. Residential real-estate loans have carried a past-due ratio between 8.8% and 12.9% since 2020, against 2.2% to 2.9% for commercial. And the rent line, which is the entire investment case in a market with no capital-gains engine, has lost to inflation: the NCR actual-rentals index is up 24.0% since January 2018 while NCR headline consumer prices are up 34.5%.

The exit is governed by a rule most buyers never see at purchase. The BSP Manual of Regulations on Foreign Exchange Transactions classifies the acquisition of a condominium unit as foreign direct investment and requires direct registration with the central bank within one year; registration is the precondition for repatriating capital through the banking system's foreign exchange resources. The structural background is set out in the companion Philippines Macro × Property analysis. 外國買方在馬尼拉面對的選擇集，並非市場上的全部標的。1987 年憲法將土地整個排除於外國人可取得的範圍之外，剩下的只有一種產品：大樓公寓單位，且受公寓法 40% 外資上限約束。該上限在區分所有權公司層級運作，並無任何中央登記制度可供查詢單一建案已使用多少額度。

在此一窄通道內，實際紀錄弱於表面數字。BIS 對 Makati 的住宅價格指數，2008 年第一季至 2026 年第一季名目上漲 219%，扣除通膨後為 70%。若自本資料集匯率序列起點的 2013 年起算，同一指數的名目披索報酬為 152%，經菲律賓 CPI 平減後為 66%，以名目美元計為 74%，期間披索兌美元貶值 31%。

產生官方價格指數的那個需求管道正在收縮。NCR 於 2026 年第一季核准 2,620 件住宅房貸，2019 年第四季為 6,494 件；同期全國其餘地區由 7,025 件變為 6,383 件，且前一季曾達 8,758 件的高點。住宅不動產放款的逾期比率自 2020 年以來介於 8.8% 至 12.9%，同期商用不動產放款為 2.2% 至 2.9%。租金端方面，在缺乏資本利得引擎的市場中，租金即為投資論述的全部內容，而該序列已跑輸物價：NCR 實付租金指數自 2018 年 1 月起上漲 24.0%，同期 NCR 整體消費者物價上漲 34.5%。

出場路徑受一項多數買方於承購時未被告知的規定約束。BSP《外匯交易法規手冊》將公寓單位之取得歸類為外人直接投資，並要求於一年內直接向央行登記；該登記為日後透過銀行體系外匯資源辦理資本匯回的前提要件。結構性成因詳見《菲律賓總經 × 房產》。

The comparison starts in the first quarter of 2013 because that is where the peso-dollar series in this dataset begins. Over the thirteen years to the first quarter of 2026 the Makati index returned +152.1% in nominal pesos, +65.6% after Philippine consumer prices, and +73.6% in nominal US dollars, against a peso that lost 31.2% against the dollar over the same window.   • The RPPI is built on acquisition cost, the contract price reported by banks in the quarterly Residential Real Estate Loans return. It is a transaction-price index, not an appraisal or an asking-price index, which puts it in the top tier of price evidence available for this market.RPPI 的基礎為 acquisition cost，即銀行於季報《住宅不動產放款》申報之合約成交價。該指數為成交價指數，非鑑價、非掛牌價，就本市場可得的價格證據而言屬最高一層。
  • Its coverage is also its limit. Cash purchases, non-bank transactions and socialized housing are outside the sample entirely. In a market where developers extend their own instalment terms at scale, the index reports on the segment that banks financed, not on the market.其涵蓋範圍同時構成其限制。現金交易、非銀行交易與社會住宅完全不在樣本內。在開發商大量提供自有分期條件的市場中，該指數描述的是銀行承作的那一段，不是整個市場。

  • On the official year-on-year measure the latest quarter reads NCR +3.5%, the rest of the country +5.7%, national +4.5%. Cumulatively the two geographies are close: NCR is up 66.7% from the first quarter of 2019 and the rest of the country 61.9%. The divergence between them to date is in the quarter-to-quarter path, not in the level.以官方年增率觀察，最新一季為 NCR +3.5%、全國其餘地區 +5.7%、全國 +4.5%。累計而言兩者接近：NCR 自 2019 年第一季上漲 66.7%，全國其餘地區為 61.9%。兩者迄今的分歧在季度路徑，不在水準值。

Two price indices, and one of them is discontinued兩個價格指數，其中一個已停用

Before 2025 the BSP published the RREPI, computed from the appraised value per square metre of new building properties. The RPPI that replaced it is computed from acquisition cost and covers new, pre-owned and foreclosed units. The BSP metadata states the difference in terms. The two indices also use different base periods, 1Q2014=100 against 2019=100. They cannot be spliced into one series, and any long-run Philippine house-price chart that appears to run from 2016 to today on a single line has done exactly that.2025 年之前，BSP 發布的是 RREPI，其計算基礎為新建物件每平方公尺的鑑價。接替的 RPPI 以 acquisition cost 計算，涵蓋新成屋、二手與法拍。BSP 的 metadata 對此差異有明文。兩指數的基期亦不同，分別為 2014Q1＝100 與 2019＝100。兩者不得串接為單一序列；任何自 2016 年連續畫至今日的菲律賓長期房價圖，皆已作了此一串接。

A second boundary matters as much. The NCR index aggregates the entire National Capital Region, most of whose stock a foreign buyer can neither purchase nor would want to. What this rent series can and cannot be used for此租金序列可用與不可用的範圍

The PSA collects rent from fixed residential units at the same addresses every survey period, following the unit rather than the tenant and recording what the current occupant actually pays. The wording is identical in the 2010 CPI User's Manual and in the current 2018-based technical notes, so the rule has not changed. Because the sample is matched to addresses, we ran the batch-repricing test that exposed the Malaysian CPI rent subindex as a quarterly series wearing a monthly label. The Philippine series passes: month-on-month values changed in 85.2% of transitions nationally, 59.4% for NCR and 87.1% outside NCR, and the changes are spread evenly across all twelve calendar months rather than clustering in one.PSA 的採價對象為每期調查皆固定於同一地址的住宅單位，追蹤標的為物件而非房客，並記錄現住戶實際支付的金額。2010 年版《CPI 使用手冊》與現行 2018 年基期技術文件的用字相同，顯示此規則未曾變動。由於樣本以地址配對，本頁對其執行了曾揭露馬來西亞 CPI 租金子項實為季頻的批次重訂價檢定。菲律賓序列通過檢定：月度數值的變動比率為全國 85.2%、NCR 59.4%、NCR 以外 87.1%，且變動平均分布於十二個日曆月，未集中於單一月份。

One limit remains and it is not resolved by that test. Land is out of the choice set土地不在選擇集內

Article XII, Section 7 of the 1987 Constitution bars the transfer or conveyance of private land save in cases of hereditary succession, and only to individuals or entities qualified to acquire public land — meaning Filipino citizens or corporations at least 60% Filipino-owned. Section 8 provides the single exception, for natural-born Filipinos who have lost their citizenship.1987 年憲法第十二條第 7 項禁止私有土地之移轉，繼承情形除外，且僅得移轉予具備取得公地資格之個人或法人，即菲律賓公民或菲籍持股至少 60% 之法人。第 8 項為唯一例外，適用於喪失菲律賓籍之天生菲律賓公民。

The 40% cap sits at the corporation, not the unit40% 上限掛在法人層級，不在單位層級

RA 4726 Section 5 places the 60% Filipino floor on the condominium corporation that holds the common areas, and provides that a transfer which would breach it is void, not merely refused. That is why a foreigner can hold an individual condominium certificate of title at all: the restriction binds at the collective level.RA 4726 第 5 條將 60% 菲籍下限置於持有共有部分之區分所有權公司，並規定違反該下限之移轉無效，而非僅遭駁回。此即外國人得以個別持有公寓所有權狀（CCT）的原因：該限制作用於集合層級。

No register tracks cap utilisation上限使用率無登記可查

Foreign ownership is tracked by each building's own nationality register, maintained by the condominium corporation or developer. No government database aggregates it, and no consultancy publishes a market-level utilisation figure. Whether a given building has room left is a question that can only be answered building by building, by asking.外資持份由各建案自行維護之國籍登記冊追蹤，管理者為區分所有權公司或開發商。並無任何政府資料庫予以彙整，亦無顧問公司發布市場層級的使用率。特定建案是否尚有額度，只能逐案詢問取得。

Presidential Decree 471 of 1974 permits a general foreign lease of private land for 25 years, renewable once, for any purpose. An individual buying a home does not qualify under RA 7652.1974 年第 471 號總統令允許外國人一般性租用私有土地 25 年並得續約一次，不限用途。1993 年 RA 7652 允許 50 年並得續約 25 年，惟僅適用於工業區、觀光專案等達最低投資額門檻之合格投資。個人購置自用住宅不符合 RA 7652 之適用要件。

Path B · The current configuration persists路徑 B · 現行狀態延續base case基準情境

Approvals stay in the 2,400 to 3,700 band, nominal prices rise at low single digits, real rent stays below its 2018 level, and the peso continues to depreciate at roughly its long-run pace. Path C · The unmeasured leverage surfaces路徑 C · 量不到的槓桿浮現unquantifiable無法量化

Developer instalment receivables deteriorate at scale and the losses appear first in developer financial statements and second in the contracts-to-sell book that banks have discounted. Remittances: level and share匯款：金額與占比

The BSP definition of personal remittances explicitly includes capital transfers for the construction of dwellings, which makes this the one demand-side series with an official textual link to housing rather than an inferred one. The dollar total rose every year from US$13.09 billion in 2005 to US$39.62 billion in 2025, with a single decline in 2020. As a share of GDP the same flow fell from 12.78% in 2005 to 9.64% in 2020. Both statements are true and they point in opposite directions: the absolute inflow that supports housing demand keeps growing, and its weight relative to everything else the economy does keeps falling. Put together, a ten-year holder is underwriting three things at once. First, a real capital-growth rate that has averaged about three points a year over eighteen years in the one geography with a long index, against a rent stream that has been losing to inflation for eight. Second, a currency that has taken 31% since 2013 and 9.2% in the last thirteen months, which for a dollar-denominated holder is the largest single term in the equation. Third, an exit that is administratively conditional: the sale itself is unrestricted, but converting the proceeds through the banking system depends on a registration that had to be filed within one year of purchase. None of these three is a market view. All three are observable, and two of them are settled at the moment of purchase rather than at the moment of sale.合併觀察，持有十年者同時承擔三件事。其一，在唯一具備長期指數的地理單位上，十八年間年均約三個百分點的實質資本成長率，對應的是一條已跑輸物價八年的租金流。其二，一個自 2013 年以來貶值 31%、過去十三個月貶值 9.2% 的貨幣；對以美元計價的持有人而言，此為方程式中最大的單一項。其三，一個受行政條件約束的出場：出售本身不受限制，但將價款透過銀行體系換匯，取決於一項須於購置後一年內完成的登記。這三項皆非市場觀點。三項皆可觀察，且其中兩項在購置當下即已決定，而非在出售當下。

Where to act配置建議

Defensible論述成立

A completed unit with a registered condominium certificate of title, bought by a holder who files the BSP Form W registration inside the twelve-month window, and who underwrites the position on rent received rather than on capital growth. This is the only configuration in which every material term is knowable at purchase.已完工、所有權狀已登記之單位，且買方於十二個月窗口內完成 BSP Form W 登記，並以收取租金而非資本成長作為部位的評估基礎。此為所有重要條件皆可於購置時確知的唯一組合。

Requires resolution first須先釐清

Off-plan units, where the title does not yet exist and the SRRV conversion route is unavailable; and anything in Bay Area, where stock rose 14.4% into a legislated removal of the tenant base and the only vacancy evidence is a phrase rather than a number.預售單位，其所有權狀尚未存在，且 SRRV 轉換路徑不適用；以及 Bay Area 的任何標的，該區存量在承租基礎遭立法移除的期間內增加 14.4%，而唯一的空置證據是一句描述而非一個數字。

Not supported by the data資料不支持

Any position underwritten on capital growth in dollar terms, and any use of a submarket price or vacancy figure as an input to a valuation. No official sub-city price series exists, and the private submarket numbers are single-sourced and internally inconsistent.任何以美元計價資本成長為評估基礎的部位；以及任何將分區房價或空置數字作為估值輸入的作法。官方並無次城市層級價格序列，而民間分區數字為單一來源且內部不一致。

Scale note: JB scores are calibrated on JB's own internal anchor scale, and the location dimension (d1) is capped at 7.0 — versus 8.0 for KL and Penang — because none of JB's eight districts carries an irreversible supply constraint the way KL's absolute-limited best addresses or Penang's UNESCO height cap do. Tier bands are also JB-specific (T1 ≥6.0 / T2 ≥5.5 / T3 ≥5.0 / T4 ≥4.5 / T5 <4.5, not the KL/Penang T1 8.0+ scale) and are provisional — derived from JB's structural score range rather than KL/Penang's absolute values, based on a six-card sample concentrated in one corridor. Tier is a ranking within this market, not a cross-market rating — a JB T2 does not mean the same thing as a KL or Penang T2. These numbers are not directly comparable to the KL, Penang, or Bangkok scorecards, which are each calibrated on their own market-specific anchor scale. Airbnb yield is an estimated gross short-stay yield (area ADR × ~60% occupancy ÷ entry price, anchored to comparable stock in the same JB submarket), shown only for serviced apartments that permit and market owner-operated short-stay — an upside scenario for an owner who actively operates (or appoints a manager for) the unit, not the base case. It is not shown for hotel rental-pool products (e.g. a branded 70/30 pool), whose short-stay income accrues on different operator-split economics, nor where building by-laws restrict daily lets. Holding-cost note: a commercial-title SA can switch from the commercial to the domestic electricity tariff (and claim owner-occupier RPGT treatment) only when genuinely used as a residence; running it as short-stay keeps the commercial tariff and forfeits those reliefs — so the cash-flow read differs for a long-let/owner-occupier versus an Airbnb operator. Airbnb 收益是估算的短租毛收益率（拿區域 ADR 乘上約 60% 入住率、再除以入手價，並對齊同一新山次市場內的可比案例），只標在「容許、而且主打屋主自營短租」的服務式公寓上，算的是屋主自己跑（或請人代管）的潛力情境，不是基準。酒店租賃池那一種，例如品牌方代管的 70/30 分潤池，走的是另一套分潤帳；分層細則禁日租的也一樣，都不列這個數。持有成本補一句：商業地契的 SA，要真的拿來自住，才能把商業電費改回住宅費率、也才適用自住 RPGT；一旦拿去跑短租，電費照商業算、那些好處也一併沒了。所以同一間房的現金流，長租自住跟 Airbnb 營運，得拆開來看。
